The Rainier Foothills Wellness Foundation (RFWF) provides essential services to enhance community health and well-being. Programs include the Care Van for transportation to medical appointments, Neighbors Feeding Neighbors for food assistance, and mental health support for students. RFWF is dedicated to serving vulnerable populations in King and Pierce Counties.
