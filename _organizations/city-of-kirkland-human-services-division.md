The City of Kirkland Human Services Division supports equity, housing stability, food assistance, and mental health initiatives through its partnerships with nonprofits. These efforts contribute to building healthy communities and exemplify the City of Kirkland’s priority of providing a strong voice for Eastside health and human services through integrated local infrastructure.
