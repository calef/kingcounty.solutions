POCAAN is a multicultural social service agency in Seattle that addresses health disparities in marginalized communities. Since 1987, it has provided vital health services, including medical case management for HIV-positive individuals, emergency financial assistance for victims of abuse, and intensive case management for individuals facing inequity.
