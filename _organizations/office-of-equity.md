The **Office of Equity** leads statewide efforts to promote equitable access to government services and reduce disparities by coordinating agency equity plans and community navigation support. Relevant to social services through:

- **Employment and Job Support:** inclusive workforce development strategies
- **Education and Learning:** improving access to public education and training opportunities

The Office of Equity helps ensure all Washington residents have fair opportunities to thrive.
