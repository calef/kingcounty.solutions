Eastside Foundation mobilizes philanthropic, corporate, and nonprofit resources to tackle issues such as affordable housing, behavioral health, and racial equity on the Eastside. By strengthening organizational capacity, fostering cross-sector partnerships, and applying research to funding decisions, the foundation aligns strongly with AEA’s mission pillars.
