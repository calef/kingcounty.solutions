The **Department of Community and Human Services (DCHS)** connects people, communities, and organizations to resources that support health, housing, and human potential. Key service areas include:

- **Shelter and Housing:** homeless prevention, affordable housing, and rental assistance
- **Health Care and Mental Health:** behavioral health treatment, recovery, and supportive care programs
- **Employment and Job Support:** workforce development and vocational training
- **Youth and Family Services:** early learning, childcare, and family stability initiatives

DCHS is a cornerstone of King County’s social service delivery system.
