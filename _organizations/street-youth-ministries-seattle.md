Street Youth Ministries Seattle (SYM) provides essential resources and support for unhoused youth and young adults aged 13-27. Services include a Breakfast Program, Drop-in Case Management, and outreach initiatives like Coffee Run, which offers meals and basic necessities. SYM fosters relationships to help clients transition from surviving to thriving.
