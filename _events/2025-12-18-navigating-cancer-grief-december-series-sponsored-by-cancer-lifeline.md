Join the online workshop "Navigating Cancer & Grief," sponsored by Cancer Lifeline, on December 18 from 6 to 7:30 p.m. PST. This compassionate virtual event is tailored for individuals affected by cancer, including patients, survivors, caregivers, and loved ones, who are dealing with grief and loss. Attendees can expect supportive discussions and resources aimed at coping with the emotional challenges associated with cancer. The workshop provides a safe space to share experiences and connect with others facing similar struggles. For more information or to register, contact Cancer Lifeline.
