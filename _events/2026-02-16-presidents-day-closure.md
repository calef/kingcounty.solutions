The Center for Active Living will be closed on Feb. 16, 2026, in observance of Presidents Day. This closure offers a chance for community members to reflect on the significance of the holiday and enjoy time with family and friends. While the center will not be open for regular activities, residents are encouraged to participate in local festivities and events honoring past presidents. Please plan accordingly and visit the center during regular hours before or after the holiday to take advantage of its programs and services.
