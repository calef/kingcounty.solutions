Join the Sisters Hifz Program on Dec. 10 from 5 p.m. to 7 p.m. This recurring event offers an enriching environment for women and girls to learn and memorize the Quran. Participants can expect engaging sessions led by experienced instructors, fostering a supportive community for spiritual growth. 

The program will be held at the designated community center, providing a welcoming space for attendees to connect and deepen their understanding of the Quran. Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your faith and build lasting friendships.
