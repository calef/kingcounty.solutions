Join the Arts & Crafts Meet-Up on Thursday, Dec. 18, 2025, from noon to 2 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA 98116. This weekly gathering invites creative individuals of all ages to work on personal projects while connecting with others who share similar interests. Attendees are encouraged to bring their own materials and creative energy to foster a supportive and inspiring atmosphere. Whether you’re a seasoned artist or just starting, you’ll find encouragement and community among fellow craft enthusiasts.
