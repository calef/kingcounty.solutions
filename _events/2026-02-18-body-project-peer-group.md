feed_content: "<article> <div> <h1>The Body Project</h1> <div> <div> <h2>What is it?</h2>
  <p>The Body Project is a 4-week evidence-based program designed to increase body
  acceptance and decrease body dissatisfaction. It is run as a group where we confront,
  discuss, and fight against appearance ideals pressed upon us by society and social
  media. We also offer an adapted 1-day group for The Body Project some quarters.
  This is a 5-hour day with breaks and lunch provided. </p> <p>The Body Project is
  FREE and offered in collaboration from UW LiveWell and the UW Counseling Center.
  This group is facilitated by trained Peer Health Educators from UW LiveWell. This
  is NOT a therapy group; it is a prevention based educational group.</p> <p>All groups
  are open to all eligible students, regardless of race, color, creed, religion, national
  origin, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, age, marital status, disability,
  or status as a protected veteran.</p> </div> </div> <h2>How do I sign-up?</h2> <p>Groups
  for Winter 2026 are now open! <strong>Sign-up by January 23</strong> at the link
  below. If none of the group times work for you, please add your name to <a>The Body
  Project future interest list</a> and we’ll send you an email when we open groups
  for Spring 2026.</p> <p>Please note that we are offering two different types of
  group: </p> <ul> <li>4-week Body Project peer group that meets for 1 hour per week
  for 4 weeks.</li> <li>1-day Body Project peer group that meets for 5 hours on 1
  day. </li> </ul> <p> <a><span>Schedule The Body Project</span></a></p> <h2>What
  is my commitment?</h2> <p>Participants engage in dialogue with each other in small
  groups of 6-8 people and are expected to attend all four sessions. Groups will have
  weekly 1-hour sessions for four weeks. There are “at-home exercises” participants
  are expected to complete on their own between sessions, that we then share and discuss
  in group. We ask that all members speak in group and take up space, as our shared
  reflections and engagement build the strength of the group.</p> <p>If you are participating
  in the 1-day peer group, you will be expected to attend all 5 hours. There will
  be exercises that participants will complete throughout the session.</p> <h2>Who
  is this for?</h2> <p>Anyone experiencing body dissatisfaction (which we know is
  the majority of adults). If you feel that your relationship with your body could
  be better, then this group is for you!</p> <p>83% of women and 74% of men report
  being dissatisfied with how their bodies look, and for younger adults it’s <a>86%
  of those aged 18-34 that report dissatisfaction</a>. Additionally, <a>the Trevor
  Project shared</a> high rates of body dissatisfaction for non-binary and transgender
  folks with 90% of those surveyed expressing dissatisfaction with their bodies.</p>
  <h2>Questions? </h2> <p>Email <a>bodyproject@uw.edu</a></p> <h2>What students have
  said about The Body Project:</h2> <blockquote> <p>“Thank you for facilitating a
  beautiful environment that feels safe enough to explore damaging costs of pursuing
  the thin ideal and expressing body image issues. This experience has been invaluable.”</p>
  <p><cite><strong>Anonymous Student</strong></cite></p> </blockquote> <blockquote>
  <p>“Thank you so much for initiating this group and crating a safe space for those
  important conversations! The facilitators did an excellent job fostering an open,
  non-judgmental environment that allowed for honest discussion. Their approach was
  both supportive and insightful, making it easier to share and learn from one another.”</p>
  <p><cite><strong>Leo L.</strong></cite></p> </blockquote> <blockquote> <p>“I loved
  it. I wished it was longer, but all good things must come to an end. It helped so
  much to focus on myself (truly and shamelessly) rather than on what is on trend
  or considered pretty right now.”</p> <p><cite><strong>Sofia G.</strong></cite></p>
  </blockquote> </div> </article>"
