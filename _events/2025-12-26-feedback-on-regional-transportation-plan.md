Join the virtual event for feedback on the Regional Transportation Plan starting Dec. 26, 2025. Participants will have the opportunity to share their thoughts and suggestions on the proposed transportation initiatives that will shape the region's future. This online forum aims to engage community members in meaningful discussions, ensuring that diverse perspectives are considered in the planning process. Your input is valuable in creating an effective transportation strategy that meets the needs of all residents. Don't miss this chance to make your voice heard and contribute to the development of a sustainable transportation system.
