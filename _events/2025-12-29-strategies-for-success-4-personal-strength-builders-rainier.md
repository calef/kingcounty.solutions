Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Strategies for Success 4: Personal Strength Builders" on December 29 from 9 a.m. to noon at the Rainier location. This workshop is designed to help participants explore and develop essential skills for workplace success. Attendees will learn how to improve their attitude, boost motivation, overcome fears, and enhance time management skills.

The session will also cover projecting self-worth and integrity, as well as financial literacy, including forming good spending habits. This workshop is part of a flexible series of life-skills modules that can be taken individually or combined for a personalized experience. Registration is required, and accommodations can be arranged by contacting Deitra Garrett at 206-721-7290. Don’t miss this opportunity to discover what makes you shine and build your personal strengths for a successful future.
