Enjoy an enchanting Electric Cello Concert featuring Gretchen Yanover on Dec. 23 from 3 to 5 p.m. at the Tukwila Library, located at 14380 Tukwila International Boulevard. Attendees can expect a captivating performance as Yanover uses her electric cello and loop sampler to create layered, atmospheric sounds that fill the library space. This event is open to all ages, making it a perfect outing for adults, teens, and children alike. Registration is not required, and reasonable accommodations for individuals with disabilities are available by request. For assistance, please email access@kcls.org at least seven days prior to the event. Join us for a unique musical experience in your community!
