Join the AA Holly Chapter for its Sunday meeting on December 7 from 6 p.m. to 8 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room. Attendees can expect a supportive environment to share experiences and gain insights into recovery. This meeting is an opportunity to connect with others on similar journeys, fostering community and personal growth. Whether you are new to the program or a long-time member, all are welcome to participate in discussions and find encouragement.
