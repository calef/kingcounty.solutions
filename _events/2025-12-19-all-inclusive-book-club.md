Join the All-inclusive Book Club on Friday, Dec. 19, 2025, from 1 to 2:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. This interactive gathering invites attendees to contribute their ideas for future book selections. Participants are encouraged to bring a book title and a brief summary to share with the group. Together, members will vote on books to discuss in the upcoming months, fostering a collaborative reading experience. For more information, contact Susan Hagy at susanhagy1@gmail.com or call 206-325-4077.
