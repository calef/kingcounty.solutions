Join the University of Washington Information School for an MLIS Prospective Student Information Session on Feb. 17, 2026, at 3 p.m. PST via Zoom. This session will provide an overview of the Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) program, highlighting how it prepares students for careers in evolving library and information environments. Attendees will learn about the program's foundational courses, elective offerings, and the commitment to diversity, inclusion, and social justice. The event will also cover the differences between residential and online learning formats. Prospective students will have the opportunity to ask questions and connect with advisors to gain insights into admissions, application deadlines, and program expectations. Don’t miss this chance to explore how the MLIS program can help you become a leader in the information field.
