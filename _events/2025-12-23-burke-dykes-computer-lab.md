Join the Burke Dykes Computer Lab on Tuesday, December 23, 2025, from 8:30 a.m. to 4:20 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. Attendees can use Windows computers to check email, browse the internet, and access popular software like Microsoft Office. For those needing assistance, one-on-one help is available through the Digital Navigation service, or you can participate in the Drop-In Digital Navigation program. This event is a great opportunity for individuals looking to enhance their digital skills in a supportive environment.
