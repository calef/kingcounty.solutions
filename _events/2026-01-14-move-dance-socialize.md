Join the "Move, Dance & Socialize" event on Jan. 14, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 10 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This lively gathering invites participants of all fitness levels to enjoy movement, dance, and social interaction. Bring a water bottle, wear comfortable walking shoes, and come ready to have fun. It's a great opportunity to meet new friends while staying active. Don't miss out on this engaging community experience!
