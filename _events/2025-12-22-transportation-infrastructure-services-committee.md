Join the Transportation & Infrastructure Services Committee on Dec. 22 from 5:30 p.m. to 6:45 p.m. for a hybrid meeting held both onsite and virtually via MS Teams. Attendees can expect discussions on key policy recommendations related to transportation, traffic, water, sewer, and public transit, among other critical infrastructure topics. The meeting will take place in the Duwamish Conference Room at the 6300 Building, located at 6300 Southcenter Boulevard. This event is open to the public, allowing community members to engage in important legislative matters. Access details will be provided on the agenda. For more information, contact Laurel Humphrey at 206-433-8993 or email citycouncil@tukwilawa.gov.
