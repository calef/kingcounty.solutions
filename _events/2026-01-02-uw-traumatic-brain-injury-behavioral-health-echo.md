Join the University of Washington for the Traumatic Brain Injury - Behavioral Health ECHO, starting Jan. 2, 2026, at 12 p.m. PST. This monthly online clinic, held on the first and third Fridays, connects front-line providers with specialists to discuss evidence-based treatments and case consultations for managing behavioral health issues in patients with traumatic brain injury (TBI).

Attendees can expect focused didactics on the complexities of TBI-related disabilities, including PTSD, depression, and anxiety, which often complicate recovery. The program is designed for any provider managing post-TBI behavioral health problems across Washington State.

Participants can earn up to 33 AMA PRA Category 1 Credits™ throughout the year. Engage in real-time discussions via Zoom and enhance your understanding of effective interventions for TBI patients. For more information, contact tbi-bh@uw.edu.
