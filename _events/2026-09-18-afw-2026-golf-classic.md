Join the AFW 2026 Golf Classic on September 18-19, 2026, at Monarch Beach Golf Links in Dana Point, California. This annual tournament is one of Southern California’s premier golf events, aimed at supporting Angel Flight West's mission of providing free flights for individuals in need of critical medical care. Participants can enjoy a day of top-tier golf and networking while making a significant impact on the lives of patients requiring essential treatments, such as those in clinical trials or organ transplant recipients. This event is perfect for golf enthusiasts, corporate sponsors, and anyone interested in contributing to a worthy cause. Registration details will be available soon. Join us to help make a difference, one flight at a time.
