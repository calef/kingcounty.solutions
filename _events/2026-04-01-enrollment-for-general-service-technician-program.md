Enrollment for Shoreline's General Service Technician Program begins April 1, 2026. This program offers students hands-on training and essential skills needed for a career in automotive service. Attendees can expect to learn about vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, and repair techniques from experienced instructors.

This quarter, prospective students will have the opportunity to explore the curriculum, ask questions, and understand the enrollment process. Whether you're looking to start a new career or enhance your skills, this program is designed to prepare you for success in the automotive industry. Don't miss your chance to join a community of aspiring technicians and take the first step toward your future.
