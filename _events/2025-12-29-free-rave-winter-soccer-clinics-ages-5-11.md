Join the Free Rave Winter Soccer Clinics for kids ages 5 to 11, starting December 29 at 9:30 a.m. at Foster High School in Tukwila. This engaging clinic will introduce young athletes to soccer through fun drills, games, and scrimmages, focusing on essential skills like ball handling, passing, and teamwork. Each participant will receive a soccer ball and shin guards, courtesy of the RAVE Soccer Foundation. The event is designed to foster a love for the sport and encourage physical activity in a supportive environment. Don't miss this opportunity to kick off the winter season with soccer fun!
