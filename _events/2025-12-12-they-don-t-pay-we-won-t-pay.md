Join us for a lively performance of "They Don't Pay! We Won't Pay!" at the Floyd and Delores Jones Playhouse Theater on Dec. 12, 2025, starting at 7:30 p.m. This comedic play by Dario Fo explores themes of social justice and resistance through humor and wit. Attendees can expect an engaging evening filled with laughter and thought-provoking moments as the characters navigate their struggles against economic inequality. Don’t miss this opportunity to experience a dynamic theatrical production that promises to entertain and inspire.
