Join the Federal Way Arts Commission for the Summer Sounds: FREE Concerts in the Park series on July 30, 2026, from 6:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. at Steel Lake Park, located at 2410 S 312th St, Federal Way, WA. This family-friendly event features a diverse lineup of live music, celebrating various genres and designed for all ages. Attendees can enjoy a vibrant community atmosphere while discovering new artists and enjoying the summer evenings. Admission is free, making it an accessible outing for everyone. Don't miss this opportunity to experience local talent and connect with the community! For more information, call 253-835-6900.
