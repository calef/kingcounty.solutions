Join the Digital Literacy Learning Lab at WorkSource Seattle-King County on Dec. 15 from 12:30 p.m. to 4 p.m. This self-paced lab offers a welcoming environment for individuals of all ages and skill levels to enhance their computer and internet skills. Attendees can expect to learn the basics, including using a mouse and keyboard, as well as more advanced topics like email, word processing, and spreadsheets.

Participants will have access to various resources, including online tutorials and personalized assistance from friendly staff. Whether you're new to computers or looking to improve your skills, this lab provides the perfect opportunity to learn at your own pace. Visit the lab during open hours to get started and receive support in navigating the learning management system. Don't miss this chance to boost your digital literacy!
