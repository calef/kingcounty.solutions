The King County Flood Control District (FCD) will hold its regular board meeting on January 12, 2027, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. in the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. This meeting occurs on the second Tuesday of each month, following the King County Council meeting. Members of the public are encouraged to attend, either in person or via live stream on the King County Council’s website, as well as on King County TV, available on Xfinity channels 22 and 322 (HD) and Astound Broadband channels 22 and 711 (HD).
