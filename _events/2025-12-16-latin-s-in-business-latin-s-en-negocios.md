Join the Latin@s in Business event on Dec. 16 from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S in Seattle. This free program is designed for entrepreneurs looking to refine their business ideas and prepare for a successful 2026. Attendees can expect to learn essential business fundamentals, develop a solid business plan, and gain insights on licenses, digital strategies, finance, and branding. The event also offers a network of experts for additional support. Don’t miss this opportunity to invest in your future and take the next step in your entrepreneurial journey.
