Join the NW Lymphedema Center for "The Importance of Fall Prevention" on December 13 from 12:30 to 1:30 p.m. PST, held online through Cancer Lifeline. This informative class will provide attendees with practical tips to enhance home safety and minimize the risk of falls, particularly for those affected by cancer treatment, which can impact balance and strength. Participants will learn how to make their living spaces safer, reducing the likelihood of slips, trips, and falls. Don't miss this opportunity to gain valuable insights into fall prevention. For registration assistance, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500, available Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
