Join us for a Career Fair on Dec. 11, 2025, in Newport News, Virginia, designed specifically for veterans seeking employment opportunities. This event will feature a variety of local employers eager to connect with job seekers. Attendees can expect to engage in one-on-one conversations with recruiters, attend workshops on resume building and interview skills, and gather valuable resources tailored to veterans transitioning into the civilian workforce.
