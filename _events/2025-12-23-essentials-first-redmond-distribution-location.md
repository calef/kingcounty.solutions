Essentials First will host a distribution event in Redmond on December 23, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Community members can walk in to receive essential hygiene kits, snack packs, and ORCA cards. This event aims to support individuals and families facing hardships, including low-income households, the unhoused, immigrants, and caregivers. There are no strict eligibility requirements, making it accessible to anyone in need of hygiene supplies. Attendees are encouraged to bring an ID and may need to fill out a short intake form, with all personal information kept confidential. All are welcome to attend and benefit from this vital community resource.
