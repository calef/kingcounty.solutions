Join us at the Tukwila Library on December 23 from 5 to 7:30 p.m. for a free screening of the classic 1955 musical, "Guys and Dolls." This Academy Award-nominated film features legendary stars Frank Sinatra, Marlon Brando, and Jean Simmons in a story about a gambler and a missionary who unexpectedly fall in love amidst a bet with hidden motives. The event is suitable for adults ages 18 and older. Reasonable accommodations for people with disabilities are available upon request; email access@kcls.org at least seven days in advance. Automated closed captioning will be provided. Don’t miss this opportunity to enjoy a beloved musical in a community setting at 14380 Tukwila International Boulevard, Tukwila, WA.
