Join the Wellness Coaching Circle on December 17, 2025, from 9 to 10:30 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. Led by certified health and wellness coach Becky Johnson, this six-week course offers a supportive environment to help you build healthy habits and enhance your overall well-being. Participants can expect guided discussions, self-discovery activities, and practical tips to inspire daily wellness. The class is limited to 12 attendees, so RSVP is required. Discover what wellness means to you and take small steps toward a healthier lifestyle. Note that there will be no class on November 26.
