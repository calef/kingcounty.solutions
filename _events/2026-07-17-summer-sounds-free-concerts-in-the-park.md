Join the Federal Way Arts Commission for the Summer Sounds: FREE Concerts in the Park on July 16, 2026, from 6:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. at Steel Lake Park, located at 2410 S 312th St, Federal Way, WA. This family-friendly event features a diverse lineup of live music, celebrating various genres every Wednesday evening throughout July. Admission is free, making it an excellent opportunity for all ages to enjoy a night of entertainment in the park. Don’t miss out on this vibrant community event! For more information, contact the park at 253-835-6900.
