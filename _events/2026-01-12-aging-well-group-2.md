Join the Aging Well - Group #2 on Monday, Jan. 12, 2026, from 11 a.m. to noon at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This weekly in-person support group provides a safe space for participants to share experiences and support one another through discussions. All conversations are confidential, fostering a trusting environment. Please note that pre-registration is required for attendance; those who are not pre-registered will not be admitted. For more information about Group #2, contact Michele Fawcett-Long at 206-928-1730.
