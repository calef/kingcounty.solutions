The KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop will take place on September 12, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. (PDT) via an online platform. This free workshop is designed for young people and their families across King County, focusing on real-life topics related to conflict, communication, and justice. Each session encourages participation in a welcoming virtual space, with no preparation required—just bring your voice and an open mind. The workshop series covers various modules, including conflict resolution foundations, skill-building, and critical awareness. This month’s session will explore foundational topics in conflict resolution. Interested participants can RSVP through the provided link. For more information, visit the Dispute Resolution Center of King County's website.
