Join us for the Course Accessibility Lunch & Learn on Feb. 3, 2026, at 12 p.m. via Zoom. This engaging session will focus on "Seven Practical Moves Toward Disability Justice in the Classroom." Attendees can expect to learn actionable strategies to make their teaching more inclusive and accessible for all students. This event is designed for educators looking to enhance their understanding of disability justice and implement effective practices in their classrooms. Registration is required to participate, so be sure to secure your spot for this important discussion on fostering an equitable learning environment.
