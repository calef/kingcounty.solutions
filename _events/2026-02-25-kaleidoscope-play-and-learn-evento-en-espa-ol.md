Kaleidoscope Play & Learn, an event conducted in Spanish, will take place on Feb. 25, 2026, from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle, WA. This gathering is designed for Family, Friend, and Neighbor caregivers, parents, and young children to connect with others in their community while learning about healthy child development and school readiness. Participants will engage in play-based learning in a culturally responsive environment, led by community members. This event is ideal for families seeking to enhance their children's early learning experiences and foster community connections.
