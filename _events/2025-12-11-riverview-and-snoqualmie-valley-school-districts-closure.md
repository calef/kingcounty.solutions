Riverview and Snoqualmie Valley School Districts will be closed on Dec. 11, 2025. This closure affects all students and staff within the districts, providing a day off from regular classes and activities. Families are encouraged to plan accordingly, as there will be no school-related events or services available. This is an opportunity for students to enjoy a break, engage in family activities, or catch up on personal projects. Please note that regular school operations will resume on the following school day.
