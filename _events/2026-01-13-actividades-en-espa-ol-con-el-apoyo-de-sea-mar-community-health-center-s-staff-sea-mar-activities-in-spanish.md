Join the Sea Mar Community Health Center staff for "Actividades En Español" on Jan. 13, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. This event welcomes all members of the Latinx community to enjoy a full day of fun activities, socialization, and lunch, along with access to valuable information and resources in Spanish. It's a great opportunity to connect with others and participate in engaging programs designed to support the community. For more information, contact Yanin Diaz at 206-227-6025.
