Join us for the Winter Accessibility POP Session 6: Accessible Presentation Delivery on March 11, 2026, at 3 p.m. This event will focus on strategies and techniques for delivering presentations that are accessible to all audiences. Attendees can expect to learn about inclusive design principles and practical tips for enhancing the accessibility of their presentations. This session is ideal for educators, professionals, and anyone interested in improving their communication skills. The event will take place online via Zoom, providing a convenient platform for participation. Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your presentation skills while promoting accessibility in your work.
