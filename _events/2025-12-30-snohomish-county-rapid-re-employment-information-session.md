Join the Snohomish County Rapid Re-Employment Information Session on Dec. 30 from 1 to 3:30 p.m. This free virtual event is designed for individuals facing layoffs and will provide valuable resources for re-employment. Attendees can expect to learn about unemployment insurance, job search assistance, education and training opportunities, and healthcare options.

The session will feature expert presenters who will guide participants through available resources to help them retrain, recertify, or secure new employment. While the presentation is primarily in English, translation services can be requested in advance. This event is open to the public, and registration is encouraged. For more information, contact Tyler Wright at 425-258-6300 or via email. Don't miss this opportunity to gain essential tools for navigating your career transition.
