Join El Centro de la Raza for the Café con El Centro de la Raza Tour on March 5 from 8:30 to 9:30 a.m. at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle. This monthly event invites community members to explore the historic Beacon Hill building, learn about the organization's rich history, and discover the impactful work of its programs. Attendees can enjoy coffee and snacks while participating in a guided tour of the facility. This is a great opportunity to find out more about volunteering and how to contribute to building a “Beloved Community.” Come connect with others and see what El Centro de la Raza has to offer.
