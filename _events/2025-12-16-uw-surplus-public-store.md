The UW Surplus Public Store will be open on Dec. 16, 2025, at the Plant Services Building. Attendees can browse a variety of surplus items, including furniture, electronics, and other goods at affordable prices. This event provides a unique opportunity to find bargains while supporting sustainability efforts by reusing university surplus. The store will be open to the public, making it a great chance for community members to discover useful items and support the University of Washington's commitment to resource conservation.
