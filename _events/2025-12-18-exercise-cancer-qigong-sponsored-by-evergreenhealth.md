Join EvergreenHealth for "Exercise & Cancer: Qigong," a virtual event designed to enhance well-being through gentle movement, breath, and mindfulness. This class will take place online on December 18 from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. PST. Participants of all ability levels are welcome, whether you are new to Qigong or have prior experience. The practice aims to boost energy, improve mental clarity, and foster emotional balance. Attendees can expect a supportive environment to explore the healing benefits of this ancient art form. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with others while focusing on your health.
