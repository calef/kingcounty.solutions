CISC will host a free Medical Insurance Consulting Service on Feb. 3 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 123, Redmond, WA. This event offers attendees the opportunity to receive person-centered assistance in navigating healthcare coverage options, including Medicare and Medicaid. The Healthcare Access Team will provide workshops and outreach to empower community members, particularly low-income seniors and individuals with disabilities, to make informed decisions about their healthcare benefits. Services will be available in Cantonese and Mandarin, ensuring accessibility for a broader audience. Join CISC to learn about various assistance programs, including the Low-income Subsidy and prescription drug assistance.
