Join Kenny Mandell for a Hand Drumming Workshop on Tuesday, Jan. 13, 2026, from 2:30 to 4 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This engaging workshop promises relaxation, energy, and well-being, making it suitable for all skill levels—no prior experience is necessary. Participants will have access to drums and rattles, though you're welcome to bring your own. Discover the joy of rhythm and community in this fun and interactive session.
