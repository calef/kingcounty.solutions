Join the Living with Cancer Support Group sponsored by Virginia Mason Franciscan Health on Dec. 17 from 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. PST. This online event offers a safe space for individuals who are newly diagnosed, undergoing treatment, or are post-treatment to connect and share their experiences. Attendees can expect educational presentations, open discussions, and valuable insights into navigating the cancer journey. This support group is specifically designed for patients, providing a compassionate environment to foster understanding and healing. If you need assistance with registration or joining the session, please contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
