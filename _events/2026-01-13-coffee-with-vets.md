Join local veterans for "Coffee With Vets" on Jan. 13, 2026, from 11 a.m. to noon at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This event offers a relaxed atmosphere for veterans to enjoy coffee and engage in meaningful conversations. Attendees can share stories from their military experiences and discuss a variety of topics. After the coffee chat, participants are welcome to stay for lunch, making it a great opportunity to connect with fellow veterans and build community bonds. Don't miss this chance to socialize and support one another.
