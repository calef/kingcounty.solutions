The Fort Hood Job Fair will take place on March 11, 2026, offering job seekers an opportunity to connect with potential employers. Attendees can expect to meet representatives from various industries, explore career options, and participate in on-site interviews. This event is designed to assist veterans, active-duty service members, and their families in finding employment. The job fair will be held at Fort Hood, providing a convenient location for those in the surrounding community. Don’t miss this chance to advance your career and network with local businesses.
