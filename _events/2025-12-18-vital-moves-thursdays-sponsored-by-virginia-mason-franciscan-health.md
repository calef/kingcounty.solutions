Join the Vital Moves Thursdays series, sponsored by Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, for an engaging online session on December 18 from 5:30 to 6:30 p.m. PST. Participants will learn gentle, upbeat movements designed to enhance energy, strength, clarity, and balance. This class is ideal for patients, survivors, and caregivers, particularly those affected by lymphedema. The supportive environment fosters connection and encourages physical well-being. Don't miss this opportunity to improve your health and meet others in the community. For assistance with registration or joining the class, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500, available Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
