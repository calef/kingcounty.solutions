Submit your claims for the HTF Repair Fund by February 27, 2026. This fund assists individuals and families who incurred costs between July 1, 2025, and January 25, 2026. Participants can expect to provide documentation of expenses related to repairs.

Ensure all claims are submitted by the deadline to secure potential reimbursement. This is a crucial opportunity for those affected to receive financial support for their repair needs. Don’t miss the chance to take advantage of this program designed to help the community recover from recent challenges.
