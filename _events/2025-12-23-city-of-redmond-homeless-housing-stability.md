The City of Redmond will host a Homeless & Housing Stability event on Dec. 23 from 1 to 3 p.m. at the Advocate Office located at Together Center, 16305 NE 87th St., Suite 111, Redmond, WA. This initiative, in partnership with other Eastside cities and nonprofits, aims to provide essential services to individuals experiencing homelessness. Attendees can expect to receive support including ID assistance, agency referrals, shelter options for men, women, and families, as well as safe parking referrals. The program team, including the City’s Homeless Program Administrator and Program Coordinator, will be available to discuss tailored solutions for those in need. This event is part of a series held every Tuesday, offering a compassionate approach to addressing homelessness in the community.
