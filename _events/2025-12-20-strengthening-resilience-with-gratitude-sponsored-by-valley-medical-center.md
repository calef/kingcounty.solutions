Join Valley Medical Center for "Strengthening Resilience With Gratitude," an online event on Dec. 20 from 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. PST. This engaging presentation, led by Melissa Layer, MA, LMHC, will explore how gratitude can enhance resilience, particularly for those living with cancer. Participants can expect a blend of photography, poetry, and journal prompts designed to foster a meaningful practice of gratitude. This supportive session aims to provide attendees with accessible tools for coping and finding joy amid challenges. Don't miss this opportunity to cultivate resilience through gratitude in a welcoming online environment.
