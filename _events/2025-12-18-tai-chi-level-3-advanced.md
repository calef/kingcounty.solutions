Join the Tai Chi Level 3 Advanced class on December 18, 2025, from 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This class, led by instructor Richard Lazeres, is designed for experienced practitioners looking to enhance their skills. Participants can expect to improve balance, energy, strength, and coordination through a blend of exercises that focus on health benefits and longevity. This is an excellent opportunity for those who have completed Levels 1 and 2 and are ready to take their tai chi practice to the next level.
