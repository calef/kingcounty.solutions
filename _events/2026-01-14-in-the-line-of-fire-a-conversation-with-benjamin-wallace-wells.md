Join The New Yorker staff writer Benjamin Wallace-Wells for "In the Line of Fire," a conversation addressing the rise of political violence in the U.S. This event will take place on January 14 at 3:30 PM PST in the Communications Building (CMU) at the University of Washington.

Wallace-Wells will discuss insights from his recent article, drawing on interviews with elected officials. Attendees can expect to learn about the increasing visibility of political violence, how officials are responding to these threats, and the broader implications for American democracy. This timely conversation promises to provide critical perspectives on one of the most pressing challenges facing civil society today. Don't miss this opportunity for engagement and discussion on a vital topic.
