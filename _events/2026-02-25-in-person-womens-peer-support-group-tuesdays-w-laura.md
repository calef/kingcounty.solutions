Join the Women’s Peer Support Group led by Laura on the second and fourth Tuesdays of each month, starting February 24, 2026, from 5:45 to 8:00 PM at Fall City Methodist Church, 4326 337th Pl SE, Fall City, WA. This in-person support group provides a welcoming, judgment-free space for women to connect, share experiences, and offer mutual support. No formal diagnosis is required, and all women are encouraged to attend. The group emphasizes confidentiality and practical coping strategies. For more information, contact Laura via email.
