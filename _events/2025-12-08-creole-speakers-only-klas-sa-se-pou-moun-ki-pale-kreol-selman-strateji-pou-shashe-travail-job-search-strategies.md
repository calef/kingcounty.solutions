Attend the "Creole Speakers ONLY - Job Search Strategies" workshop on Dec. 8 from 3 to 4 p.m. via Zoom. This event is designed specifically for individuals who speak Creole and are seeking effective job search techniques. Participants will learn various strategies, including how to identify potential employers and utilize personal networks to uncover job opportunities that may not be publicly advertised.

To join, attendees must register in advance. The workshop is approved as a job search activity for those receiving unemployment insurance. For more information, contact Maryse Brock at Maryse.Brock@esd.wa.gov. Don't miss this chance to enhance your job search skills in a supportive, language-appropriate environment.
