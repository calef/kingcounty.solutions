Join us for Drop-in Digital Navigation on Tuesday, Dec. 23, 2025, from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. This event offers attendees the opportunity to receive personalized assistance with their cell phones, tablets, or personal computers. Participants can queue up for a 25-minute session with a tech expert, making it easy to get answers to your digital questions. No appointment is necessary, and a suggested donation of $5 is appreciated. Before attending, please call 206-932-4044, ext. 114, to confirm the computer lab schedule for the day.
