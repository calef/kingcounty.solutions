Organizations are reminded that the deadline to submit responses for the Washington State School-Based Health Alliance (WA SBHA) 2024-2025 Census is February 27, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. PST. This census provides vital data that helps shape school-based health programs across the state. Participants can expect to contribute to a comprehensive understanding of the services offered in their communities, ensuring that school health initiatives are effectively tailored to meet student needs. By engaging in this important process, organizations will play a crucial role in enhancing health resources and support for students. Don’t miss the opportunity to make your voice heard and positively impact school health services in Washington.
