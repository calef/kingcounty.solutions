Join the Men's Support Group #2 on Monday, December 22, 2025, from 11:45 a.m. to 12:45 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This group provides a safe and supportive environment for men to discuss the challenges of aging, share ideas, and explore resources together. Whether you're looking for camaraderie or insights, this gathering is an opportunity to connect with others facing similar experiences. To register, contact social worker Josh Maduell at 206-932-4044, ext. 2, or via email.
