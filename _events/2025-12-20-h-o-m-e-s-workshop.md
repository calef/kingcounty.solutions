Join the H.O.M.E.S Workshop on December 20 from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at First Place School, located at 172 20th Ave, Seattle. This free event is designed to assist seniors, veterans, and individuals with disabilities in completing applications for property tax exemptions. With the income limit recently raised to $84,000, more residents in King County may now qualify for this valuable assistance.

Attendees will have the opportunity to meet with counselors who will guide them through the application process, ensuring they have the support needed to navigate the forms. Whether you're unsure about your eligibility or need help with paperwork, this workshop aims to empower you with the knowledge and resources necessary for successful application.
