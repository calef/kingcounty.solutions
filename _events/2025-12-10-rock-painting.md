Join us for a fun-filled rock painting session on Dec. 10 from 1 to 2:30 p.m. at the Lux Room. Attendees can unleash their creativity while painting rocks, which make great gifts and can brighten anyone's day. No prior experience is necessary, and all materials will be provided for just $4. This event is part of a series held on the second and fourth Wednesdays of each month, making it a perfect opportunity to relax and express yourself. Don't miss out on this enjoyable personal enrichment activity!
