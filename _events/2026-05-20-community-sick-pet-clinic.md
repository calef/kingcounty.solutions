The Community Sick Pet Clinic will take place on May 20, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle, WA. This event offers sliding-scale veterinary care for individuals experiencing homelessness or those with low incomes. Services are provided on a first-come, first-served basis, with a limit of two pets per family. Attendees should be aware that a code of conduct applies. This clinic is an essential resource for pet owners in need of affordable veterinary services.
