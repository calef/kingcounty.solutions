Starting January 12, 2026, northbound I-5 will experience a months-long two-lane reduction. Motorists should expect delays and plan for additional travel time. This reduction is part of ongoing maintenance aimed at improving road safety and infrastructure.

Drivers are encouraged to seek alternate routes when possible and stay informed about traffic updates. Be prepared for potential congestion, especially during peak travel hours. Your patience is appreciated as crews work to enhance the roadway for safer travel.
