WorkSource will take place on December 22 from 1 p.m. to 5 p.m. at the Advocate Office in the Together Center, located at 16305 NE 87th St., Suite 111, Redmond, WA. This event offers attendees the opportunity to explore employment options, receive job training, and get assistance with job searches. Whether you are looking for a new job or seeking to enhance your skills, WorkSource provides valuable resources and support in a welcoming environment. Drop by during the event hours to connect with experts and learn more about available opportunities.
