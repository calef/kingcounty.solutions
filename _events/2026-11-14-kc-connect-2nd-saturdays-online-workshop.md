Join the KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop on November 14, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. PST. This free, youth-centered event is designed for young people and their families throughout King County, Washington. Each month, participants will engage in discussions about real-life topics related to conflict, communication, and justice in a welcoming virtual environment. The workshop series covers various modules, with the November session focusing on "Power, Privilege, and Identity in Conflict." No preparation is required—just bring your voice and an open mind. This is a great opportunity for youth to develop skills in conflict resolution and build community connections. For more information and to RSVP, visit the Dispute Resolution Center of King County's website.
