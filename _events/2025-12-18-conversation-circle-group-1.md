Join the Conversation Circle - Group #1 on Thursday, December 18, 2025, from 1 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This engaging group offers a welcoming space for attendees to share stories, build connections, and enhance their communication skills. Participants will learn to enjoy open discussions that foster deeper friendships and help combat loneliness. For more information about Group #1, contact Cynthia Fuda at cindifuda@yahoo.com.
