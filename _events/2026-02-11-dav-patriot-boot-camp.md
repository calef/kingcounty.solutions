Join us for the DAV Patriot Boot Camp in Myrtle Beach, South Carolina, from February 11 to February 13, 2026. This empowering program is designed for transitioning service members, veterans, and military spouses, offering valuable resources in education, mentorship, and community support.

Attendees will engage in hands-on workshops, hear from experienced speakers, and connect with fellow veterans and military families. Participants will gain insights into career development, entrepreneurship, and personal growth, all tailored to the unique challenges faced by military communities.

Don’t miss this opportunity to network, learn, and build a supportive community that fosters success and resilience. Whether you’re looking to enhance your skills or explore new career paths, the DAV Patriot Boot Camp is a vital step toward achieving your goals.
