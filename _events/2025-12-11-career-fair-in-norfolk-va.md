Join us for a Career Fair in Norfolk, Virginia, on Dec. 11, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. This event is specifically designed to support veterans in their job search. Attendees will have the opportunity to connect with local employers actively seeking to hire veterans, explore various career paths, and access valuable resources tailored to their unique needs.

Participants can attend workshops on resume writing and interview techniques, helping them enhance their job readiness. Networking opportunities will allow veterans to meet fellow job seekers and industry professionals, fostering connections that could lead to employment. Whether you are a recent veteran or transitioning to civilian life, this career fair aims to empower you on your journey to finding meaningful work. Don’t miss this chance to take the next step in your career.
