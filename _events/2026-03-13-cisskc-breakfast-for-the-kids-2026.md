Join the CISSKC for the Breakfast for the Kids fundraiser on March 13, 2026, from 7 to 9 a.m. at Kent-Meridian High School, located at 10020 SE 256th St. in Kent, Washington. This annual event will feature inspiring stories from students, showcasing their successes and needs, while highlighting collaborative efforts across the Kent School District and Federal Way Public Schools. Attendees can enjoy a complimentary breakfast and learn more about the impact of their support. Donations are welcome, both online and at the event. Don't miss this opportunity to engage with the community and support local students.
