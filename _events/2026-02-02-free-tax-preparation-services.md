Get your taxes done for free at this community event, offering tax preparation services for individuals earning less than $80,000 and couples earning less than $96,000. Starting February 2, 2026, you can visit one of 20 locations across King County or take advantage of a virtual option.

Trained volunteers will assist you in filing your tax returns accurately and efficiently, ensuring you receive any eligible credits and deductions. Bring necessary documents, such as your W-2s, 1099s, and identification, to make the process smoother.

This event aims to alleviate the financial burden of tax preparation and provide essential support to the community. Don’t miss this opportunity to get your taxes filed at no cost.
