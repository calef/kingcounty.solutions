The DAV Scholarships program will begin on Jan. 1, 2026, providing $140,000 in scholarships to students under 21 who complete 100 hours of volunteer service in the name of Disabled American Veterans (DAV). Attendees can learn about the application process and requirements while discovering how volunteering can impact their education and future. This initiative not only supports students but also encourages community involvement and service. Join us to explore opportunities for making a difference and earning financial assistance for your education.
