Join EvergreenHealth for "Exercise & Cancer: Gentle Yoga," an online class designed to support individuals affected by cancer. Starting December 19 at 10 a.m. PST, participants can expect a calming session featuring easy stretches, postures, guided meditation, and breathing exercises. This gentle therapeutic yoga series aims to enhance well-being and promote relaxation in a supportive environment. Classes are held every Friday morning, providing a consistent opportunity for self-care and connection. No prior experience is necessary, making it accessible for everyone. For any registration assistance, contact the Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
