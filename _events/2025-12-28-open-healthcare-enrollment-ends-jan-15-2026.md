Open Healthcare Enrollment begins on Dec. 28, 2025, and runs until Jan. 15, 2026. This virtual event offers attendees the opportunity to explore new health and dental plans for the upcoming year. Participants can connect with WA Health Plan Finder for personalized assistance in over 200 languages, ensuring that everyone can find a plan that fits their needs. Individuals who qualify may also gain access to Apple Health (Medicaid), which provides free or low-cost health insurance coverage. Don't miss this chance to secure your healthcare coverage for 2026.
