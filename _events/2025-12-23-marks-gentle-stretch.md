Join us for Mark's Gentle Stretch on Dec. 23 from 11 a.m. to noon in the VelDwyk Room. This event focuses on stretching techniques that enhance joint movement and improve posture. Participants can expect to relieve muscle tension, reduce injury risk, and boost circulation, balance, and coordination. The session is suitable for all fitness levels, making it a perfect opportunity to unwind and connect with friends. Registration is encouraged, and the cost is $3 for members and $5 for non-members. Don’t miss this chance to enhance your well-being through gentle stretching.
