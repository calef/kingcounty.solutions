Join Anna Ponder for a lively Soul Line Dance class on December 10 from 1 to 2 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room. This fun and engaging event welcomes participants of all ages, making it a perfect opportunity to learn new dance moves while enjoying a vibrant atmosphere. The class is designed to promote fitness and social interaction, encouraging attendees to connect with others in the community. The cost is $3 for members and $5 for non-members. Don’t miss out on this chance to get moving and have fun!
