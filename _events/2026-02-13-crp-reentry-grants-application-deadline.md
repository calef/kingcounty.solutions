The application deadline for the CRP Reentry Grants is February 13, 2026. These grants are designed to support individuals transitioning back into their communities after incarceration. Applicants can expect financial assistance to help cover costs related to housing, employment, and other essential needs. This program aims to promote successful reintegration and reduce recidivism by providing vital resources. Interested individuals are encouraged to prepare their applications in advance to ensure they meet the deadline.
