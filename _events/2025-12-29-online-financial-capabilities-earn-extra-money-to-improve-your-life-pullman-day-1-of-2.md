Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for an online workshop, "Financial Capabilities: Earn Extra Money to Improve Your Life," on December 29 from 2:30 p.m. to 4:30 p.m. This is the first of a two-part series designed to help participants learn about managing debt and strategies to enhance their monthly income. The workshop is part of a flexible six-week program that allows attendees to customize their experience by choosing from various units that can be taken in any order. Registration is required, and participants are encouraged to create an account at WorkSourceWA.com prior to attending. This workshop is conducted in English and is approved as a job search activity for unemployment insurance. For accommodations, please contact Karla Hood at karla.hood@esd.wa.gov.
