Join the fun at Touch-a-Truck on May 16 from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Wild Waves Theme & Water Park in Federal Way. This free family event invites children and adults to explore a variety of vehicles, including police cars, dump trucks, and ambulances. Attendees will have the opportunity to climb aboard and get hands-on experience with the equipment that serves and protects the community. With vehicles from Federal Way Public Works, the Federal Way Police Department, South King Fire & Rescue, and more, this event promises to spark imagination and excitement. Rain or shine, come out and enjoy a day of exploration and fun!
