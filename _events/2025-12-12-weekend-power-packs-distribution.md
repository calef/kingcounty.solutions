Join us for the Weekend Power Packs Distribution on Dec. 12, 2025, starting at 10 a.m. This community event will take place at two locations: Empower Youth Network, located at 31951 E. Bird Street in Carnation, and the Riverview School District Office at 15510 1st Ave NE in Duvall. 

Families are invited to pick up nutritious food packs designed to support Riverview students over the weekend. Each pack contains healthy snacks and meals, ensuring students have access to food when school is not in session. This initiative aims to alleviate food insecurity and promote well-being among local children. 

Come and learn more about the resources available in our community while helping to nourish our youth. We look forward to seeing you there!
