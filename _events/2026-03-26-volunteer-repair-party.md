Join Bike Works for a Volunteer Repair Party on March 26, 2026, from 6 p.m. to 9 p.m. at the Bike Works Warehouse, located at 3715 S Hudson St #111 in Seattle. This event invites adults aged 18 and up to participate in bike repair, regardless of experience level. It's a great opportunity to learn new skills and support the community. Volunteers will assist with various bike repairs and maintenance tasks, helping to keep bicycles in good condition. Prior to attending, all participants must complete an application and orientation process. Whether you're a seasoned mechanic or a beginner, your help is welcome at this engaging community event.
