Join Antioch University Santa Barbara for a virtual information session about the Master of Arts in Clinical Psychology program on Jan. 15, 2026, from 4 to 5 p.m. PST. Attendees will have the opportunity to learn about degree requirements, career outcomes, and specialized concentrations in Latinx Mental Health and Somatic Psychotherapy. The session will feature faculty discussions, emphasizing the program's commitment to fostering authentic relationships and understanding diverse identities. This award-winning program focuses on promoting positive change within diverse communities and prioritizes social justice. Don't miss this chance to explore how you can advance your education and career in clinical psychology.
