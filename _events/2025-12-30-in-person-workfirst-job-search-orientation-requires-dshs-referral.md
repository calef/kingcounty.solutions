---

Attend the WorkFirst Job Search Orientation on Dec. 30 from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at WorkSource Spokane, located at 130 S. Arthur St., Spokane, WA. This in-person event is designed for individuals who have received a referral from the Department of Social and Health Services (DSHS).

Participants will learn about job search strategies and resources available through WorkSource. To attend, you must complete a job seeker account and an Employment Skills Assessment prior to the orientation. Arrive on time, as latecomers will need to reschedule. Childcare arrangements are recommended, as the orientation may last up to 2.5 hours. For further inquiries, contact the WIOA Program at 509-532-3120.
