Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Strategies for Success 3: Communication" on Dec. 15, 2025, from 9 a.m. to noon at the Rainier location. This workshop focuses on enhancing communication skills essential for job seekers. Attendees will learn about interpersonal and business communication, develop listening and writing abilities, and discover their unique communication styles. The session will cover verbal and non-verbal cues, conflict resolution techniques, and tips for effective self-presentation across various platforms, including phone, email, and social media. This workshop is part of a series designed to provide flexible and customizable life skills training. Registration is required, and participants are encouraged to create a job-seeker account at WorkSourceWA.com before attending. For accommodations, contact Deitra Garrett at 206-721-6052.
