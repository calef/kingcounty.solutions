Join the Brothers Hifz Program, a recurring event designed for individuals seeking to enhance their memorization of the Quran. Attendees can expect a supportive environment focused on developing their skills and knowledge. The program meets every Tuesday and Thursday, starting December 9, from 5 p.m. to 8 p.m. 

This event will take place at the designated community location. Whether you are beginning your journey or looking to refine your memorization techniques, the Brothers Hifz Program offers valuable guidance and community support. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with others and deepen your understanding of the Quran.
