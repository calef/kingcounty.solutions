The Service Patriots Hiring Event will take place on Dec. 15, 2025, from 10 a.m. to noon at 6511 E. 18th St., Vancouver, Washington. This event is an excellent opportunity for job seekers interested in Sales Associate positions. Attendees can expect to meet with hiring representatives, showcase their resumes, and discuss potential employment opportunities. Ideal candidates will have a go-getter attitude and a dedication to thriving in the workplace. Previous sales experience is encouraged, and applicants should dress for success. The hiring event will also provide information about hourly pay plus commission. This is a drug-free workplace, including THC. Don’t miss the chance to jumpstart your career with Service Patriots!
