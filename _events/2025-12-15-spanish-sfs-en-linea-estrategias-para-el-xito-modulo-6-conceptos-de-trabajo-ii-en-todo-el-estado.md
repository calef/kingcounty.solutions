Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the (Spanish SFS) EN LINEA Estrategias para el Éxito: Modulo 6 Conceptos de Trabajo II, a series of online workshops designed to enhance life skills. This event runs from December 15 to December 18, 2025, with sessions held daily from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. Participants will engage in flexible and customizable workshops that can be taken individually or combined with other modules for a tailored experience. Each four-day module can be attended in any order.

Registration is required, and attendees should create an account on WorkSourceWA.com before joining. The workshops will be conducted via Zoom, with access details provided via email at least one hour prior to the start. For more information, contact Raquel Castillo at 509-766-4144 or maria.castillo@esd.wa.gov.
