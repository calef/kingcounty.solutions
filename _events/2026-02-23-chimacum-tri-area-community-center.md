The SmileMobile will visit the Tri Area Community Center in Chimacum from Feb. 23 to Feb. 27, offering free dental care for infants, children, teenagers, and pregnant or postpartum individuals. This fully equipped mobile clinic provides essential services without requiring insurance, although those with Apple Health should bring their cards. Attendees can expect a welcoming environment where families can receive necessary dental care at no cost. Appointments are encouraged and can be made by calling 888-286-9105. The Tri Area Community Center is located at 10 W. Valley Rd., Chimacum, Washington, 98325.
