The Family Resource Center will host Drop-In Hours on Feb. 20, 2026, from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. at 1005 NE 67th St, Seattle, WA. This event invites families to connect with a Community Connector to learn about essential resources and services available in the community. Attendees can also access the Diaper Bank and shop at the Community Closet, which offers children's clothes, toys, and books. Families seeking support and resources are encouraged to attend. For more information, contact the Family Resource Center at theteam@familyworksseattle.org or call (206) 647-1790.
