Experience an afternoon of exquisite performances at the Chamber Music Showcase on March 10, 2026, at 2 p.m. in the Music Building. This event will feature talented musicians presenting a diverse repertoire, allowing attendees to enjoy the beauty of chamber music in an intimate setting. Whether you are a longtime fan or new to this genre, the showcase promises to be an enriching experience for all music lovers. Join us for an inspiring celebration of artistry and collaboration.
