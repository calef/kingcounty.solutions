Join the DAV Community Impact Day on April 4, 2026, where community members are encouraged to volunteer at least one hour to support veterans. This event aims to double participation compared to previous years, fostering a spirit of service and camaraderie. Attendees can expect to engage in various activities designed to make a meaningful difference in the lives of those who have served. Whether you can lend a hand or share your time, your involvement is invaluable. Come together with fellow community members to honor and uplift our veterans.
