Join the New Member Concierge event at The Center for Active Living on Dec. 17, 2025, from 11 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. Located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle, this event is designed for new and newer members to get acquainted with the center. Cynthia, a dedicated volunteer, will be available to answer questions, introduce attendees to other members, and recommend classes and programs. She will also share information about upcoming events. The Member Concierge service is offered on Mondays, Wednesdays, and Thursdays during the same hours, making it a great opportunity to learn more about what the center has to offer.
