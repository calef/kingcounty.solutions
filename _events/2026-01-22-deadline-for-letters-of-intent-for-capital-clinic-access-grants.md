Submit your letters of intent for the Capital Clinic Access grants from the Arcora Foundation by January 22, 2026, at 5 p.m. PST. This funding opportunity aims to support initiatives that enhance access to dental care in underserved communities.

Participating organizations are encouraged to outline their projects and demonstrate how they will improve clinic services. The grants are designed to foster innovative solutions that address barriers to dental health access.
