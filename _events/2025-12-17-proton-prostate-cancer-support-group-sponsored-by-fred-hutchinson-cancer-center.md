Join the Proton Prostate Cancer Support Group, sponsored by Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center, for an informative online discussion on December 17 from 5:30 to 7 p.m. PST. This supportive environment allows attendees to explore current treatment options, including promising new therapies, from initial diagnosis to active surveillance. Participants will have the opportunity to engage with others who are also navigating their prostate cancer journey. Whether you are newly diagnosed or seeking ongoing support, this group offers valuable insights and shared experiences. To register or for assistance, contact the Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center's Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
