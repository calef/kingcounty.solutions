Join us for the THV Workshops for Service Providers, taking place on February 26, 2026. This event will be held both virtually and in-person, offering flexibility for all attendees.

Participants can expect to engage in hands-on sessions focused on the design, development, and management of Tiny House Villages. Experts in the field will share valuable insights and best practices, equipping service providers with the knowledge needed to create sustainable and supportive housing solutions.

Whether you’re a seasoned professional or new to the concept, this workshop is an excellent opportunity to network with peers and gain practical skills. Don’t miss this chance to contribute to innovative housing solutions in your community.
