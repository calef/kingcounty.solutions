Join the Aging Well - Group #2 on Monday, Dec. 22, 2025, from 11 a.m. to noon at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This weekly support group provides a safe space for participants to engage in discussions and share experiences, fostering a sense of community and mutual support. All conversations are confidential, ensuring a trusting environment. Please note that pre-registration is required for admission. For more information about Group #2, contact Michele Fawcett-Long at 206-928-1730.
