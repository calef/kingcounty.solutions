Join NorthWest Share for its Free Weekly Food Truck event on December 18 from 6:30 to 8 p.m. at the Together Center, located at 16305 NE 87th St, Redmond, WA. This community initiative aims to provide free vegan and vegetarian meals to those in need, ensuring no one goes hungry in the area. Attendees can enjoy a variety of dishes, including rice, lentil soup, vegetable or tofu curry, Indian flatbread, hot herbal tea, and seasonal fruits, while supplies last. Everyone is welcome, so come by and grab a meal or share the information with someone who might benefit.
