  <div> <h1>Pathology Grand Rounds</h1> <p>Pathology Grand Rounds sessions serve as
  a platform for the presentation and discussion of topics relevant to the clinical
  practice of pathology, and may encompass a wide range of areas, including, but not
  limited to:</p> <ul> <li>Introduction of new and emerging testing/diagnostic modalities
  and platforms (including digital pathology and artificial intelligence applications).</li>
  <li>Advancements in diagnostic techniques and practice trends (including molecular
  pathology).</li> <li>Translational research initiatives bridging laboratory discoveries
  to clinical applications.</li> <li>Emerging topics related to Pathology training,
  wellness, and education.</li> <li>Promotion of Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion
  (DEI) efforts in clinical practice and patient care.</li> <li>Topics related to
  patient/practice advocacy, compliance, quality assurance, and patient safety.</li>
  <li>Leadership development, professionalism, and ethical considerations in pathology
  practice.</li> </ul> <p>Pathology Grand Rounds occurs on 2nd Mondays, 12:00 PM,
  Quarterly in October, February, May, &amp; August. Pathology Grand Rounds is free
  and open to all.  </p> <div>CME credit is available to participants who <strong>attend
  in person or via teleconference and who have signed-in or filled out an attestation
  form.</strong> You must pay the appropriate fee to the UW CME office at the end
  of the academic year in order to receive the credit(s).</div> <h2>CME Course Objectives</h2>
  <p>At the conclusion of this activity, participants will be able to:</p> <ul> <li>Discuss
  recent developments in pathology practice, focusing on rapidly evolving diagnostic
  modalities including expanding integration of molecular testing and advances in
  digital pathology and artificial intelligence.</li> <li>Enhance knowledge of emerging
  tissue-based biomarkers and new testing modalities to enhance Pathology diagnostics.</li>
  <li>Become familiarized with evolving educational modalities in pathology, laboratory
  regulation and leadership principles, ethical considerations, diversity, equity
  and inclusion and strategies for professional development, promoting excellence
  and inclusive and ethical practice standards in regulatorily compliant healthcare
  delivery.</li> <li>Evaluate cost-effective approaches in pathology analysis to optimize
  resource utilization and improve clinical decision-making.</li> </ul> <h2>Accreditation
