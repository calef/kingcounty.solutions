Join the KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop on December 12, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. PST. This free, youth-centered workshop is designed for young people and their families across King County, offering a welcoming virtual space to explore important topics related to conflict, communication, and justice. Each month features a different theme, with December focusing on emotional aspects of conflict. No preparation is needed—just bring your voice and an open mind. The series continues monthly, covering various modules that include conflict resolution skills and cultural awareness. To RSVP, visit the event page at [Calendly](https://calendly.com/kcdrc-youth-development/2nd-saturdays-with-kc-connect?month=2025-10). Don't miss this opportunity to engage and learn together!
