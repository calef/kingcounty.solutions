Join the Kaleidoscope Play and Learn event on December 31 from 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle, WA. This free program offers a welcoming space for Family, Friend, and Neighbor caregivers, parents, and young children to connect and engage in activities that promote healthy child development and school readiness. Participants can expect to learn through play in a culturally-responsive environment, guided by community members. It’s an excellent opportunity for families to build connections while fostering essential skills for their children.
