Join the community for the grand opening of the Olympic Hills Tiny House Village on Feb. 19 at 2:30 p.m. at 3121 NE 133rd St. in Seattle. This event will feature inspiring speeches from Seattle Mayor Katie B. Wilson and LIHI Executive Director Sharon Lee, highlighting the importance of affordable housing solutions.

Attendees can explore the tiny house village, learn about sustainable living, and engage with community members and local organizations dedicated to addressing homelessness. Enjoy light refreshments and connect with neighbors as you discover how this innovative project aims to provide a safe and supportive environment for those in need.
