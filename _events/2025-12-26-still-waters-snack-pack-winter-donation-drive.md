Still Waters' Snack Pack Winter Donation Drive begins on December 26 at 8 a.m. at the Still Waters Snack Pack location in Tukwila. This event invites community members to contribute to the fight against food insecurity among local students experiencing homelessness. Donations will help provide essential food items that allow these students to focus on their education and future. Attendees can donate via PayPal or by sending checks to Still Waters Services for Families in Transition at PO Box 88984, Tukwila, WA 98138. Volunteers are also needed to help assemble and distribute weekly Snack Packs. For those interested in volunteering, email Stillwatersfamilyservices@gmail.com for more information. Your support makes a difference in the lives of Tukwila students.
