The Disaster Assistance Center will open at the Auburn Library on Jan. 3, 2026, at 1 p.m. This center is designed to support residents affected by recent floods. Attendees can expect to receive vital resources, including information on recovery programs, financial assistance, and community support services. Representatives will be available to answer questions and guide residents through the recovery process. The center aims to provide a welcoming environment where individuals can connect with local agencies and access the help they need to rebuild their lives. Whether you are seeking immediate assistance or information on long-term recovery options, the Disaster Assistance Center is here to help.
