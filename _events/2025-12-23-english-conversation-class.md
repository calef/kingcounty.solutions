Join the English Conversation Class on December 23, 2025, from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This engaging class pairs foreign language speakers with volunteers for one-on-one English lessons, focusing on pronunciation and comprehension skills. Participants will enjoy a supportive atmosphere while making new friends and improving their language abilities. Whether you're looking to enhance your English skills or connect with others in the community, this class offers a fun and interactive experience. For more information or to volunteer, contact Jerie Milici at jerie.milici@gmail.com.
