The Executive Committee Meetings will take place on February 3, 2027, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. These regular meetings occur on the first Wednesday of each month and are open to the public. Attendees can participate in person or watch the live video feed streaming on the King County Council’s website and on King County TV, available on Xfinity channels 22 and 322 (HD) and Astound Broadband channels 22 and 711 (HD). Community members and stakeholders interested in local governance and flood control issues are encouraged to attend.
