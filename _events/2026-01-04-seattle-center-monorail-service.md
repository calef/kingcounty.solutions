Experience the Seattle Center Monorail, which will operate daily from 8:30 a.m. to 9 p.m., starting Jan. 4, 2026. This iconic transportation system offers a unique and scenic ride connecting Seattle Center to downtown Seattle.

As you glide above the city, enjoy stunning views and easy access to popular attractions, including the Space Needle and Chihuly Garden and Glass. Whether you're commuting, sightseeing, or just looking for a fun way to explore, the monorail provides a convenient and enjoyable experience for all ages.
