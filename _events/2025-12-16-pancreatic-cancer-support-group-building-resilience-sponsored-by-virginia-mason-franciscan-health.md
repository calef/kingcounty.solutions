Join the Pancreatic Cancer Support Group: Building Resilience on December 16 from 10 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. PST. This online event, sponsored by Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, offers a supportive environment for patients and caregivers to share experiences and gain insights. Attendees can expect educational presentations and discussions facilitated by Kathryn Swingle, MSW, LICSW, and Jennifer Bryan, BSN, RN, OCN, the Pancreatic Nurse Navigator. The group meets every other Tuesday, providing ongoing support for those affected by pancreatic cancer. To participate, please RSVP by calling 206-341-0930.
