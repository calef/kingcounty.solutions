Join the "Get Moving!" class on Dec. 23, 2025, from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. Led by physical therapist Sherryl DeVries, this multi-level workout is designed for everyone, particularly those with a Parkinson’s diagnosis. Participants can expect a dynamic session that includes big movements, cardio, weight training, dance, and boxing. All exercises can be modified to accommodate different fitness levels, including options to use a chair. Come ready to engage and enhance your physical well-being in a supportive environment.
