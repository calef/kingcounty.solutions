Join the Playful Winter Collage Botanical Art Workshop on Feb. 28, 2026, at the Douglas Research Conservatory. This in-person event invites participants to explore their creativity by crafting unique collages using botanical materials. Attendees will learn techniques to incorporate natural elements into their artwork, fostering a connection with nature during the winter months. Whether you are an experienced artist or a beginner, this workshop offers a fun and engaging way to express yourself through art. Supplies will be provided, and participants are encouraged to bring their own inspirations. Don't miss this opportunity to create and connect with fellow art enthusiasts in a beautiful setting.
