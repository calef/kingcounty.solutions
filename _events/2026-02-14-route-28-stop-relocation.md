Route 28 will have its southbound stop relocated starting Feb. 14, 2026, at the intersection of 8th Avenue NW and NW 80th Street. This change is part of ongoing construction efforts in the area.

Passengers can expect a new, more convenient stop location designed to enhance safety and accessibility. Please plan accordingly, as this relocation may affect your travel schedule.

Local transit officials encourage riders to stay informed about the changes and to look for signage at the current stop for further details. Your understanding and cooperation during this transition are appreciated as we work to improve public transportation in the community.
