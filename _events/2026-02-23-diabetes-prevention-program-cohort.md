Join us for the next cohort of the Diabetes Prevention Program starting on February 23, 2026. This comprehensive program is designed to help participants reduce their risk of developing type 2 diabetes through education, support, and lifestyle changes. Attendees will engage in interactive sessions that cover nutrition, physical activity, and behavior modification strategies.

Participants can expect to learn from experienced facilitators, connect with others facing similar challenges, and gain valuable tools to make lasting changes. The program fosters a supportive environment where everyone can share their experiences and encourage one another. Don't miss this opportunity to take proactive steps toward better health and well-being.
