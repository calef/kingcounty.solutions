Community members are invited to ARCH on January 8, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 119, Redmond, WA. This open office hours event provides an opportunity to ask questions about ARCH programs and access valuable resources on affordable housing. Attendees can pick up informational packets and receive assistance with signing up for ARCH email alerts, which notify them of new low-income housing options. Whether you're seeking guidance or looking to stay informed about housing opportunities, this event is designed to help you navigate affordable housing resources in the community.
