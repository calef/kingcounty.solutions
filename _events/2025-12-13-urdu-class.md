Join us for an engaging Urdu class starting December 13 at 10:30 a.m. This weekly event, held every Saturday, is designed for both beginners and advanced learners, providing a welcoming environment to explore the Urdu language. Participants can expect to enhance their language skills through interactive lessons and cultural insights. Classes will run from 10:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at our community center. Whether you're looking to start your language journey or improve your proficiency, this class offers a unique opportunity to connect with others while learning. Don’t miss out on this enriching experience!
