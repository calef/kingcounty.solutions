Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the Career Circle, a virtual job club and networking fellowship designed to support job seekers. Attendees can expect to enhance their job search strategies, receive coaching on resumes and cover letters, and participate in mock interviews. The event offers a chance to connect with fellow job seekers, share experiences, and learn from each other in a collaborative environment. 

The Career Circle takes place on December 11, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. Participants will receive a Teams link to join the session, which will be sent within 24 hours of registration. It is recommended to create an account at WorkSourceWA.com before attending. Registration is required, and those signing up after 8 a.m. the day prior should email cany235@lni.wa.gov for assistance.
