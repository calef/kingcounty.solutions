The Executive Committee Meetings will take place on October 7, 2026, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. These regular monthly meetings are essential for members of the Executive Committee (FCDEC) and provide an opportunity for discussion and decision-making on important matters. The meeting will also be available for public viewing via live stream on the King County Council’s website and on King County TV, accessible through Xfinity and Astound Broadband channels. Community members, local officials, and interested stakeholders are encouraged to attend and participate in the discussions.
