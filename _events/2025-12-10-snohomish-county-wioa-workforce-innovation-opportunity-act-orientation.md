Join the Snohomish County WIOA (Workforce Innovation Opportunity Act) Orientation on Dec. 10, 2025, from 1:30 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. at WorkSource Everett, located at 728 134th St SW, Suite 102, Everett, WA. This informative session will provide attendees with an overview of the WIOA program, detailing how it assists job seekers in their employment journeys. Participants will learn about available resources and enrollment processes to take full advantage of the program's offerings. Pre-registration is required for those attending online. Don't miss this opportunity to gain valuable insights into workforce development and support services.
