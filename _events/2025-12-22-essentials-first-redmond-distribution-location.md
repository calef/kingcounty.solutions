Essentials First will host a distribution event in Redmond on December 22 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. Community members are invited to walk in and receive essential hygiene kits, snack packs, and ORCA cards. This event aims to support those in need, including low-income families, unhoused individuals, immigrants, and anyone experiencing hardship. No specific eligibility requirements are needed, but attendees are encouraged to bring an ID and be ready to fill out a brief intake form with staff assistance. All personal information will remain confidential. Essentials First is dedicated to providing and advocating for essential hygiene access, ensuring that everyone has the support they need.
