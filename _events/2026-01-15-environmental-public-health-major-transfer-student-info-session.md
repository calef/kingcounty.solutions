  <article> <div> <div> <div> <section> <div> <div> <div> <div> <div> <h3>Contact
  an adviser</h3> <p>We are happy to meet with you in person or virtually to help
  you transfer in</p> <p><a>Contact us</a></p> </div> <p>We are ready to make transferring
  into the Department of Environmental &amp; Occupational Health Sciences a welcoming
  and tailor-made experience for each student. Our goal is to ensure your time in
  the department is fulfilling, which begins with the moment you explore transferring
  into our department.  </p> <p>As a transfer student, you can declare the Bachelors
  of Science in Environmental Public Health major after you are admitted to UW. Please
  consult the <a>transfer admission website</a> for more information about applying.
  Local students should plan to attend the weekly UW Admissions <a>Transfer Thursday</a>
  sessions, if possible.  </p> <p>Washington state community college students should
  use the transfer guides and sample transfer plans below as planning tools only.
  For updated courses, consult the <a>online equivalency guide</a>.  </p> <p>A UW
  Environmental Public Health adviser can guide you through the details of the major
  and is a wonderful resource to meet with before declaring the major. To schedule
  an appointment either in person or over the phone, see the <a>Contact an Adviser</a>
  page. </p> <h2>Transfer steps: </h2> <ol> <li>Students transferring in as juniors
  (6 quarters/90 credits or more) should complete the Environmental Public Health
  major’s upper division prerequisite courses prior to enrolling at UW.</li> <li>Transfer
  students can declare the major after they have been admitted to the UW. Please consult
  the transfer admission website for more information about applying to the UW.</li>
  <li>Once you’ve applied and are admitted to the UW. Submit an Environmental Public
  Health application at <a>https://apps.sphcm.washington.edu/envh/Apply</a> </li>
  </ol> <div> <h3>Sample transfer plan</h3> <p>This plan can give you an idea of what
  classes to take if you want to transfer into DEOHS</p> <p><a>Download now</a></p>
  </div> <p>Students should apply for entry to Fall quarter. See application windows
  here <a>https://deohs.washington.edu/declaring-major</a></p> <p>If you have any
  questions about declaring the Environmental Public Health major, you can schedule
  an appointment with an adviser at <a>https://deohs.washington.edu/prospective-and-current-student-advising</a></p>
  <h2>UW transfer resources  </h2> <ul> <li>Check the UW Equivalency Guide to see
  which courses at your institution will satisfy UW requirements <a>https://admit.washington.edu/apply/transfer/equivalency-guide/</a>
  </li> <li>UW Transfer Student Portal Website: <a>https://transfer.uw.edu/</a> </li>
  <li>Local students should plan to attend the weekly UW Transfer Thursday sessions:
  <a>https://transfer.uw.edu/thursday/</a> </li> </ul> </div> </div> </div> </div>

Join the Environmental Public Health Major Transfer Student Info Session on January 15, 2026, at 4 p.m. This event is designed for students interested in transferring to the University of Washington's Department of Environmental & Occupational Health Sciences. Attendees will learn about the transfer process, including how to declare a Bachelor of Science in Environmental Public Health after admission.

Participants will have the opportunity to meet with advisers who can provide personalized guidance and answer questions about the major and transfer requirements. This session is a valuable resource for prospective transfer students, especially those from Washington state community colleges. Don't miss this chance to explore your future in environmental public health and get the support you need to make a successful transition.
