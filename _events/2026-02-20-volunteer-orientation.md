NAMI Eastside will host a Volunteer Orientation on February 19, 2026, from 7 p.m. to 8 p.m. via Zoom. This online session is designed for new and prospective volunteers interested in making a positive impact in the community. Attendees will learn about NAMI Eastside’s mission, various volunteer roles, time commitments, and training opportunities, along with a Q&A segment to address any inquiries. The orientation occurs every third Thursday of the month, providing a consistent opportunity for engagement. Interested individuals can register to attend this informative event and take the first step toward volunteering.
