Join Essentials First on January 14 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at the Redmond Distribution Location in Washington for essential hygiene support. This event welcomes community members to receive hygiene kits, snack packs, and ORCA cards on a walk-in basis. Essentials First advocates for access to hygiene for those in need, including low-income families, unhoused individuals, immigrants, and caregivers.

No concrete eligibility requirements exist, making this service accessible to anyone experiencing hardship. Attendees are encouraged to bring an ID and will complete a short intake form with the help of staff. Personal information will remain confidential. Everyone is welcome to come and benefit from this vital resource.
