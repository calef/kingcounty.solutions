Join the Living with Metastatic Cancer Support Group, sponsored by EvergreenHealth, on Dec. 27 from 10 a.m. to noon PST. This online support group offers a safe space for individuals facing advanced-stage cancer to share experiences and emotions. Participants can connect with others who understand their challenges, fostering a sense of community and support. Caregivers are also welcome to attend. Engage in meaningful discussions and find comfort in a group setting. For those needing assistance with registration or participation, support is available through EvergreenHealth’s resources. Don’t miss this opportunity to connect and share with others who are navigating similar journeys.
