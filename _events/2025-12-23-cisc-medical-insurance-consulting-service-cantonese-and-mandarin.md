CISC will host a Medical Insurance Consulting Service on December 23 from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. at their office located at 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 123, Redmond, WA. This free event offers attendees the opportunity to receive personalized assistance regarding healthcare coverage options. The Healthcare Access Team will be available to help clients navigate benefits such as Medicare, Medicaid, and various support programs tailored for low-income seniors and individuals with disabilities. Services will be provided in Cantonese and Mandarin, ensuring accessibility for a diverse community. Join us to empower yourself with the knowledge needed to make informed healthcare decisions.
