Shop for unique bargains at the UW Surplus Public Store on Jan. 6 from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the Plant Services Building. The store offers a variety of items from unwanted goods donated by university departments and medical facilities, with new inventory arriving daily. Attendees can explore a changing selection of furniture, equipment, and more, all aimed at promoting reuse and recycling. The event is open to everyone, making it a great opportunity to find affordable items while supporting sustainability efforts at the University of Washington. For questions about accessibility, contact surplus@uw.edu.
