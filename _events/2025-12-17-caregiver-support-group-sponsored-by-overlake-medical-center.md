Join the Caregiver Support Group sponsored by Overlake Medical Center on December 17 from 4 to 5:30 p.m. PST. This online event is designed for anyone supporting a loved one with cancer. Attendees can expect to connect with others who understand the caregiver experience, sharing ideas, resources, and challenges. The group will feature presentations and discussions aimed at providing the support you need. This is an opportunity to engage with a community that values shared experiences and mutual understanding.
