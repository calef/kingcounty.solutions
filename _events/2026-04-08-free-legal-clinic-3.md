The Latino/o Bar Association of Washington (LBAW), in collaboration with the King County Bar Association and El Centro de la Raza, will host a Free Legal Clinic on April 8, 2026, from 6:00 to 8:00 PM at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle, WA. Registration begins at 4:00 PM on a first-come, first-served basis. The clinic offers free general consultations on various legal topics, including immigration, family law, auto accidents, personal injury, worker’s rights, wage claims, and criminal law. Bilingual attorneys and interpreters will be available to assist. This event is open to anyone seeking legal advice and will take place on the second Wednesday of each month from January to November. For more information, call (844) 502-9832.
