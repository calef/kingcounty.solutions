The MCRC Community Clinic will take place on December 13 from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the MCRC Seattle office. Attendees can expect to receive medical appointments and consultations with healthcare professionals. This event aims to provide essential health services to the community, ensuring that individuals have access to necessary medical care. Whether you need routine check-ups or specific health advice, the clinic is designed to support the well-being of all participants.
