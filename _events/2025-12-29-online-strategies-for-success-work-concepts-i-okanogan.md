Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Online Strategies for Success: Work Concepts I" starting December 29, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. This four-day workshop, conducted via Zoom, will help you create a roadmap to success by focusing on essential skills such as self-motivation, career exploration, and understanding employer expectations. Participants will learn how to effectively prepare for job applications, present themselves professionally, and grasp key concepts like customer service and teamwork. The workshop is part of a series of customizable life-skills sessions open to everyone. Registration is required, and attendees should create a job-seeker account at WorkSourceWA.com prior to the event. For accommodations, contact Matt Gladd at matthew.gladd@esd.wa.gov.
