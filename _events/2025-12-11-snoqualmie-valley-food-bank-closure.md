The Snoqualmie Valley Food Bank will be closed on Dec. 11, 2025. During this time, community members are encouraged to plan ahead for their food needs. The food bank typically provides essential groceries and support to local families facing food insecurity, so please take note of this temporary closure. 

For those in need of assistance, consider reaching out to alternative local resources or neighboring food banks. The Snoqualmie Valley Food Bank will resume regular hours following this closure. Thank you for your understanding and support as we work to serve the community effectively.
