The Stride bus rapid transit system will officially open on Jan. 1, 2028, in the Lake Washington area. Attendees can expect a day filled with festivities, including live entertainment, food vendors, and informational booths about the new transit system. The event will highlight the benefits of the Stride system, which aims to enhance public transportation and connectivity in the region. Community members are encouraged to join in the celebration and learn how the Stride system will improve travel options for residents and visitors alike.
