Join the Community Dining event on Dec. 23, 2025, from 11:45 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. Attendees can enjoy a hot, nutritious meal prepared from scratch by Chef Francisco in a welcoming, communal atmosphere. The menu varies daily, offering culturally diverse options. A suggested donation of $5 is requested for those ages 60 and older, while those under 60 are required to contribute $10. This event is a great opportunity to meet new friends and enjoy a delicious meal in the community.
