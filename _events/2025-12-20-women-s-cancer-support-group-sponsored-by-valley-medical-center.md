Join the Women's Cancer Support Group sponsored by Valley Medical Center on December 20 from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. PST. This online event welcomes women diagnosed with any type of cancer, whether newly diagnosed, undergoing treatment, or finishing treatment. Participants can expect a safe and confidential environment to share their experiences and receive support from others facing similar challenges. The group features educational speakers and open discussions, focusing on ways to manage the impact of cancer on daily life. Connect with others and gain valuable insights on navigating your cancer journey. For registration assistance, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
