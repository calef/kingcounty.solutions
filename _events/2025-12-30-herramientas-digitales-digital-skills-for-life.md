Join El Centro de la Raza for "Herramientas Digitales/Digital Skills for Life," a free training event designed to enhance your digital skills. This program takes place on December 30 from 5:30 p.m. to 7:30 p.m. at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle, WA. Participants will learn to use various digital tools that can benefit both personal and business needs. The training is part of a series running from October 28 to November 20, held twice a week on Tuesdays and Thursdays. This is a great opportunity to learn, connect, and grow within your community. Don’t miss out on this chance to embrace new opportunities this fall!
