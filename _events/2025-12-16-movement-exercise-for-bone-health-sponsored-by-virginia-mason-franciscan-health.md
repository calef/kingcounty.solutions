Join Virginia Mason Franciscan Health for "Movement & Exercise for Bone Health," an online exercise class on December 16 from 10 to 11 a.m. PST. This session is specifically designed for individuals looking to enhance bone health and reduce the risk of complications associated with cancer treatment. Attendees can expect a series of safe and effective exercises tailored to help rebuild bone density, improve physical function, and enhance overall quality of life. Whether you're recovering from treatment or looking to maintain your health, this class offers valuable support. For registration assistance, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
