Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Online Strategies for Success: Work Concepts I" on December 29, 2025, at 9 a.m. This virtual workshop, held via Zoom, will help attendees create a personalized roadmap to success. Participants will explore self-motivation, career options, and employer expectations, while learning essential skills such as job preparation, professional packaging, and effective communication.

The workshop is part of a flexible series of life-skills modules that can be taken individually or combined for a comprehensive experience. Registration is required, and attendees will receive meeting links via email prior to the event. This session is particularly suitable for job seekers and those looking to enhance their professional skills. For more information, contact PJ Hall at phyllis.hall@esd.wa.gov.
