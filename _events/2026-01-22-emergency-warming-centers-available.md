Emergency warming centers will open on Jan. 22 at 5 p.m. in various locations throughout King County, including libraries and city halls. These centers provide a safe, warm space for vulnerable populations during severe weather conditions.

Attendees can expect a welcoming environment where they can find shelter from the cold, access to hot beverages, and resources for additional support. The centers are designed to help those in need, including individuals experiencing homelessness and others affected by the harsh weather.

Community members are encouraged to spread the word to ensure that those who may benefit from these services can find assistance. The warming centers will remain open as long as severe weather persists.
