Join the "Resilient Wednesdays – Bipolar Peer Support" group on February 25, 2026, from 6:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. ET via Zoom. This weekly online support session is designed for adults living with bipolar disorder and their family members. Participants can share experiences and receive support in a safe environment. The group has been providing assistance for over a decade. To attend, please register in advance and check your email for the meeting link. For more information, contact Mark at mt****@*****il.com. This event is an excellent opportunity for those seeking community and understanding while managing bipolar disorder.
