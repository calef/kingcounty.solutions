Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the "Skills and Abilities" workshop on Dec. 10, 2025, from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m. at 2707 I St. NE, Auburn, WA. This event is designed to help participants identify and showcase their transferable skills and personal qualities that are essential in today’s job market. Attendees will learn effective oral communication techniques and practice delivering a compelling 60-second presentation to potential employers. Registration is required, so sign up in advance at WorkSourceWA.com. If you need accommodations due to a disability, please contact Auburn WorkSource at 253-804-1177. Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your employability and connect with local job resources.
