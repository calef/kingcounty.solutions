Join us for a screening of the documentary "The Librarians" on Dec. 11, 2025, at 6 p.m. at the Central Library in Seattle. This thought-provoking film explores the vital role of librarians in today's society, showcasing their impact on communities and the preservation of knowledge.

Following the screening, engage in a panel discussion featuring filmmaker Kim Snyder and a group of experts in the field. They will delve into the themes of the documentary and answer questions from the audience, providing insights into the challenges and triumphs faced by librarians.

Don’t miss this opportunity to celebrate the importance of libraries and the dedicated individuals who work to keep them thriving. Come ready to participate in an enlightening conversation about the future of information access and literacy.
