Join the Loving Self-Meditation event on Dec. 23 from 1:30 to 2 p.m. in the VelDwyk Room. This session, led by SESSC Volunteer Anjali, offers participants an opportunity to experience the calming effects of meditation. Attendees will learn techniques to help them stay centered, relax, and cope with stress. Donations are welcome to support future events. This is a perfect chance to enhance your personal well-being and discover the benefits of mindfulness in a supportive environment.
