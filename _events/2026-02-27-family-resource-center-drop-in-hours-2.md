The Family Resource Center in Seattle will host drop-in hours on Feb. 27, 2026, from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. This event invites families to meet with a Community Connector to explore essential resources and services available in the community. Attendees can access the Diaper Bank and shop at the Community Closet, which offers children's clothes, toys, and books. Families in need of support or looking for resources are encouraged to attend. For more information, contact the center at theteam@familyworksseattle.org or call (206) 647-1790.
