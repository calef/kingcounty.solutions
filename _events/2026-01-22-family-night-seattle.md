Join us for Family Night in Seattle on Jan. 22, 2026, from 6 to 7:30 p.m. at TOPS K-8 School, located at 2500 Franklin Ave E. This event is designed for Deaf and Hard of Hearing youth ages 0-18 and their families. Attendees can expect an engaging open discussion focused on social-emotional learning, along with crafts and food. Families are encouraged to bring their own beverages and a snack to share. ASL interpretation will be provided, and other language accommodations are available upon request. For more information, contact Laura Gramer at Lobara@gmail.com.
