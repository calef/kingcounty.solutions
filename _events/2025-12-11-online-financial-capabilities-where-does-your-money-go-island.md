Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the online workshop, "Financial Capabilities: Where Does Your Money Go?" on Dec. 11, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. This interactive session invites participants to explore their spending habits and learn how to create a monthly budget. 

This workshop is part of a six-week series that meets twice a week, offering flexible and customizable financial education. Each unit can be taken independently or combined with others, allowing for a personalized experience. Registration is required, and participants will receive Zoom meeting details via email prior to the event. For accommodations, contact Kim Newkirk at 360-499-0004 or via email. Don’t miss this opportunity to gain valuable financial insights and make informed decisions about your money.
