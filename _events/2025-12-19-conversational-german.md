Join the Conversational German group on Friday, December 19, 2025, from 9:45 to 10:45 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This informal weekly gathering invites participants to practice their German language skills with native speakers while making new friends. Whether you're a beginner or looking to improve your fluency, this welcoming environment encourages conversation and cultural exchange. Don't miss the chance to enhance your language abilities in a relaxed setting.
