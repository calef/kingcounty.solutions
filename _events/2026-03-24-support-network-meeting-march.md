Join The Sophia Way for its March Support Network Meeting on March 24, 2026, from 12 p.m. to 1 p.m. at 11061 NE 2nd Street, Suite 223, Bellevue, WA. This gathering is open to individuals from faith communities, civic groups, or anyone interested in making a difference. Attendees will receive updates on The Sophia Way's programs, discuss the current needs of the women served, and share resources and support ideas. This meeting provides an opportunity to connect with others committed to fostering positive change in the community. For more information, visit [The Sophia Way website](https://secure.qgiv.com/for/2026supportnetwork/event/march/) or contact info@sophiaway.org.
