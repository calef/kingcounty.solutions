Join the Low Vision Group on Jan. 13, 2026, from 2 to 3 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. This supportive gathering invites individuals experiencing low vision to share their challenges and connect with others facing similar situations. Attendees will have the opportunity to receive valuable resources and support. Pre-registration is required; contact social worker Josh Maduell at 206-932-4044, ext. 2, or via email at joshm@wscenter.org to secure your spot. Please note that those who are not pre-registered will not be admitted.
