Join the AA Holly Chapter for its Sunday Meeting on December 28 from 6 to 8 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room. This gathering offers a supportive environment for individuals seeking recovery and personal growth through shared experiences and discussions. Attendees can expect to connect with others, share their stories, and find encouragement in their journeys. Whether you are new to recovery or a long-time member, all are welcome to participate in this uplifting meeting.
