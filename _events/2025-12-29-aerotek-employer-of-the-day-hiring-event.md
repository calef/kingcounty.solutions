Join AEROTEK for an Employer of the Day Hiring Event on December 29 from 9 a.m. to noon at WorkSource Kitsap, located at 3120 NW Randall Way, Silverdale, Washington. This event offers job seekers the opportunity to connect with a leading recruiting and staffing agency focused on improving access to career development.

Attendees can explore various positions available at the Bangor and PSNS shipyards, including roles for Marine Electricians, Shipyard Labor/Firewatch, and Welders, with competitive pay rates. Candidates are encouraged to bring two forms of identification for shipyard entry. This event is an excellent chance to meet potential employers, learn about job opportunities, and enhance your career in a supportive environment. Don't miss this opportunity to advance your career in the manufacturing and production sectors.
