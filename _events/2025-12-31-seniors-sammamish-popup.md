Join the SENIORS Sammamish Popup on December 31 from 10:30 a.m. to 2 p.m. at Beaver Lake Lodge, located at 25099 SE 24th St in Kirkland. This event offers a welcoming space for seniors to connect with others in the community. Attendees can participate in engaging activities, enjoy refreshments, and share experiences in a friendly atmosphere. Whether you're looking to make new friends or catch up with old ones, this popup is a great opportunity to socialize and have fun. Don't miss out on this chance to celebrate the new year with fellow seniors!
