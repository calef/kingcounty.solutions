Join the community at the Bloodworks Donation Center in Renton on December 21 from 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. for the "Give Blood to Save 3 Lives" event. Attendees will have the opportunity to donate blood, which can save up to three lives. Your contribution is vital, as someone in the U.S. needs blood every two seconds. Additionally, participants can fill out an entry form for a chance to win a big prize this month. The donation center is located at 660 SW 39th St., just one block from IKEA. To schedule an appointment, call 1-800-398-7888. Your donation can make a significant difference in the lives of those in need.
