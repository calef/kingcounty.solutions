  Practicum</span> </h1> <article> <div> <div> <div> <div> <p>The University of Washington
  School of Public Health (UWSPH) requires that every MPH student complete a Practicum
  to receive their degree. The Practicum is a planned, supervised, and evaluated field-based
  experience under the guidance of an SPH <a>Faculty Adviser</a> and a <a>Practicum
  Site Supervisor</a>, requiring a minimum of 160-hour work (4-6 credits) for a public
  health organization. This does not include: 1) classroom-based work; 2) work completed
  under the supervision of a faculty member without a practice organization-based
  lead.</p> <p>It is recommended that students participate with an organization in
  the same way that others at the organization engage in order to have the most interactive
  experience possible (ex: if an organization is in-person, an in-person Practicum
  would be preferred).</p> <p><a>Explore Practicum Resources</a>  <a>Submit an Opportunity

Join the University of Washington School of Public Health for the MPH Practicum Site & Faculty Convening on March 12, 2026, at 10 a.m. via Zoom. This event is designed for MPH students, site supervisors, and faculty advisers to learn about the practicum requirements and processes essential for degree completion. Attendees will gain insights into the roles and responsibilities of each participant, fostering a collaborative environment for public health education. Students will explore resources and opportunities to enhance their practicum experience, while site supervisors and faculty advisers will receive guidance on their respective roles. This convening aims to strengthen connections between students and their practicum sites, ensuring a successful and engaging field-based experience.
