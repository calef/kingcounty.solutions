Join the Federal Way Arts Commission for the Summer Sounds: FREE Concerts in the Park series at Steel Lake Park, located at 2410 S 312th St, Federal Way, WA. The concerts will take place every Wednesday evening in July, with the first concert scheduled for July 9 from 6:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. This family-friendly event features a diverse lineup of live music, celebrating various musical genres suitable for all ages. Admission is free, making it an excellent opportunity for community members and families to enjoy a summer evening of entertainment. Don't miss out on this vibrant celebration of music and community!
