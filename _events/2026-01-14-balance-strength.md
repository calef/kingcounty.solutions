Join personal trainer Denise Geroux for the "Balance & Strength" class on Jan. 14, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This interactive session focuses on enhancing balance and preventing falls through the Feldenkrais Method, Tai Chi, and Chi Gung exercises. Participants will learn to improve posture while sitting and standing, strengthen the lower body and core, and work on joint stability and flexibility. The class also emphasizes breathing and mindfulness techniques to foster better balance. Chairs will be provided, making this class accessible for all abilities.
