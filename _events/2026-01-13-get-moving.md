Join us for "Get Moving!" on Jan. 13, 2026, from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. Led by physical therapist Sherryl DeVries, this inclusive workout class caters to all fitness levels, particularly individuals with a Parkinson’s diagnosis. Participants can expect a dynamic mix of exercises, including big movements, cardio, weight training, dance, and boxing, all of which can be modified with a chair. This is a great opportunity to engage in physical activity while connecting with others in the community.
