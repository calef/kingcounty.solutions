Join the BFET-Basic Food Employment & Training Orientation on Dec. 11, 2025, from 11 a.m. to noon, tailored for Clark County residents. This informative session is designed for individuals receiving food benefits (SNAP) who are seeking employment. Attendees will learn how the BFET program can assist them with job training, job search support, and access to essential resources such as transportation and work clothing.

Participants are encouraged to register in advance and create an account at WorkSourceWA.com before attending. For those needing accommodations, please contact Catherine Nelson at 360-735-5017 or via email at catherine.nelson@esd.wa.gov. This orientation will take place at Vancouver WorkSource, where dedicated case managers will be available to provide personalized assistance on your career journey.
