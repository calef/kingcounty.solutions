Join playwright and director Tammi Doyle for a monthly play reading on Dec. 17, 2025, from 1 to 3 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. Attendees will have the opportunity to read a variety of classic and contemporary plays, celebrating the rich history of theater while engaging with new voices. Copies of the plays will be provided, ensuring everyone can participate. This event is a great way to enjoy literature and connect with fellow theater enthusiasts.
