Join the BIPOC Support Group sponsored by Cancer Lifeline on December 13 from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. PST. This online event invites individuals who identify as Black, Indigenous, or people of color (BIPOC) and are affected by cancer, whether personally or through a loved one. Participants will have the opportunity to connect with others in the BIPOC community, share experiences, and engage with speakers and presentations tailored to their unique challenges. The group is facilitated by a fellow BIPOC member, fostering a supportive environment. To participate, download the Zoom app and register in advance. For assistance with registration or any questions, contact Cancer Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
