Join Bike Works for a Volunteer Repair Party on March 10, 2026, from 1 to 4 p.m. at the Bike Works Warehouse, located at 3715 S Hudson St #111, Seattle, Washington. This weekly event invites adults aged 18 and older to participate in bike repair activities, regardless of their mechanical skills. It's a great opportunity for newcomers to start volunteering with Bike Works. Interested participants must complete an application and orientation process prior to attending. Whether you're a seasoned mechanic or a beginner, your help is welcome as we work together to promote cycling in the community.
