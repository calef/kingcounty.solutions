Starting Jan. 1, 2026, Husky Health Center and all UW Medicine facilities will no longer be in-network for certain health insurance plans. This change may impact your access to healthcare services, so it’s important to stay informed.

Join us at the Husky Health Center to learn more about the implications of this transition. Attendees will have the opportunity to ask questions, understand how it affects their coverage, and explore alternative options. Healthcare representatives will be available to provide guidance and support.

Make sure to attend this informative session to ensure you have the resources needed to navigate your healthcare choices effectively. Don’t miss this chance to stay updated on your health insurance status.
