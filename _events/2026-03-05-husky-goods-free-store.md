  Resource Center</a>.</p> <p>This is a COMPLETELY FREE event open to all UW students

  <ul> <li>Clothing - shirts, jackets, pants</li> <li>Accessories - bags, hats, jewelry</li>
  <li>Room decor - mirrors, signs, string lights</li> <li>Small appliances - an electrical
  kettle that you bought but you never use</li> </ul> <p><strong>Husky Goods Free
  Store does NOT allow:</strong></p> <ul> <li>Food</li> <li>Items in bad or broken
  condition</li> <li>Oversized items</li> </ul> <h3>Donate</h3> <p>You can bring items
  to donate during the event, or you can donate items ahead of time at the collection
  bins at <a>Oliver Hall</a> and <a>Lander Hall </a>Desks. The bins will be at the
  locations Tuesday February 17 – Sunday March 2, 2026.</p> <p>Have any questions?
  Email UW Recycling at <a>recycle@uw.edu</a> or direct message them on <a>Instagram</a>.</p>
  <p><strong>Meet the hosts</strong></p> <p><a>UW Recycling</a> provides daily waste
  and recycling collection for the UW Seattle campus. Our staff also serve as contract
  administrators, education and outreach experts, waste diversion innovators and sustainability
  enthusiasts for the UW Seattle campus.</p> <p><a>HuES</a> (Huskies for Equitable
  Sustainability) connects students with projects that impact sustainability on our
  campus and in our community. The group is student-run and supported by <a>UW Sustainability</a>.</p>
  <p><a>SEED</a> (Students Expressing Environmental Dedication) is a student organization
  that works with residents and Housing &amp; Food Services to bring awareness to
  sustainable issues that impact our campus. SEED is a part of Residential Community
  Student Association (RCSA).</p> <p><em>The University of Washington is committed

Join the Husky Goods Free Store on March 5, 2026, from 5 to 7 p.m. at Thaddeus and Lois Spratlen Hall. This completely free event is open to all University of Washington students on the Seattle campus. Attendees can browse a variety of donated items, including clothing, accessories, room decor, and small appliances.

Donations are welcome, and you can bring items to the event or drop them off at collection bins located at Oliver Hall and Lander Hall from February 17 to March 2. The event is hosted by UW Recycling, along with student groups HuES and SEED, which focus on sustainability initiatives. Don't miss this opportunity to find new treasures while promoting sustainability on campus. For any questions, contact UW Recycling via email or social media.
