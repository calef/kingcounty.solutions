  <div> <a><span>Save to your calendar</span></a> </div> </div> <p>The HCDE Research
  Showcase highlights ongoing work by faculty, research scientists, postdocs, PhD
  students, and undergraduates in the University of Washington's Department of Human
  Centered Design &amp; Engineering. This event is designed to build community around
  HCDE research and foster conversation across our community of students, alumni,
  friends, and the broader UW community.</p> <p>Showcase attendees will explore posters
  and interactive project demos and have the opportunity to speak with researchers
  across <a>HCDE’s research clusters and topic areas</a>.</p> <p>This event is open
  to the public, with light refreshments and a cash bar available.</p> <h3>Call for
  submissions</h3> <p>We invite submissions from the HCDE research community in three
  formats:</p> <ol> <li> <strong>Poster</strong> </li> <li> <strong>Interactive demonstration</strong>
  </li> <li> <strong>Lightning talk</strong> (3 minutes, 1 slide; a high-impact overview
  of research or project)</li> </ol> <p>You may submit multiple presentation ideas,
  and you may submit a lightning talk in addition to a poster or demo.</p> <h4>How
  to submit</h4> <p>Submit your poster, demo, and/or lightning talk ideas via <a>this
  form</a> by Tuesday, February 17, at 5 p.m.</p> </div> </div> </article> </div>

Join the University of Washington's Department of Human Centered Design & Engineering for the HCDE Research Showcase on April 28 from 4 to 6 p.m. at the University of Washington Club (Faculty Center). This event will feature ongoing research from faculty, postdocs, PhD students, and undergraduates, fostering community and dialogue among students, alumni, and the broader UW community. Attendees can explore informative posters and interactive project demonstrations, engaging directly with researchers across various HCDE clusters. The showcase is open to the public and will include light refreshments and a cash bar. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with innovative work in human-centered design and engineering.
