Join the Chess Club on Tuesday, December 16, 2025, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This event welcomes participants of all skill levels, offering lessons and short tutorials to sharpen your chess skills. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, you’ll find a welcoming environment to enhance your game and meet fellow chess enthusiasts. For more information, contact Conwell Dickey at conwell@conwelld.net.
