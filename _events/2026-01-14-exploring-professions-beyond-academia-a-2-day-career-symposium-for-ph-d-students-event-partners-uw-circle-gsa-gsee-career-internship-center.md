Ph.D. students are invited to the "Exploring Professions Beyond Academia" symposium, a two-day career event designed to broaden career horizons. Scheduled for January 14-15, 2026, at the Student Union Building (HUB), attendees can participate in workshops, panel discussions, and networking opportunities with professionals from various fields. This event, hosted by UW CIRCLE, GSA, GSEE, and the Career & Internship Center, will provide valuable insights into career paths outside of traditional academic roles. Participants will learn about skill development and job search strategies tailored for diverse professions.
