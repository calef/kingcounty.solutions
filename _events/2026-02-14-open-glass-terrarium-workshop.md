Join the Open Glass Terrarium Workshop on Feb. 14, 2026, at the Douglas Research Conservatory. Supplies will be provided, ensuring everyone leaves with a beautiful, personalized terrarium to brighten their home or office. Don't miss this chance to explore your creativity and connect with fellow plant enthusiasts in a nurturing environment.
