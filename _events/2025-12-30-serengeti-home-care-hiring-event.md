Serengeti Home Care is hosting a hiring event on Dec. 30, 2025, from 10:30 a.m. to 2:30 p.m. at WorkSource Kitsap, located at 3120 NW Randall Way in Silverdale, Washington. Attendees can expect to interview one-on-one with hiring managers and learn about job opportunities for caregivers, with both full-time and part-time positions available. Serengeti Home Care offers competitive wages ranging from $18 to $21 per hour, flexible schedules, and employee benefits. No prior experience is necessary, and paid training will be provided to obtain HCA certification. Interested candidates should bring their resumes and ensure they are registered on WorkSourcewa.com before attending. This event is supported by the U.S. Department of Labor’s WIOA Title III Wagner-Peyser Employment Services.
