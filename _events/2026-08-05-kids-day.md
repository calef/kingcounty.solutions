Join the fun at Kid's Day on August 5, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Steel Lake Park in Federal Way, Washington. This free event promises a day filled with games, prizes, stage performances, and delicious food, making it perfect for families looking for entertainment and community engagement. Activities will include a family swim, but capacity is limited to 25 participants. Don’t miss this opportunity to create lasting memories with your children in a vibrant park setting. For more information, contact the City of Federal Way Parks and Recreation at 253-835-6900.
