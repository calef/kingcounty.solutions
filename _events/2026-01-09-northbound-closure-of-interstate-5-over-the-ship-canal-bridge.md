A full northbound closure of Interstate 5 over the Ship Canal Bridge in Seattle is scheduled to begin on Jan. 9, 2026, as part of the Revive I-5 project. Motorists should anticipate significant delays and plan alternative routes during this time. The closure is necessary for essential repairs and resurfacing work aimed at improving safety and traffic flow on one of the region's busiest highways. The project is expected to enhance the driving experience for all users once completed. Travelers are encouraged to stay informed about the construction timeline and to allow extra time for their commutes.
