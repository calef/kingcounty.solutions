Join us for the Community Dining Lunch on December 12 from noon to 12:45 p.m. at the Chamberlain Room. Enjoy a delicious meal prepared from scratch by Chef Corday Hatfield and Sous Chef Linda Harris. This event offers a welcoming atmosphere for all, with a suggested donation of $5 for attendees aged 60 and older, and $10 for others. Caregivers under age 60 are considered patrons aged 60+. Punch cards are available for frequent diners. Don’t miss this opportunity to connect with the community over a hearty lunch.
