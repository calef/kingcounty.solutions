Join certified yoga instructor Nadine Blinn for a Hatha Yoga class on Friday, Dec. 19, 2025, from 11:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This class is tailored for seniors and features gentle poses held for longer periods, allowing participants to ease into each movement. Attendees can expect a calming atmosphere with floor and chair exercises, making it accessible for all skill levels, from beginners to experienced yogis. Chairs will be provided for assistance with balancing and pose adaptations. Please bring a mat to participate fully in this relaxing and restorative session.
