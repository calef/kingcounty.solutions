Join the English Conversation Class on Jan. 13, 2026, from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This engaging event pairs foreign language speakers with volunteers for one-on-one English lessons, focusing on pronunciation and comprehension skills. Participants can expect a friendly atmosphere, making it a great opportunity to practice English while meeting new friends. Whether you're looking to improve your language skills or volunteer, this class welcomes everyone. For more information, contact Jerie Milici at jerie.milici@gmail.com.
