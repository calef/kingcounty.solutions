Shop sustainably at the UW Surplus Online Store on Dec. 17, 2025, starting at 10 a.m. at the Plant Services Building (PSB). This special event allows attendees to browse a variety of furniture, office essentials, and more, all sourced from unwanted items across the University of Washington. Each Wednesday, new items are added to the online storefront, making it easy to purchase and pick up within a week. The goal is to promote reuse and recycling, helping to lessen the university's climate impact. With inventory changing daily, there’s always something new to discover. For more information, contact surplus@uw.edu.
