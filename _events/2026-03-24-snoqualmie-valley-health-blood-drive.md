Join Snoqualmie Valley Health for a community blood drive on March 24, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Snoqualmie Valley Health. This event, in partnership with Bloodworks NW, invites community members to roll up their sleeves and donate blood, helping to save lives in the region.

Attendees can expect a welcoming atmosphere with trained staff on hand to guide them through the donation process. Refreshments will be provided to ensure donors feel energized and appreciated. Every donation can make a significant impact, so come out and do your part to support local hospitals and patients in need. Whether you are a first-time donor or a regular contributor, your participation is vital. Mark your calendar and help make a difference in your community!
