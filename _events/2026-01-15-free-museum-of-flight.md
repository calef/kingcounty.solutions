Enjoy a night of exploration at the Museum of Flight on January 15, 2026, from 5 p.m. to 9 p.m. This special event, part of the Free First Thursday series, allows visitors to experience the museum's Great Gallery, Personal Courage Wing, Simonyi Space Gallery, and Aviation Pavilion—all at no cost.

Local aerospace and technology organizations will be on hand to showcase their cutting-edge technologies and provide interactive hands-on experiences. Families can also engage in fun STEM workshops, making it an educational outing for all ages. Reservations are not required, but if you arrive before 5 p.m., standard admission fees apply. Join the community for an evening of discovery at the Museum of Flight, located at 9404 East Marginal Way South in Seattle.
