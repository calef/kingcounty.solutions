Registration for Normandy Park resident families opens on February 2, 2026, for the 2026-2027 school year at Normandy Park Preschool. This event offers an opportunity for families to secure a spot for their children in a nurturing educational environment. Attendees can learn about the preschool’s curriculum, meet the staff, and discover the programs available to foster early childhood development. Families are encouraged to bring any questions they may have about the enrollment process. Don’t miss this chance to ensure your child’s place in a vibrant learning community.
