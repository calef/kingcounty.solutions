Join us for a fun afternoon of Mexican Train Dominoes on December 12 from 1 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room. This event is open to everyone, and no prior experience is necessary, making it a perfect opportunity to learn and enjoy the game with friends and neighbors. Whether you're a seasoned player or a newcomer, come share in the excitement and strategy of this popular domino game. Don't miss out on the chance to socialize and engage in some friendly competition!
