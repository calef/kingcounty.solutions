Join the free "Herramientas Digitales/ Digital Skills for Life" event on December 11 from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S in Seattle. This interactive training, offered by the Business Opportunity Center, focuses on teaching essential digital tools that can enhance your daily life and business operations. Attendees will have the opportunity to learn, connect, and grow within the community during this autumn season. The program runs twice a week, on Tuesdays and Thursdays, from October 28 to November 20. Don't miss this chance to improve your digital skills and engage with fellow community members.
