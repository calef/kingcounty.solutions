Join NAMI Eastside for an in-person peer support group on December 18 from 6:30 p.m. to 8 p.m. at the Together Conference Center, located at 16305 NE 87th St., Redmond, WA 98052. This supportive environment is designed for individuals living with any form of mental illness, offering a space to share experiences and connect with others facing similar challenges. The group meets every second and fourth Thursday, providing a consistent opportunity for community and understanding. Attendees can expect to engage in meaningful discussions and build supportive relationships. Access the Together Center through the east side doors of the building. For more information, contact NAMI Eastside.
