Join the Ovarian Cancer Support Group sponsored by Cancer Lifeline on December 20 from 11:30 a.m. to 1 p.m. PST. This online event offers a supportive environment for women affected by ovarian cancer, whether newly diagnosed, in treatment, or survivors. Attendees can share experiences, gain insights, and connect with others facing similar challenges. The group meets monthly, providing ongoing support and community. For assistance with registration or to join the session, contact Cancer Lifeline at 206-297-2500 during business hours.
