  <strong>Wednesday, April 8th, 2026 from 3 PM – 5 PM</strong> in the <a>HUB Ballroom</a>.</p>

  MPH students to tackle real-world public health challenges. The annual MPH Practicum
  Symposium &amp; Reception brings together students, families, faculty, site supervisors,
  donors, agency and academic leaders, and more every year to celebrate students’
  practice work, faculty mentorship, and community partnership.</p> <p> </p> </div>
