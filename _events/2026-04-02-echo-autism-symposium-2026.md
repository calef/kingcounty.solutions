Join us for the ECHO Autism Symposium 2026, a free, full-day virtual event on Zoom, taking place on April 2, 2026. This symposium is designed to enhance autism services, access, care, and advocacy through a blend of evidence-based practices and personal experiences.

Attendees can expect insightful presentations from experts in the field, interactive discussions, and valuable networking opportunities. Participants will gain practical tools and strategies to improve autism services and support. Whether you are a professional, caregiver, or advocate, this event offers a unique platform to share knowledge and foster collaboration in the autism community. Don't miss this chance to be part of an enriching experience that aims to make a difference in the lives of individuals with autism and their families.
