Family members enrolled in specific health care programs can request IRS Form 1095-B by phone starting Jan. 31, 2026. This form is essential for reporting health coverage on tax returns. During the event, participants will have the opportunity to learn about the process of obtaining the form and have their questions answered by knowledgeable representatives. It’s a convenient way to ensure you have the necessary documentation for tax season. Make sure to have your personal information ready when you call. Don’t miss this chance to get the assistance you need for a smooth tax filing experience.
