Child care providers are invited to attend an important informational session regarding upcoming changes to reimbursement rates. Starting July 1, 2026, reimbursement rates will decrease from 85% to 75% of the Market Rate Value. This event will provide essential insights into how this change may impact your operations and finances. Attendees will have the opportunity to ask questions, share concerns, and discuss strategies to adapt to the new rates. Engage with fellow providers and gain valuable resources to navigate this transition effectively. Don't miss this chance to stay informed and connected in the child care community.
