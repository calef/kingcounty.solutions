The King County Regional Homelessness Authority will activate Tier 3 Protocols on Jan. 22, 2026, at 5 p.m. This initiative responds to forecasted overnight temperatures dropping below 30°F, ensuring the safety and well-being of vulnerable individuals experiencing homelessness in the community.

During this event, residents can expect increased outreach efforts, including the deployment of additional resources and services to provide shelter and warmth. Community members are encouraged to support these efforts by volunteering or donating supplies to assist those in need.

Join us in fostering a safe and compassionate environment for everyone in King County as we come together to address the challenges posed by extreme weather conditions. Your involvement can make a significant difference in the lives of those facing homelessness.
