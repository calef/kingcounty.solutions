Join the WorkSource Seattle-King County for the Work Smart Program on Dec. 29 from 1 to 3 p.m. This online workshop, "LinkedIn as an Effective Job Search Tool," is designed for those who have completed the "Introduction to LinkedIn" workshop and want to take their job search to the next level. Attendees will learn how to actively use LinkedIn to enhance their job search strategies.

Participants should have general computer and internet skills, as well as access to their LinkedIn accounts during the session. If you don’t have a LinkedIn account, please create one before attending. Registration is required, and a waitlist will be available if the event reaches capacity. For more information or accommodation requests, contact Marvin Stern at marvin@tracassoc.com.
