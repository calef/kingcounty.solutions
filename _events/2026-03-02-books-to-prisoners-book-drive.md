Join us for the Books to Prisoners Book Drive on March 2, 2026, at the Wallingford Community Senior Center. This event invites community members to donate books that will support the education and rehabilitation of incarcerated individuals.

Attendees can drop off gently used or new books, including fiction, non-fiction, and educational materials. Volunteers will be on hand to assist with donations and provide information about the impact of your contributions.

Help us promote literacy and empower those behind bars by participating in this meaningful initiative. Together, we can make a difference in the lives of individuals seeking to turn their lives around through education. Join us in fostering a culture of compassion and support.
