Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the ONLINE Strategies for Success: Communication workshop from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. on December 29, 2025. This four-day virtual event, conducted via Zoom, is designed to enhance your communication skills, essential for career advancement. Participants will explore interpersonal and business communication, develop better listening and writing techniques, and learn about verbal and non-verbal cues. The workshop also covers conflict resolution and emotional control, ensuring you present yourself effectively across various platforms, including phone, email, and social media. Registration is required, and a Zoom link will be sent via email one hour before the workshop begins. For more information, contact Kim Newkirk at 360-499-0004 or kimberly.newkirk@esd.wa.gov.
