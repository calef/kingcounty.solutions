Join us for Seattle’s Innovation for Unlocking Homeownership Webinar on Feb. 18, 2026, at 10 a.m. This engaging online event will explore how homeownership-led development can help prevent displacement and promote wealth-building in our communities.

Participants will learn about the potential of accessory dwelling units (ADUs) and middle housing as solutions to the housing crisis. Experts will share insights on innovative strategies and policies that can make homeownership more accessible and sustainable for all residents.

Whether you are a homeowner, a prospective buyer, or simply interested in housing issues, this webinar offers valuable information and resources to empower you in the quest for affordable housing solutions. Don’t miss this opportunity to be part of the conversation and contribute to shaping a more inclusive housing landscape in Seattle.
