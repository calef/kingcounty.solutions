Join local bird enthusiasts for "Backyard Birding" on Wednesday, Dec. 17, 2025, from 1 to 3 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. Led by experienced birder Richard Lazeres, this event will introduce attendees to winter sea ducks and other birds commonly seen along the West Seattle waterfront. Participants can expect an engaging workshop filled with insights and tips for birdwatching in the area. RSVP is required to secure your spot for this informative session.
