Join the fun at Move, Dance & Socialize on December 24, 2025, from 9 to 10 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This event welcomes all fitness levels to come together for a lively session of movement, dancing, and social interaction. Participants are encouraged to bring a water bottle, wear comfortable walking shoes, and embrace a positive attitude. It's a great opportunity to connect with friends and meet new people while enjoying an energetic atmosphere. Don't miss out on this chance to stay active and have fun!
