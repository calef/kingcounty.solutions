Join us for the Winter Break Make & Build event at the Northgate Branch on Dec. 23, 2025. This family-friendly gathering invites participants of all ages to unleash their creativity through hands-on activities. Attendees can engage in crafting projects, building fun structures, and exploring various materials.

Whether you're an experienced builder or trying something new, this event offers a chance to collaborate with others and create unique holiday-themed items. Supplies will be provided, so just bring your imagination and enthusiasm.

Don't miss this opportunity to make lasting memories with your family while celebrating the joy of the season. We look forward to seeing you there!
