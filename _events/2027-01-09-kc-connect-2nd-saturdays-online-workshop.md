Join the KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop on January 9, 2027, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. PST. This free, youth-centered workshop is designed for young people and their families across King County, focusing on real-life topics related to conflict, communication, and justice. Each monthly session explores different themes, fostering a welcoming virtual space for discussion. Attendees are encouraged to participate openly, with no preparation required. This workshop is part of a series that includes skill-building and critical awareness modules. To RSVP, visit the event's [Calendly page](https://calendly.com/kcdrc-youth-development/2nd-saturdays-with-kc-connect?month=2025-10). Don’t miss this opportunity to engage and learn valuable conflict resolution skills!
