The Disaster Assistance Center will open at the King County Library System Administrative Office in Issaquah on Jan. 4, 2026, at 11 a.m. This center is designed to support residents impacted by recent floods. Attendees can access vital resources, including information on recovery programs, financial assistance, and housing support. Trained professionals will be available to answer questions and provide guidance tailored to individual needs. This event aims to help the community navigate the challenges following the disaster and connect with necessary services. Residents are encouraged to bring any relevant documentation to facilitate the assistance process. The center will remain open for several days, providing ongoing support to those affected.
