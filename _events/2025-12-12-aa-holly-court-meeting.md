Join the AA Holly Court Meeting on December 12 from 2 p.m. to 3:30 p.m. in the Lux Room. This gathering offers a supportive environment for individuals seeking to share experiences and connect with others on their recovery journey. Attendees can expect engaging discussions, encouragement, and valuable resources focused on personal growth and sobriety. Whether you are new to recovery or have been on the path for some time, this meeting provides a welcoming space to find community and support.
