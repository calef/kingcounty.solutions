Join us for the Summer Movie in the Park on July 25, 2026, from 9 p.m. to 11 p.m. at Town Square Park, located at 31600 Pete von Reichbauer Way South in Federal Way, Washington. This free community event invites families and friends to enjoy a movie under the stars. Attendees are encouraged to bring blankets and lawn chairs for a comfortable viewing experience. Complimentary popcorn will be provided. Don’t miss this opportunity for a fun evening in the park! For more information, contact the City of Federal Way Parks and Recreation at 253-835-6900.
