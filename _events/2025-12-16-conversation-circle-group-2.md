Join the Conversation Circle - Group #2 on Tuesday, Dec. 16, 2025, from 1 to 2:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This event offers a welcoming environment for participants to share stories, build connections, and enhance their communication skills. Attendees can expect to engage in open discussions that foster deeper friendships and help alleviate feelings of loneliness. For more information about Group #2, contact Teressa Keenehan at teekay3@comcast.net.
