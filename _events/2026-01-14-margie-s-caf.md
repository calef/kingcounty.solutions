Join us for Margie’s Café on Jan. 14, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. Attendees can enjoy a cozy atmosphere while savoring a rotating menu of delicious, freshly prepared items, including cookies, sandwiches, salads, and soups. Coffee, lunch, and snacks are available à la carte, making it a perfect spot for a casual meal or a quick bite. Whether you're looking to socialize or simply enjoy good food, Margie’s Café offers a welcoming environment for all.
