Join the City of Federal Way for the Summer Movie in the Park on June 27, 2026, from 8:30 PM to 11:00 PM at Town Square Park, located at 31600 Pete von Reichbauer Way South, Federal Way, Washington. This free community event invites families and friends to enjoy a movie under the stars. Attendees are encouraged to bring blankets and lawn chairs for a comfortable viewing experience. Additionally, free popcorn will be provided. The specific movie title will be announced closer to the event date. Don't miss this opportunity for a fun, family-friendly evening! For more information, contact Federal Way Parks and Recreation at 253-835-6900.
