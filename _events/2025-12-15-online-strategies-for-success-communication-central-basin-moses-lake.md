Join the ONLINE Strategies for Success: Communication Central Basin workshop from December 15 to December 18, 2025, starting at 9 a.m. each day. This four-day online event, hosted by WorkSource Seattle-King County, focuses on enhancing communication skills essential for job seekers. Attendees will learn about interpersonal and business communication, develop listening and writing skills, and practice conflict resolution. Participants will also receive tips on presenting themselves effectively across various platforms, including phone, email, and social media. This workshop is designed to be flexible, allowing attendees to customize their learning experience. Registration is required, and a Zoom link will be provided via email prior to the event. For more information, contact Raquel Castillo at 509-766-4144 or Denise Marion at 509-665-3728.
