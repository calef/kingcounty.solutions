Join the Friends of Normandy Park Foundation and local families for the Family Foresters event on Jan. 17, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Nist Park in Normandy Park, Washington. This family-friendly gathering invites participants to engage in tree planting and noxious weed removal, contributing to local environmental efforts. Attendees will receive tools, supplies, and education on best practices for planting trees and shrubs, along with fun themed coloring and crafts. The event aims to foster awareness about air quality and climate change while providing a hands-on experience in nature. Space is limited, so early registration is encouraged. Parking is available at the park, with overflow options nearby. Enjoy a day of community service and learning while making a positive impact on the environment.
