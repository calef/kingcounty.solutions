El Centro de la Raza invites the community to participate in "Digital Skills for Life," a free Spanish-language digital literacy training. The event will take place on February 18, 2026, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. via Zoom. Attendees can expect to build confidence and foundational skills in digital technology, including understanding computer basics, using Office programs like Word, Excel, and PowerPoint, and learning best practices for safe internet browsing. The training also offers tools and tips to help protect children from inappropriate digital content. Space is limited, so interested participants should contact Luis Jaimez at 206-833-2986 to secure a spot. Join us to enhance your digital skills and support your learning journey.
