Applications for the HTF Repair Fund will be accepted starting January 26, 2026. This funding opportunity is designed to support the repair of affordable housing units that have previously received public funding.

Attendees can learn about the application process, eligibility criteria, and the types of repairs covered by the fund. This event is ideal for property owners, housing organizations, and community members interested in improving affordable housing in their area.

Join us to gain valuable insights and ensure your housing projects meet the necessary standards for funding. Don’t miss this chance to secure financial support for vital repairs that enhance living conditions in your community.
