Join us for an Accessibility Features Workshop on Dec. 31, 2025, at 1 p.m. at the Exercise Room in the Shoreline-LFP Senior Activity Center, located at 18560 1st Ave. NE, Bldg. G, Shoreline, WA. This interactive session will explore accessibility settings on Apple and Android devices, highlighting tools that assist users with hearing, vision, speech, and other impairments. Attendees will gain valuable insights and hands-on experience to enhance their understanding of these essential features, making technology more accessible for everyone.
