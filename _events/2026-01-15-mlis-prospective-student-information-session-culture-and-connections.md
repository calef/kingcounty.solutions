Join the MLIS Prospective Student Information Session: Culture and Connections on Jan. 15, 2026, at 11 a.m. PST via Zoom. This virtual event is designed for individuals considering a Master of Library and Information Science degree. Attendees will have the opportunity to learn about the program, engage with faculty and current students, and explore how the MLIS can enhance their career prospects. Participants can expect to gain insights into the culture of the program and the connections that can be made within the field. Don't miss this chance to discover how the MLIS can shape your future in information management.
