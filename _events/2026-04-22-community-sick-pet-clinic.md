The Community Sick Pet Clinic will take place on April 22 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at El Centro de la Raza, located at 2524 16th Ave S in Seattle. Attendees can expect sliding-scale veterinary care for pets, specifically designed for individuals who are unhoused or have low incomes. This event operates on a first-come, first-served basis, with limited spaces available. Each family may bring a maximum of two pets. A code of conduct will be enforced to ensure a respectful environment for all participants.
