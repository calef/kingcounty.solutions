Residents can dispose of flood debris at the Free Flood Debris Dropoff event on December 20 and 21 at the Bow Lake Recycling & Transfer Station, located at 18800 Orillia Rd S in Tukwila. This event provides an opportunity for community members to safely drop off items affected by flooding, helping to keep neighborhoods clean and safe.

Please note that there are specific items not accepted at the recycling and transfer stations. For more details, including operational hours and guidelines for hazardous waste disposal, attendees are encouraged to reach out to local waste management services. This is a crucial chance for residents to manage flood-related debris responsibly while contributing to community recovery efforts.
