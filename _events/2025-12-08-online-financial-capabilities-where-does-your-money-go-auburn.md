Join the ONLINE Financial Capabilities workshop, "Where Does Your Money Go?" hosted by WorkSource Seattle-King County, starting December 8, 2025, from 1:30 to 3:30 p.m. This six-week series, held on Mondays and Tuesdays, empowers participants to analyze their spending habits and build a monthly budget. Each session is designed to enhance your financial decision-making skills in a flexible format, allowing you to customize your learning experience. 

The workshop will take place online via Zoom, and attendees will receive meeting links via email shortly before the start. Registration is required, so be sure to sign up in advance. For more information or to request accommodations, contact Erik Hontz at erik.hontz@esd.wa.gov. Don’t miss this opportunity to take control of your finances!
