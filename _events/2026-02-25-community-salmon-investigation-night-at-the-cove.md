Join the Community Salmon Investigation Night at The Cove on February 24, 2026, from 6 to 8 p.m. at the Normandy Park Community Club, located at 1500 SW Shorebrook Dr, Normandy Park, WA. This event will feature an exploration of the 2025 salmon survey results from the Miller-Walker Basin and discussions on their implications for salmon health. Attendees will hear from Nate Ivy of Washington State University, who will present his latest research on the impact of tire dust on juvenile salmon. Survey volunteers are encouraged to bring any gear and salmon survey kits for return at the gear drop-off table. This event is open to community members interested in local salmon conservation efforts. For more information, visit the event website.
