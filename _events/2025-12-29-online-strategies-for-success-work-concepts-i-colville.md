Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Online Strategies for Success: Work Concepts I" on December 29, 2025, from 9 a.m. to noon. This online workshop aims to help participants develop a personalized roadmap to success. Attendees will explore essential topics such as self-motivation, career exploration, and effective job application strategies.

The workshop is part of a flexible series of life-skills modules that can be taken individually or combined for a comprehensive experience. Classes will also be held on December 30, January 2, and January 3, with no session on New Year's Day. Registration is required, and participants will receive Zoom meeting details via email at least one hour before the workshop begins. For accommodations, contact Karla Hood at 509-685-6141 or karla.hood@esd.wa.gov.
