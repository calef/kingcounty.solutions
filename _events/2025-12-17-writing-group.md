Join the Writing Group on Wednesday, Dec. 17, 2025, from 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This group, facilitated by Natalie Bumgardner, provides a supportive environment for writers to share stories and receive feedback. Whether you're writing for family or personal reflection, you'll find encouragement from fellow participants. Space is limited, so it is recommended to contact Natalie in advance for materials and additional information. Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your writing skills and connect with others who share your passion.
