The application deadline for $3.6 million in funding from the National Housing Trust Fund and HOME programs is March 16, 2026. This funding is aimed at supporting multifamily rental housing projects.

Interested applicants can submit their proposals to secure financial assistance for affordable housing initiatives in their communities. This is an excellent opportunity for developers and organizations focused on creating or improving rental housing options for low-income families.

Ensure your application highlights the project's impact on community needs and aligns with the goals of the funding programs. Don't miss this chance to contribute to affordable housing solutions in your area.
