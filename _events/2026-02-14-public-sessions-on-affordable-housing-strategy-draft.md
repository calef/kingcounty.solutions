Join us for public sessions on the draft of the 2026-2032 Affordable Housing Strategy, starting on February 14, 2026. These sessions provide an opportunity for community members to engage directly with city planners and share their thoughts on the proposed strategy. Attendees will be able to ask questions, offer feedback, and contribute ideas that will shape the future of affordable housing in our community. Your input is crucial in ensuring that the strategy reflects the needs and priorities of residents. Don’t miss this chance to make your voice heard and help create a more inclusive housing plan. All community members are encouraged to attend and participate in this important discussion.
