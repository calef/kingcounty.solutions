  <article> <div> Submitted by <span><span>Leo Carmona</span></span> on <span><time>January
  5, 2026 - 11:03 am</time> </span> </div> <div> <div> <div> <div> <p>We are excited
  to begin the year with the 2026 Jacob Lawrence Legacy Residency. This year’s resident,
  indira allegra, has arrived and is settling into the gallery, which will function
  as their studio throughout the residency. During this time, indira will work in
  the gallery with two student studio assistants, conduct visit sites across Seattle,
  connect with partnering academic departments, and engage UW resources to inform
  their research and artistic practice.</p> <p>The residency culminates in the exhibition
  indira allegra: <em>The Book of Zero</em>, on view from <a>February 4 through April
  4, 2026</a>. Please join us for the opening celebration on <a>February 4 from 5–7pm</a>,
  as well as a series of community-centered opportunities to engage with Indira’s
  practice before and after the exhibition opens.</p> <h2>About the Exhibition</h2>
  <p>Death is a fundamental first step toward rebirth—but this transition can feel
  daunting without a compassionate guide. In <em>The Book of Zero</em>, our 2026 Jacob
  Lawrence Legacy Resident indira allegra presents a multimedia, meditative experience
  shaped by their research into doula work, death care, and the cyclical nature of
  bodies and environments.</p> <p>Water, weaving, and projected imagery animate the
  earth and its afflictions, creating choreographed spaces for reflection and transformation.
  The systems that have long shaped and harmed our communities — white supremacy,
  patriarchy, capitalism, and bigotry—are understood here as structures in their final
  death throes, requiring care, intention, and collective effort to fully dissolve.
  Drawing on the narrative rhythms and poetic repetition of sacred texts, The Book
  of Zero moves through the difficulty of endings with compassion, opening space for
  renewal.</p> <p>Rebirth unfolds on both collective and individual levels. As visitors
  move through the exhibition, they are invited to ask: What do I need to release
  in order to move forward? <em>The Book of Zero</em> encourages openness to the transformations
  necessary to usher in an era of healing.</p> <h2>Opportunities to Engage</h2> <ul>
  <li>January 22, 2:30pm:<a> Community Office Hours (Informal Q&amp;A and tea with
  indira)</a> </li> <li>February 4, 5–7pm: <a>Opening Celebration</a> </li> <li>February
  6, 3:00pm: <a>Community Sit (A guided meditation hosted by indira)</a> </li> <li>February
  17, 5:30–7:30pm: <a>Liberation Book Club x Jacob Lawrence Legacy Residency</a> </li>
  <li>February 27: Stay tuned for details about an interdisciplinary convening bringing
  together doulas and scientists to consider the themes of The Book of Zero.</li>
  </ul> <h2>About the Resident</h2> <p>indira allegra (they/them) is a conceptual
  artist and founder of Cazimi Studio. allegra's work has been featured in <em>The
  Art Newspaper</em>, <em>Artnet</em>, <em>Art Journal</em>, <em>BOMB Magazine</em>,
  <em>SF Chronicle</em>, <em>e-flux</em>, A<em>ll Arts</em>, <em>Topical Cream </em>and
  <em>ARTFORUM</em> and presented in exhibitions and performances at the Museum of
  Arts and Design (New York, NY), Blaffer Art Museum (Houston, TX), KADIST (San Francisco,
  CA), Center for Craft (Asheville, NC), Museum of the African Diaspora (San Francisco,
  CA), and SFMOMA (San Francisco, CA) among others. allegra is the author of <em>Tension
  Studies</em> (2024) and <em>Dispersal of a Feeling: Bloodnotes on Choreography and
  Illness</em> (2024) and <em>Blackout </em>(2017, Sming Sming Books). Their writing
  has been featured in <em>Theater</em>, <em>TEXTILE: Cloth and Culture</em>, <em>American
  Craft Magazine</em>, <em>Panorama Journal</em>, <em>Leonardo</em> and <em>Material
  Intelligence</em> among others. allegra has been the recipient of numerous awards
  including the Burke Prize, Creative Capital, United States Artists Fellowship, Gerbode
  Choreographer Award, Art Matters Fellowship, Pew Center for Arts &amp; Heritage
  Creative Project Grant and CripTech Metaverse Fellowship.</p> <p>Learn more at <a>indiraallegra.com</a>.</p>
  <h2>About the Residency</h2> <p>The 2026 Jacob Lawrence Legacy Residency is supported
  in part by the Names Family Foundation, University of Washington Black Opportunity
  Fund, Donald E. Petersen Endowment for Excellence, Charles Delahunt, Carole Fuller,
  Floyd and Delores Jones Endowed Fund for the Arts, Kreielsheimer Arts Endowment,
  and Tricia Tiano and Kent Mettler. </p> <p>Learn more about the residency <a>here</a>.</p>

Join us for the Interdisciplinary Convening on February 27, 2026, at the Art Building. This event will bring together doulas and scientists to explore the themes presented in the exhibition "The Book of Zero," created by the 2026 Jacob Lawrence Legacy Resident, indira allegra. Attendees can expect engaging discussions that bridge artistic and scientific perspectives on death, rebirth, and community healing.

As part of the residency, allegra has been working in the gallery with student assistants and conducting site visits across Seattle. This convening is an opportunity to deepen your understanding of the exhibition's multimedia experience and the cyclical nature of life and environments. Don't miss this chance to participate in a thought-provoking dialogue that encourages reflection and transformation.
