Join Bike Works for a Volunteer Repair Party on March 24, 2026, from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. at the Bike Works Warehouse, located at 3715 S Hudson St #111 in Seattle. Whether you're a seasoned mechanic or a beginner, your help is welcome at this collaborative event aimed at promoting bike repair and sustainability.
