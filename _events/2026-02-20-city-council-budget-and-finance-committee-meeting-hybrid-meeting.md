The City Council Budget & Finance Committee will hold a hybrid meeting on Feb. 20, 2026, from 2 to 3:30 p.m. The meeting will take place at City Hall, located at 17425 Ballinger Way NE, Lake Forest Park, WA, and will also be accessible via Zoom. Community members, local stakeholders, and anyone interested in the city’s budget and finance matters are encouraged to attend. This meeting provides an opportunity for participants to engage in discussions regarding financial planning and budgeting for the city. For more information, visit the city’s official calendar.
