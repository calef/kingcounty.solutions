Community members are invited to visit ARCH on December 18 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 119, in Redmond. Attendees can ask questions about ARCH programs, pick up affordable housing information packets, and receive assistance with signing up for email alerts about new low-income housing opportunities. This open office hours event provides a valuable chance to connect with ARCH staff and learn more about available resources.
