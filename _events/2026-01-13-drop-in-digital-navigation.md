Join us for Drop-in Digital Navigation on Tuesday, Jan. 13, 2026, from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This event offers attendees the opportunity to receive personalized support for their cell phones, tablets, or personal computers. Each participant will have a 25-minute session to ask questions and get assistance. No appointment is necessary, but a suggested donation of $5 is appreciated. To ensure the computer lab is open, please call 206-932-4044, ext. 114, to confirm the schedule for the day.
