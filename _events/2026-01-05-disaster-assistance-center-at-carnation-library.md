The Disaster Assistance Center will open at Carnation Library on Jan. 5, 2026, at 11 a.m. This center is designed to support residents affected by recent floods. Attendees can access vital resources, including information on recovery services, financial assistance, and community support programs. Trained staff will be available to answer questions and provide guidance on navigating the recovery process. The center aims to help residents regain stability and connect with local services. This is a crucial opportunity for those impacted to receive the assistance they need. The center will remain open for several days, offering a welcoming environment for individuals and families seeking help.
