Join Overlake Medical Center for "Cancer & Nutrition: Seasonal Herbs & Spices" on Dec. 17 from 2 to 3:30 p.m. PST. This online event will explore the benefits of herbs and spices, which can significantly enhance your diet while mitigating cancer-related side effects. Attendees will learn practical tips on incorporating these nutrient-rich ingredients into everyday meals, adding flavor without heat. This interactive session is ideal for anyone looking to improve their nutritional approach during cancer treatment. For registration assistance, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500.
