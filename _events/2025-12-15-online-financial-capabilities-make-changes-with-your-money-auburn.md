Join the ONLINE Financial Capabilities: Make Changes With Your Money workshop series, hosted by WorkSource Seattle-King County, starting December 15 at 1:30 p.m. This six-week program, held via Zoom, meets twice a week on Mondays and Tuesdays, focusing on empowering participants to make informed financial decisions. Attendees will explore their financial goals and learn how to effectively use banks and lending institutions. Each session is designed to be flexible, allowing participants to customize their learning experience. Registration is required, and links will be sent prior to the start of the meeting. Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your financial literacy and take control of your money.
