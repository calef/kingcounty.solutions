Join the Prostate Cancer Support Group sponsored by EvergreenHealth on December 18 from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. PST. This online event offers a supportive environment for individuals affected by prostate cancer to discuss current treatment options, including promising new therapies. Attendees will have the opportunity to share experiences and gain insights from others navigating similar challenges, from initial diagnosis to active surveillance. Connect with fellow participants and enhance your understanding of the latest advancements in prostate cancer care. For registration assistance or questions, contact EvergreenHealth's Lifeline at 206-297-2500 during business hours.
