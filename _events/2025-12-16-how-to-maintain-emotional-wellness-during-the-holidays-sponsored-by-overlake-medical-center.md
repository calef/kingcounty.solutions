Join Overlake Medical Center on December 16 from 5:30 to 6:30 p.m. PST for an online event focused on maintaining emotional wellness during the holiday season. This session will explore simple art activities and creative expression as calming outlets to help restore balance and peace amidst the chaos of shopping and social gatherings. No artistic skills or experience are required—just bring your willingness to embrace hope and inspiration. This hour is a valuable gift to yourself, offering a chance to prioritize self-care during a hectic time of year.
