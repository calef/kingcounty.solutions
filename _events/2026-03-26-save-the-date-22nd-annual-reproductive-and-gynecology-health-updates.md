  <div> <p><span>These learning objectives are designed to address identified practice
  gaps by improving interprofessional competence and performance, clarifying professional
  roles, and fostering collaborative practice to enhance patient outcomes in reproductive
  and gynecologic care. </span></p> <p><span>At the conclusion of this activity, physicians,
  nurses, and other healthcare professionals will be able to:</span></p> </div> <ol>
  <li>Collaborate as an interprofessional team to apply current evidence-based updates
  in reproductive and gynecologic healthcare, improving shared clinical decision-making
  and patient-centered care. </li> <li>Integrate nutrition counseling strategies into
  preventive care through coordinated team approaches to reduce cancer risk and support
  holistic patient outcomes. </li> <li>Enhance team competence in delivering inclusive,
  gender-affirming gynecologic care and collectively address health disparities across
  diverse populations. </li> <li>Assess the impact of evolving legislation and systemic
  barriers on access to reproductive healthcare, and work together to identify strategies
  for advocacy and equitable care. </li> <li>Demonstrate improved team-based competence
  in diagnosing and managing abnormal uterine bleeding using both medical and surgical
  approaches, leveraging each profession’s expertise.</li> <li>Identify practical,
  collaborative strategies to overcome systemic barriers such as insurance limitations,
  time constraints, and access to specialty care in reproductive health settings.</li>
  <li>Demonstrate commitment to shared ethical values by the interprofessional team
  in delivering reproductive healthcare with respect, equity, and cultural humility.</li>

Join healthcare professionals for the 22nd Annual Reproductive and Gynecology Health Updates, taking place from March 26 to March 27, 2026, at the University of Washington's South Lake Union Campus in Seattle, WA. This hybrid conference offers a comprehensive clinical update for providers who care for women in outpatient settings. Attendees can expect engaging sessions on reproductive health, nutrition strategies for cancer prevention, and inclusive gender-affirming care, among other relevant topics.

The program aims to enhance interprofessional collaboration and improve patient outcomes by addressing systemic barriers and advocating for equitable care. Participants can earn up to 15 AMA PRA Category 1 Credits™. Registration is now open, with early bird rates available until January 31, 2026. Don't miss this opportunity to stay informed and improve your practice in reproductive healthcare.
