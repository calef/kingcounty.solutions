WorkSource Seattle-King County will host the online workshop "Estrategias para el Éxito: Modulo 1 - Conceptos de Trabajo I" on December 29 from 9 a.m. to 12 p.m. This event is part of a series of life skills workshops designed to enhance participants' job readiness. Attendees can expect a flexible and customizable experience, as each module can be taken individually or combined with others.

The workshops will be conducted in Spanish via Zoom, and participants are encouraged to register in advance. Those interested should create a job seeker account at WorksourceWA.com before attending. If the class is full, a waitlist will be established, and registrations will be prioritized based on specific criteria. For more information or accommodation requests, contact Raquel Castillo at 509-766-4144 or via email.
