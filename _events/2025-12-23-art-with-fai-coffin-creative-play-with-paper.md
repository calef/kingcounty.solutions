Join Fai Coffin for "Creative Play with Paper" on Dec. 23 from 10 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. in the Lux Room. This engaging session invites participants of all skill levels to explore the art of paper crafting. Attendees will have the opportunity to create unique paper projects using provided materials, making it a perfect way to unleash creativity. The cost is $4 for members. Whether you're a seasoned artist or just looking to try something new, this event promises a fun and enriching experience. Don't miss out on this chance to express your artistic side!
