  <div> <article> <h3>Prepare to retire from UW</h3> <h5>Attend a retirement workshop
  livestream using Zoom</h5> <p>Attending a UW Benefits retirement workshop when planning
  for your retirement is critical in understanding the process of retiring from the
  University of Washington. Most workshops are available in two formats—join the workshop
  using livestream or watch at your own pace with an on-demand webinar. Our UWSRP
  workshop is only available via livestream.</p> <p>If you prefer to keep your pre-retirement
  process confidential, the livestream option allows you to change your name to <em>anonymous</em> and
  with the on-demand webinar option, you can watch short segments of the retirement
  workshop whenever you would like without attending a live workshop.</p> <p>Spouses
  and partners are welcome to view the livestream workshop or the on-demand webinar.</p>
  <h4>Retiring from a DRS plan (PERS, TRS, PSERS or LEOFF)</h4> <p>The workshop is
  intended for employees participating in the Public Employees Retirement System (PERS)
  plans 1, 2, or 3, Teachers’ Retirement System (TRS) plans 1 or 3, Public Safety
  Employees Retirement System (PSERS) plan 2, and Law Enforcement Officers’ and Fire
  Fighters’ Retirement System (LEOFF) plan 2.</p> <p><a>Livestreamed DRS workshop</a>
  <a>Webinar: Retiring from a DRS plan</a></p> <h4>Retiring from UWRP: An Overview</h4>
  <p>This workshop is intended for employees participating in the University of Washington
  Retirement Plan (UWRP).</p> <p><a>Livestreamed UWRP workshop</a> <a>Webinar: Retiring
  from UWRP</a></p> <h4>UWSRP: UW Supplemental Retirement Plan Workshop</h4> <p>This
  workshop is intended for employees who were active participants in the UW Retirement
  Plan (UWRP) on February 28th, 2011 and who, at least time of retirement, anticipate
  being at least age 62 with at least 10 years’ continuous participation in the UWRP.</p>
  <p><a>UWSRP workshop</a></p> </article> <aside> <div> <h4>Required for retirement</h4>
  <div> <p><a>You must submit an online UW Retirement Application</a> within 6 months
  of your UW separation date and no later than 60 days after the separation date.</p>
  <h4>UW Retirement Checklist</h4> <p>The UW Retirement Checklist has deadlines and
  resources that will get you through the retirement process.</p> <p><a>Retirement
  – UW Retirement Checklist</a></p> </div> </div> </aside> </div> </section>"
