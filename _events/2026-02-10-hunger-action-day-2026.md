Join hundreds of anti-hunger advocates for Hunger Action Day 2026 on Tuesday, February 10, from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m. at the United Churches of Olympia, located at 110 11th Ave. SE in Olympia. This annual event, organized by the Washington Anti-Hunger and Nutrition Coalition, focuses on advocating for anti-hunger legislation. Attendees will have the opportunity to meet with their legislators and share their voices on critical funding for programs that support vulnerable populations in Washington. If you're interested in participating, please sign up by February 3. For those looking to carpool, email info@hungerintervention.org for coordination. Your presence is vital in the fight against hunger in the state.
