The UW Surplus Public Store invites the community to explore a variety of items available for purchase on Feb. 17, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. at the Plant Services Building (PSB). Attendees can expect to find unique bargains as the store receives new inventory daily. The store offers a range of surplus items from University of Washington departments and medical facilities, promoting sustainability through reuse and recycling. Whether you’re looking for furniture, equipment, or other goods, this event provides a great opportunity to discover valuable items while supporting environmental efforts. Don't miss out on the chance to find something special! For accessibility inquiries, contact surplus@uw.edu.
