Join the Digital Literacy Learning Lab on Dec. 23 from 12:30 p.m. to 4 p.m. at WorkSource Spokane, located at 130 S. Arthur, Spokane, WA. This self-paced lab offers a welcoming environment for individuals of all ages and skill levels to enhance their computer and internet skills.

Attendees can expect to learn essential topics, including basic computer use, email, word processing, and spreadsheets. With personalized assistance from friendly staff, participants can navigate the curriculum at their own pace, making it ideal for beginners or those looking to refresh their skills. Resources such as online tutorials and practice exercises will also be available. Whether you're a student, job seeker, or senior wanting to connect with family online, this lab is designed to help you succeed in the digital world.
