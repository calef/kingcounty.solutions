Join us for a Free Weekend Storm Debris Drop-Off event in King County, starting December 19 and running through January 11. Residents can dispose of flood debris, including yard waste and garbage, at three designated locations throughout the county. This is an excellent opportunity to clear your property of storm-related debris while helping to maintain the community's cleanliness and safety.

Bring your items to one of the drop-off sites on weekends, where staff will be available to assist you. This service is free of charge, making it easier for everyone to participate in the cleanup effort. Don’t miss this chance to contribute to a healthier environment and ensure your neighborhood is ready for the new year.
