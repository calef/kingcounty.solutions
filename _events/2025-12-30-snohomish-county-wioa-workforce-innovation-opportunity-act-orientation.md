Join the Snohomish County WIOA (Workforce Innovation Opportunity Act) Orientation on Dec. 30 from 10 to 11 a.m. at WorkSource Everett, located at 728 134th St. SW, Suite 102, Everett, WA 98204. This informative session will provide attendees with insights into the WIOA program, including its benefits for jobseekers and the enrollment process. Participants will gain valuable knowledge about available resources and support for their job search. Pre-registration is required for those attending online. Be sure to create an account at WorkSourceWA.com before the event. For more information, contact the WIOA program at 425-372-7205.
