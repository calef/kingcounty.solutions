Join the MSIM Information Session featuring a student panel on Jan. 20, 2026, at 4:30 p.m. PST via Zoom. This event offers prospective students the chance to hear firsthand experiences from current Master of Science in Information Management students. Attendees will gain insights into the program, learn about the entrepreneurial journey, and discover how to develop a startup from the ground up. Engage with the panelists, ask questions, and explore the opportunities that await in the field of information management. Don't miss this opportunity to connect and gather valuable information that can help shape your educational and professional path.
