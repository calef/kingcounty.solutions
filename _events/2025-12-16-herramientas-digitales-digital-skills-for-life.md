Join El Centro de la Raza on Dec. 16 from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. for "Herramientas Digitales/Digital Skills for Life" at 2524 16th Ave S, Seattle. This free training session is designed to help attendees learn essential digital tools that can enhance both daily life and business operations. Participants will have the opportunity to connect with their community while gaining valuable skills in a supportive environment. The event is part of a series aimed at fostering growth and opportunity during the fall season. Don't miss this chance to learn, connect, and thrive!
