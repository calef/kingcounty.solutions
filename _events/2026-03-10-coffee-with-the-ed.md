Join Dietra Clayton, Executive Director of The Sophia Way, for "Coffee with the ED" on March 10, 2026, from 9 to 10 a.m. at The Sophia Way, 11061 NE 2nd Street, Suite 223, Bellevue, WA. This gathering offers a unique opportunity to connect and reflect on efforts to support women experiencing homelessness on the Eastside. Dietra will present the 2025 Impact Report, showcasing how community support has positively impacted the women served, and unveil the Strategic Plan 2030, outlining the organization’s vision and priorities for the next five years. Attendees can enjoy coffee and light refreshments while engaging in this important community conversation. All are welcome to attend and contribute to the discussion. For more information, visit The Sophia Way's website.
