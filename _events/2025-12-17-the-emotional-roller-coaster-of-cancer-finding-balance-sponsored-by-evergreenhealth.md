Join EvergreenHealth for "The Emotional Roller Coaster of Cancer: Finding Balance" on Dec. 17 from 9:30 to 10:30 a.m. PST. This online event will provide valuable skills and strategies for coping with the complex emotions that often follow a cancer diagnosis, including fear, anxiety, and sadness. Attendees will learn healthy ways to manage these feelings and find balance during challenging times. Whether you are a patient, caregiver, or loved one, this session aims to equip you with tools to navigate the emotional ups and downs associated with cancer. Don't miss this opportunity for support and guidance in a safe, virtual environment.
