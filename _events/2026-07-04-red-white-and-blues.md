Join the Red, White & Blues Festival on July 4, 2026, from 4 p.m. to 11 p.m. at Celebration Park, 1095 S. 324th St, Federal Way, WA. This free event features live music, food vendors, and family-friendly activities, culminating in a spectacular fireworks display at 10:15 p.m. The main stage will showcase a variety of performers starting at 4 p.m. Attendees are encouraged to bring lawn chairs and blankets for comfort. Admission and parking are free, with parking available on a first-come, first-served basis. This festival is perfect for families and anyone looking to celebrate Independence Day with great entertainment and community spirit. For more information, visit the City of Federal Way Parks and Recreation website or call 253-835-6900.
