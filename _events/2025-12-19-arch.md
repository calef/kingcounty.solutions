Community members are invited to visit ARCH on December 19 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at 16305 NE 87th St, Suite 119, Redmond, WA. During this open office hours event, attendees can ask questions about ARCH programs, gather information about affordable housing options, and receive assistance with signing up for email alerts regarding new low-income housing opportunities. This is a great chance to connect with resources and get support for housing needs.
