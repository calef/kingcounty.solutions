  <div> <p>Each Foster Study Abroad Program will include the following fees in the

  Foster Study Abroad Fee and <a>UW Study Abroad Fee</a></p> <p>The Foster Study Abroad
  Fee is an Administrative Fee that funds all Foster Study Abroad activities, including
  the organization and operations of the programs; the recruitment, selection, and
  support of program participants; and the offering of study abroad advising. This
  fee is charged to all students participating on a Foster Study Abroad Program regardless
  of major. This is a non-refundable fee charged to students even if they withdraw
  from their study abroad program.</p> <p>The Foster Study Abroad Fee is $375 through
  Spring Quarter 2026 and the fee will increase starting with programs happening in
  Summer 2026 to $390.</p> <p>If you cannot participate in a Foster study abroad program

  Fee). Please note that you will also need to submit an <a>emergency withdrawal petition
  to UW Study Abroad</a> to be considered for partial relief from the other financial

  <div> <h3>How do I apply to study abroad?</h3> <ul> <li>Students apply to all study
  abroad program at UW on the <a>UW Study Abroad Office</a> website.</li> <li>When
  the application is open, students apply to all study abroad program at UW on the <a><span>UW
  Study Abroad Office</span></a> website. Programs will be shared on the <a><span>Foster
  Study Abroad Options</span></a> website well in advance of the application being
  open so that students can make plans about courses and dates.</li> </ul> <h3>What
  should I do to make sure that study abroad fits into my degree plan and does not
  case me to extend my graduation date?</h3> <ul> <li>Meet with a <a>Foster Academic
  Adviser</a> and a <a>GBC Foster Study Abroad Adviser</a>.</li> <li>You can get upper
  division business core credit abroad, as well as elective credit for your business
  major, general business credit, or non-business credit…so study abroad can work
  for everyone! You just have to do a little planning.</li> <li>Come learn about all
  the programs on offer even if you are in your first year or not yet in the Foster
  School. Attend an <a><span>info session</span></a> or come in for <a><span>advising</span></a>.</li>
  </ul> <h3>What happens if I click APPLY NOW on the application brochure page on
  the <a>UW Study Abroad Office website?</a> </h3> <ul> <li>An application is started
  which you can revisit at any time. You can save items if you want to return to work
  on things later.</li> <li>There are no application fees, except for CIEE. Start
  an application to see what is involved!</li> </ul> <h3>What are the components of
  an application?</h3> <ul> <li>Each program has a personal statement component. This
  is your essay of why you want to go on the program. We suggest touching on the personal,
  academic and/or career relevance of the program in your essay. Tell the story of
  why you think going abroad in important and what about the specific program you
  are applying for make it is a good fit.</li> <li>In addition to the personal statement,
  some programs require a transcript to be uploaded or a faculty/TA/adviser recommendation.
  The recommendation is a short online survey that takes only a few minutes to complete.</li>
  <li>Programs will also have a required in-person or Zoom interview which takes place
  after the online application deadline has passed.</li> </ul> <h3>How many programs
  can I apply to?</h3> <ul> <li>You can apply to up to 3 programs in the same time
  period/term, this is only going to be a constraint for Early Fall Start when there
  are 30+ programs happening at the same time. This means you can apply to 3 programs
  happening in Fall Quarter, and another 3 happening in Winter Quarter, etc.</li>
  <li>If you are interested in more than one program, definitely apply to multiple!
  Again, there is no application fee.</li> </ul> <h3>How does a semester match up
  with the UW quarter system on Foster Exchange &amp; Direct Enroll?</h3> <ul> <li>For
  Fall Semester, you earn 18-24 UW credits (roughly 4-6 courses), pay 1.5 your UW
  tuition rate and only miss Fall Quarter. (Paying more and earning more credit than
  if you were on campus)</li> <li>For Spring Semester, you earn 18-24 UW credits (roughly
  4-6 courses), pay 1.5 your UW tuition rate and miss Winter and Spring Quarters.
  (Paying less and earning less credit than if you were on campus)</li> <li>RSM is
  the one Foster Exchange partner on the quarter system. For RSM, students miss just
  one quarter and pay one quarter of tuition to UW to earn 12-17 UW credits.</li>
  <li>We have 2 Direct Enroll Programs (University College Dublin in Ireland and Universidad
  de San Andres in Argentina) where students pay a program fee for one semester, instead
  of their UW tuition. These two universities will be cheaper for out-of-state students
  than an Exchange. Students will still earn 18-24 UW credits for one semester.</li>
  </ul> <h3>How much does it cost to study abroad?</h3> <ul> <li>All programs, besides
  Exchanges, are based on a program fee model; so all students pay the same amount
  to participate on the program regardless of residency status. Some program fees
  include housing (Faculty-Led Programs, CIEE and IES Abroad) and some do not (ALBA
  Barcelona).</li> <li>Exchange Programs are 1.5 times a student’s quarterly tuition
  rate for one semester.</li> </ul> <h3>Can I use my financial aid?</h3> <ul> <li>Yes
  you can! A note for programs happening during Summer, summer aid is separate from
  the academic year so you have to apply specifically for summer aid in late Winter
  Quarter or early Spring Quarter.</li> <li>You must earn the minimum amount of UW
  credit required by Financial Aid for all quarters you are abroad. Meaning if you
  are missing both Winter and Spring Quarters on a Foster Exchange or Direct Enroll
  Program, you must earn at least 24 UW credits since you are enrolled abroad for
  2 UW quarters (12 credit minimum per quarter).</li> <li>To help you demystify financing
  your study abroad experience, check out the <a>UW Study Abroad Office resources</a>.</li>
  </ul> <h3>Are there scholarships specifically for study abroad?</h3> <ul> <li>Yes!
  If you are a Foster School student or you are applying to Foster, apply for the
  <a>Global Business Center Study Abroad Scholarships</a>.</li> <li>Also check the
  scholarship box when you apply to a program via the UW Study Abroad Office program
  application. That considers you for the UW Study Abroad Office Scholarships.</li>
  <li>Beyond that, there are many more scholarships out there. Learn more on the <a>UW
  Study Abroad Office resources</a> webpage.</li> </ul> <h3>How competitive are the
  Foster Study Abroad Programs?</h3> <ul> <li>This is very hard to say, it really
  depends on the year. Just apply!</li> </ul> <h3>Can I have someone look over my
  personal statement?</h3> <ul> <li>The Global Business Center cannot review any personal
  statements.</li> <li><span>Go to the <a><span>Odegaard Writing and Research Center
  (OWRC)</span></a> or the <a><span>CLUE Writing Center</span></a>.</span></li> </ul>
