Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the online workshop "Financial Capabilities: Where Does Your Money Go?" on Dec. 9, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. This interactive session invites participants to explore their spending habits and learn how to create a monthly budget. The workshop is part of a six-week series designed to empower attendees to make informed financial decisions. Each unit can be taken independently or combined for a more comprehensive experience. Registration is required, and participants will receive a Zoom link via email prior to the event. For more information or accommodation requests, contact Kim Newkirk at 360-499-0004 or via email. Don't miss this opportunity to take control of your finances!
