Celebrate New Year's Eve with free transit rides across King County and surrounding areas. On Dec. 31, 2025, enjoy complimentary rides on King County Metro, Sound Transit, Community Transit, Everett Transit, Pierce Transit, and the Seattle Streetcar.

This initiative allows you to explore the festivities without worrying about transportation costs. Whether you're headed to a party, a local event, or simply want to enjoy the night out, take advantage of this opportunity to travel safely and conveniently.

Rides are available throughout the evening, making it easy to join in the celebrations and return home afterward. Don’t miss out on this chance to ring in the New Year with ease and excitement while supporting local transit services.
