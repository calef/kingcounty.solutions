Join Sherry for "Fitness For Fun" on Friday, December 19, 2025, from 9 to 10 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This engaging class is designed to activate both your body and brain through upbeat music and easy-to-follow steps. Each session offers a unique experience to enhance flexibility and enjoyment, making fitness a fun part of your routine. Don't miss this opportunity to move, groove, and connect with others in a lively atmosphere.
