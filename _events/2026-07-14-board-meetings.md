The King County Flood Control District will hold its regular Board Meeting on July 14, 2026, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. The meeting will take place in the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. This monthly meeting occurs after the King County Council meeting and is open to the public. Attendees can also watch the live stream on the King County Council’s website or tune in on King County TV via Xfinity channels 22 and 322 (HD) and Astound Broadband channels 22 and 711 (HD). Community members, stakeholders, and anyone interested in local flood control issues are encouraged to attend.
