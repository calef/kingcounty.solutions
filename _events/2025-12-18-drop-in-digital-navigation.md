Join the Drop-in Digital Navigation event on December 18, 2025, from 11:30 a.m. to 3 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. This event offers a chance for attendees to receive assistance with their cell phones, tablets, or personal computers in a supportive environment. Participants can take advantage of a 25-minute session in the computer lab, where they can ask questions and get hands-on help. No appointment is necessary, and a suggested donation of $5 is appreciated. To confirm the computer lab schedule for the day, please call 206-932-4044, ext. 114, before your visit.
