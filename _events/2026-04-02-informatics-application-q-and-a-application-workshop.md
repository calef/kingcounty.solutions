Join the Informatics Application Q&A and Workshop on April 2, 2026, at 3 p.m. via Zoom. This event is designed for prospective students interested in the Informatics program at the University of Washington. Attendees will have the opportunity to ask questions about the application process, learn about the program's commitment to diversity, and understand the various admission paths available, including options for current UW students, transfer students, and incoming first-year students. Engage with faculty and staff to gain insights into the application requirements and how to strengthen your application. This interactive session aims to foster an inclusive environment where all participants can feel valued and supported. Don’t miss this chance to get your questions answered and prepare for your future in Informatics.
