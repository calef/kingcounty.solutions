Join the Free Rave Winter Soccer Clinics for children ages 5 to 11 on Dec. 31 from 9:30 to 11 a.m. at Foster High School, located at 4242 S. 144th St. in Tukwila. Participants will enjoy an introduction to soccer, focusing on essential skills such as ball handling, passing, and teamwork through various drills and games. The event will culminate in a fun scrimmage. Thanks to the RAVE Soccer Foundation, each participant will receive a soccer ball and shin guards to take home. This engaging clinic is a great opportunity for young athletes to enhance their skills while having fun.
