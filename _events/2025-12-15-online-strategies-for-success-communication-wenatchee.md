Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Online: Strategies for Success: Communication" from December 15 to December 18, 2025, starting at 9 a.m. each day. This four-day online workshop focuses on enhancing communication skills essential for job seekers. Participants will learn to improve interpersonal and business communication, develop effective listening and writing abilities, and master presentation skills. The workshop will also cover verbal and non-verbal cues, conflict resolution techniques, and tips for managing emotional responses. Attendees will gain insights into presenting themselves professionally across various platforms, including phone, email, and social media. Registration is required, and the Zoom link will be provided via email prior to the event. For more information, contact Denise Marion at 509-665-3728 or Denise.Marion@esd.wa.gov.
