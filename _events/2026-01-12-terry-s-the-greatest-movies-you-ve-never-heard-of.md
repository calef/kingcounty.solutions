Terry’s The Greatest Movies You’ve Never Heard Of returns to The Center for Active Living in Seattle on Jan. 12, 2026, at 12:30 p.m. Join fellow film enthusiasts for an engaging afternoon featuring newsreels, popcorn, previews, and classic cartoons.

This week’s screening will showcase "Thelma," a 2024 film starring June Squibb and Richard Roundtree. The series continues weekly, with upcoming films including "Indiana Jones and the Kingdom of the Crystal Skull" on Jan. 26 and "Carousel" on Feb. 2.

Don’t miss this opportunity to discover hidden cinematic gems every Monday in the Nucor Room. The Center is located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA 98116.
