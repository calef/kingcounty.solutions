Join us for "Tech Made Easy" on Monday, Jan. 12, 2026, from 3 p.m. to 4 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This event is designed to help participants navigate their digital devices with ease. Attendees will learn how to make technology work for them, enhancing their experience while ensuring safety and satisfaction. The group meets weekly on Mondays for discussions and information sharing, with additional practice sessions on Wednesdays for hands-on experience with personal devices. Don't miss this opportunity to turn tech frustration into tech benefits.
