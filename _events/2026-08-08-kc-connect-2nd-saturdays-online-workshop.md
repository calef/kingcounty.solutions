Join the KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop on August 8, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. (PDT). This free, youth-centered workshop is open to young people and their families across King County and will take place virtually. Each month, participants explore various real-life topics related to conflict, communication, and justice in a welcoming environment. The August session, titled "Taking Back Power," is part of a series designed to enhance conflict resolution skills and promote critical awareness. No preparation is required—just bring your voice and an open mind. To RSVP, visit the event's Calendly page. Don't miss this opportunity to engage and learn in a supportive online community.
