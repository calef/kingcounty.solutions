Join us for a Listening Session for Multifamily Housing Providers on Dec. 18, 2025, at 1 p.m. This event will take place at the community center, where attendees will have the opportunity to share their insights and experiences regarding support for less experienced funding applicants.

Participants can expect an open dialogue aimed at identifying challenges and solutions in the multifamily housing sector. Your feedback is crucial in shaping future initiatives and resources to assist new funding applicants.

This session is designed to foster collaboration and strengthen the community of housing providers. Whether you are a seasoned professional or new to the field, your voice matters. Don’t miss this chance to contribute to meaningful change in our housing landscape.
