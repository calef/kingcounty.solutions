Join the Support Network Meeting on February 24, 2026, from 12 p.m. to 1 p.m. at The Sophia Way, located at 11061 NE 2nd Street, Suite 223, Bellevue, WA. This monthly gathering invites individuals from faith communities, civic groups, or anyone passionate about making a difference. Attendees will receive updates on The Sophia Way’s programs, learn about the needs of the women served, and exchange ideas and resources for support. This meeting is an excellent opportunity to connect with others committed to fostering positive change in the community. For more information, visit [The Sophia Way website](https://secure.qgiv.com/for/2026supportnetwork/event/february/) or email info@sophiaway.org.
