US 2 near Stevens Pass will reopen to daytime pilot car operations starting Dec. 29, 2025. This event marks the resumption of traffic flow following essential repairs on the highway.

Travelers can expect guided passage between Scenic (milepost 58) and Mill Creek Road (milepost 71), ensuring a safe and efficient journey through the area. The pilot car will lead vehicles through the work zone, allowing for controlled access while construction activities continue.

Motorists are encouraged to plan for potential delays and to follow the pilot car closely for a smooth travel experience. This is a crucial step in maintaining the safety and integrity of US 2, allowing the community to reconnect with this vital route.
