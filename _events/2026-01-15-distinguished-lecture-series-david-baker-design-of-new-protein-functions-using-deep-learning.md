Join the Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science & Engineering for the Distinguished Lecture Series featuring David Baker on Jan. 15, 2026, at 3:30 p.m. in the Gates Center (CSE2), G20 | Amazon Auditorium.

Baker, a Nobel laureate and director of the Institute for Protein Design at the University of Washington, will discuss his groundbreaking work on designing new protein functions using deep learning. Attendees can expect insights into how proteins can be engineered from scratch to address modern challenges in medicine, technology, and sustainability.

The lecture will also highlight recent advances in using machine learning for functional protein generation. Live captioning will be provided, and the event will be recorded and streamed on the Allen School's YouTube channel. For accessibility requests, contact the Disability Services Office at least ten days in advance.
