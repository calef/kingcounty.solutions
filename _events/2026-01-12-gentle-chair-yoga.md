Join certified yoga instructor Nadine Blinn for Gentle Chair Yoga on Monday, Jan. 12, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 11 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This class features a blend of chair and standing poses, designed to enhance breathing, flexibility, balance, and overall mood, all without the need for floor sitting. Whether you are a beginner or have limitations, poses will be adapted to suit your abilities. Experience a fun and relaxing environment while improving your well-being.
