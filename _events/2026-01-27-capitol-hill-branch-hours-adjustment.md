The Capitol Hill Branch of The Seattle Public Library will adjust its operating hours starting Jan. 27, 2026. On Tuesdays and Wednesdays, the branch will close two hours earlier, while on Thursdays, it will also reduce its hours by closing two hours earlier than usual.

This change allows for enhanced service during peak hours and ensures staff can better assist patrons. Visitors can still enjoy a wide range of resources, including books, community programs, and study spaces. Stop by to explore the library’s offerings and connect with your community in a welcoming environment.
