Join the Scrabble Club for a fun and engaging afternoon of wordplay on Friday, December 19, 2025, from 12:30 p.m. to 1:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. Gather with friends and fellow enthusiasts at Margie’s Café to challenge your vocabulary skills and enjoy the social atmosphere. Whether you're a seasoned player or new to the game, this event promises to be stimulating and good for your brain.
