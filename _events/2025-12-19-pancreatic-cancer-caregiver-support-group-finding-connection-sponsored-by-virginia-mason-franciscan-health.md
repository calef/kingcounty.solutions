Join the Pancreatic Cancer Caregiver Support Group, "Finding Connection," sponsored by Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, on December 19 from 2 to 3 p.m. PST. This online event offers caregivers a chance to connect with others facing similar challenges while providing educational presentations and a supportive environment. Facilitated by Kari Hilwig, LICSW, the group meets every other Friday, allowing participants to share experiences and gain valuable insights. To attend, please RSVP by emailing Kari or calling 206-356-8547. This supportive space aims to foster connections among caregivers navigating the complexities of pancreatic cancer.
