Join the UW Surplus Online Store event on Dec. 31, 2025, at the Plant Services Building (PSB). Attendees can browse and purchase a variety of sustainable items, including furniture and office essentials, that have been collected from various University of Washington departments. The online storefront features new items added every Wednesday at 10 a.m., making it easy to shop and pick up purchases within a week. This initiative promotes reusing and recycling to reduce the university's environmental impact. With fresh inventory daily, there’s always something new to discover. Don't miss this opportunity to shop sustainably while supporting the university's mission. For more information, contact surplus@uw.edu.
