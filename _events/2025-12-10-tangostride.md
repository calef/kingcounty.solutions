Join the TangoStride program on Dec. 10 from 2 to 3 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room for a unique dance-based walking experience. This engaging event is designed to enhance balance, posture, and strength for participants of all abilities, including those who can stand for just 5 seconds. Attendees can enjoy a lively atmosphere while using walkers, wheelchairs, or canes. The cost is $10, but participants can pay what they can. TangoStride meets every Wednesday, making it a great opportunity to incorporate fitness into your routine while having fun. Don’t miss out on this chance to dance and walk your way to better health!
