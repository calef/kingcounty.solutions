Veterans can access IRS Form 1095-B electronically starting January 31, 2026, through their VA.gov accounts. This form is essential for reporting health coverage to the IRS. Attendees will learn how to navigate their accounts to obtain the form easily and understand its significance for tax purposes. This event provides an opportunity for veterans to ensure they have the necessary documentation for their tax filings. Participants are encouraged to log in to their VA.gov accounts in advance to facilitate the process. Join us to simplify your tax preparation and ensure compliance with IRS requirements.
