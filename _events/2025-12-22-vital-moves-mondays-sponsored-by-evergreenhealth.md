Join EvergreenHealth for "Vital Moves Mondays," an online event series starting December 22 at 12 p.m. PST. Participants can learn gentle, upbeat movements designed to enhance energy, strength, clarity, and balance. This session is particularly beneficial for patients, survivors, and caregivers, especially those affected by lymphedema. Attendees can expect a supportive environment that promotes wellness through movement. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with others while improving your physical well-being from the comfort of your home. For registration assistance, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500, available Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
