Cities, counties, ports, public transit agencies, and regional planning organizations are invited to submit their pre-applications for the Regional Mobility Grant by March 3, 2026. This grant aims to enhance transportation systems and improve mobility options across the region. Participants can expect to learn about the application process and eligibility requirements, ensuring they are well-prepared to secure funding for their projects. This is an essential opportunity for local governments and agencies to advance their transportation initiatives and better serve their communities. Be sure to mark your calendars and prepare your submissions ahead of the deadline.
