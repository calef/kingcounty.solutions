Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for "Online Strategies for Success: Work Concepts II" from 9 a.m. to noon on Dec. 15, 2025. This virtual workshop is designed to help job seekers learn how to land and maintain their ideal jobs. Attendees can expect to explore essential skills such as multitasking, motivation, personal leadership, and organization. The program also covers how to manage unexpected challenges and understand cognitive biases that affect decision-making.

Registration is required, and participants will receive a Zoom link via email before the event. This workshop is part of a series of life-skills sessions aimed at providing flexible and customizable learning experiences. Don't miss the opportunity to develop a solid vision for your professional future.
