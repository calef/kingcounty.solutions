Join the Volunteer Repair Party at Bike Works Warehouse in Seattle on April 21, 2026, from 1 to 4 p.m. This weekly event invites adults aged 18 and up to assist with bike repairs, regardless of their mechanical skills. The Volunteer Repair Parties are an excellent opportunity for newcomers to get involved with Bike Works, learn valuable skills, and contribute to the community.
