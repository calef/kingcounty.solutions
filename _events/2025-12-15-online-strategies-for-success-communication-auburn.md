Join the ONLINE Strategies for Success: Communication workshop hosted by WorkSource Seattle-King County from December 15 to December 18, 2025. This four-day virtual class runs daily from 8:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. 

Attendees will enhance their communication skills, focusing on interpersonal and business communication. Participants will learn to develop better listening, writing, and presentation skills while exploring their own communication styles. The workshop covers verbal and non-verbal cues, conflict resolution techniques, and tips for emotional control. Additionally, attendees will receive guidance on how to effectively present themselves through phone calls, emails, and social media.

Registration is required, and participants will receive Zoom meeting links via email before the workshop begins. This event is open to everyone seeking to improve their communication abilities and increase their chances of securing employment.
