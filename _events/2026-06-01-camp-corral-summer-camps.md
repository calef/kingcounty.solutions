Camp Corral offers no-cost, weeklong summer camps for children ages 8 to 15 of disabled veterans or those who died in service. Starting June 1, 2026, and held at various locations across the United States, these camps empower youth through engaging activities designed to build confidence and foster camaraderie. Attendees can expect a supportive environment where they can connect with peers who share similar experiences. Each camp features outdoor adventures, team-building exercises, and creative workshops, all aimed at helping children develop new skills and lasting friendships. Camp Corral is dedicated to honoring the sacrifices of military families while providing a fun and enriching summer experience.
