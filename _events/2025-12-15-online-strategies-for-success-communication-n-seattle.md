Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the ONLINE Strategies for Success: Communication workshop, taking place from December 15 to December 18, 2025, from 8:30 a.m. to 11:30 a.m. each day. This four-day online class focuses on enhancing your communication skills, crucial for personal and professional growth. Participants will learn about interpersonal and business communication, develop better listening and writing skills, and discover their unique communication styles. The workshop also covers verbal and non-verbal cues, conflict resolution, and emotional control. Additionally, attendees will receive tips on presenting themselves effectively through various channels, including phone, email, and social media. Registration is required, and participants will receive a Zoom link via email after signing up. Don’t miss this opportunity to improve your communication skills and boost your job prospects.
