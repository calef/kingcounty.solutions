Entry-level firefighter registration opens Dec. 30 at 11 p.m. and runs through March 20, 2026. This virtual event allows aspiring firefighters to register with several Washington Fire Careers partner agencies, including Enumclaw Fire Department and King County Fire District 20. Participants can expect to learn about the recruitment process and prepare for upcoming academies.

To register, visit the National Testing Network, choose "Firefighter" from the job options, select "Washington," and pick the desired partner agencies. This streamlined process ensures applicants are signed up for multiple departments with a single test fee. WA Fire Careers is continuously recruiting to fill positions for the August 2026 academy, making this a crucial opportunity for those interested in a firefighting career.
