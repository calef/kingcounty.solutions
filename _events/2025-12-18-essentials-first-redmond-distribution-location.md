Join Essentials First on December 18 from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at the Redmond Distribution Location in Washington for a community event focused on providing essential hygiene access. Attendees can receive hygiene kits, snack packs, and ORCA cards on a walk-in basis, making it a vital resource for those in need, including low-income families, the unhoused, immigrants, and individuals experiencing hardship.

No specific eligibility requirements are needed, although attendees are encouraged to bring ID and complete a short intake form with staff assistance. Essentials First prioritizes confidentiality and security for all personal information. This event is open to everyone in the community, ensuring that essential resources are accessible to all who may need them.
