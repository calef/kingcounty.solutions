Digital Literacy Classes will be held from January 3 to January 31, 2026, offering essential skills for navigating the digital world. Classes are scheduled for Mondays and Wednesdays (Class A) and Tuesdays and Thursdays (Class B) from 9:00 AM to 11:00 AM at the local community center. Class B repeats the content of Class A, providing an additional opportunity for participants who miss a session or wish to reinforce their learning. This format allows attendees to join multiple times throughout January, ensuring comprehensive training. The classes are open to anyone interested in improving their digital skills, making it a valuable resource for students, professionals, and community members alike.
