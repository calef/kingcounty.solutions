Join us at Margie’s Café on Jan. 13, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. Attendees can enjoy a variety of freshly prepared offerings, including cookies, sandwiches, salads, and soups, all available à la carte. This community gathering is a perfect opportunity to savor delicious food while connecting with neighbors. Whether you're stopping by for coffee or a light lunch, Margie’s Café promises a welcoming atmosphere and a rotating weekly menu to delight your taste buds.
