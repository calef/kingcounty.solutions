Join us for a Donation Drop-off on Jan. 22, 2026, at the American Legion Hall, located at 3620 SW Alaska St. From 6 p.m. to 8 p.m., community members are invited to contribute essential kitchen supplies to support local families in need.

This is a great opportunity to help your neighbors by donating items such as pots, pans, utensils, and non-perishable food. Volunteers will be on hand to assist with collection and provide information about the impact of your donations. Together, we can make a meaningful difference in our community.

We encourage everyone to participate and show support for those who could benefit from a helping hand. Your contributions will help ensure that every household has the tools they need to prepare meals and thrive.
