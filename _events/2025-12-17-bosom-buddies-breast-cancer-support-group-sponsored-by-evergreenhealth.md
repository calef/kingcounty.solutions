Join the Bosom Buddies Breast Cancer Support Group, sponsored by EvergreenHealth, for a supportive online gathering on Dec. 17 from 9:30 to 11:30 a.m. PST. This monthly meeting offers educational presentations and a welcoming environment for women at all stages of their breast cancer journey, whether newly diagnosed, undergoing treatment, or thriving as survivors. Attendees can share experiences and connect with others facing similar challenges. To participate, registration is required. For assistance with registration, please call the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500, available Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
