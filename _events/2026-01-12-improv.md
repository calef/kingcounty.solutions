Join us for an engaging improv session on Jan. 12, 2026, from 1:30 to 2:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. Led by theatre professor Tammi Doyle, participants will explore the art of improvisation by creating characters and stories on the spot. This fun and interactive experience encourages creativity, focus, and collaboration, allowing attendees to express themselves while enjoying laughter and connection. Whether you're a seasoned performer or a curious beginner, come ready to say "yes" and dive into the world of improv.
