The Arbor Day Art Poster Contest, themed "Rooted Together," invites participants of all ages to create artwork that highlights the relationship between trees and people. The contest runs from Jan. 1, 2026, to April 15, 2026, and will take place at City Hall in Normandy Park. Artists can submit entries across various age categories, with prizes awarded to first-place winners. This event encourages community engagement and creativity, making it a perfect opportunity for families, schools, and local artists to participate and celebrate Arbor Day.
