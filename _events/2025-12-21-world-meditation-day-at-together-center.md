Join the community for World Meditation Day at the Together Conference Center in Redmond on December 21 from 3:30 p.m. to 5:00 p.m. This free event invites participants of all experience levels to engage in meditation aimed at promoting global peace. Attendees can expect guided sessions, a supportive atmosphere, and the opportunity to connect with others who share a passion for mindfulness. No prior meditation experience is necessary, making it accessible for everyone. Come and be part of this collective effort to foster tranquility and unity within the community.
