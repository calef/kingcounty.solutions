Westside Neighbors Shelter will host an event on Jan. 22, 2026, at 5 p.m. to announce an increase in its cot capacity to 45 people. This expansion aims to provide additional support during severe weather conditions. Attendees can learn about the shelter's enhanced services, including the addition of an overnight staff member to ensure safety and comfort for guests. The event will offer an opportunity for community members to engage with shelter staff, ask questions, and understand how they can assist those in need. Join us at Westside Neighbors Shelter to discover how we are working to support our community during challenging weather events.
