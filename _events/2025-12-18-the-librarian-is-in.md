Join us for "The Librarian Is In!" on December 18 from 10 a.m. to noon in Washington. This engaging event offers attendees the opportunity to ask questions, seek guidance, and explore resources available at the library. Whether you're looking for book recommendations, research assistance, or information on library programs, the librarian will be available to help. Don’t miss this chance to connect with your local library and enhance your knowledge and skills.
