The Russian-Speaking Senior Day Program will take place on March 31, 2026, from 9:30 a.m. to 2:00 p.m. at the North Bellevue Community Center, located at 4063 148th Ave NE, Bellevue, WA. This program is designed for older adults who speak Russian or Ukrainian, providing a welcoming environment with culturally relevant resources and activities that promote health, social connections, and overall well-being. The program operates on Tuesdays and Fridays, funded by the City of Bellevue and Amazon. Seniors are encouraged to attend for an engaging experience tailored to their needs.
