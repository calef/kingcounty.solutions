Join the Beginner Spanish Class on December 18, 2025, from 9 to 10 a.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St. in Seattle. This class is perfect for those with little or some experience in Spanish. Participants can expect to learn foundational vocabulary and grammar, setting the stage for a smooth transition into the Casual Spanish Conversation class. Whether you want to enhance your language skills for travel, work, or personal enrichment, this welcoming environment is ideal for starting your Spanish-speaking journey. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with fellow learners and immerse yourself in the language.
