Join the KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop on February 13, 2027, from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. PST. This free, youth-centered workshop is designed for young people and their families across King County, providing a welcoming virtual space to explore topics related to conflict, communication, and justice. Each month features a different theme, and participants are encouraged to bring their voices and an open mind—no preparation is needed. This workshop is part of a series that aims to build skills in conflict resolution and promote critical awareness. To RSVP, visit the event's website at https://calendly.com/kcdrc-youth-development/2nd-saturdays-with-kc-connect?month=2025-10. Don’t miss this opportunity to engage and learn!
