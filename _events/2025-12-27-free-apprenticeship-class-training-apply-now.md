Join the Free Apprenticeship Class Training on Dec. 27 from 8 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. online. This event, hosted by AJAC’s Manufacturing Academy, offers participants the chance to gain essential skills and industry-recognized certifications in aerospace and manufacturing sectors. Attendees will learn about the program's curriculum and how it connects them to career opportunities through AJAC's extensive network of local employers.

Classes begin in January at South Seattle College, and Washington State residents who meet eligibility requirements can enroll at no cost. Interested individuals are encouraged to apply now to secure their spot. For more information, call 206-764-7940 or email info@ajactraining.org.
