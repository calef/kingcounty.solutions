Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the ONLINE Strategies for Success: Communication workshop from December 15 to December 18, 2025, from 1 p.m. to 4 p.m. each day. This virtual event will focus on enhancing communication skills essential for job seekers, including interpersonal and business communication, listening, writing, and presentation skills. Participants will learn to recognize verbal and non-verbal cues, practice conflict resolution, and gain tips on emotional control. The workshop aims to improve attendees' chances of landing their next job by refining their communication style and effectiveness in various formats, including phone, email, and social media. Registration is required, and a Zoom link will be emailed prior to the event. For more information, contact Kim Newkirk at 360-499-0004 or via email.
