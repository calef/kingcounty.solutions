The Disaster Assistance Center will open at the Auburn Library on Jan. 2, 2026, at 10 a.m. This center is designed to support residents affected by recent floods. Attendees can access vital resources, including information on recovery programs, financial assistance, and community support services. Trained professionals will be available to answer questions and provide guidance tailored to individual needs. This event aims to help residents navigate the recovery process and connect them with local agencies and organizations dedicated to disaster relief. Whether you need immediate assistance or long-term recovery options, the Disaster Assistance Center is here to help.
