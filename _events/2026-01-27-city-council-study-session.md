The City Council of Normandy Park will hold a study session on Jan. 27, 2026, from 7 p.m. to 10 p.m. in the Council Chambers at 801 SW 174th Street. Attendees can expect discussions on city matters, providing an opportunity to engage with council members and learn about upcoming initiatives. The session will be held in person and also available remotely via Zoom. This is part of a series of study sessions that occur on the fourth Tuesday of each month. For more information, contact the City Clerk at 206-248-8248.
