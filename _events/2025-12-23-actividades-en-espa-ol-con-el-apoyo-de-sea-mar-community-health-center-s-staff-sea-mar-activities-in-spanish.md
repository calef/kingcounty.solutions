Join the Sea Mar Community Health Center staff for "Actividades En Español" on Tuesday, December 23, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This event welcomes all members of the Latinx community for a day filled with engaging activities, socialization, and a complimentary lunch. Attendees can also access valuable information, assistance, and resources in Spanish. It's a great opportunity to connect with others and enjoy a supportive environment. For more details, contact Yanin Diaz at 206-227-6025.
