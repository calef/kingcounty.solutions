Join the "Your Next Employment Opportunity - Job Club - Virtual" event on December 12, 2025, from 2 p.m. to 4 p.m. This online gathering, hosted by WorkSource Seattle-King County, offers job seekers a chance to network, share experiences, and discuss strategies for securing employment. Each session features a facilitated group discussion that focuses on topics essential for job hunting, including elements of the “Success Mindset,” which can provide a competitive edge in the job market. Participants are encouraged to register and create an account on WorkSourceWA.com before attending. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with others and enhance your job search skills.
