Join us for the Course Accessibility Lunch & Learn on Jan. 6, 2026, at noon via Zoom. This interactive session invites attendees to ask questions and learn about the accessibility resources available at the University of Washington Libraries. Whether you're an educator, student, or staff member, you'll gain valuable insights into how to improve course accessibility and support all learners. Registration is required to participate, so be sure to secure your spot for this informative event. Don't miss the opportunity to enhance your understanding of accessibility in education!
