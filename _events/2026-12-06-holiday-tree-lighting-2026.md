Join the City of Federal Way for the Holiday Tree Lighting on December 5, 2026, from 4:00 to 5:00 p.m. at Town Square Park, located at 31600 Pete von Reichbauer Way South, Federal Way, WA. This festive, free event invites families and community members to celebrate the holiday season together. Enjoy the enchanting atmosphere as the tree is lit, marking the beginning of the holiday festivities in the area. For more information, contact the City of Federal Way Parks and Recreation at 253-835-6900.
