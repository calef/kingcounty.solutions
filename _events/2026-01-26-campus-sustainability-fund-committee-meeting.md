Join the Campus Sustainability Fund (CSF) committee meeting on Jan. 26, 2026, from 6:30 to 8 p.m. at Condon Hall. This open meeting welcomes all members of the University of Washington Seattle community. Attendees can expect to participate in discussions about sustainability initiatives and can request a spot on the agenda in advance. While participation is encouraged, please remember to stay on topic and refrain from initiating off-agenda discussions. If you are not part of the UW community, contact the Committee Co-Chairs before attending. This hybrid meeting format allows for both in-person and virtual participation, making it accessible to everyone interested in promoting sustainability on campus.
