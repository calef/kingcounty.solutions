Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for an online workshop series, "Financial Capabilities: Earn Extra Money to Improve Your Life," starting December 30, 2025. This two-day event runs from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. on Tuesday and Wednesday, providing participants with valuable insights into managing debt and increasing monthly income.

This flexible, six-week series allows attendees to customize their learning experience, with each unit designed to stand alone or be combined with others. Registration is required and can be completed by creating an account at WorkSourceWA.com.

For questions or accommodation requests, contact Kim Newkirk at 360-499-0004 or via email. Don't miss this opportunity to empower yourself with smart financial strategies.
