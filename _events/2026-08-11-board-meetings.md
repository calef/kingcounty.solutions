The King County Flood Control District will hold its regular board meeting on August 11, 2026, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. This monthly meeting occurs on the second Tuesday of each month, following the King County Council meeting. Members of the public are encouraged to attend in person or watch the live stream available on the King County Council's website, as well as on King County TV through Xfinity and Astound Broadband channels. This event is an opportunity for community members to engage with local government and stay informed about flood control initiatives in the area.
