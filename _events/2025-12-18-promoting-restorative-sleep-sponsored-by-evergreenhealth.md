Join EvergreenHealth online on December 18 from 6:30 to 7:30 p.m. PST for "Promoting Restorative Sleep," a presentation led by Dr. Meghan Zech. This event will focus on enhancing deep sleep, particularly for individuals living with prostate cancer. Attendees can expect to learn practical strategies to improve their sleep quality and overall well-being. This informative session aims to empower participants with tools to achieve rejuvenating sleep, essential for physical and mental health. Don't miss this opportunity to gain valuable insights into restorative sleep practices.
