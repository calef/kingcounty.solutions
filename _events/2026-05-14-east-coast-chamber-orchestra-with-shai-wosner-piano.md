Experience an evening of exceptional music with the East Coast Chamber Orchestra (ECCO) featuring pianist Shai Wosner on May 14, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. in Meany Hall. This dynamic collective, known for blending the power of orchestral music with the intimacy of chamber performances, will present a captivating program that includes Edward Elgar's "Serenade for Strings," David Ludwig's "Virtuosity: Five Micro-Concertos for String Orchestra," and Vijay Iyer's "Handmade Universe," a new piano concerto specifically composed for Wosner and ECCO. This concert promises to showcase the talent of some of today's leading young musicians, making it a must-see event for classical music enthusiasts. Don't miss this opportunity to witness the future of classical music unfold.
