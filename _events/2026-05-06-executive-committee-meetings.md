The Executive Committee Meetings will take place on May 6, 2026, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. These regular meetings occur on the first Wednesday of each month and are open to the public. Attendees can participate in person or watch the live stream on the King County Council’s website, as well as on King County TV, available on Xfinity channels 22 and 322 (HD) and Astound Broadband channels 22 and 711 (HD). This event is ideal for community members, local officials, and stakeholders interested in regional governance and flood control issues.
