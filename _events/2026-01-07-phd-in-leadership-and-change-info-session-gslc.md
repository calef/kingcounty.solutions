Join Antioch University for a virtual information session about the PhD in Leadership and Change on Jan. 7, 2026, from 7 to 8 p.m. EST. This event is designed for those considering a doctoral program and will provide an overview of the curriculum, as well as an opportunity to meet faculty and current students. Attendees can expect to explore how this program engages working professionals in the study and practice of leading positive change across various sectors, including workplaces, schools, and communities. Don't miss this chance to learn more about advancing your career through this interdisciplinary program.
