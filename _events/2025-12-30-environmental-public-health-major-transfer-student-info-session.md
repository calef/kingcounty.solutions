Join the University of Washington for an Environmental Public Health Major Transfer Student Info Session on Dec. 30, 2025, at 4 p.m. This event is designed for prospective transfer students interested in learning more about the Environmental Public Health program. Attendees will have the opportunity to meet faculty, ask questions about the curriculum, and explore the benefits of studying public health. This informative session will help students understand the transfer process and the resources available to support their academic journey. Don't miss this chance to discover how you can make an impact in the field of public health.
