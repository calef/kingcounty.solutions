  </h1> <article> <div> <div> <p>The BS in Environmental Public Health is a "minimum
  requirements" major, which means that all students who meet the minimum requirements
  listed below, and who can complete the degree within the <a>UW''s maximum time frame</a>, are
  admitted. Students can request to declare the major at any time by submitting an
  online declaration form. </p> <div> <h4>On-demand Information Session</h4> </div>
  <h3>Application Dates</h3> <p>We review applications as they are submitted, and
  students should hear back from us within 10 business days. The application deadline
  for entry to the following quarter is the Friday of finals week. The upcoming application
  windows for entry to the following quarters are: </p> <ul> <li>Autumn 2025: CLOSED</li>
  <li>Winter 2026: <strong>Aug 25 - Dec 12 OPEN</strong> </li> <li>Spring 2026: Dec
  13 - Mar 20</li> <li>Summer 2026: Mar 21 - Jun 12</li> </ul> <h3>Minimum Admission
  Requirements</h3> <p><span>Students with 30-90 credits, and/or fewer than 6 quarters
  completed at the time of application will apply under early admission. Students
  with more than 90 credits and/or 6 quarters or more completed will apply under upper
  division requirements. Applicants must have a 2.0 in each of the prerequisites courses.
  Early admission applicants must have a cumulative GPA of 3.0. Upper division applicants
  must have a cumulative GPA of 2.5.</span></p> <p><strong>Math Preparation: </strong>Students
  do not have to complete the <a>math graduation requirement</a> before applying to
  the major. However, we recommend that students complete a math course before they
  register for the other supporting science courses. The <a>recommended preparation
  for PHYS 114</a> is a previous algebra course covering concepts such as trigonometry
  and exponential functions. The <a>recommended preparation for CHEM 142</a> is a
  good working knowledge of algebra.</p> <p><strong>Students may have prerequisites
  courses in progress at the time of application.</strong><span> Students who have
  questions about the major or the admission process are encouraged to </span><a>meet
  with an adviser</a>.</p> <h4>Early Admission Prerequisites</h4> <p>30-90 credits
  / fewer than 6 quarters completed at time of application</p> <p><strong>CHEM 142,
  152</strong> Chemistry series<strong>ENGL 131 or </strong><a><strong>equivalent</strong></a>
  English compositionCumulative GPA of 3.0</p> <h4>Upper Division Admission Prerequisites</h4>
  <p>90 or more credits / 6 or more quarters completed at time of application</p>
  <p>complete all early admission prerequisites above plus:</p> <p><strong>BIOL 180,
  200, 220 (15)</strong> Introductory Biology with labs(choose <strong>one</strong>
  chemistry sequence) <strong>CHEM 142, 152, CHEM 220 </strong><span><strong>OR</strong></span> <strong>CHEM
  142, 152, CHEM 223, 224, 241 </strong><span><strong>OR</strong></span><strong>CHEM
  142, 152, 162, CHEM 237, 238, 241 </strong>General and Organic ChemistryCumulative
  GPA of 2.5</p> <h3>Transfer Student Information</h3> <p>As a transfer student, you
  can declare the Bachelors of Science in Environmental Public Health major after
  you are admitted to the UW. Please consult the <a>Environmental Public Health Transfer
  page</a> for more information. </p> <h3>How to Declare the Major</h3> <p>The application
  consists of three components which you should prepare before you access the <a><strong>online
  application form</strong></a></p> <p><strong>1. Personal statement: </strong> Your
  statement should be approximately 500 words, and should address the following questions:</p>
  <ul> <li>Why do you want to be an Environmental Public Health major?</li> <li>How
  will completing this degree help you to achieve your professional and academic goals?</li>
  </ul> <p><strong>2. Unofficial UW transcript: </strong>Current students may access
  unofficial transcripts through the Grades and Transcripts section found on the Academics
  block on <a>MyUW</a>. Select the <em>Academics block</em> at the bottom of the page.
  Next, select <em>Unofficial Transcript</em> under the <em>Grades and Transcripts
  </em>section. Select Printer-Friendly Version on the Unofficial Transcript page.
  It will create a PDF file of your unofficial transcript, which you can save and
  then upload into the application form.</p> <p><strong>3. Prerequisites and satisfactory
  progress: </strong>In the application you will be asked whether you are applying
  for early or upper division admission, and you will need to list out the prerequisites
  that you''ve already completed. You will also need to indicate how many quarters
  and credits you have completed.  If you have any questions about early versus upper
  division admission, or prerequisites, please <a>contact an advisor</a>. </p> <p><a>online
  application form</a></p> <p><strong>Notification:</strong> Applications are reviewed
  as they are submitted. Applicants who meet the minimum prerequisites and can complete
  the degree within the <a>UW''s maximum time frame</a> will be notified within 10
  business days and asked to come in for an advising appointment to formally declare
  the major. </p> <p> </p> </div> </div> </article> <section> <div> <div> <section>

Join the University of Washington for an information session about the Bachelor of Science in Environmental Public Health program on Jan. 8, 2026, at 1 p.m. This event offers prospective students the opportunity to learn about the curriculum, career paths, and the impact of environmental health on communities. Attendees will hear from faculty and current students, gaining insights into the program's hands-on experiences and research opportunities. The session will be held at the University of Washington, providing a chance to explore the campus and ask any questions about the application process. Don't miss this opportunity to discover how you can contribute to public health through environmental science.
