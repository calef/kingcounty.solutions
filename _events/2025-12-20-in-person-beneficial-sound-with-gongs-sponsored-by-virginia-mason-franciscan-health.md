Join us for "Beneficial Sound with Gongs," a restorative sound bath meditation sponsored by Virginia Mason Franciscan Health, on Dec. 20 from 1 to 2:30 p.m. at Cancer Lifeline, located at 6522 Fremont Ave N, Seattle.

Attendees can expect a soothing experience as calming tones and vibrations help quiet the mind and relax the body. This immersive practice aims to release stress, ease tension, and promote a deep sense of peace. Many participants report feeling balanced and renewed, with enhanced physical relaxation and emotional clarity.

While the event is in person, registration is facilitated through Zoom. For assistance with registration, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500, available Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m.
