Join the online workshop "Financial Capabilities: Where Does Your Money Go?" on December 10 from 2:30 to 4:30 p.m. This event, hosted by WorkSource Seattle-King County, is designed to help attendees investigate their spending habits and construct a monthly budget. This is the second day of a two-part workshop series aimed at empowering participants to make informed financial decisions. The workshop is customizable, allowing units to be taken in any order, and is suitable for individuals looking to enhance their financial literacy. Registration is required, and participants are encouraged to create an account at WorkSourceWA.com before attending. For assistance or accommodations, contact Karla Hood at karla.hood@esd.wa.gov.
