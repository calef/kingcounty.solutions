Join us for a workshop on Supporting Family Caregivers on March 25, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 10:30 a.m. PDT, held online via Zoom. This interactive session will equip participants with essential tools to assess and care for family caregivers. Attendees will learn to identify signs of caregiver burnout and develop practical skills for both caregivers and clinicians.

Engage with experts in the field and connect with others who share similar experiences. This workshop aims to empower caregivers, enhancing their well-being and providing strategies to manage the challenges they face. Whether you are a caregiver or a health professional, this event offers valuable insights to support those who dedicate themselves to caring for loved ones. Don't miss this opportunity to enhance your knowledge and skills in caregiver support.
