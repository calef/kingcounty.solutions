Join the I'm Hooked Family Fishing event on May 2, 2026, from 7 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Steel Lake Park in Federal Way. This free event, hosted in partnership with I'm Hooked Inc. and the Washington Department of Fish and Wildlife, offers a day of outdoor fun for families. Attendees can enjoy angler education, shoreline fishing, and health and wellness activities, all guided by community mentors who aim to instill a lifelong passion for fishing and nature.

Participants will have access to fishing gear, including rods, reels, bait, and life jackets. Engage in hands-on learning at various stations, including fish cleaning and aquatic ecology. Don't miss this opportunity to bond with your family while exploring the joys of fishing.
