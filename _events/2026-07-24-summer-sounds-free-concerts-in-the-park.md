Join the Federal Way Arts Commission for the Summer Sounds: FREE Concerts in the Park series at Steel Lake Park on July 23, 2026, from 6:30 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. This family-friendly event features a diverse lineup of live music, celebrating various genres suitable for all ages.
