Join us for the grand opening of the second Bridge Pointe home on Jan. 1, 2026, at its new location in your community. This facility is dedicated to supporting young adults as they transition from inpatient behavioral health treatment to independent living.

Attendees will have the opportunity to tour the home, meet the staff, and learn about the programs designed to empower residents. Enjoy refreshments and hear inspiring stories from those who have benefited from Bridge Pointe's services.

This event highlights the importance of community support in fostering mental health and well-being. Come celebrate this significant milestone and discover how Bridge Pointe is making a difference in the lives of young adults. Your presence can help raise awareness and support for this vital initiative.
