Join Sound Generations at the Together Center on December 18 from 10 a.m. to noon for a free event focused on connecting older adults and individuals with disabilities to essential community resources. Attendees can expect to learn about services such as food security, transportation options, health and wellness programs, and caregiver support. This event is part of a monthly series held every third Thursday, providing vital information and assistance to enhance the quality of life for aging adults and their families. The Together Center is located at 16305 NE 87th S Suite 111 in Redmond, Washington. For more information, contact Together Center at 425-869-6699.
