Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the "Meet the Recruiter" event at the Advanced Manufacturing Skills Center (AMSC) on Dec. 15, 2025, from 1 to 3 p.m. at 728 134th St. SW, Building A, Suite 102, Room 104, Everett, WA. Attendees will have the opportunity to learn about building a career in robotics manufacturing through the Robotics in Manufacturing program at Edmonds College. This program offers training in automation, collaborative robots, and mechatronics principles, preparing students for high-demand jobs. The session will provide insights into the 10-12 week hybrid program starting on Jan. 16, with no required placement tests and a program cost of $3,200. Doors open at 12:45 p.m. for early arrivals. For more information, contact Petru Mihaluta at business.engagement@workforcesnohomish.org.
