Join the Exploring Art Class on December 23, 2025, from 10:30 a.m. to 12 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. This engaging class invites participants to experiment with watercolor, acrylics, and drawing techniques, including classic, impressionist, and abstract styles. Attendees can use provided supplies or bring their own materials and projects. This is an excellent opportunity to discover your artistic style while connecting with fellow artists. Whether you are a beginner or have some experience, come explore your creativity in a supportive environment.
