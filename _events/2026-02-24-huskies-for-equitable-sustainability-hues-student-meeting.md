  <section> <h1>Huskies for Equitable Sustainability (HuES)</h1> </section> <section>
  <div> <div> <div> <section> <article> <div><div><div> <p><em>Growing together through
  projects, skills, and community - <strong>You are welcome here!</strong></em></p>
  <p>HuES is open to all UW students who care about sustainability, equity, and building
  community. You don't need prior experience - just curiosity and a willingness to
  learn and collaborate.</p> <p><a>See upcoming meetings</a></p> <h2>What is HuES?</h2>
  <p>Huskies for Equitable Sustainability (HuES) is an opportunity for students to
  actively engage in the UW's sustainability efforts, organized by UW Sustainability.
  HuES will connect students with projects that impact sustainability on our campus
  and in our community. You'll get the chance to work together on tangible efforts
  - whether that’s contributing to UW’s Sustainability Action Plan, volunteering at
  existing sustainability events or projects, or helping develop your own student-led
  initiatives. Whether you volunteer at an event or jump in to a project, you'll leave
  not just with ideas but with something you've helped bring to life.</p> <p>In addition,
  HuES will host workshops to grow your sustainability knowledge and skills. You'll
  gain experiences that translate into leadership opportunities on campus - and beyond.</p>
  <h2>Who can participate?</h2> <p><strong>All students are welcome and encouraged</strong>
  to join HuES. You can drop in to any meeting to learn more and discover how you
  can get involved.</p> <p>HuES is intended to be more than just a club; it’s a community.
  We’re carving out space not only to work and learn, but also to connect with each
  other as peers and friends. HuES will be led by students, allowing for deeper peer-to-peer
  connections, while UW Sustainability staff will be on hand to provide insight on
  how best to move projects forward. Expect time for informal conversations, social
  events, and shared meals. Because building a sustainable future starts with building
  relationships.</p> <h2>What projects are we working on?</h2> <p>Getting involved
  with sustainability projects on campus is one of the most impactful ways for students
  to turn ideas into action. These projects help students build skills, collaborate
  across disciplines, and leave a lasting mark on the UW community. By working together
  on campus initiatives, students not only gain experience in leadership and problem-solving
  but also help shape a more equitable and sustainable future right where they live,
  study, and grow.</p> <p>Students can choose to help with one of several ongoing
  projects, or work with UW Sustainability and other HuES students to identify or
  create other projects. See below for some of the currently ongoing projects.</p>
  <ul> <li> <strong>Waste reduction &amp; reuse</strong> <ul> <li>Reusable cup ambassadors: Be
  part of the Alaska Arena sustainability crew by staffing waste sorting stations
  and helping fans properly sort and learn about their reusable cup.</li> <li>Reusable
  cup LCA analysis: Conduct a Life Cycle Analysis comparing reusable and compostable
  cups used on campus. Your findings will help guide future purchasing decisions.</li>
  <li>Swag @ UW policy: Help develop a campus-wide policy for sustainable event giveaways.</li>
  <li>Zero waste thrift pop-up: Work with UW Recycling to plan and host another (possibly
  reoccurring) thrift pop-up market to encourage reuse.</li> </ul> </li> <li> <strong>Campus
  sustainability &amp; policy</strong> <ul> <li>Salmon Safe certification: Support
  UW’s journey toward certification by identifying and addressing areas on campus
  that affect watershed and habitat health.</li> <li>University District Farmers Market
  working group: Collaborate to expand the market’s presence, strengthen engagement,
  and address food insecurity by improving access and outreach.</li> </ul> </li> <li>
  <strong>Food systems &amp; equity</strong> <ul> <li>Food Recovery: Collaborate with
  partners through the FreeFoodAlert platform to build a reliable, equitable food
  recovery system at UW.</li> </ul> </li> <li> <strong>Community &amp; events</strong>
  <ul> <li>Earth Week Committee: Collaborate with other students, RSOs, and campus
  partners to plan events and programs for Earth Week 2026.</li> <li>Climate Counseling
  Chats: Co-host quarterly group discussions on climate anxiety with counselors and
  peers in a supportive, open setting.</li> </ul> </li> </ul> <p><span>How to be a
  part of HuES</span></p> <p>HuES is open to all UW students who care about sustainability,
  equity, and building community. You don’t need prior experience - just curiosity
  and a willingness to learn and collaborate. </p> <p>We know there are lots of students
  who are interested in sustainability and want to make a difference, but don't know
  where to start or how to move their ideas forward. HuES helps connect you with existing
  volunteer opportunities, RSOs and projects - or lets you pitch your ideas to students
  who want to help. </p> <h3>Easy ways to get involved:</h3> <ul> <li> <strong>Sign
  up</strong>: <a>Fill out our interest form</a> so we can keep you updated on meetings,
  events, and projects.</li> <li> <strong>Come to a meeting or event</strong>: See
  above for the meeting schedule. You’re welcome to drop in anytime, whether you want
  to check it out once or become a regular.</li> <li>HuES members can choose to get
  engaged in a variety of ways , such as event planning, campus campaigns, volunteering,
  and hands-on projects throughout the year. All of these opportunities are a great
  way to grow your knowledge, build skills, meet people, and make a tangible impact.</li>
  </ul> <h3>What You’ll Get Out of HuES:</h3> <ul> <li>Community of students who care
  about change</li> <li>Workshops and trainings to grow your skills</li> <li>Leadership
  opportunities to run events and projects</li> <li>A chance to help shape sustainability
  efforts at UW</li> </ul> <p><a>Follow UW Sustainability on Instagram</a> to learn
  about events, meetings and other HuES opportunities.</p> <p>If you have any questions,
  please reach out to Emily Adams at <a>emiadams@uw.edu</a>.</p> <h2>More about HuES</h2>
  </div></div></div> <div> <div> <fieldset> <legend> <a><span>The history of HuES</span></a>
  </legend> <div> <div> <p>Huskies for Equitable Sustainability (HuES) launched in
  Fall 2024 as a space for students to learn more about sustainability, equity, and
  the ways they intersect on campus and beyond. We started with a model that paired
  short lectures with group activities - a chance to hear from peers and guest speakers,
  and then try something hands-on.</p> <p>It was a great starting point. We learned
  together, built connections, and started conversations that mattered. But as we
  wrapped up the year, one theme kept coming up in your feedback: you wanted more.
  More opportunities to put ideas into action. More ways to develop concrete skills.
  And more time to just be in community with one another.</p> <p>So we listened, and
  in Fall 2025 HuES evolved to have a more collaborative, project-based focus.</p>
  </div> </div> <div> <fieldset> <legend> <a><span>Videos</span></a> </legend> <div>
