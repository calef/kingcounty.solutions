The deadline for the affordable housing community to provide feedback on the draft Notice of Funding Availability (NOFA) is February 4, 2026, at 5 p.m. PST. Your input will help shape funding priorities and ensure that community needs are addressed effectively.

Engaging in this process not only supports the development of affordable housing but also fosters collaboration among community members. Don’t miss the chance to make your voice heard and contribute to the future of housing in our community.
