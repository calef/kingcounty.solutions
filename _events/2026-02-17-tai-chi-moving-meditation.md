Join us for "Tai Chi: Moving Meditation" on Feb. 17, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 10 a.m. at the Greenwood Senior Center, located at 525 N. 85th St., Seattle, WA. This engaging session, led by instructor Larry Spangler, invites participants of all ability levels to explore the ancient practices of Tai Chi and Qigong. Attendees can expect to enhance their strength, balance, and agility through slow, flowing movements and deep breathing techniques, all aimed at improving overall quality of life and longevity. Don't miss this opportunity to relax and rejuvenate in a supportive community setting. For more information or to register, contact the Greenwood Senior Center at 206-297-0875.
