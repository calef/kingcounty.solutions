Join the Foster School of Business for an informational session on the Foster Exploration Seminars on Jan. 26, 2026, at PACCAR Hall. Attendees will learn about unique study abroad opportunities that immerse students in hands-on experiences while earning academic credits before the Fall Quarter begins. The Exploration Seminars are short-term programs led by UW faculty, focusing on real-world learning through company visits and cultural engagement in various countries. With over 30 seminars available, students can explore diverse topics and locations without specific prerequisites. This session will provide insights into program details, costs, and application procedures. Don’t miss this chance to discover how these seminars can enhance your academic journey.
