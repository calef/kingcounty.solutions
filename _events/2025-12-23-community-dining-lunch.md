Join us for the Community Dining Lunch on Dec. 23 from noon to 12:45 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room. This event offers a warm, welcoming atmosphere where attendees can enjoy a delicious, made-from-scratch meal prepared by Chef Corday Hatfield and Sous Chef Linda Harris.

The suggested donation is $5 for those aged 60 and older and $10 for all others. Caregivers under 60 are treated as patrons aged 60+. Punch cards are available for regular diners. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with others in the community while enjoying a hearty lunch.
