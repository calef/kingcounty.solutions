Join us at the Burke Dykes Computer Lab on Jan. 14, 2026, from 8:30 a.m. to 4:20 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. Attendees can use Windows computers to check email, browse the internet, and access popular software like Microsoft Office. A printer and scanner are available for use. If you need assistance while navigating the technology, feel free to drop by during our Digital Navigation program hours for support. Whether you’re looking to improve your digital skills or simply need a place to work, the Burke Dykes Computer Lab offers a welcoming environment for all.
