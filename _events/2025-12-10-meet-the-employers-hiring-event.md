Join WorkSource Seattle-King County for the "Meet the Employers: Hiring Event" on Dec. 10, 2025, from 1 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. at 130 S. Arthur St., Spokane, WA. This in-person event allows job seekers to connect directly with employers about current job openings. Attendees will gain insights into what employers are looking for, including effective résumé and cover letter formats that showcase skills and attract attention. Participants can expect to hear overviews from various employers, ask questions, and engage in meaningful conversations with company representatives. Early arrival is recommended for check-in at the WorkSource front desk. Don’t miss this opportunity to enhance your job search and network with potential employers.
