Join us for a series of engaging craft events leading up to New Year’s celebrations on Dec. 31, 2025. Located at the community center, attendees will have the opportunity to create unique decorations, handmade cards, and festive gifts to ring in the new year.

Participants of all ages are welcome, and materials will be provided. Whether you’re an experienced crafter or just looking to try something new, this is a perfect chance to unleash your creativity and connect with others in the community.

Don't miss out on this fun-filled day of crafting that will help you celebrate the arrival of 2026 in style!
