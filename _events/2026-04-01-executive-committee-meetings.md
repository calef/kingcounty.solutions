The Executive Committee Meetings will take place on April 1, 2026, from 1:30 p.m. to 3:00 p.m. at the King County Council Chambers, located on the 10th floor of the King County Courthouse in Seattle, WA. These regular monthly meetings are open to the public and are an opportunity for community members to engage with the committee's discussions and decisions. Those interested in local governance, community issues, or the operations of the King County Flood Control District are encouraged to attend. For those unable to attend in person, the meeting will be streamed live on the King County Council’s website and on King County TV, accessible via Xfinity and Astound Broadband channels.
