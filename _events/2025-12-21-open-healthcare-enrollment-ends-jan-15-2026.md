Open Healthcare Enrollment begins on Dec. 21, 2025, offering individuals the opportunity to explore and select health and dental plans for 2026. This virtual event allows attendees to connect with WA Health Plan Finder representatives, who can assist in finding plans that fit their needs. Services are available in over 200 languages, ensuring accessibility for everyone. Those who qualify may also explore free or low-cost options through Apple Health (Medicaid). Open enrollment runs until Jan. 15, 2026, so don’t miss the chance to secure your healthcare coverage. Join online and take the first step toward a healthier future.
