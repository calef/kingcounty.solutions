Join us at Margie’s Café on Friday, December 19, 2025, from 9 a.m. to 1:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle. Enjoy a cozy atmosphere while savoring a rotating menu of freshly prepared items, including cookies, sandwiches, salads, and soups. Whether you're looking for a morning coffee or a light lunch, Margie’s Café offers delicious options at à la carte prices. It's a great opportunity to connect with the community and enjoy good food in a welcoming environment.
