Join the KC CONNECT: 2nd Saturdays Online Workshop on May 9 from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. This free, youth-centered workshop is designed for young people and their families across King County. Each month, participants will engage in discussions around real-life topics related to conflict, communication, and justice in a welcoming virtual environment.

The May session will focus on "Digital Conflict & Social Media Drama." Attendees can expect to share their thoughts, learn new skills, and participate in meaningful interactions. No preparation is required—just bring your voice and an open mind. The workshop is part of a series that occurs on the second Saturday of each month, promoting youth development and community engagement.
