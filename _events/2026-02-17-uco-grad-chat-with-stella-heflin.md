  </strong></p> <ul> <li> <span>Instagram </span><a><span>@uwpccuco</span></a> </li>
  <li><span>Email <a>uwpccuco@uw.edu</a></span></li> <li><span>Climate Undergraduate
  <a>List-Serve</a></span></li> </ul> <h3><strong>About</strong></h3> <p>The Program
  on Climate Change <strong>U</strong>ndergraduate <strong>Co</strong>hort (UCo) is
  an undergraduate community and Registered Student Organization (RSO) that serves
  the undergraduate population interested in advancing climate science. With mentorship
  from P-GraSC and the PCC community, members and student leaders mend UCo to fulfill
  the group’s passions, interests, and goals each academic year. By participating
  in UCo, undergraduates from across the UW are able to connect with professional
  development resources, experts in the field of climate science, and fellow undergrads
  interested in climate science, helping strengthen and embolden the next generation
  of climate scientists at the UW.</p> <h3><strong>Our 2025-2026 Mission</strong></h3>
  <p>This year we strive to further build an interpersonal community of students and
  direct our events around the interest of our members. We will continue to uphold
  our tradition of student networking and professional development, this year with
  events focused on internships and campus resources. We plan to host casual social
  events to engage with our student community and branch out by partnering with other
  environmental RSOs. We strive to work alongside and serve our greater Seattle community
  by working on environmental service projects. Finally, we want to pursue peer education
  events including paper and current event discussions.</p> <h3><strong>Background</strong></h3>
  <p>As youth engagement in the movement against climate change has grown, it has
  become increasingly important to connect and engage the multitudes of undergraduates
  at the UW that are invested in climate science. <span>The PCC </span><b>U</b><span>ndergraduate
  </span><b>Co</b><span>hort (UCo) was announced 3 years ago with the goal of providing
  climate-minded undergrads the opportunity to receive interdisciplinary mentorship,
  professional development, and connection, while increasing undergraduate engagement
  in the PCC community. Since then, through a partnership between undergrad and graduate
  student leaders, the UCo has been quite successful in that goal, providing hundreds
  of undergrads the chance to engage with climate science through quarterly workshops,
  meet-and-greets, and social events. To ensure that it can continue to facilitate
  this growth, </span><a><span>UCo has become an official UW Registered Student Organization</span></a><span>,
  providing it new access to a variety of resources for event planning and outreach.
  UCo is excited to make the most of these resources throughout the next year!</span></p>
  <p>If you would like to get PCC undergraduate-relevant climate updates, plesae sign
  up for the <a>Climate Undergrads List-serve.</a></p> </section> </article> </div>
