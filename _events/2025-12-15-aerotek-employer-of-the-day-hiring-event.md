AEROTEK will host an Employer of the Day hiring event on Dec. 15, 2025, from 9 a.m. to noon at WorkSource Kitsap, located at 3120 NW Randall Way, Silverdale, WA. Attendees can expect to meet with Aerotek recruiters and explore various job opportunities, including positions for Marine Electricians, Shipyard Laborers, and Welders at the Bremerton Navy Yard. Participants should bring two forms of identification for shipyard entry and are encouraged to bring their resumes for one-on-one interviews. This event aims to improve access to career development opportunities in the local community. Registration on WorkSourceWA.com is required to attend.
