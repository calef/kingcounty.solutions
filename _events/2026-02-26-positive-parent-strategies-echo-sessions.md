Join the Positive Parent Strategies ECHO Sessions, a 10-week virtual series beginning Feb. 26, 2026, designed for parents and caregivers of children ages 3 to 12 with developmental disabilities. Participants will engage in skill-building sessions that focus on practical strategies to support their children's unique needs. Each week will include interactive family case discussions, allowing attendees to share experiences and learn from one another. This supportive environment fosters community and collaboration, equipping caregivers with valuable tools and insights to enhance their parenting journey. Don't miss this opportunity to connect with others and gain knowledge that can make a difference in your family's life.
