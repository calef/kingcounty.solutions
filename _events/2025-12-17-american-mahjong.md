Join the fun at the American Mahjong event on December 17, 2025, from 12:30 p.m. to 2:30 p.m. at The Center for Active Living, located at 4217 SW Oregon St., Seattle, WA. This ancient Chinese tile game, similar to Rummy, is a great way to stimulate your brain and socialize. Players of all skill levels, including newcomers, are welcome to participate. Gather with friends and enjoy an afternoon of strategy and camaraderie in a welcoming environment. Don't miss this opportunity to learn the game or enhance your skills while connecting with others in the community.
