The Park Commission meeting scheduled for December 10 at 5:30 p.m. has been cancelled. This meeting typically provides an opportunity for community members to engage with city officials regarding the acquisition, development, and operation of parks and recreational facilities. The meeting is usually held in the Executive Conference Room at the Tukwila Community Center, located at 12424 42nd Avenue South in Tukwila, Washington. Attendees can also participate virtually via Microsoft Teams. For further information, residents are encouraged to contact Matthew Austin at the Tukwila Community Center.
