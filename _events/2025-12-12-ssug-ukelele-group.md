Join the SSUG Ukulele Group on December 12 from 2 to 4 p.m. in the Chamberlain Room for a lively musical experience. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced player, attendees can expect a welcoming environment to strum along and enjoy the company of fellow ukulele enthusiasts. The group is facilitated by Fabiola Rodriguez, who brings her passion for music to every session. Donations are appreciated, making this an affordable way to unwind and embrace your musical side. Don’t miss this opportunity to kick off your weekend with fun and creativity!
