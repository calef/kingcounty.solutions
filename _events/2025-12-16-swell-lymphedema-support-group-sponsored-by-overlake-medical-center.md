Join the SWELL Lymphedema Support Group, sponsored by Overlake Medical Center, on December 16 from 5:30 to 7 p.m. PST. This online event offers a supportive space for individuals living with lymphedema to connect, share experiences, and gain valuable insights through educational presentations. Attendees can expect to engage in discussions that emphasize community support and understanding of this challenging condition. The meetings foster a safe and non-judgmental environment, encouraging participants to give and receive support. For registration assistance, contact the Telephone Lifeline at 206-297-2500 during business hours.
