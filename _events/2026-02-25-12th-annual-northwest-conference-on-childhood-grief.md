Join us for the 12th Annual Northwest Conference on Childhood Grief on Feb. 25, 2026, at the Seattle Children’s Sand Point Learning Center. This one-day event brings together clinicians, therapists, counselors, educators, social workers, childcare providers, first responders, and healthcare professionals dedicated to supporting grieving children and their families.

Attendees will participate in engaging workshops and presentations led by experts in the field, focusing on practical strategies and innovative approaches to help children cope with loss. Network with peers, share experiences, and gain valuable insights into the unique challenges faced by grieving families.

Whether you are looking to enhance your skills or connect with others in the field, this conference offers a supportive environment to foster learning and collaboration. Don’t miss this opportunity to make a positive impact on the lives of children navigating grief.
