Join us for the Martin Luther King Jr. Health Fair on Jan. 19, 2026, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Crossroads Bellevue. This community event aims to improve health outcomes and foster connections among residents. Attendees can participate in free health screenings, interactive workshops, and fitness demonstrations suitable for all ages. Families can enjoy activities for children, while adults can learn about nutrition, wellness resources, and community services. Local health experts will be available to answer questions and provide guidance. Celebrate Dr. King's legacy by prioritizing health and wellness in our community.
