  class=\"field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden
  field--item\"><h2>Leaders to outline plans for the year and take questions from
  attendees</h2><p>SEATTLE – Washington State Ferries leadership will look back on
  2025 and what’s to come in 2026 at two online community meetings at noon and 6 p.m.
  Wednesday, Jan. 21.</p><p>Each meeting will begin with a recap of 2025, which saw
  the restoration of full domestic service and the system’s highest ridership since
  2019. There will also be updates on workforce development, the upcoming state legislative
  session, new ferries and vessel charging. The majority of the meeting time will
  be reserved for questions from the public.</p><h3>WSF online community meetings
  information</h3><p><strong>When: &nbsp;</strong><a href=\"https://wsdot-wa.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_N9QxdgtfS1SAVUXR8O8bzw\">Noon</a>
  and&nbsp;<a href=\"https://wsdot-wa.zoom.us/webinar/register/WN_w7TRXYSLQ-qIZbryKUzhbQ\">6
  p.m.</a> Wednesday, Jan. 21</p><p><strong>Where: &nbsp;</strong>Online via Zoom</p><p><strong>Details:
  &nbsp;</strong>Prior registration is required for these virtual meetings. Each meeting
  will cover the same material and is designed to give participants the option to
  join the meeting that best fits their schedule. Participants will be able to ask
  questions and anyone can also provide comments&nbsp;<a href=\"mailto:wsfcomms@wsdot.wa.gov\">ahead
  of time</a> via email. After the meetings, video recordings will be available online
  on WSF’s&nbsp;<a href=\"/travel/washington-state-ferries/about-us/community-participation\">community
  participation page</a>.</p><p>Free, temporary internet access is available to those
  who do not have broadband service in locations throughout the state. To find the
  nearest access, visit the state’s <a href=\"http://www.commerce.wa.gov/building-infrastructure/washington-state-drive-in-wifi-hotspots-location-finder/\">drive-in
  WiFi hotspot location finder</a>.</p><p><a href=\"/travel/washington-state-ferries\">WSF</a>,
  a division of the Washington State Department of Transportation, is the largest
  ferry system in the U.S. and safely and efficiently carries tens of millions of
  people a year through some of the most majestic scenery in the world. For the latest
  service updates, sign up for&nbsp;<a href=\"/Ferries/RouteAlerts/\">rider alerts</a>
  and track each ferry using the&nbsp;<a href=\"https://wsdot.com/ferries/vesselwatch/default.aspx\">real-time
  map</a> online.</p></div>\n      \n  </div>\n\n</article>\n\n\n  </div>\n\n          </section>\n\n

topic_titles: []

Washington State Ferries (WSF) will hold two online community meetings on Jan. 21 at noon and 6 p.m. to discuss plans for 2026 and recap 2025. The meetings will highlight the restoration of full domestic service and record ridership levels, the highest since 2019. Attendees will receive updates on workforce development, the upcoming state legislative session, new ferries, and vessel charging. Most of the meeting time will be dedicated to questions from the public.

Participants must register in advance for the Zoom meetings, which will cover the same topics. This format allows attendees to choose the session that fits their schedule. Questions can also be submitted via email before the meetings. Video recordings will be made available afterward on WSF’s community participation page.

For those without broadband access, free temporary internet is provided at various locations statewide. WSF, part of the Washington State Department of Transportation, is the largest ferry system in the U.S., serving millions of passengers each year in scenic locations. For real-time updates and ferry tracking, riders can sign up for alerts online.
