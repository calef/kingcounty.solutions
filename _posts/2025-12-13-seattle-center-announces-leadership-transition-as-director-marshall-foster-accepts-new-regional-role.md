  Accepts New Regional Role

Seattle Center announced a leadership transition as Director Marshall Foster has accepted a new regional role. Foster will depart from his position, with Deputy Director Diamatris Winston stepping in as the acting director effective Jan. 17, 2026. Foster has served as the director since 2015 and has played a crucial role in various projects, including the redevelopment of the Seattle Center Campus. His leadership has been marked by a focus on community engagement and enhancing the visitor experience. Winston, who has been with Seattle Center for several years, will lead during the transition period. The organization expressed gratitude for Foster's contributions and looks forward to Winston's leadership as they continue to serve the community.
