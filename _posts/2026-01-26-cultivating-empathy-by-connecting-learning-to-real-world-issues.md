Vy Nofua, operations manager at Northwest Furniture, provides feedback on a First Creek student’s cardboard prototype. At TAF, social-emotional learning (SEL) is viewed as essential for preparing students for success. SEL encourages students to become intentional citizens who collaborate to address complex issues. This approach helps build confidence and empathy among students, connecting their learning to real-world problems. By engaging with practical projects, students learn to work together and develop skills that are crucial for their future.
