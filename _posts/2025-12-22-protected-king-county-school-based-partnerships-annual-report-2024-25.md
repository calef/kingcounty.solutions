King County's School-Based Partnerships Annual Report for 2024-25 highlights the collaboration between schools and health services to support student well-being. The report details various initiatives aimed at improving access to mental health resources, physical health care, and overall student support. Key achievements include increased participation in health programs and expanded services in schools. The report emphasizes the importance of addressing students' physical and mental health needs to enhance their academic performance and social development. Partnerships with local health organizations have proven beneficial, leading to more comprehensive care for students. The report also outlines future goals for expanding these partnerships and increasing community involvement in health initiatives. Overall, the annual report showcases the positive impact of these collaborations on the health and success of students in King County.
