  \           <div>\n<p><strong>For immediate release</strong>: February 11, 2026  
  (26-023)</p>\n\n<p>Contact: <a>DOH Communications</a><br>\nPublic inquiries: Health
  Systems Customer Service 360-236-4700</p>\n\n<p><strong>OLYMPIA</strong> -- The
  Washington State Department of Health has taken disciplinary actions or withdrawn
  charges against the following health care providers in our state.</p>\n\n<p>The
  department’s Health Systems Quality Assurance Division works with boards, commissions,
  and advisory committees to set licensing standards for more than 80 health care
  professions (e.g., dentists, nursing assistants, counselors).</p>\n\n<p>Information
  about health care providers is on the agency website. Click on “<a>Health Care Provider
  Lookup</a>” under the “Find it Fast” section of the <a>Department of Health website</a>
  (doh.wa.gov).The site includes information about a health care provider’s license
  status, the expiration and renewal date of their credential, disciplinary actions
  and copies of legal documents issued after July 1998. This information is also available
  by calling 360-236-4700. Consumers who think a health care provider acted unprofessionally
  are encouraged to call and report their complaint.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Clark County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  January 2026 the Board of Physical Therapy charged assistant physical therapist
  <strong>Brenda Lee Jensen </strong>(P160031661) with unprofessional conduct. In
  September 2024, Jensen allegedly billed for patient care time when she was not engaged
  in patient care.</p>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health terminated the
  probation on <strong>Jacob Michael Phillips’</strong> (CG61240416) agency affiliated
  counselor credential.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Grays Harbor County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  November 2025 the Department of Health denied <strong>Sarah Sam Sotomish’s</strong>
  (HM61544998) application for a home care aide credential following an Adult Protective
  Services referral alleging financial exploitation and neglect and her failure to
  respond to the department’s request for information.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>King County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  January 2026 the Department of Health charged <strong>Nicci Allen</strong> with
  the unlicensed practice of counseling and notified her of its intent to issue a
  cease-and-desist order. Between March 2021 and September 2023, Allen offered and
  provided counseling services in Washington state without holding the required credential.</p>\n\n<p>In
  January 2026 the Department of Health released mental health counselor, mental health
  counselor associate and surgical technologist <strong>Katlin Marie Kellow</strong>
  (LH61664304, MC61339119, ST61094353) from the terms and conditions of her 2025 agreement
  and terminated monitoring.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Kitsap County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  December 2025 the Department of Health denied <strong>Jodi N. Piper’s</strong> (HM61558000)
  application for a home care aide credential. Between October 2023 and December 2024,
  Piper asked a client to pay her $10,000 per month for caregiving services outside
  of her employing agency.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Thurston County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  November 2025 the Department of Health denied <strong>Samantha Kay Borja’s</strong>
  (HM61489334) application for a home care aide credential. In February 2025, the
  Department of Social and Health Services placed Borja on its long-term care abuse
  and neglect registry. Placement on the registry disqualifies Borja from working
  with vulnerable adults and from certification as a home care aide.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Walla
  Walla County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health charged
  agency affiliated counselor and independent clinical social worker associate <strong>Rainee
  Ann Arevalo</strong> (CG61343122, SC61220297) with unprofessional conduct. Between
  October 2023 and March 2024, Arevalo allegedly engaged in inappropriate professional
  boundaries with incarcerated patients while providing mental health services, including
  excessive and unauthorized contact, written communications with sexual content and
  other sexually inappropriate conduct during therapy sessions.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Yakima
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Dental Quality Assurance Commission
  and <strong>Julie Evette Fernandez </strong>(D160362120) entered an agreed order
  that reactivated her dental assistant credential. Fernandez must comply with all
  terms and conditions of the order.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Out of State</strong></h2>\n\n<p><strong>Utah</strong>:
  In January 2026 the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission terminated the probation
  on<strong> Zackary W. Beard’s</strong> (PH60028060) pharmacy credential.</p>\n\n<p><strong>Note
  to Editors</strong>: Health care providers charged with unprofessional conduct have
  20 days to respond to the Department of Health in writing. The case then enters
  the settlement process. If no disciplinary agreement can be reached, the case will
  go to a hearing.</p>\n\n<p>Our <a>website</a> is your source for a healthy dose
  of information. Get updates by following us on <a>social media</a>.</p>\n\n<p>###</p>\n</div>\n

The Washington State Department of Health has announced disciplinary actions against several health care providers. In Clark County, assistant physical therapist Brenda Lee Jensen faces charges of unprofessional conduct for billing for patient care not provided. The department also terminated the probation of counselor Jacob Michael Phillips. In Grays Harbor County, Sarah Sam Sotomish's application for a home care aide credential was denied due to allegations of financial exploitation and neglect. King County's Nicci Allen was charged with practicing counseling without a license, while Katlin Marie Kellow was released from monitoring terms of her agreement. Jodi N. Piper in Kitsap County had her home care aide application denied after demanding $10,000 monthly from a client. Samantha Kay Borja from Thurston County was also denied due to placement on a long-term care abuse registry. Rainee Ann Arevalo in Walla Walla County faces charges for inappropriate conduct with incarcerated patients. In a separate case, Julie Evette Fernandez's dental assistant credential was reactivated under agreed conditions. Following charges, providers have 20 days to respond before the case proceeds to settlement or hearing.
