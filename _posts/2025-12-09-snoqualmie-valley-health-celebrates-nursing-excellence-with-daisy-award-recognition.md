  **SNOQUALMIE, WA** – Snoqualmie Valley Health proudly celebrated Irene Mburu, RN, as a recent recipient of the DAISY Award® for Extraordinary Nurses, a recognition that highlights the exceptional care delivered every day by nurses at SVH.

  At Snoqualmie Valley Health nursing is more than a role, it is a calling rooted in skill, compassion, vigilance and teamwork. Irene’s recognition reflects the standard of care patients can expect across SVH, which is attentive, responsive, and deeply patient-centered.

  “Irene embodies the very best of nursing,” said Tricia Ralston, MPH, BSN, RN, Chief Nursing Executive. “Her compassion and clinical excellence are matched by her ability to connect with patients and her team. We are proud to recognize Irene, and we are equally proud of the remarkable nursing team at Snoqualmie Valley Health that delivers high-quality care to our community every day.”

  Irene was nominated by a grateful patient who shared the following experience:

  _“I have spent the last four years waiting for my hip replacement during which I underwent four surgeries, endured massive bouts of pain, and was limited in mobility. After surgery, I went to SVH for rehab and met Irene. She listened, showed true empathy, and did her absolute best to help me, minimize pain, and make me comfortable. Her response time and efforts to reach solutions quickly were impressive. All the while, her sense of humor helped keep me cheerful and positive. She is a model nurse and a wonderful human being.”_

  The DAISY Award is a national and international program that recognizes nurses for extraordinary clinical skill and compassionate care. At SVH, the award is one of many ways the organization celebrates the people behind the care, nurses who deliver excellence in our hospital, clinics, urgent care, and specialty services every day.

  To nominate an SVH nurse for a DAISY Award, please visit https://team.getwell.health/public/rounds/daisy/4600.&nbsp; Additional information about the DAISY Award® for Extraordinary Nurses can be found online at www.DAISYfoundation.org.&nbsp;

  For more information about Snoqualmie Valley Health services, please visit SnoqualmievalleyHealth.org.

Snoqualmie Valley Health honored Irene Mburu, RN, with the DAISY Award® for Extraordinary Nurses, recognizing her exceptional nursing care. This award underscores the commitment to high-quality, patient-centered care at SVH, where nursing is seen as a calling that requires skill, compassion, and teamwork.

Chief Nursing Executive Tricia Ralston praised Irene, stating she exemplifies the best qualities of nursing through her clinical excellence and ability to connect with patients. A patient nominated Irene, sharing their experience of undergoing multiple surgeries and rehabilitation at SVH. The patient highlighted Irene's empathy, responsiveness, and humor, which helped make a challenging recovery process more bearable.

The DAISY Award is part of a national and international program that acknowledges nurses for their outstanding clinical skills and compassionate care. SVH utilizes the award to celebrate the dedication of its nursing staff, who deliver excellent care across various services. Those wishing to nominate a nurse for the DAISY Award can find more information on the hospital's website.
