  <p>Perhaps you are enjoying the new television series <em>Heated Rivalry</em>. Maybe you have watched every episode as it has been released, read <a>this interview</a> with the actors and <a>this one too</a>. You probably found <a>this interview with Chala Hunter</a>, the intimacy coordinator for the series, fascinating. Perchance you even started learning Russian for free with your library card and <a>Mango Languages</a> so you can understand the conversations between Ilya and his father. If so, there is a strong possibility that you are also one of the over 500 people with the eBook of <strong><a>Heated Rivalry</a></strong> by Rachel Reid on hold, wondering what you could possibly read while waiting for the full story of Scott, Shane, Ilya, and other players to be named later. The Seattle Public Library is here to support you during this difficult time.</p>
  <ul>
  <li>You can check out the Libby audiobooks of <strong><a>Game Changer</a></strong> (Scott and Kip) and <a><em>Heated Rivalry</em></a> (Shane and Ilya) right now!</li>
  <li>Sarina Bowen has written multiple riveting hockey romance series. Start one with <strong><a>Him</a></strong>, which she co-write with Elle Kennedy, <strong><a>The New Guy</a></strong>, <strong><a>Rookie Move</a></strong>, or <strong><a>The Year We Fell Down</a></strong>.</li>
  <li>The YA graphic novel duology <strong>Check Please!</strong> by Ngozi Ukazu has a great cast of characters on an elite college hockey team. Start with <strong><a>Book 1, #Hockey!</a></strong>.</li>
  <li>Other hockey series worth looking at start with <strong><a>Like Real People Do</a></strong> by E.L. Massey, <strong><a>Winging It</a> </strong>by Ashley Kane and Morgan James, <strong><a>The Long Game</a></strong> by Rachel Reid, <strong><a>Off the Ice</a> </strong>by Avon Gale, and <strong><a>Season’s Change</a></strong> by Cait Nary.</li>
  <li>Women’s sports get some love (and so do the athletes) in <strong><a>Out on the Ice</a></strong> by Kelly Farmer,<strong> <a>Cleat Cute</a> </strong>by Meryl Wilsner, <strong><a>Edge of Glory</a></strong> by Rachel Spangler, and <strong><a>Only When It’s Us</a></strong> by Chloe Liese.</li>
  <li>If you are willing to move off the ice and onto the baseball diamond, <strong><a>You Should Be So Lucky</a></strong> by Cat Sebastian has impeccable yearning, the series starting with <strong><a>Unwritten Rules</a></strong> by KD Casey explores the darker side of professional sports, and debut novel <strong><a>The Prospects</a></strong> by KT Hoffman is a home run.</li>
  <li>Some football player romances to go long for include <strong><a>The Cheat Sheet</a> </strong>by Sarah Adams, <strong><a>The Dating Playbook</a></strong> by Farrah Rochon, and the stylish and addictive series that starts with <strong><a>Intercepted</a> </strong>by Alexa Martin.</li>
  <li>Even further off the ice are books set in the fast, dangerous, and rather photogenic world of F1 racing, including <strong><a>Pole Position</a></strong> by Rebecca J. Caffrey and <strong><a>Cross The Line</a></strong> by Simone Soltani.</li>
  <li>Some of the 2025 sports romances steaming up my to-read pile includes <strong><a>Futbolista</a></strong> by Jonny Garza Villa, <strong><a>No Ordinary Love</a></strong> by Myah Ariel, <strong><a>Red Card</a></strong> by Maren Moore, <strong><a>Summer Skate</a> </strong>by Sean Avery, and <strong><a>Play You For It</a></strong> by Samantha Saldivar.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>What else would you recommend to other <em>Heated Rivalry</em> fans?</p>

Fans of the new series "Heated Rivalry" may be eagerly awaiting the eBook by Rachel Reid, which has over 500 holds at the Seattle Public Library. While waiting, viewers can enjoy audiobooks available through the Libby app, including "Game Changer" and "Heated Rivalry." For those looking for similar content, several hockey romance series are recommended. Notable titles include "Him" by Sarina Bowen and Elle Kennedy, "The New Guy," "Rookie Move," and "The Year We Fell Down." The graphic novel duology "Check Please!" by Ngozi Ukazu is also highlighted. Additional hockey-themed books like "Like Real People Do" by E.L. Massey and "Winging It" by Ashley Kane and Morgan James are worth exploring.

Beyond hockey, the article suggests romances in various sports, including baseball with "You Should Be So Lucky" by Cat Sebastian, and football with "The Cheat Sheet" by Sarah Adams. For fans of racing, titles like "Pole Position" by Rebecca J. Caffrey are mentioned. Readers are encouraged to share their recommendations for other titles that would appeal to "Heated Rivalry" enthusiasts.
