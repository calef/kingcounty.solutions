ELAP welcomes four new members to its Board of Directors: Heather Bresnahan from Auctane, Brad Goergen from HomeStreet Bank, Rekha Thomas from Path Forward Marketing, and Haley Tynes from Microsoft. They join the existing board members for 2026, which includes Pamela Anderson from Perkins Coie as Board President, Ramina Dehkoda-Steele from Wong Fleming as Board Vice President, Tasha Murphy from the University of Washington as Board Treasurer, and Matthew Widmyer from Davis Wright Tremaine, LLP as Board Secretary. Other board members include Debbie Akhbari from T-Mobile, Dan Choi and Emily Schlesinger from Microsoft, and several community members: Hannah Hamilton, Ben Laws from Weber Shandwick, Joan McBride, Mary Sakaguchi from Sakaguchi, Felbeck, Story & Reese, Kris Taylor, and Leslie Veloz from Hintze Law. The board is made up of volunteers from the legal community and other sectors, providing guidance and strategic vision for the organization. Information on how to get involved with ELAP is available for those interested.
