Meals on Wheels in Shoreline is seeking a volunteer caller to assist with taking weekly meal orders from clients on Tuesday mornings from 9:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m. Volunteers will be part of a team that helps seniors maintain their health and independence while also combating social isolation. The volunteer caller will take meal orders, update notes, and connect clients with additional support, ensuring they feel valued and heard. Long-term commitment is preferred, and training will be provided. Ideal candidates should have a commitment to the well-being of elders, good communication skills, reliability, attention to detail, and a positive attitude. Volunteers must be at least 18 years old, complete an application, consent to a background check, and be able to lift 15 pounds. This position will serve as a fill-in role when the regular caller is unavailable. Interested individuals can contact Meals on Wheels for more information.
