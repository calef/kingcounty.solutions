Meals on Wheels in Bellevue is seeking volunteers to deliver meals to homebound seniors on Monday mornings. Volunteers will pick up meals between 9 and 9:30 a.m. and usually complete their routes by 11:00 a.m. The program not only provides nutritious meals but also aims to reduce social isolation among seniors, helping them maintain their health and independence. Volunteers are needed for ongoing commitments rather than just one-time assistance. Mileage for deliveries is reimbursable. This opportunity is open to individuals and is kid-friendly. Interested volunteers can contact Meals on Wheels at mealsonwheels@soundgenerations.org for an application to get started.
