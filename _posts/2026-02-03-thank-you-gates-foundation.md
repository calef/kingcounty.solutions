  <p>The Refugee Women’s Alliance (ReWA) was honored to be invited to the Gates Foundation for its Gender Identity Day of Service on Monday, February 2nd. The day brought together Gates Foundation staff and inspiring local nonprofits—including Mary’s Place, Jubilee Women’s Center, and Westside Baby—for a meaningful Lunch &amp; Learn highlighting the vital work being done across King County. It was a powerful opportunity to connect, learn from one another, and celebrate our shared commitment to supporting women, families, and communities.</p>


  <div>
  <figure><img src="https://www.rewa.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/IMG_4928-600x471.jpeg"></figure>
  </div>


  <p><br>Following the Lunch &amp; Learn, members of the Gates Foundation’s Gender Identity group participated in hands-on service projects at each partner organization. At Mary’s Place, volunteers sorted warehouse donations and packed essential items for families. At Jubilee Women’s Center, they cleaned living spaces and served lunch to women accessing day services. At Westside Baby, volunteers organized and packed supplies for mothers and infants.</p>



  <p><br>At ReWA, the Gates Foundation team visited our MLK office to support survivors in our Domestic Violence (DV) Program. Together, they packed 10 carefully assembled boxes filled with hygiene supplies, blankets, and household essentials—items that represent safety, dignity, and the first steps toward stability for survivors rebuilding their lives. Our DV team shared more about the program and the individuals these care packages will support, grounding the work in the real stories of resilience and hope behind each box. Volunteers also supported ReWA through donor outreach by hand-writing heartfelt notes to members of our community.</p>



  <p><br>We are deeply grateful to the Gates Foundation staff for their time, care, and generosity. It was truly meaningful to welcome another organization into our space and mission, and we look forward to continued partnership and collaboration in the future.</p>

The Refugee Women’s Alliance (ReWA) participated in the Gates Foundation’s Gender Identity Day of Service on February 2. This event brought together Gates Foundation staff and local nonprofits, including Mary’s Place, Jubilee Women’s Center, and Westside Baby, for a Lunch & Learn that highlighted essential work in King County. Attendees shared insights and celebrated their commitment to supporting women, families, and communities.

After the discussion, volunteers engaged in hands-on service projects. At Mary’s Place, they sorted donations and packed essential items for families. At Jubilee Women’s Center, they cleaned living spaces and served lunch to women in need. At Westside Baby, they organized supplies for mothers and infants.

The Gates Foundation team also visited ReWA’s MLK office to assist survivors in the Domestic Violence Program, packing 10 boxes with hygiene supplies, blankets, and household essentials aimed at providing safety and dignity. ReWA's DV team shared stories of resilience connected to the care packages. Additionally, volunteers participated in donor outreach by writing notes to community members. ReWA expressed gratitude for the Gates Foundation’s support and looks forward to future collaboration.
