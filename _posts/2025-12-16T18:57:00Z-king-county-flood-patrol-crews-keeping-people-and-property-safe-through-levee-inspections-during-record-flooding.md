King County's Flood Patrol is actively monitoring levees along rivers in response to unprecedented flooding affecting Western Washington. As the region faces record water levels, patrol crews are conducting inspections to ensure levee integrity. Their proactive fieldwork enables timely reinforcement of levees to prevent breaches and facilitates a coordinated response for repairs when necessary. This critical monitoring helps protect both people and property from potential flood damage. The efforts of the Flood Patrol are vital in managing the ongoing crisis and mitigating risks associated with the extreme weather conditions impacting the area.
