The City of Normandy Park has launched its Third Annual Citizen Satisfaction Survey to gather feedback from residents. This initiative, started in 2024, aims to assess community satisfaction with services such as public safety, parks, public works, community development, and city communication. Residents are encouraged to participate in the survey, which includes questions about their experiences and allows for additional comments.

City Manager Amy Arrington emphasized the importance of community input in improving city services. The results from the survey will be compiled into a report and used to create an Action Plan. Last year, the plan addressed issues like vegetation management, park enhancements, and local road safety.

The survey can be completed electronically, and participants can reach out to City Clerk Erin Smith for any questions. The City Council remains committed to delivering high-quality services and values resident feedback as a guiding force for future initiatives.
