IACS Youth Council is seeking applications for the 2025-2026 term. The council aims to empower young leaders and provide them with opportunities to engage in community service, leadership training, and advocacy. Interested individuals can apply through the IACS website. The application process includes submitting a form and participating in an interview. The council focuses on issues that matter to youth and encourages diverse participation. Selected members will work together to develop initiatives and programs that benefit their communities. This is a chance for young people to make a difference and gain valuable experience.
