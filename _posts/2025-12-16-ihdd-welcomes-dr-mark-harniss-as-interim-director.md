The Institute on Human Development and Disability (IHDD) has appointed Dr. Mark Harniss as its Interim Director. He takes over from Dr. Sandra Juul, who has led the Institute and will continue as Principal Investigator of the IDDRC. Dr. Juul's leadership has been credited with enhancing collaboration across various programs, research, and partnerships within the Institute. Dr. Harniss, who has served as Associate Director and Director of the University Center for Excellence in Developmental Disabilities (UCEDD) for the past two and a half years, is recognized for his extensive knowledge and commitment to IHDD's mission. His experience is expected to help maintain continuity as the Institute progresses. The organization expresses gratitude to Dr. Juul for her service and extends a warm welcome to Dr. Harniss in his new role.
