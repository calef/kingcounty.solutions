Friends of Youth held its inaugural End-of-Year Celebration at Novelty Hill – Januik Winery, gathering community members, partners, and supporters to reflect on the year and commit to future efforts. The event provided a joyful atmosphere before the holiday season, emphasizing gratitude for those who attended. Attendees discussed the challenges facing young people today and shared the organization's vision for a future where every young person and family involved with Friends of Youth thrives. The evening fostered connections and reinforced the commitment to support youth in the community.
