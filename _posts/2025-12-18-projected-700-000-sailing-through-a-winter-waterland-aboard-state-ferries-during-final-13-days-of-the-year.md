  class=\"field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden
  field--item\"><h2>Travel rush set to push annual ridership over 20M for first time
  since 2019</h2><p>SEATTLE – It’s better than a one-horse open sleigh, so there will
  be a lot of people using Washington State Ferries to get to their destinations this
  holiday season.</p><p>Around 700,000 passengers are expected from Friday, Dec. 19,
  through Wednesday, Dec. 31. Those boarding in a vehicle should be ready for long
  waits.</p><p>The expected surge, combined with the restoration of full domestic
  service earlier this year, is projected to push 2025 ridership above 20 million.
  That would mark the system’s first return to that level since 2019.</p><h3>Plan
  before you go&nbsp;</h3><p>People boarding a vessel in a vehicle should use the
  <a href=\"/travel/mobile-app-and-social-media\">WSDOT mobile app</a> or visit the&nbsp;<a
  href=\"/travel/washington-state-ferries\">WSF website</a>&nbsp;before heading to
  the terminal. Both feature <a href=\"/travel/washington-state-ferries/schedules\">sailing
  schedules</a>, live <a href=\"https://wsdot.com/ferries/vesselwatch/terminals.aspx\">terminal
  conditions</a>, <a href=\"/Ferries/RouteAlerts\">rider alerts</a>, a <a href=\"https://wsdot.com/ferries/vesselwatch/\">real-time
  map</a> and <a href=\"https://secureapps.wsdot.wa.gov/ferries/reservations/vehicle/default.aspx?op=Make+reservations\">vehicle
  reservations</a>.</p><h3>Busy travel times</h3><p>With Christmas falling on a Thursday,
  it’s difficult to predict when people will be traveling. However, the busiest sailings
  will likely be centered around the weekends before and after the holiday. Historically,
  fewer people catch a ferry during New Year’s week. To reduce or eliminate waiting,
  riders should consider taking an early-morning or late-night sailing or using transit
  to walk or bike onto the ferry if possible.</p><h3>Holiday schedules</h3><p>There
  will be a few changes to the daily schedules on the Edmonds/Kingston and&nbsp;Point

Around 700,000 passengers are expected to travel on Washington State Ferries during the holiday season from Dec. 19 to Dec. 31. This surge in ridership is anticipated to push annual totals above 20 million for the first time since 2019. Travelers using vehicles should prepare for long waits at terminals. To avoid delays, passengers are encouraged to check the WSDOT mobile app or the WSF website for sailing schedules, terminal conditions, and vehicle reservations before heading out.

The busiest travel days are expected to be the weekends before and after Christmas, while New Year’s week typically sees lower ferry traffic. Riders may find it beneficial to take early-morning or late-night sailings or to walk or bike onto the ferry using transit.

Schedule changes will occur on Christmas Day and New Year’s Day for certain routes, with the Seattle/Bainbridge Island and Fauntleroy/Vashon/Southworth runs operating on weekend timetables. The winter sailing season begins on Dec. 28, introducing additional changes, particularly for the Seattle/Bremerton route due to construction at the Bremerton terminal. Travelers should also monitor real-time traffic updates for potential delays on state highways leading to ferry terminals.
