The dining menu for the Ballard Senior Center from December 15 to 19 features a variety of meals.

On Monday, the menu includes Sheppard Pie, Garden Salad, a cookie, and fruit. Tuesday offers a Seahawks-themed lunch with cheeseburgers, hot dogs, potato salad, chips, fruit, and cookies.

Wednesday features Artichoke Lemon Soup, a grilled cheese sandwich, salad, and fruit. On Thursday, Chicken Marsala is served alongside mashed potatoes, seasonal veggies, soup, and fruit. Friday's meal includes bacon and corn chowder, salad, cornbread, and fruit.

Additionally, a Wednesday night dinner requires an RSVP and will consist of pork roast with roasted potatoes, cheesy cauliflower, salad, and dessert.
