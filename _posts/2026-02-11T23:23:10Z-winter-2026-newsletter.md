C., a graduate of the Jubilee Women’s Center program, has made significant progress in rebuilding her life. During her time at Jubilee, she secured employment and focused on her parenting plan to reunite with her children. C. recently transitioned to a long-term supportive program in Kitsap County and saved $4,000 through the center's Matched Savings Program, providing her with a solid foundation for her future.

The newsletter also highlights the contributions of Rosemary Dunigan, who completed her term as Board Chair after three years of dedicated service. Her leadership was instrumental during the COVID-19 pandemic and other challenges faced by the organization. Current Board Chair Pamela Rogers-Kinkead praised Dunigan for her integrity and commitment to Jubilee's mission.

Additionally, Jubilee Women’s Center expressed gratitude to Stanley and the Stanley 1913 StrongHer Women’s Ambassador Group for their financial support and donation of drinkware for new arrivals. The center is also seeking donations of toiletries to assist the women they serve.

Lastly, the center announced its third annual Jubilee Jazz Night fundraiser on April 30, 2026, aimed at supporting women experiencing poverty.
