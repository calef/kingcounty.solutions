Sound Transit has selected MSC and Shelter Resources Inc. to develop about 230 units of affordable housing near the Federal Way Downtown Station. The project will utilize surplus property owned by Sound Transit. MSC expressed its honor in being chosen for this development, which aligns with their mission to provide affordable housing solutions. The development aims to address the growing need for affordable housing in the area, supporting community growth and accessibility. This initiative is part of Sound Transit’s broader effort to enhance the region's housing options while promoting transit-oriented development.
