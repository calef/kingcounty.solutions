King County Solid Waste Division is providing free drop-off services for flood debris, including yard waste and garbage, on weekends through January 11. This service is available to individual customers at three locations. To enhance support for communities affected by recent flooding, two additional temporary storm debris collection sites will also be opened. The goal is to assist residents in managing storm-related waste efficiently during this period.
