A study aimed to identify factors influencing prognosis in non-muscle invasive bladder cancer (NMIBC) patients treated with radical cystectomy (RC) and to create a predictive model for overall survival (OS). Researchers analyzed data from 1,129 patients in the SEER database, dividing them into a training group of 790 and a validation group of 339. They used univariate and multivariate Cox regression analyses to identify prognostic factors and determined the best predictive model based on the Akaike Information Criterion (AIC). A risk classification system was developed using a total score from the column line graph and the X-Tile method. The model was validated through various metrics, including the C-index and area under the curve (AUC) analyses. In the training group, the C-index was 0.67, with AUC values for 1-, 5-, and 10-year OS being 0.655, 0.704, and 0.722, respectively. In the validation group, the AUCs were 0.738, 0.694, and 0.705. The predictive model outperformed traditional staging systems, offering a foundation for patient counseling, follow-up scheduling, and treatment decisions.
