King County's Flood Warning Center was closed on Dec. 30 at 4:55 p.m. as river levels receded following a period of widespread flooding. The center had been active for three weeks, during which staff monitored river conditions, coordinated emergency responses, and facilitated repairs in collaboration with various partners. Additionally, they provided training for members of the National Guard, who were deployed by Gov. Bob Ferguson to assist with flood response efforts. The closure marked the end of a significant emergency response initiative aimed at managing the impacts of the flooding in the region.
