  \           <div>\n<p><strong>For immediate release</strong>: February 9, 2026  
  (26-020)</p>\n\n<p>Contact: <a>DOH Communications</a><br>\nPublic inquiries: Health
  Systems Customer Service 360-236-4700</p>\n\n<div>\n<p><strong>OLYMPIA</strong>
  -- The Washington State Department of Health has revoked or suspended the licenses,
  certifications, or registrations of the following health care providers in our state.
  The department has also immediately suspended the credentials of people who have
  been prohibited from practicing in other states.</p>\n\n<p>The department’s Health
  Systems Quality Assurance Division works with boards, commissions and advisory committees
  to set licensing standards for more than 80 health care professions (e.g., dentists,
  nursing assistants, counselors).</p>\n\n<p>Information about health care providers
  is on the agency’s website. <a>Click on “</a><a>Health Care Provider Lookup</a>”
  under the “Find it Fast” section of the <a>Department of Health website</a> (doh.wa.gov).
  The site includes information about a health care provider’s license status, the
  expiration and renewal date of their credential, disciplinary actions and copies
  of legal documents issued after July 1998. This information is also available by
  calling 360-236-4700. Consumers who think a health care provider acted unprofessionally
  are also encouraged to call and report their complaint.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Clallam
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health revoked<strong>
  Britni L. Gordon’s</strong> (HM60633596) home care aide credential. <a>In April
  2024, the Department of Social and Health Services found Gordon financially exploited
  a vulnerable adult and placed her on its long-term care abuse and neglect registry.
  Placement on the registry prohibits Gordon from working with or having unsupervised
  access to vulnerable adults.</a></p>\n\n<h2><strong>Clark County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  December 2025 the Department of Health suspended <strong>Gerrianne Aguon’s</strong>
  (NC10098955) certified nursing assistant credential for at least five years. In
  July 2024, the Department of Social and Health Services determined Aguon financially
  exploited a vulnerable adult and placed her on its long-term care abuse and neglect
  registry. Placement on the registry prohibits Aguon from working with or having
  unsupervised access to vulnerable adults. Aguon must comply with all terms of the
  order, including paying a $1,000 fine, before petitioning for reinstatement.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>King
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission
  indefinitely suspended <strong>Ashley Browning’s </strong>(VB61249559, VA61352438)
  pharmacy technician and pharmacy assistant credentials. In January 2024, Browning
  was ordered to comply with a monitoring program and appear before the Washington
  Medical Commission within 12 months in the matter of her license to practice as
  a physician assistant. Browning failed to respond to the commission’s request for
  information.</p>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health and <strong>Faleuli
  Leota </strong>(CO60662657) entered an agreed order indefinitely suspending Leota’s
  substance use disorder professional trainee credential. In May 2023, Leota self-reported
  to the department that she reported to work under the influence of alcohol.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Pierce
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health and <strong>Liana
  Marie Mozee</strong> (HM61478680, NA61359268) entered an agreed order suspending
  Mozee’s home care aide and registered nursing assistant credentials for at least
  three years. In July 2024, Mozee stole patients’ financial information and altered
  checks for personal use.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Spokane County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  January 2026 the Department of Health indefinitely suspended <strong>Carl J. Diana’s</strong>
  (NC10075049) certified nursing assistant credential. In March 2024, Diana appeared
  intoxicated at work, admitted to drinking alcohol and refused to submit to an alcohol
  test. Diana failed to respond to the department’s request for information.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Thurston
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health summarily suspended
  <strong>Victoria Anne Molina’s</strong> (NA61107460) registered nursing assistant
  credential pending further disciplinary action. In May 2023, Department of Social
  and Health Services’ Adult Protective Services determined Molina neglected a vulnerable
  adult. In September 2025, the finding became final and Molina was placed on the
  Department of Social and Health Services vulnerable adult abuse registry, effective
  September 9, 2025. Placement on the registry prohibits Molina from working with
  or having unsupervised access to vulnerable adults.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Out of State</strong></h2>\n\n<p><strong>Arizona</strong>:
  In December 2025 the Dental Quality Assurance Commission indefinitely suspended
  <strong>David Gimre Richardson’s</strong> (DE00005754) dentist credential. Between
  2018 and 2019, Richardson failed to meet the required standard of care while providing
  dental treatment to a patient. Richardson failed to respond to the department’s
  requests for information.</p>\n</div>\n\n<p><strong>Oregon</strong>: In January
  2026 the Department of Health permanently revoked <strong>Jamon Peter Fritsch’s
  </strong>(LR60407724) respiratory care practitioner credential. In March 2025, Fritsch
  was convicted in Oregon of second-degree murder constituting domestic violence,
  an unclassified felony, and first-degree abuse of a corpse, a class B felony.</p>\n\n<p>Our
  <a>website</a> is your source for a healthy dose of information. Get updates by
  following us on <a>social media</a>.</p>\n\n<p>###</p>\n</div>\n      \n  <div>\n

title: State revokes, suspends licenses, certifications, registrations of health care
  providers (02-09-2026)
topic_titles: []
---

The Washington State Department of Health has revoked or suspended the licenses, certifications, or registrations of several health care providers due to various infractions. Notable cases include Britni L. Gordon and Gerrianne Aguon, both found to have financially exploited vulnerable adults, leading to their credentials being revoked or suspended. In King County, Ashley Browning's pharmacy credentials were indefinitely suspended after she failed to comply with monitoring requests. Faleuli Leota also faced an indefinite suspension for reporting to work under the influence of alcohol. Liana Marie Mozee in Pierce County had her credentials suspended for stealing patients’ financial information. Carl J. Diana in Spokane was suspended after appearing intoxicated at work and refusing an alcohol test. Victoria Anne Molina in Thurston County was summarily suspended following findings of neglecting a vulnerable adult. Out-of-state providers, including David Gimre Richardson from Arizona and Jamon Peter Fritsch from Oregon, also faced serious disciplinary actions for failing to meet care standards and felony convictions, respectively. The Department of Health encourages consumers to report unprofessional conduct by health care providers.
