Rhonda Denet-Weems has worked at Seattle City Light since March 2006, nearly 20 years. She manages a team of 12 customer service representatives and two senior representatives. Her team plays a crucial role in assisting both residential and commercial customers of Seattle City Light. They handle various customer inquiries and are responsible for remotely disconnecting meters when necessary. Denet-Weems’ leadership helps ensure that the customer operations run smoothly, contributing to the overall efficiency of the utility service. Her extensive experience in the field allows her to provide valuable support to her team and the community.
