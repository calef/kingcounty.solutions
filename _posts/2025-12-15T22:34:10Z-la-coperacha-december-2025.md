El Centro de la Raza is encouraging community support through its #LightTheWorld Giving Machine located at Alderwood Mall, available until January 1. This unique initiative allows individuals to donate essential items directly to those in need by choosing from a selection displayed on the machine, which functions like a vending machine. Items available for donation include books, winter coats, holiday meals, school supplies, and soccer balls. Each contribution aims to address immediate needs for children, youth, and families in the community, especially during the winter months. The Giving Machine ensures that 100% of donations go to trusted organizations, including El Centro de la Raza, providing tangible support to help improve lives. The campaign emphasizes the importance of generosity during the holiday season, inviting individuals to take action and make a positive impact before the New Year. Donors are encouraged to visit the Giving Machine and participate in fostering a sense of community through their contributions.
