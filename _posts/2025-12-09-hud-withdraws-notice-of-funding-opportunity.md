KCRHA is pausing local requests for proposals and application submissions for the FY2025 Notice of Funding Opportunity (NOFO) indefinitely. The decision comes after the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) withdrew the NOFO. This withdrawal affects funding opportunities that were anticipated for local projects. KCRHA will provide updates as more information becomes available regarding future funding and application processes.
