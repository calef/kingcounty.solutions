Issaquah Community Services’ Neighbors Helping Neighbors campaign has successfully raised over $106,000 to aid local families in financial difficulty. This achievement highlights the community's generosity and compassion. A special acknowledgment goes to St. Michael and All Angels Episcopal Church for hosting a Messiah Singalong Benefit, which contributed more than $7,000 to the cause. Attendees praised the event for its beautiful performances and the strong sense of community fostered through music. The appreciation extends to the organizers, musicians, volunteers, and all who participated and donated. Such events are vital in helping neighbors maintain housing and stability during challenging times. Although the campaign is concluding, the need within the community remains. Thanks to continued support, Issaquah Community Services can provide assistance with care and dignity to those in need. The organization expresses gratitude to everyone involved in the Neighbors Helping Neighbors initiative.
