During the 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan, snowboarders competing in the slopestyle event are using tape on their pants to reduce drag and gain speed, as the course design has drawn criticism for being slow. NBC commentator Todd Richards highlighted that the course features a lot of flat ground before the final jump, which hinders athletes' momentum and has led to numerous wipe-outs. Richards noted that the course has become a significant obstacle for competitors, who are struggling to maintain speed. In addition to taping their pants, some snowboarders have been tucking one side of their required number bibs under their arms to prevent them from obstructing their vision during runs, as the bibs can flap up and pose a safety risk. These adaptations reflect the athletes' efforts to optimize their performance under challenging course conditions at the Winter Games.
