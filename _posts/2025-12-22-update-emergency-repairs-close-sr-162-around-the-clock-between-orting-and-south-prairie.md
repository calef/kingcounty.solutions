State Route 162 has reopened to traffic between Orting and South Prairie after emergency repair work was completed. The repairs were necessary to address issues that required immediate attention. Officials had previously closed the route around the clock to ensure the safety of drivers while the repairs were made. With the completion of the work, travelers can now use the highway again without restrictions.
