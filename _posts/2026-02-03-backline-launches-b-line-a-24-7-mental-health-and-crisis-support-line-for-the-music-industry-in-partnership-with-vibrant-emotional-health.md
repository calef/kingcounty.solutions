  <p><strong>NEW YORK, NY — February 3, 2026</strong> — <a>Vibrant Emotional Health (Vibrant)</a>, one of the nation’s leading mental health organizations, has partnered with <a>Backline</a>, the nonprofit dedicated to connecting music industry professionals and their families with mental health and wellness resources, to launch B-LINE, a 24/7 mental health and crisis support line designed specifically for the music industry.</p>
  <p>B-LINE offers immediate confidential support from trained counselors who understand the realities of working in music. Artists, crew members, staff, executives, and family members can connect by <strong>calling 1-855-BLINE99 or texting 254-639 (BLINE9).</strong></p>
  <p>The launch of B-LINE reflects Backline’s commitment to expanding mental health support for a workforce that frequently operates on the road and outside traditional systems of care.</p>
  <p>“Mental health crisis doesn’t wait for business hours, and neither should access to care,” <strong>said Cara A. McNulty, DPA, Chief Executive Officer of Vibrant Emotional Health.</strong> “This collaboration reflects our commitment to providing care that is compassionate, accessible, and responsive to lived experience. It is another example of how partnerships can close gaps in access.”</p>
  <p>“Our teams worked closely with Backline to design and operationalize a support model that truly serves the music industry,” <strong>said Michele Giordano, Vice President, Contact <strong>Services at Vibrant Emotional Health. </strong></strong>“B-LINE brings together trusted partners and trained counselors to provide timely, informed support for individuals and families whenever they reach out.”</p>
  <p>Since 2019, Backline has invested $3.5 million in mental health and wellness initiatives for the music industry and has supported more than 84,000 people. Backline’s team continues to encounter urgent situations requiring immediate support, reinforcing the need for a dedicated, real-time crisis line for the music community.</p>
  <p><strong>Backline’s Co-Founder and Executive Director Hilary Gleason said</strong> the launch of B-LINE represents a long-held vision becoming reality. “This has always been the dream. We’ve supported thousands of music industry professionals in their mental health and wellness journeys, but one critical piece was missing: real-time access to care. B-LINE changes that.”</p>
  <p>As part of this effort, Backline’s trusted online resource hub will continue to be available at <a>backline.care,</a> offering ongoing mental health information, referrals, and support for the music community.</p>
  <p>B-LINE complements Vibrant’s broader portfolio of crisis response services and innovative mental health programs that collectively deliver life-saving support to millions each year through numerous lifelines, community-based initiatives, and system-level advocacy.</p>
  <p>Together, Vibrant and Backline are working to strengthen the mental health safety net for the music industry, ensuring support is accessible whenever it is needed. To learn more about Vibrant Emotional Health, visit <a>www.vibrant.org.</a></p>

Vibrant Emotional Health has partnered with Backline to launch B-LINE, a new 24/7 mental health and crisis support line specifically for the music industry. This service provides confidential support via phone and text to artists, crew members, staff, executives, and their families. Individuals can reach B-LINE by calling 1-855-BLINE99 or texting 254-639 (BLINE9). The initiative aims to address the unique mental health needs of a workforce that often operates outside of traditional care systems. Cara A. McNulty, CEO of Vibrant, emphasized the need for immediate access to care, stating that mental health crises require timely support. Backline’s Co-Founder Hilary Gleason noted that B-LINE fulfills a long-held vision of providing real-time access to mental health resources. Since its inception, Backline has invested $3.5 million in mental health initiatives, helping over 84,000 individuals. B-LINE complements Vibrant’s existing crisis response services and aims to strengthen mental health support within the music community, making it more accessible when needed. Backline's online resource hub will remain available, offering ongoing mental health information and support.
