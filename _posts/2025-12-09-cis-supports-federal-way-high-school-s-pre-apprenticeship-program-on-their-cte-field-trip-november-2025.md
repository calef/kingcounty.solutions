CIS of South King County staff member Jenise Quinn supported Federal Way High School's Pre-Apprenticeship Program during a Career and Technical Education (CTE) field trip. Quinn, a former School Outreach Coordinator at the school, expressed her commitment to assisting Mr. DuFresne in connecting students with resources and opportunities. She highlighted the program's focus on teaching students educational, leadership, and life skills that will benefit them beyond the classroom and into their future careers in the trades.
