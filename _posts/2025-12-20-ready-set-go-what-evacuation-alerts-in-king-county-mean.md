King County uses a three-level evacuation alert system called Ready, Set, Go to help residents understand the urgency of evacuation situations.

- **Level 1 (READY)**: This alert indicates a possible evacuation in the area. Residents are encouraged to start preparing for evacuation by gathering supplies and making a plan.

- **Level 2 (SET)**: This alert means that an evacuation is likely. Residents should be ready to leave their homes at a moment's notice and should have their emergency kits ready.

- **Level 3 (GO)**: This is a mandatory evacuation order. Residents must leave immediately for their safety.

The Ready, Set, Go framework aims to provide clear instructions during emergencies, helping residents to respond effectively and stay safe.
