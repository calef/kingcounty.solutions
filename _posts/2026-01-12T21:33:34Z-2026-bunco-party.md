The Boyer Children’s Clinic is hosting its annual Bunco Party on Friday, January 30, 2026, at the Ballard Elks Club starting at 6:00 PM. This event promises a night filled with fun, laughter, and friendly competition, welcoming participants of all experience levels. Attendees can enjoy dinner and one complimentary drink with their ticket. No prior knowledge of Bunco is necessary, as the emcee will provide instruction for newcomers. The event encourages friends to come together for an enjoyable evening of gameplay. Registration is open for those interested in participating.
