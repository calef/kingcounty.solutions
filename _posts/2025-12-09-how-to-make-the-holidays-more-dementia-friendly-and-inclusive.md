The holiday season brings gatherings filled with conversation, food, and gifts, but for individuals with Alzheimer’s or dementia, these events can be overwhelming. Increased activity and changes in routine may lead to confusion and stress for those affected by cognitive decline. To create a more dementia-friendly environment, consider simplifying activities and maintaining familiar routines. Include calming spaces where individuals can retreat if they feel overwhelmed. Use clear, gentle communication to help them feel included, and consider their preferences in planning. Incorporating familiar music or holiday traditions can also promote comfort and joy. Ensuring a supportive atmosphere allows everyone to enjoy the festivities while accommodating the needs of loved ones with dementia.
