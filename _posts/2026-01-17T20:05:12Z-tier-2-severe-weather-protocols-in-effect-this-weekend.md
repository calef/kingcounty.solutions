The King County Regional Homelessness Authority will activate Tier 2 Severe Weather Protocols from Saturday, January 17, through Monday, January 19, winding down the morning of Tuesday, January 20, with potential extensions based on weather conditions. During this period, the Westside Neighbors Shelter will increase its capacity from 35 to 45 cots and will add an extra staff member for overnight supervision. Once the severe weather advisory concludes, the shelter will revert to its standard operations.

The Severe Weather Shelter Navigation Hotline, available daily from 8:00 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. at (206) 245-1026, can assist individuals seeking shelter, including families with children. The hotline can help connect them to shelter locations and provide transportation as needed.

Additionally, the shelter is requesting donations of food and warm clothing to support those in need during this severe weather event. Donations can be dropped off at the American Legion Hall located at 3620 SW Alaska St or sent via an Amazon wish list.
