The Bellevue City Council has adopted the Diversity Advantage Plan 2035, a strategic plan aimed at promoting equity across the city over the next decade. This plan outlines 41 long-term equity objectives that span all six of the council’s strategic areas, focusing on improving both community services and the internal culture of the city’s operations. Key commitments include engaging authentically with the community, enhancing access to city services, providing necessary resources for staff to apply an equity lens, connecting equity initiatives to other strategic priorities, and measuring progress. Mayor Lynne Robinson expressed pride in the inclusive process that shaped the plan, which involved extensive community engagement facilitated by the Centering Communities of Color Coordinating Team. Input was gathered from various events, culminating in a preliminary report shared with the community before the council's vote. The updated plan builds on the city’s initial Diversity Advantage Plan from 2014, which set forth 60 recommendations aimed at fostering a more inclusive community. Bellevue aims to enhance access to services and expand its positive impact on all residents, workers, and visitors.
