  <p>LOCATION:  1350 Cole St, Enumclaw, WA.</p>



  <p>DESCRIPTION:   The Enumclaw Senior Center serves a hot, nutritious lunch to seniors on Thursdays.  Help is needed between 11:00 am and 1:30 pm.</p>



  <p>CURRENT POSITION OPEN: Dishwasher, Clean-up, Server.</p>



  <p>DURATION:  Ongoing </p>



  <p>GROUP OR INDIVIDUAL:  Individual </p>



  <p>REQUIREMENTS:  Minimum 18 years old.  Food Worker Card required  </p>



  <p>QUALIFICATIONS:  No experience required. Looking for people who are reliable and friendly. </p>



  <p>BENEFITS: a free hot lunch on the day you volunteer. </p>



  <p>If interested, please submit a volunteer inquiry below.</p>

The Enumclaw Senior Center in Enumclaw, Washington, is seeking volunteers to assist with its Thursday lunch service for seniors. Volunteers are needed between 11:00 a.m. and 1:30 p.m. for various positions, including dishwasher, clean-up, and server. This opportunity is ongoing and open to individuals aged 18 and older. A Food Worker Card is required, but no prior experience is necessary. The center is looking for reliable and friendly volunteers. Benefits include a free hot lunch on the day of service. Interested individuals can apply online to become a volunteer.
