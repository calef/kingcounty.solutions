DAV emphasizes that America’s veterans deserve high-quality, timely, and veteran-focused care. The organization supports initiatives aimed at improving the efficiency and effectiveness of the VA health care system for all enrolled veterans, especially those who are injured or ill due to their service. DAV looks forward to receiving further details regarding the planned reorganization of the Veterans Health Administration. The organization is dedicated to collaborating with the VA, Congress, and other veteran stakeholders to enhance the quality of health care for the nation’s disabled veterans.
