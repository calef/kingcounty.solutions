Water District 19 has issued a boil water advisory for some customers following a water main break caused by an excavator accident on SW 216th Street and 86th Ave SW. The damage resulted in water loss and low pressure in the area. Although crews have repaired the pipe, the Washington Department of Health mandates water testing to ensure safety before lifting the advisory. Lab results are expected by Tuesday, Feb. 3, and the boil order may be lifted then.

Affected customers are instructed to boil tap water for one minute before using it for drinking, ice-making, brushing teeth, washing dishes, and food preparation. The district warned that low pressure can allow contamination from environmental or waste sources into the water system, which may lead to short-term health issues such as diarrhea and nausea, particularly affecting vulnerable populations like infants and the elderly.

Customers should ensure that Water District 19 has their current contact information for updates. For further inquiries, residents can call 206-463-9007, email water19@water19.com, or visit water19.com.
