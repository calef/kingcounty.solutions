During a cross-country skiing event at the Winter Olympics in Milan-Cortina, a dog unexpectedly joined the race, creating a memorable moment for spectators and athletes alike. The incident occurred during the qualifications for the women's team sprint when a two-year-old Czechoslovakian wolfdog named Nazgul dashed onto the course and raced alongside the competitors. Broadcasters commented on the dog's participation, noting that it garnered the loudest cheers of the day, even if it wasn't an official competitor.

Nazgul, who had a collar and belonged to a nearby bed-and-breakfast owner, casually exited the course after crossing the finish line without disrupting the race. The event's chief of competition later attended to the dog. Athletes reacted with a mix of amusement and surprise; while gold medalist Jonna Sundling found the moment enjoyable, Swiss skier Nadja Kaelin expressed relief at not having to ski near the dog due to her fear of them.

The playful incident was celebrated on social media, with many sharing the dog’s photo finish, showcasing the lighthearted spirit of the Olympic Games amidst serious competition.
