In this Thanksgiving season, gratitude is expressed for the support and partnership of the community in efforts to combat domestic abuse. The organization highlights the importance of breaking the cycle of abuse, which often thrives in secrecy and isolation, leaving victims feeling alone. The Rise support group provides a safe environment for women to share their experiences and heal from trauma. Participants in the group have found connection and understanding among peers who have faced similar challenges. One participant shared their appreciation for the group, emphasizing the significance of community support in their healing journey. The organization remains committed to helping families and communities thrive through these vital programs.
