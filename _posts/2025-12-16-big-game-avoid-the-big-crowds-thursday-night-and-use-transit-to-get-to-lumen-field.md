Big game excitement is building as the Rams face the Seahawks for Thursday Night Football at Lumen Field. This matchup is crucial for both teams, as they vie for first place in the NFC West and aim for a potential home field advantage in the playoffs. Fans are encouraged to avoid heavy traffic and large crowds by using public transit to get to the stadium. King County Metro offers several transit options to make travel easier for game attendees. Utilizing these services can help fans enjoy the game without the stress of parking and congestion.
