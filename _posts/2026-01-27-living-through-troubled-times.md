A series of adult forums will be held following worship on Sundays during the season after Epiphany, focusing on how faith impacts various aspects of life and vocation. Each session will feature a different facilitator, allowing participants to attend based on their availability. On January 25, Rev. Dr. Peter Rodgers will discuss the intersection of Christian faith and biblical scholarship, sharing insights from his 56 years as a priest and scholar. On February 1, Rev. Brian will lead a discussion on faith and vocation, exploring personal and collective callings as followers of Jesus. Participants will consider how to integrate their faith with their work and daily lives. Deacon Vince will present on faith and culture on February 8, with details to be announced. On February 15, The Rev. Canon Britt Olson will address faith and politics, examining the concept of Christian Nationalism and its effects on the church. Olson will also discuss how to respond to differing political perspectives as a person of faith. All sessions stand alone, and attendees are encouraged to invite friends or neighbors interested in these topics.
