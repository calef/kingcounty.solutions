Christian Estoque, a 38-year-old man from Pomona, California, has been charged in connection with a stolen aircraft case linked to Auburn Municipal Airport in Washington. The single-engine plane was reported stolen on January 3 and recovered on January 6 at Corona Municipal Airport in California. Authorities noted that the plane’s transponder had been altered to hide its location during flight, although it was spotted in Kelso, Washington, on January 4.

FBI agents, part of the Los Angeles Joint Terrorism Task Force, arrested Estoque after observing him entering another unregistered aircraft and powering on the engine, leading them to believe he was attempting to steal it. Estoque, who is not a licensed pilot and is not listed as a registrant of any aircraft, reportedly admitted to being under the influence of methamphetamine while operating the plane.

He was charged in a federal complaint filed on January 28 in U.S. District Court in Los Angeles. Estoque appeared before a federal magistrate on January 29 and was released on bond. The prosecution of this case is being handled by the U.S. Attorney’s Office in Los Angeles.
