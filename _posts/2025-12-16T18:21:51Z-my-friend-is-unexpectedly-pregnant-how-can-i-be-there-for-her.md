When a friend shares unexpected pregnancy news, it's essential to provide support rather than solutions. Start by listening empathetically, allowing her to express her emotions without judgment. Instead of contributing to her fears, offer hope and reassurance, reminding her of her strengths and past resilience. Avoid placing pressure on her by saying you'll support any decision; instead, affirm your commitment to help her navigate this journey.

Encourage her to confirm the pregnancy with a medical-grade test and ultrasound, which can provide clarity and rule out complications. Offer to accompany her to appointments to show your support. Additionally, help her in practical ways, such as running errands or preparing meals, while also ensuring you don’t overextend yourself.

If she is considering abortion, suggest seeking education from trusted professionals in a confidential environment. It's important for her to have access to accurate information and support to make informed choices. Care Net of Puget Sound offers free pregnancy consultations and resources to assist her in evaluating her options and ensuring her health and safety. Ultimately, your presence and support can make a significant difference during this challenging time.
