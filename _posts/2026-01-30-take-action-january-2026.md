  <h2>Stay Informed and Get Registered to Vote</h2>



  <p>our voice matters—make sure it’s heard. Staying informed and registered to vote is one of the most powerful ways to create change in your community. Whether it’s local elections or national issues, every ballot helps shape the future we all share.</p>



  <p><strong>Here’s how to take action:</strong></p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Get Registered:</strong> Check your voter registration status or register to vote today.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Stay Informed:</strong> Learn about upcoming elections, ballot measures, and candidates who represent your values.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Vote:</strong> Participate in every election—it’s your right and your civic duty.</li>
  </ul>



  <p>Together, we can ensure that every voice counts and every vote makes a difference.</p>

Stay informed and registered to vote to make your voice heard in your community. Every ballot impacts local and national issues. To take action, check your voter registration status or register to vote. Learn about upcoming elections, ballot measures, and candidates who align with your values. Participating in every election is both a right and a civic duty that helps ensure every voice counts.

Additionally, there is an opportunity to urge the Senate to add accountability measures to the Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agency. You can take action quickly by using the 5 Calls advocacy tool, which allows you to contact your Senators by entering your phone number and ZIP code. Alternatively, you can look up your U.S. Senators directly and reach out via phone or email. A suggested script for contacting your Senator includes introducing yourself, stating your city and ZIP code, and urging them to oppose any Department of Homeland Security appropriations bill that continues funding ICE in its current form, advocating instead for humane and accountable immigration policies.
