In 2025, the University of Washington's social media team highlighted significant moments and events from the campus community. Robert J. Jones was named the 34th president of the UW and expressed his excitement to lead the university. A snow day allowed students to enjoy a delayed start in Seattle. For Valentine’s Day, UW researchers shared personal stories about their passion for their work. In spring, crowds visited the campus for the blooming cherry blossoms, marking the season's arrival.

During National Library Week, Julie Tanaka showcased a unique edition of Dante Alighieri’s “Divine Comedy.” The second season of the podcast “Ways of Knowing” was launched, exploring the humanities. Over 7,400 graduates participated in the June commencement ceremony at Husky Stadium, the largest class to pre-register. The Vera C. Rubin Observatory released its first images, marking advancements in astronomy. President Jones officially began his tenure ahead of the fall quarter, while a newly installed story pole shared Coast Salish legends.

The year concluded with UW alum Mary Brunkow winning the Nobel Prize in Medicine. An anonymous donor also gifted $50 million to fund senior-year clinical rotations for Medical Laboratory Science students.
