The conversation also touches on the role of information dissemination in bridging gaps in knowledge about female athletes' experiences.

In another segment, the podcast delves into the use of psychedelics in therapy. Licensed therapists Brian Pilecki and Temple Morris share their insights on how psychedelics can enhance psychological flexibility and promote healing by helping individuals confront deeply buried emotions and experiences. They emphasize the importance of preparation and integration in the therapeutic process to ensure safety and effectiveness. The therapeutic use of psychedelics, they argue, can reveal personal values and foster acceptance, ultimately guiding individuals toward meaningful changes in their lives.
