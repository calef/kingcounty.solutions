The United States women's hockey team will face Canada in the gold medal game at the Milan-Cortina Olympics, marking their seventh gold-medal matchup since the sport's Olympic debut in 1998. The game is set for Thursday at 1:10 p.m. ET. The U.S. team, led by captain Hilary Knight, aims to secure its third Olympic gold medal, having previously won in 1998 and 2018. They come into the game with an impressive record, having outscored opponents 31-1 throughout the tournament and not allowing a goal in over 331 minutes of play. In contrast, Canada, the defending champion, suffered a 5-0 defeat to the U.S. in the preliminary round, marking its first shutout in Olympic history. The game will be broadcast live on USA Network and Peacock, with a re-air at midnight ET. Additionally, Switzerland will compete against Sweden for the bronze medal earlier that day at 8:40 a.m. ET, also available on USA Network and Peacock.
