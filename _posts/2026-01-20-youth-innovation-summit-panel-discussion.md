  <h2>On Thursday, January 22 at 2:00 PM at the Sammamish Boys &amp; Girls Club</h2>
  <p><!-- Start Blackbaud Form Embed --></p>
  <p>We are excited to share that Boys &amp; Girls Clubs of America President Jim Clark will be visiting Boys &amp; Girls Clubs of King County on January 21 and 22 to take part in our Youth Innovation Summit.</p>
  <p>This two-day event will highlight an important and timely conversation about young people and technology. The summit will feature a panel discussion focused on artificial intelligence, responsible technology use, and digital safety for kids and teens. Club members will have the chance to ask questions, share their experiences, and help shape the conversation alongside community leaders and industry experts.</p>
  <p>At Boys &amp; Girls Clubs, we believe young people deserve a voice in decisions that affect their future. The Youth Innovation Summit is designed to create that space, bringing together youth perspectives with real-world expertise to explore how AI and new technologies can be used in positive, safe, and empowering ways.</p>
  <p>Your support makes opportunities like this possible. Because of caring community members like you, Club kids are able to learn, lead, and engage with the issues that will shape their world.</p>
  <p>We would be honored to have you join us in supporting this important work.</p>

Boys & Girls Clubs of America President & CEO Jim Clark will visit the Boys & Girls Clubs of King County on January 21 and 22 for the Youth Innovation Summit. Scheduled for January 22 at 2:00 PM, the summit will focus on important discussions about young people and technology. A key feature will be a panel discussion addressing artificial intelligence, responsible technology use, and digital safety for kids and teens. Club members and Youth of the Year candidates will have the opportunity to engage by asking questions, sharing experiences, and contributing to the conversation alongside community leaders and industry experts. The event aims to empower young people by incorporating their perspectives into discussions about how AI and new technologies can be used positively and safely. The Boys & Girls Clubs emphasize the importance of giving youth a voice in decisions that impact their futures, and the summit is a platform for fostering that dialogue. Community support plays a crucial role in creating opportunities like this, enabling kids at the Club to learn, lead, and engage with relevant issues.
