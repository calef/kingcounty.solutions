Seattle and the surrounding area are facing high winds and heavy rain due to a strong atmospheric river event expected to last from today through Wednesday. This weather pattern could lead to flooding and landslides across western Washington. The City of Seattle is preparing for the impact, urging residents to stay informed and take precautions. There are predictions of significant rainfall, which may cause streams and rivers to rise, increasing the risk of flooding. The city is also advising people to avoid driving through flooded areas and to be cautious of falling trees and debris. Emergency services are on standby to respond to any incidents resulting from the severe weather. Residents are encouraged to monitor local weather updates and to have an emergency plan in place.
