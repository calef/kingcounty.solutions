On December 9, the Normandy Park City Council held its monthly meeting, addressing several key actions. The council approved the Miller and Walker Creeks Basin Stewardship 2026 Technical Services Agreement, continuing the partnership with Burien, SeaTac, King County, and the Port of Seattle. This agreement focuses on protecting and improving Miller and Walker Creeks by hiring technical experts to enhance environmental management, water quality, fish and wildlife habitat, and compliance with the City’s stormwater permit requirements. Additionally, the council amended the interlocal agreement with the City of Sunnyside for jail services, increasing the daily cost from $64 to $76.59 per person. Despite the price hike, Sunnyside remains the most cost-effective jail service option available.
