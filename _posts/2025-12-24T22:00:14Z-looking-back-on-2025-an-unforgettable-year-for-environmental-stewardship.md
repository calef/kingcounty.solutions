title: Looking back on 2025, an unforgettable year for environmental stewardship
topic_titles: []
---

In 2025, King County's environmental stewardship efforts marked a notable year, highlighted by a comprehensive response to historic flooding that affected the region. The county's department coordinated a 24/7 emergency response, prioritizing the safety of residents while simultaneously safeguarding local ecosystems, fish, and wildlife. Key achievements included the restoration of critical habitats and the implementation of new environmental initiatives aimed at enhancing resilience against future climate-related challenges. The collaborative efforts of employees and community partners were crucial in these successes, demonstrating a strong commitment to environmental protection and public safety. The department expressed gratitude to all contributors for their dedication to improving the health of King County's natural resources and communities. Looking forward, the county plans to build on these achievements to further advance its environmental goals in the coming years.
