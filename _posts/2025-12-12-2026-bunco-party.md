Boyer’s Bunco Party is set for January 30 at 6 p.m. at the Ballard Elks Club. Attendees are encouraged to bring their friends and enjoy a night filled with fun, laughter, and competition. Tickets include dinner and one complimentary drink, making it an enjoyable evening for all. No prior Bunco experience is necessary, as an emcee will provide instructions for those new to the game. The event aims to create a lively atmosphere where everyone can participate, regardless of skill level.
