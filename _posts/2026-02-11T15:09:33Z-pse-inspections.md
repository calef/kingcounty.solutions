Puget Sound Energy (PSE) is conducting routine power pole inspections on Vashon Island in partnership with Osmose Utilities Services, Inc. This maintenance aims to ensure the safety and reliability of electric service by assessing the strength of power poles. Residents without power poles on their property will not be directly affected, but they may see Osmose crews and vehicles during normal business hours.

For properties with power poles, crews will initially knock on doors to inform residents before beginning inspections. If residents are unavailable and access is safe, work will proceed; otherwise, a door tag will be left with contact information for a PSE representative. The initial inspections are expected to take about 30 minutes and will not disrupt electric service.

In some cases, pole restoration may be required to extend the lifespan of a pole, which involves reinforcing the structure with steel. If restoration is needed, a crew will return within one to four months, with this work taking one to two hours and also not affecting electric service. PSE encourages residents to visit their website for more information about the maintenance program and appreciates the community's patience during this process.
