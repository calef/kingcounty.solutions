In the January 30, 2026, episode of the Peace Meal podcast, dietitian Gretchen Wallace discusses strategies to help children develop a healthy relationship with food. She emphasizes that body image issues in adolescents should not be trivialized, as these common struggles can have lasting effects. Wallace highlights the negative impact of environmental factors, such as inappropriate health class content and curated social media, on young people's perceptions of food and their bodies.

She advocates for a compassionate approach to nutrition, moving away from a “math-based” perspective toward trusting one's body. Wallace provides practical guidance for families, encouraging supportive boundaries and curiosity about food. She stresses the importance of parents as vital members of the treatment team and warns against normalizing body dissatisfaction and restrictive eating behaviors among teens.

Wallace's experience in treating eating disorders, particularly among adolescents, informs her collaborative, evidence-based practice. She also maintains a list of anti-diet and body-inclusive resources on her Substack, The Full Life. The podcast aims to address eating disorders, body image concerns, and the societal influences on these issues.
