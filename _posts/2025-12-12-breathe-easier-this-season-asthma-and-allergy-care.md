Asthma is a common chronic condition affecting both children and adults, often triggered by seasonal allergies, exercise, infections, or environmental factors. It causes inflammation and narrowing of the airways, leading to symptoms like wheezing, shortness of breath, chest tightness, and persistent coughing. Poorly managed asthma can result in frequent flare-ups, disrupted sleep, missed days at school or work, and increased medical visits.

Snoqualmie Valley Health has introduced specialized asthma and allergy care with allergist Dr. Jocelyn Cooper. This expanded service offers local patients access to comprehensive support, including allergy testing, personalized asthma management plans, education on inhaler use, and advanced treatments for severe asthma.

Seeking early diagnosis and ongoing treatment is crucial for managing asthma and preventing flare-ups. Patients are encouraged to discuss referrals to the allergist at Snoqualmie Ridge Clinic to improve their respiratory health and enhance their quality of life.
