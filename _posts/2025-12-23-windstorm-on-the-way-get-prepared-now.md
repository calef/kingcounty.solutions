The National Weather Service in Seattle has issued a High Wind Watch for the Puget Sound region, warning of a potential major windstorm on December 24. Wind gusts could exceed 60 miles per hour in King County, posing risks of damage to trees and power lines. Residents are urged to prepare for possible power outages and hazardous conditions. Officials recommend securing loose outdoor items, staying indoors during the storm, and having an emergency kit ready. The windstorm may disrupt travel and lead to downed branches, power outages, and other safety concerns.
