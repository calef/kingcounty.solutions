Angie Senter is a dedicated advocate for social justice and community empowerment. As the Director of Community Engagement at the Institute for Nonprofit Advancement, she works to eliminate barriers that marginalized populations face in accessing resources and opportunities. Senter has a strong background in nonprofit management, having previously served in leadership roles at various organizations focused on social equity.

Her approach emphasizes collaboration and building relationships within the community. Senter believes in the power of grassroots efforts to drive meaningful change and is committed to amplifying the voices of those often overlooked in decision-making processes. She engages with diverse groups to foster inclusivity and mobilize support for community-driven initiatives.

In addition to her professional work, Senter is actively involved in local advocacy efforts, focusing on issues such as affordable housing, education, and healthcare access. She aims to empower individuals by providing them with the tools and skills necessary to advocate for themselves and their communities. Through her leadership and passion for social justice, Senter continues to inspire others to take action and create a more equitable society.
