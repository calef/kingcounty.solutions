Snoqualmie Tribal Council members visited Washington, D.C., in September for meetings with federal lawmakers and agency officials to advocate for their ancestral lands, sacred sites, and sovereignty. A key focus was on protecting Snoqualmie Falls, a site listed on the National Register of Historic Places. The council called for action from the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) to address concerns over aircraft intrusions. FAA data revealed that over 3,400 flights occurred within a mile of the Falls in just over a year, with many flying below 900 feet. These low-altitude flights disrupt traditional religious and cultural practices of the Snoqualmie people, affecting the formations and pathways of the rising mists. The Tribe emphasizes the importance of safeguarding this sacred site from further disturbances to preserve their cultural heritage.
