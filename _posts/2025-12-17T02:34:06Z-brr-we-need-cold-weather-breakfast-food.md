Westside Neighbors Shelter is seeking donations to replenish essential breakfast items at its morning warmup center, particularly as cold weather approaches. Among the most requested items are hot chocolate packets, which help clients warm up, as well as bulk oatmeal and pancakes served each morning. The shelter's supply is currently low on several key items, including hot chocolate, Cup-o-Noodles, bulk oatmeal, sugar, pancake syrup, and Krusteaz buttermilk pancake mix. The shelter also needs paper towels, hot cups, and bowls for serving.

Community members can contribute by purchasing items from the shelter's Amazon wish list, which will be delivered directly to the facility. Alternatively, donations can be dropped off at the shelter during designated hours, from 7 to 11 a.m. or 5 to 9 p.m. The shelter expresses gratitude for any support to help provide warm meals during the cold weather.
