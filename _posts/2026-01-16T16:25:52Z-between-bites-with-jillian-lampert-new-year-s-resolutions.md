In the latest episode of "Between Bites," Dr. Jillian Lampert addresses the challenges of New Year's resolutions, particularly for individuals recovering from eating disorders. The episode highlights that nearly 80% of people set resolutions focused on body image or fitness, which can be particularly triggering for those with a history of disordered eating. Dr. Lampert offers insights on managing the pressure and anxiety that often accompany this time of year, emphasizing the importance of self-awareness and support. She discusses how to recognize the onset of disordered thoughts, strategies to maintain recovery tools, and the need for self-care over self-discipline during this season. Dr. Lampert, a seasoned expert in nutrition and public health with extensive experience in eating disorders, aims to provide listeners with practical advice to navigate the influx of body-focused messaging that can disrupt mental health. The podcast, "Peace Meal," covers various topics related to eating disorders and body image, aiming to foster meaningful conversations and support for those affected.
