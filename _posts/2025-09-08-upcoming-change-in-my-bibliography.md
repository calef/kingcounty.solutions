My Bibliography, a tool for tracking and managing publications, is undergoing significant changes. The National Center for Biotechnology Information will transition My Bibliography to a new platform, which will enhance its functionality and user experience. Users will be able to access improved features, including easier ways to manage citations, track publications, and connect to other resources.

The new platform will offer a streamlined interface, making it more intuitive for users to navigate. This upgrade aims to support researchers in maintaining accurate records of their work and ensuring compliance with funding agency requirements. The transition is expected to occur in late 2025. Users are encouraged to prepare for this change by familiarizing themselves with the new system and taking steps to ensure their data is up to date.

Additional resources and support will be provided to assist users during the transition. More information will be available as the launch date approaches, ensuring that researchers can smoothly adapt to the new My Bibliography platform.
