Friends of Youth held a ribbon cutting ceremony last month at their Kirkland campus to celebrate Bridge Pointe, a new housing program for young adults aged 18 to 24 transitioning from inpatient behavioral health treatment. This event recognized the successful operation of their first Bridge Pointe house over the past three months and looked forward to the opening of a second home planned for 2026. Local leaders, funders, and community members gathered to support the initiative, which aims to provide a stable living environment for young adults in need. The program underscores the commitment to helping young individuals heal and regain independence after treatment.
