Federal charges against Canadian woman Lindsay Anne Moffatt have been dismissed following an incident at Peace Arch State Park where she was accused of kicking a U.S. Border Patrol agent. The charges, which included assault on a federal officer and improper entry into the United States, stemmed from Moffatt's attempt to cross the border with a marijuana vape pen on December 30, 2022. After being denied entry, she allegedly crossed into the U.S. unlawfully and resisted arrest, resulting in the charges filed in January. Moffatt was deported to Canada after her arrest, and the case was dismissed at the government's request on January 13.

Despite the dismissal, Moffatt is now seeking the return of property seized during her arrest, including $1,230 in cash, jewelry valued at 5,000 Canadian dollars, and personal items. Her attorney filed a motion on February 2, stating that some items were reportedly missing when her partner attempted to retrieve them. The matter has been assigned to a U.S. District Court judge as a civil dispute, following the dismissal of the criminal case. The court records indicate that the resolution of Moffatt's claim for her belongings is still pending.
