  <a>Apply For Position</a>
  <p>Department: Snoqualmie Gaming Commission<br>
  Job Status: Temporary<br>
  Salary/Hourly: An amount not to exceed $30.29 per hour.<br>
  Reports To: Tribal Council<br>
  Grade/Level: Stipend<br>
  Travel Required: Minimal travel required<br>
  Work Schedule: Varied<br>
  Positions Supervised: SGC Executive Director</p>
  <p>POSITION DESCRIPTION</p>
  <p>The Snoqualmie Gaming Commission has the authority and responsibility to regulate Gaming Activities within the Tribe’s Indian Lands. This authority includes the power to regulate the Gaming Operations, Gaming Facility, and to license all person’s subject to the Commission’s licensing authority under the Tribe’s Gaming Act and Regulations. The Commission ensures that all Gaming Activities conducted within the Tribe’s Indian Lacnodms ply with applicable Tribal, State, and Federal laws.</p>
  <p>ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Read and interpret gaming laws and regulations consistent with the Gaming Act.</li>
  <li>Protect the integrity of gaming activities, and prevent the appearance of impropriety.</li>
  <li>Prepare for and attend meetings with the Tribal Council, SGC, and others as directed.</li>
  <li>Work diligently with nonconflicted Commissioners as an appellate body for an administrative hearing on a limited and temporary basis.</li>
  <li>Maintain high ethical standards.</li>
  <li>Other duties as assigned</li>
  </ul>
  <p>QUALIFICATIONS</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Must be an enrolled Snoqualmie Indian Tribe Member, unless otherwise advertised.</li>
  <li>High School diploma or equivalent required. Bachelor’s degree from a regionally accredited institution preferred.</li>
  <li>Current and valid driver’s license in state of residenty.</li>
  <li>Demonstrated professional experience with oversight and monitoring of regulated operations.</li>
  <li>Demonstrated understanding of the gaming licensing process.</li>
  <li>Must possess sensitivity to Native American culture.</li>
  <li>Must be able to obtain and maintain a Gaming License.</li>
  <li>Excellent customer service skills and written and verbal communication. Demonstrated ability to communicate with guests, coworkers, state representatives, federal representatives, and the Casino’s entire  workforce in a professional and courteous manner.</li>
  <li>Demonstrated ability to write clear and accurate reports such as formal hearing decisions and orders.</li>
  <li>Proficiency with Microsoft Office programs.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>CONDITIONS OF EMPLOYMENT</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Must agree to be subject to the Tribe’s Controlled Substance and Alcohol Testing Policy to include pre-employment screening.</li>
  <li>Must pass background check and be eligible to obtain a level 1 gaming license.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>ADDITIONAL INFORMATION</p>
  <ul>
  <li>This is a temporary limited appointment by Tribal Council for the purpose of determining an administrative hearing, in line with the Snoqualmie Tribal Gaming Act.</li>
  <li>Compensation is not to exceed $30.29 per hour.</li>
  </ul>

  <p>TRIBAL PREFERENCE<br>
  The Snoqualmie Indian Tribe follows the Tribal Hiring Preference as outlined in tribal policies, which allows hiring preference to qualified individuals that are enrolled in a federally recognized Indian tribe.</p>

The Snoqualmie Tribe is seeking applicants for a temporary position as a Conflict Commissioner for the Snoqualmie Gaming Commission. The position, which offers a salary not exceeding $30.29 per hour, involves regulating gaming activities on Tribal lands and ensuring compliance with Tribal, State, and Federal laws. Essential functions include interpreting gaming laws, attending meetings with the Tribal Council, and maintaining high ethical standards.

Applicants must be enrolled members of the Snoqualmie Indian Tribe, possess a high school diploma (a bachelor’s degree is preferred), and have relevant experience in regulated operations. A valid driver's license and the ability to obtain a gaming license are also required. Candidates should demonstrate strong communication skills and an understanding of Native American culture.

The role is a temporary appointment intended for administrative hearings under the Snoqualmie Tribal Gaming Act. Applicants must pass a background check and agree to the Tribe's substance testing policy. The workplace is an office environment with low to moderate noise levels. The Tribe gives hiring preference to qualified individuals from federally recognized tribes.
