As temperatures in King County are expected to drop below 30°F, the King County Regional Homelessness Authority (KCRHA) is implementing Tier 3 Protocols starting at 5:00 PM on January 22, lasting through January 24, with a potential extension based on severe weather conditions. This protocol represents the highest level of response, involving coordination with transportation partners to establish emergency warming centers and overnight shelters, particularly for vulnerable populations such as the medically fragile or disabled. Local city halls and other city-owned properties may also be utilized as warming centers. The Westside Neighbors Shelter will increase its capacity to 45 cots and add an overnight staff member during this period. Once the severe weather advisory concludes, operations will revert to the typical winter arrangement of 35 cots and two staff members. The community is encouraged to support these efforts by donating food and warm clothing, which can be dropped off at the American Legion Hall or ordered via an Amazon wish list.
