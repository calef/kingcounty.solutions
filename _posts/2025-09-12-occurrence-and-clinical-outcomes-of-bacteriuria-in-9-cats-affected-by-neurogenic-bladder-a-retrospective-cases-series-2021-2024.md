A study evaluated bacteriuria in nine paraplegic cats with neurogenic bladder (NB) due to chronic spinal cord injury. Medical records from the Veterinary Teaching Hospital of the University of Bari, covering January 2021 to May 2024, were analyzed. All cats experienced at least one episode of bacteriuria, with a total of 18 positive urine cultures identified during 27 clinical evaluations. These included 12 cases of subclinical bacteriuria (SB) and five urinary tract infections (UTIs). Notably, none of the SB cases received antibiotic treatment, and only two cats progressed from SB to UTI. The findings indicate a high prevalence of bacteriuria among cats with NB, with SB being the most common form. The study suggests that, in the absence of comorbidities, a conservative management approach—monitoring SB and using antibiotics only for UTIs—might be suitable for this population.
