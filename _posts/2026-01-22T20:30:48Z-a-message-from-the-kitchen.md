As cold weather increases the number of people in need, Chef Tim has identified essential items that the kitchen is running low on. The list includes paper towels, paper plates, bowls, coffee cups, potatoes, rice, ground coffee, powdered creamer, pancake mix, oatmeal, syrup, sugars, cup noodles, hot cocoa packets, corn starch, and large chicken bouillon. The community is encouraged to donate these items to support those facing hunger. Donations can be dropped off at the American Legion Hall located at 3620 SW Alaska St. during specified hours: 7-11 a.m. or 6-9 p.m., with the hall closed for a break from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. Alternatively, contributions can be made through an Amazon Wish List. The kitchen appreciates the community's support in helping to feed hungry neighbors.
