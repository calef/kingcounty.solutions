Friends of Youth emphasizes the importance of community building through shared spaces. They recently opened the large conference room at their Kirkland Youth Services Center (KYSC) to local partners for two collaborative events. The first event hosted Eastside Pathways, where regional leaders and partners came together to discuss community issues and foster connections. The focus was on collaboration, equity, and shared goals. These gatherings aim to strengthen community ties and support the well-being of all members. By providing a venue for these discussions, Friends of Youth encourages a culture of care and cooperation among various organizations and individuals in the region. The initiative reflects their commitment to creating an inclusive environment where everyone can come together for positive change.
