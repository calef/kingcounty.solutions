A networking social for eating disorder clinicians and professionals will take place on Jan. 15, 2026, from 5:30 to 8:00 p.m. at Opal: Food+Body Wisdom in Seattle, Washington. Attendees can access the building by ringing the doorbell labeled Opal, which will prompt staff to unlock the door and escort guests to the sixth floor. Paid parking is available nearby, and the venue is within walking distance from the UDistrict Light Rail station. The event will feature vegan and gluten-free soup with various toppings, as well as a selection of non-alcoholic and alcoholic beverages. An RSVP is requested to ensure sufficient food for all guests. The event welcomes everyone interested in supporting those struggling with eating disorders.
