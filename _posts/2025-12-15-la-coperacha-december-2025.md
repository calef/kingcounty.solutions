Support El Centro de la Raza through the #LightTheWorld Giving Machine at Alderwood Mall until January 1. This unique vending machine allows people to donate essential items instead of snacks. Users can choose from various items that directly benefit children, youth, and families in the community. Donations can include books, winter coats, holiday meals, school supplies, and soccer balls, each representing warmth, learning, nourishment, and joy during the winter months. The Giving Machine ensures that 100% of donations go to trusted organizations, including El Centro de la Raza, to provide meaningful aid. This initiative encourages individuals to make a tangible difference in their community through simple acts of generosity. If you visit Alderwood Mall, consider stopping by the Giving Machine to contribute and help light the world with your kindness.
