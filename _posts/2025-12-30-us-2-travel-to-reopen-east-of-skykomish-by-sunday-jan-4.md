  class=\"field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden
  field--item\"><h2>Pilot car operations also continue east of Stevens Pass Resort
  and a detour around Tumwater Canyon remains in place</h2><p>SKYKOMISH – Another
  section of US 2 is reopening for limited travel following historic storms and damage.
  The new section – providing west-side Stevens Pass access between Skykomish and
  the Stevens Pass Resort – will reopen by Sunday, Jan. 4.</p><p>This section of the
  road, from milepost 50 to 64 on the west side of the pass, will be open in both
  directions. However, the road will close each night at 6 p.m. at the Stevens Pass
  Resort, because access on the east side of the pass is still limited to daytime
  and led by a pilot car.</p><p>Except for the Christmas Day holiday, crews have worked
  around the clock clearing debris and doing roadway and streambed repairs. The progress
  allowed inspections to take place which determined the road and bridges between
  Skykomish and Stevens Pass can handle limited travel.</p><p>This new section reopening
  to traffic is in addition to pilot car-led access on the east side of the closure
  that began Monday, Dec. 29, between the Stevens Pass Resort (milepost 64) and Mill
  Creek Road (milepost 71). A detour around a third section of damaged highway, around
  Tumwater Canyon using Chumstick Highway, also remains in place.</p><p>“We know how
  important US 2 access is for the success and vitality of communities and businesses
  and we are pleased that the quick work from our maintenance crews, inspectors and
  our contractor will allow us to open the road in a limited capacity,” said Brian
  Nielsen, WSDOT Northwest Region administrator. “We will continue to repair the damage
  along US 2 from this historic storm.”</p><h3>Be prepared and plan ahead</h3><p>US
  2 is an active work zone and delays are to be expected. Travelers should consider
  other cross-state travel routes, like Interstate 90 or US 12, when possible. Contractor
  crews continue repair work on the east side of Stevens Pass Resort through Tumwater
  Canyon. Travelers are asked to adjust to conditions during these limited reopenings,
  including following all signs and directions, not passing the pilot car or cutting
  the queue and planning ahead for winter weather.</p><h3>Timeline</h3><p>Several
  sections of US 2 were damaged and closed during the recent, historic storm and flooding.
  Crews have worked to clear and repair damaged roads and have reopened sections of
  US 2 as soon as it was safe to do so.</p><ul><li>Wednesday, Dec. 10: WSDOT crews
  closed the highway between Index and Coles Corner (mileposts 35-85) due to multiple
  washouts and mudslides.</li><li>Thursday, Dec. 11: The closure was extended to include
  Tumwater Canyon on the east side (milepost 99) of the pass.</li><li>Friday, Dec.
  12: Crews were able to clear the roadway to Skykomish (milepost 50) on the west
  side.</li><li>Thursday Dec. 18: Emergency repairs began on US 2 over Stevens Pass
  between mileposts 58 and 70.</li><li>Saturday, Dec. 20: Emergency work to remove
  debris on US 2 east of Skykomish began.</li><li>Monday, Dec. 29: A pilot car began
  limited travel on US 2 between milepost 71 to the resort at milepost 64.</li></ul><p>Real-time
  traffic information is available on the WSDOT <a href=\"/travel/travel-information-phone\"
  target=\"_blank\">mobile app</a> and the <a href=\"https://wsdot.com/Travel/Real-time/Map/\"
  target=\"_blank\">real-time travel map</a>. Current weather and highway conditions
  are posted on the WSDOT <a href=\"https://wsdot.com/travel/real-time/mountainpasses\">mountain

title: US 2 travel to reopen east of Skykomish by Sunday, Jan. 4
topic_titles: []
---

US 2 is reopening for limited travel between Skykomish and the Stevens Pass Resort starting Sunday, Jan. 4. This section, from milepost 50 to 64, will be accessible in both directions but will close nightly at 6 p.m. due to ongoing limited access on the east side, where a pilot car is guiding traffic. Crews have worked continuously, except on Christmas Day, to clear debris and repair the road following significant storm damage. Inspections confirmed that the road and bridges can support limited travel.

In addition to this reopening, pilot car-led access has been in place since Dec. 29 for a section on the east side of Stevens Pass. A detour remains in effect around Tumwater Canyon using Chumstick Highway. Travelers should prepare for delays and consider alternative routes, such as Interstate 90 or US 12. WSDOT emphasizes the importance of following signs and instructions as repairs continue. The highway had been closed since early December due to multiple washouts and mudslides caused by historic storms.
