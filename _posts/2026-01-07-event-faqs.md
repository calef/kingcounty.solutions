  Kids benefit?</strong></span><br>\nYour registration fee for the RBC Race for the
  Kids benefits Boyer Children’s Clinic. As the largest provider of Early Support
  for Infants and Toddlers (ESIT) services in Seattle, Boyer is recognized as one
  of the region’s premier resources for children with neuromuscular disorders or developmental
  delays. For nearly 85 years, our highly-skilled experts have provided thousands
  of families with support, guidance, and hope for a brighter future.</p>\n<p><span><strong>Is
  my donation tax deductible?</strong></span><br>\nYES! Boyer Children’s Clinic is
  a 501(c)3 tax-exempt organization and your donation is tax-deductible within the
  guidelines of U.S. law. To claim a donation as a deduction on your U.S. taxes, please
  keep your email donation receipt as your official record. We’ll send it to you upon
  successful completion of your donation.</p>\n<p><span><strong>How can my company

  are the race routes?</strong><br>\n</span>We have 3 distances for the Race, each
  is a different route.</p>\n<p><strong>5K</strong> – Head south from the start line
  and follow the signs.  Out and backs, a U-turn and a few loops create this fun and
  varied 5K distance.   This course has paved paths as well as dirt and gravel.  There
  will be plenty of signs on the course to guide you along the course.</p>\n<p><strong>10K</strong>
  – Two loops of the 5K route.  Pass by the start line once on your second loop and
  have your friends and family cheer you on!</p>\n<p><strong>Family Fun Run/Walk/Roll</strong>
  – Approximate distance is 1.5K. This course is paved and hard pack gravel providing
  easy access for the rollers. Head south from the start line, U-turn and head north
  eventually looping back to your finish line.</p>\n<p><a>VIEW 10K/5K COURSE MAP</a>

  USATF Certified?</strong></span><br>\nNo they are not.</p>\n<p><span><strong>Are
  the races timed?</strong></span><br>\nThe 10K and 5K races are timed and you will
  receive a timing chip with your race packet.  You will receive a split time as well
  if you are running the 10K.  The Family Fun Event is not timed, however you will
  still receive a race packet.</p>\n<p><strong><span>What support is on the course
  for runners? </span><br>\n</strong>The RBC Race for the Kids will have water stations
  and plentiful course signage to aid in your race day needs.  The course will be
  staffed with volunteers who can answer questions, root you on, and guide you.  After
  the race, there will be food and nutritional support both complimentary and for
  purchase.</p>\n<p><strong><span>How much is the registration fee?</span></strong></p>\n<p><strong>Before
  May 1st (Early Bird pricing):</strong> 10K/5K = $45; Family Fun Event= $25</p>\n<p><strong>May
  1st – June 5th:</strong> 10K/5K = $55; Family Fun Event= $35</p>\n<p><strong>Day
  of Pricing (June 6th):</strong> 10K/5K = $65; Family Fun Event= $45</p>\n<p><em>We
  would like all members of our community to participate, regardless of finances.
  Please email <a>kimberly.mcdonald@boyercc.org</a> to request a scholarship.</em></p>\n<p><span><strong>When/where
  can I pick up my race packet?</strong></span><br>\nRace packets (including bib numbers/timing
  chips) will be available to pick up on both Thursday, June 4th and Friday, June
  5th from 9am – 4pm at our Montlake Clinic, or on race day at Magnuson Park beginning
  at 7:30am.</p>\n<p><strong><span>What is included with my registration fee? </span><br>\n</strong>Included
  with all registrations:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Sponsor swag</li>\n<li>Finishing medal</li>\n<li>Entry

  for the event?</span><br>\n</strong>Without your support and effort, RBC Race for

  the fun, <a>please complete this form</a>.</p>\n<p><span><strong>Will there be a
  sponsor/vendor fair?</strong> </span><br>\nYes!  Our Family Fun Fair will be open
  from 9 am – 11 am the day of the race.  Sponsors will be at the Fair to offer products,
  guidance and assistance for runners of all distances.  Some sponsors will have free
  merchandise for race participants.  In addition, there will be a raffle (all race
  participants will be entered to win) for prizes.</p>\n<p><span><strong>What activities
  will be at the Race for the Kids?</strong> </span><br>\nDuring the Family Fun Run,
  there will be activities for the kids as they run/walk/roll the course.  The Family
  Fun Fair will be available from 9am – 11am, after the completion of the Family Fun
  Run.  The Family Fun Fair is completely free and offers a variety of activities. 
  Balloon twisters, Face Painting, Cornhole, Arts and Crafts, a scavenger hunt and
  more!</p>\n<p><span><strong>Will there be food at the race?</strong> </span><br>\nFood
  will be available for purchase via food trucks as well as a coffee vendor.</p>\n<p><span><strong>Can
  I change my distance?</strong></span><br>\nYES! You can <a>email us</a> by May 23rd,
  or notify the registration booth day-of to make the change. If you are changing
  to a more expensive event you can pay the difference on the day of race at the registration
  booth. No refunds will be given for changing to a shorter distance race.</p>\n<p><span><strong>May
  I bring my dog?</strong></span><br>\nThis is a family-friendly event and we welcome

The RBC Race for the Kids will take place on June 6 at Magnuson Park, benefiting Boyer Children’s Clinic, which provides early support for infants and toddlers with developmental delays. Registration fees are tax-deductible. The event features three race distances: a 5K, a 10K, and a Family Fun Run approximately 1.5K long. The 5K and 10K courses are not USATF certified but are timed, while the Family Fun event is not timed. Water stations and volunteers will support participants throughout the race.

Registration fees before May 1 are $45 for the 10K/5K and $25 for the Family Fun event. Fees increase after that date and are higher on race day. Participants can request scholarships if needed. Race packets can be picked up on June 4 and 5 or on race day starting at 7:30 a.m. The Family Fun Fair will be open from 9 to 11 a.m. on race day, featuring activities for children and local vendors. Dogs are welcome if well-behaved. Parking is available at E4, E1, and E2 lots. For questions, contact Boyer Children’s Clinic.
