  <p><strong>Wrapping Up a Joyful Month at Our Christmas Tree Lot</strong> </p>



  <p>This December has been full of joy, community, and impact at our North Parking Lot Christmas Tree Lot! Every tree purchased supports El Centro de la Raza’s vital programs, helping more than 22,000 children, adults, and families each year.</p>



  <p>The lot has been alive with Christmas Tree Lot staff, families, children, neighbors, and daily volunteers, all coming together to celebrate the season while giving back. Adding to the festive spirit, Lita-Rosita Food Cart from our Business Opportunity Center’s Food Incubator Program served delicious tamales, delighting everyone who stopped by.</p>



  <p>Thank you to all who joined us this month—your support makes a real difference and strengthens our community.</p>

  <p><strong>Celebrating Community and Tradition: Las Posadas &amp; Christmas Market </strong></p>



  <p>What a magical evening at Plaza Roberto Maestas! Our Las Posadas &amp; Christmas Market on December 12 brought together families, neighbors, and community members for a festive celebration full of culture, music, and joy.</p>



  <p>The night came alive with a special UW Drama performance, inspired by the life and artistry of Frida Kahlo, followed by the playful Calaca Clown Show. and a soulful musical performance by the José Martí All-Star Singers.</p>



  <p>Guests enjoyed the vibrant market, connected with local artisans, and shared in the warmth of community traditions. Thank you to everyone who joined us—your presence made the evening truly unforgettable and brought the spirit of the season to life at Plaza Roberto Maestas.</p>

  <p>Every purchase prints a receipt, and 100% of your donation goes straight to the organizations providing the items. You have all the way until January to give and make an impact this season.</p>

  <p><strong>Bringing Joy This Holiday Season: Our Annual Toy Drive</strong></p>



  <p>Rising food and living costs are making the holidays harder than ever for families, which is why El Centro de la Raza’s Annual Toy Drive is so important this year. A single donated toy can bring joy, dignity, and a moment of celebration to a child who might otherwise go without.</p>



  <p>We’re collecting new, unwrapped toys and gifts for children ages 0–17 until December 14. Drop-off locations were spread throughout Beacon Hill, Seattle, South King County, and at the El Centro Skate Rink, making it easy for our community to give back.</p>



  <p>Help ensure every child in our community feels seen, supported, and celebrated this holiday season—your gift makes a lasting difference.</p>

In December 2025, El Centro de la Raza hosted a vibrant Christmas Tree Lot, where each tree purchase supported essential programs benefiting over 22,000 community members annually. The lot served as a festive gathering place for families and volunteers, with local food vendor Lita-Rosita Food Cart offering tamales to enhance the holiday spirit. 

On December 12, the organization held its annual Las Posadas & Christmas Market at Plaza Roberto Maestas, featuring performances inspired by Frida Kahlo, the Calaca Clown Show, and the José Martí All-Star Singers. Attendees enjoyed connecting with local artisans and celebrating community traditions.

Additionally, El Centro de la Raza partnered with The Giving Machine Seattle, providing a unique opportunity for holiday giving, with 100% of donations going directly to local organizations. The Annual Toy Drive aimed to collect new, unwrapped toys for children aged 0-17, addressing the increasing challenges families face during the holiday season. Donations were accepted until December 14 at various locations, ensuring every child could experience joy and celebration during this time.
