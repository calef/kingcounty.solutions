The dining menu for the Ballard Senior Center from February 9 to 13 features a variety of meals.

On Monday, the menu includes open face hot turkey served with mashed potatoes, buttered green beans, and fruit. Tuesday offers jambalaya paired with a marinated veggie salad, cornbread, fruit, and dessert. Wednesday features spinach and feta spanakopita, soup, and fruit.

On Thursday, diners can enjoy a Cubano sandwich with citrus slaw, fruit, and dessert. The week concludes with rosemary chicken salad, soup, and fruit on Friday. Additionally, there is a Wednesday night dinner that requires an RSVP, featuring chicken Marsala, mashed potatoes, veggies, and dessert.
