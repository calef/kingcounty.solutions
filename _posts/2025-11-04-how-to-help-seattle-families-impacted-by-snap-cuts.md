Local restaurants, organizations, and community members in Seattle are mobilizing to support families affected by cuts to the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP). These grassroots efforts aim to ensure that everyone who needs food can access it. Various initiatives, including food drives and community meals, are being organized to provide assistance to those struggling with food insecurity. The community is coming together to create resources and support networks to help families navigate these challenging times. Efforts are focused on raising awareness and providing immediate food relief to those impacted by the SNAP cuts. Through collaboration and support, locals are working to ensure that no one goes hungry.
