Veterans seeking employment can explore various job listings for the week of Jan. 12, 2026, showcasing opportunities across multiple locations. Employers recognize the unique skills veterans bring, including adaptability, leadership, and a strong work ethic, which enhance workplace culture and innovation. Available positions include a Product Management Manager at Wells Fargo in Phoenix, a Lead Software Engineer in Wilmington, an Account Executive at GuidePoint Security in Tampa, and a Field Operations Manager in Indianapolis. Other roles span lab assistance, security, HR, and operations management in various cities.

The U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) also lists clinical and non-clinical positions nationwide, including roles for Peer Specialists, Primary Care Physicians, and Registered Nurses. Veterans can find additional resources and opportunities through the VA to translate their military service into valuable skills for federal employment. Career fairs in Newport News and Norfolk, Virginia, are also scheduled weekly to connect veterans with educational and employment resources.
