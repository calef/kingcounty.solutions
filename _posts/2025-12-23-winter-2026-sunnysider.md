The Winter 2026 edition of the Sunnysider from the Wallingford Community Senior Center highlights various activities and programs for seniors. The center offers fitness classes, arts and crafts, and social events to engage the community. Notable events include weekly exercise sessions tailored for different fitness levels, art workshops for creative expression, and regular social gatherings to foster connections among seniors.

Special programs are also featured, such as informational seminars on health and wellness, as well as opportunities for volunteering. The center emphasizes the importance of staying active and connected during the winter months.

Participants are encouraged to sign up for events in advance due to limited space. The Sunnysider serves as a vital resource for seniors in Wallingford, promoting both physical health and social interaction. The center aims to create a welcoming environment for all seniors looking to enhance their winter experience.
