The City of Bellevue will host its Martin Luther King Jr. Health Fair on Jan. 19 from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Crossroads Bellevue. In partnership with the Bellevue Alumnae Chapter of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., this year’s event focuses on improving health outcomes and building community connections. Activities for all ages include health and wellness demonstrations, storytelling by the Tooth Fairy, and a live jazz performance by Sonny Byers. Delta Sigma Theta members will present a health and wellness session and lead a line dancing demonstration. Vendors will represent health and wellness organizations, small businesses, and cultural groups. This marks the 22nd anniversary of Bellevue’s collaboration with Delta Sigma Theta for this event. The fair is free and open to the public, offering a chance for community members to engage with health experts as they work toward a healthier 2026. For additional information, contact diversity@bellevuewa.gov or call 425-452-7855. Requests for alternate formats or accommodations should be made at least 48 hours in advance.
