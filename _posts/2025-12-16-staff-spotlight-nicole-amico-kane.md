Nicole Amico Kane serves as the Director of Care Management at Aging Wisdom. She began her career in child and family services but experienced burnout after a few years. A chance opportunity led her to work for a hospice, which shifted her focus to the aging population. This experience ignited her passion for supporting older adults and their families. Kane's background in social work has equipped her with valuable skills that she applies in her current role, where she is dedicated to improving care for seniors and enhancing their quality of life.
