Food serves as more than just nourishment; it represents memory, dignity, and hope for young people like Sam. During his first holiday without a home, the shared meals and laughter at Friends of Youth’s Willows Young Adult Shelter provided him with a sense of belonging. Sweet treats from the farmers’ market were more than snacks; they created cherished moments. Sam lived at the shelter for ten months, facing challenges due to his disability that made finding work difficult and navigating benefits overwhelming.

As the year comes to a close, there is an opportunity to support young people like Sam by giving gifts that create lasting memories. Donations can help provide essential items and experiences that contribute to their well-being and happiness. It’s not too late to give a gift that makes a difference in a young person's life. Through contributions, community members can help ensure that these individuals feel valued and connected, creating a brighter future and fostering hope.
