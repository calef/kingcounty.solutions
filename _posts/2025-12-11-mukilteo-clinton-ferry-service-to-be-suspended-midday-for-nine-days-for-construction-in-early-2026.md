Washington State Ferries will suspend service on the Mukilteo/Clinton ferry route for nine days in early 2026 to conduct in-water construction at the Mukilteo terminal. The service will be interrupted during midday for four hours on specific dates: Jan. 6, 8, 9, 13, 16 and Feb. 3, 4, 6, 9. The Clinton route will halt from 8:30 a.m. to 1 p.m., while the Mukilteo route will pause from 9 a.m. to 1:30 p.m.

Additionally, overnight sailings will be adjusted from Jan. 19-23, 26-30, and Feb. 16-20, with the last sailing from Clinton at 11:30 p.m. and from Mukilteo at 12:15 a.m. Weather conditions, such as fog, may alter these plans, as workers require clear visibility for safety and marine mammal monitoring.

Washington State Ferries will host two drop-in sessions for the public to ask questions about the project on Dec. 18 and Dec. 30, 2025. The ferry system, part of the Washington State Department of Transportation, is the largest in the U.S., serving millions of passengers annually.
