Boyer Children's Clinic has made a significant impact on the family of Ben, who was struggling with speech development at 14 months old. After a quick referral from their pediatrician, Ben's family received an evaluation and began in-home therapy sessions with speech-language pathologist Kalli. Now almost 18 months old, Ben has shown steady progress and enjoys his time with Kalli, who provides helpful strategies for the family to support his development.

Boyer stands out for its affordability, making quality care accessible without overwhelming financial burdens. The family appreciates the support from the entire Boyer team, including a dedicated coordinator who checks in regularly. They feel that Boyer cares for the whole family, not just the child in therapy. Ben's mother, Stephanie, expresses deep gratitude for the clinic's care, responsiveness, and commitment, noting the positive difference it has made in both Ben's development and the family's confidence as parents.
