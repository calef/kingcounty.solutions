A 28-year-old woman was arrested in Tukwila, Washington, for assaulting two state troopers and a Seattle police officer. The incident occurred on December 22 around 5:45 p.m. when officers responded to a report of a disturbance. Upon arrival, the woman kicked the troopers and spat on a police officer. She was taken into custody and faces multiple charges, including assault. The officers involved were not seriously injured during the altercation. The police continue to investigate the incident.
