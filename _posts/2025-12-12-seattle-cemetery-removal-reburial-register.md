Denny Park, known as Seattle’s first city park, was originally the city’s first municipal cemetery. Burials took place there from the 1860s until 1884, when Mayor Henry G. Struve signed Ordinance 571, transforming Seattle Cemetery into a public park. The land was donated by David and Louisa Denny. Following the ordinance, city commissioners were appointed to oversee the conversion process.
