Veterans are encouraged to explore job opportunities available for the week of Dec. 15, 2025. Employers recognize the valuable skills and work ethic that Veterans bring to the workplace. Active job listings include positions such as a Senior Workers Compensation Claim Representative in Diamond Bar, CA, and a Project Manager in Fort Meade, MD, among others across various locations. Notable roles also include a Heavy Equipment Operator in Jessup, MD, and a Security Concierge Officer in Sparks, NV.

Additionally, the Veterans Affairs (VA) offers a range of clinical and non-clinical positions, including a Registered Nurse in Montgomery, AL, and a Pharmacy Technician in Tulsa, OK. The Veterans Canteen Service is hiring for roles like Retail Sales Associate in Phoenix, AZ, and Cook positions in various locations. Career fairs are scheduled in Newport News and Norfolk, VA, providing further opportunities for Veterans to connect with potential employers. Veterans should verify job details with the respective organizations, as most listings close within 7 to 30 days after posting.
