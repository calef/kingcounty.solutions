Sound Transit is transitioning to vinyl seat inserts on Link light rail vehicles to enhance cleanliness and comfort for riders. This change, which began in November 2024, follows extensive feedback from passengers who expressed concerns about the appearance and maintenance of fabric seats. A pilot program conducted from March to June 2024 involved 10 vehicles with vinyl seats and a control group with fabric seats. Over 2,300 riders participated in surveys, and results indicated a strong preference for vinyl, which scored an average satisfaction rating of 4.06 out of 5, compared to 3.08 for cloth seats.

Riders highlighted cleanliness as a top priority, with many feeling more comfortable on the cleaner-looking vinyl seats. The pilot also assessed maintenance efficiency, revealing that vinyl seats require about 12.5% less cleaning time than fabric seats. Additionally, vinyl seats are less prone to absorbing liquids, reducing the need for replacements. As part of the rollout, priority seating areas will be updated with distinct ADA logos for better visibility. Sound Transit plans to expand vinyl seating across the fleet, reflecting a commitment to improving the passenger experience based on direct feedback.
