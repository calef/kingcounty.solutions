  <p>Once again, USAA has teamed up with Call of Duty Endowment to offer a free Call of Duty Endowment (C.O.D.E.) Legacy: Tracer Pack for Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 and at the launch of Season 1 in Call of Duty: Warzone to military service members and Veterans. It’s just one of the ways we can say thank you for your service.</p>



  <p>Once you click the link for redemption below, you will be redirected to a GovX account sign-in page to claim your pack. While supplies last. Quantities limited. <a>Access the official rules.</a></p>

  <h2>Welcome to Season 1 Reloaded</h2>



  <p>The Call of Duty Mid-Season update (Jan. 8, 2026) brings more maps, modes, challenges, Loadout items and additional content across Multiplayer, Zombies and Endgame, including a nuclear collaboration with Fallout involving themed LTMs, the new Deathclaw Hunt Limited Time World Event in Endgame, and the Quantum Exchange Event, bringing epic rewards straight from the Wasteland.</p>



  <h2>About Call of Duty Endowment</h2>



  <p>The Call of Duty Endowment uses a performance-driven approach to fund organizations delivering the highest standards of quality and cost-efficient Veteran job placement services. Beyond funding, C.O.D.E. partners with grantees to provide advice and support aimed at maximizing their impact.</p>



  <p>In 2024, the organization’s average cost to place a Veteran in a job was $628. This is made possible by a commitment to high-performance standards that are measured on a quarterly basis, including:</p>



  <ul>
  <li>Cost per placement</li>



  <li>Average starting salary.</li>



  <li>6-month and 12-month retention rates.</li>



  <li>Full-time vs. part-time employment.</li>
  </ul>



  <p>In addition to supporting its grantees, the Endowment is committed to highlighting high performance in the Veteran employment space through its Seal of Distinction program, which provides both a $30,000 prize and national recognition to winners of the award.</p>

title: USAA and Call of Duty Endowment offer free gaming Legacy Tracer Pack for Veterans
topic_titles: []
---

USAA has partnered with the Call of Duty Endowment to offer a free Legacy: Tracer Pack for Call of Duty: Black Ops 7 and the launch of Season 1 in Call of Duty: Warzone to military service members and veterans. This initiative aims to thank veterans for their service. To claim the pack, users must click a link that redirects them to a GovX account sign-in page. Supplies are limited.

The Call of Duty Endowment focuses on funding organizations that deliver high-quality, cost-efficient job placement services for veterans. In 2024, the average cost to place a veteran in a job was $628. The organization measures performance on various metrics, including cost per placement, average starting salary, and retention rates. It also recognizes outstanding organizations through its Seal of Distinction program, which offers a $30,000 prize and national recognition to award winners.
