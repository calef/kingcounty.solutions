feed_content: "<h4><em>“They helped me find my purpose.”</em></h4> \n<p>During David's
  childhood, he grew up around a lot of negative influences. This led to him becoming
  a \"product of my environment.\"</p>  \n<p>“And it wasn't till I was about 40 that
  I had an epiphany and I and I I was just fed up with the lifestyle. I just wanted
  to change.”</p> \n<p>Tired of battling addiction, David came to the Bread of Life
  Mission seeking a fresh start.</p> \n<p>“My biggest challenge before coming to the
  bread of Life mission was overcoming my addiction,” David says. \"I was living in
  Bremerton and I was staying with my mom and I was four days into a detox, like trying
  to quit. And I was really sick and nobody wanted to see me like that. So my mom
  started calling around for places that could take me in and help me and a better
  life. I took the ferry over that night and I've been here since November 19th. <strong>That
  phone call made by his mom led to David finding stability and sobriety at our mission.</strong></p>
  \n<p><strong>“They they showed me how to deal with a lot of things - how to face
  things that I couldn't before.”</strong></p> \n<p>At a time when he didn’t know
  where to turn for help, David is grateful that the Mission was there for him. “They're
  giving me a job. They've they've made me family. They help me find myself &amp;
  my purpose.” he says.</p> \n<p>Through nightly chapel services at the Mission, David
  found hope in a challenging time of his life. “My relationship with God and Jesus
  is like 180° compared to how it was. I've always been a God-fearing, God-loving
  person. I just diligently seek a relationship with Him the right way.”</p> \n<p>Thanks
  to the guidance of Mission staff, David got sober and was hired on at the Mission
  as our head chef! \"Two weeks ago, I was volunteering cooking breakfast every morning
  from 3:00 AM to 8:00 AM and then going to work at 9:00 AM in Bremerton. And just
  recently, they hired me as their full time chef.\"</p> \n<p><strong>Your generous
  support has filled David’s life with love, encouragement, and most of all, hope.

David, who faced numerous challenges during his childhood, sought a fresh start at the Bread of Life Mission after years of battling addiction. At 40, feeling fed up with his lifestyle, he decided to change. With support from his mother, who reached out for help, David arrived at the Mission on November 19th. There, he found stability and sobriety. The Mission staff taught him how to confront his issues, helping him rediscover his purpose.

David expressed gratitude for the support he received, which included employment as the head chef after he volunteered in the kitchen. He now works full-time, cooking breakfast early each morning. Through nightly chapel services, he strengthened his relationship with God and experienced a profound transformation in his faith. David credits the Mission for turning his life around, stating he was lost before but feels saved now. The encouragement and love he received have filled his life with hope, demonstrating the impact of community support in overcoming personal struggles.
