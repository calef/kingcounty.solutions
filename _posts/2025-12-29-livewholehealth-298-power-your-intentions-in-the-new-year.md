  <p>Along with each new year comes the temptation to make New Year’s resolutions (fixed outcome goals). But what if we leave behind this all-or-nothing approach to finding health and well-being? </p>



  <h2>Who do you want to be in 2026?</h2>



  <p>This year, instead of making a new year’s resolution, imagine who you want to be in the world and how you want to show up each day. This type of intention-setting sparks growth as we align our goals with our personal values. </p>



  <h2>Superfood salad for the new you!</h2>



  <p>Empower your food and drink choices in 2026 with the “New You” Salad. For its base, this superfood salad uses Brussels sprouts. There’s no need to boil or roast these raw, shredded Brussels sprouts, which are at peak freshness from late autumn throughout the winter. Unlike the more tender leafy greens, Brussels sprouts are frost-hardy, and the frost even adds sweetness to the sprouts by changing starches into sugars. </p>



  <p>(If you don’t have Brussels sprouts on hand, this salad can be adapted by using lettuce instead.) </p>



  <p>For extra crunch, the recipe includes carrots, almonds and edamame (a whole soy food that is an excellent source of protein). Savory Parmesan cheese is balanced with the sweetness of honey, dates and cranberries. With its powerhouse ingredients, the “New You” salad can brighten any winter’s day.</p>

  <p>Yield: 4 servings</p>



  <p> Serving Size: 8 oz. or 1 cup</p>



  <h2>Ingredients</h2>



  <h3>Salad</h3>



  <p>• 8 oz. bag of Brussels sprouts</p>



  <p>• 1 cup kale</p>



  <p>• 1 cup of shredded carrots</p>



  <p>• ½ cup of frozen edamame, thawed</p>



  <p>• ¼ cup of dried cranberries</p>



  <p>• ¼ cup of dates</p>



  <p>• ¼ cup of sliced almonds</p>



  <p>• 2 tablespoons of Parmesan cheese, grated</p>



  <h3>Vinaigrette Dressing</h3>



  <p>• 2 tablespoons of olive oil</p>



  <p>• 1 tablespoon of milled flaxseed</p>



  <p>• ¼ cup of lemon juice</p>



  <p>• 2 tablespoons of honey, locally sourced</p>



  <p>• 1 tablespoon of chopped parsley</p>



  <p>• Salt and pepper to taste</p>



  <h3>Instructions</h3>



  <p>1. Add Brussels sprouts in the food processor and pulse until shredded but not minced. Pour into a bowl.</p>



  <p>2. Chop dates by hand and add to the bowl.</p>



  <p>3. To the bowl, add the kale, shredded carrots, sliced almonds, cranberries, edamame and Parmesan cheese. Mix well.</p>



  <p>4. Make the vinaigrette. In a small bowl add all the ingredients for the vinaigrette and whisk fully combined. Drizzle over your salad. Serve and enjoy!</p>

As the new year approaches, many people consider making resolutions. Instead of setting fixed outcome goals, individuals are encouraged to focus on intention-setting, which aligns personal values with their desired growth. This approach emphasizes envisioning who one wants to be in 2026 and how to embody that daily.

To support this journey, a recipe for a "New You" superfood salad is provided. The salad features raw, shredded Brussels sprouts, known for their sweetness in winter. Additional ingredients include kale, shredded carrots, thawed edamame, dried cranberries, dates, sliced almonds, and Parmesan cheese. The salad's vinaigrette is made with olive oil, milled flaxseed, lemon juice, honey, chopped parsley, salt, and pepper. This nutritious salad, which serves four, is designed to enhance well-being and can brighten up any winter day.

The recipe reinforces the idea of empowering food choices as part of a holistic approach to health and well-being.
