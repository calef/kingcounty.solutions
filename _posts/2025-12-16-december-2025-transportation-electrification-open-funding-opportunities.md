  <p><a>U.S. DOE – Advanced Technology Vehicles Manufacturing Loan Program</a></p>



  <p><strong>Qualifying applicants:  </strong>Manufacturers of advanced technology vehicles that achieve defined fuel economy targets and producers of components or materials that enhance eligible vehicles’ fuel economy and emissions performance.</p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Qualifying projects:  </strong>Must 1) manufacture eligible vehicles or components that are used in eligible vehicles, 2) build new facilities; reequip, modernize or expand existing facilities, and/or support engineering integration performed in the U.S. related to the manufacturing of eligible vehicles or components, 3) be located in the U.S., and 4) provide a reasonable prospect of repayment.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Total funding amount: </strong> $10 billion.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Application due date:</strong>  Rolling applications.</li>
  </ul>



  <p><a>WA Dept. of Commerce – WA EV Charging Program 2 (WAEVCP 2)</a></p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Who can apply: </strong>Public agencies, tribal entities, retail electric utilities, nonprofits, and private entities.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Qualifying projects: </strong> Funds multifamily residential, publicly accessible, fleet, or workplace charging.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Total funding amount:</strong> $19.4 million.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Application due date: </strong>December 18, 2025, by 4 p.m.</li>
  </ul>



  <p><a>WA Dept. of Ecology – Rails, Keels and Wheels</a></p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Who can apply: </strong>Organizations licensed in Washington state. State, local or tribal governments and related entities in Washington.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Qualifying projects:</strong> Available to transition switcher locomotives, tugboats, emergency vehicles, paratransit, and other medium-and heavy-duty local freight vehicles to all-electric models to reduce toxic diesel emissions from diesel vehicles, engines, and equipment in Washington.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Total funding amount: </strong>Approximately $25 million.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Application due date:</strong> January 8, 2026</li>
  </ul>



  <p><a>WA Dept. of Transportation – Zero-emission Vehicle Infrastructure Partnership grant</a></p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Who can apply:</strong> Nonprofit organizations, tribes, and state and local government agencies.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Qualifying projects:</strong> Installation of new or upgraded DCFC equipment, within 1 travel mile of a state route, and with a minimum of four networked charging ports delivering 150 kilowatts of simultaneous charging.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Total funding amount:</strong> Approximately $22.5 million.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Application due date:</strong> February 27, 2026.</li>
  </ul>

The U.S. Department of Energy is offering a $10 billion loan program for manufacturers of advanced technology vehicles that meet specific fuel economy standards. Eligible applicants include manufacturers of vehicles and related components located in the U.S. with a reasonable chance of repayment. Applications are accepted on a rolling basis.

Washington's Department of Commerce has launched the Washington EV Charging Program 2, which provides $19.4 million for multifamily, public, fleet, or workplace EV charging. Applications are due by December 18, 2025.

The Washington Department of Ecology's Rails, Keels and Wheels program offers around $25 million for organizations to convert switcher locomotives, tugboats, emergency vehicles, and other medium- and heavy-duty local freight vehicles to all-electric models, aimed at reducing diesel emissions. Applications close on January 8, 2026.

Additionally, the Washington Department of Transportation's Zero-emission Vehicle Infrastructure Partnership grant allocates approximately $22.5 million for installing or upgrading DC fast charging equipment near state routes. Applications are due by February 27, 2026. A complete list of funding opportunities is available in the EV Council Program Catalog.
