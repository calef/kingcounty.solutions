The City of Seattle is offering free mental health care for young people. Residents can call or text 988 for assistance at any time. In response to the 2024 Youth Mental Health Report from the City’s Innovation & Performance team, three city departments have collaborated to create a new youth wellness program called Community Hub. This program aims to address various needs expressed by the community, focusing on mental health support for young individuals. The initiative seeks to provide accessible resources and improve overall youth well-being in Seattle.
