The Seattle Department of Education & Early Learning will hold Community Conversations in December focused on renewing the Families, Education, Preschool & Promise Levy. These events aim to engage residents in discussions about investments in childcare and education. Families are encouraged to attend, as the events will feature free dinner and activities for children. Due to limited space, attendees must RSVP in advance to secure their spots.
