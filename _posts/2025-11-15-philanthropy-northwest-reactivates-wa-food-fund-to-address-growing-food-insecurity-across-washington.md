Philanthropy Northwest is reactivating the WA Food Fund to tackle increasing food insecurity in Washington. The state has seen a rise in food insecurity, with many families facing challenges in accessing federal nutrition assistance. The WA Food Fund aims to coordinate philanthropic efforts to deliver food to communities that are most in need. This initiative comes in response to the urgent need for support as many families experience uncertainty regarding their food sources.
