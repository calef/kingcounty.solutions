LCSNW Tri-Cities is enhancing access to youth mental health care with the addition of two Advanced Registered Nurse Practitioners, Dr. Alejandra Elizondo and Donna Braswell. The Outpatient Mental Health (OPMH) program aims to remove barriers and provide timely support for youth up to age 21. The program offers mental health assessments by licensed prescribers, individual, family, and group therapy utilizing evidence-based methods, and medication monitoring and education for safe treatment. Telehealth options are available to improve access and convenience for families. This integrated care model focuses on the unique needs, goals, and strengths of each youth, ensuring they receive comprehensive support. Families and referral partners can contact the Tri-Cities team at 509-735-6446 for more information or to make a referral.
