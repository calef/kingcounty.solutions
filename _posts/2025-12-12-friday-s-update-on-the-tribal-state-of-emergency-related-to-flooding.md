As of 1 p.m. on December 12, 2025, the Snoqualmie River Flood Phase has been downgraded to Phase 2, indicating minor flooding with some road closures and ongoing monitoring. All major highways and State Routes in the Snoqualmie Valley are open, although some smaller roads remain blocked as local governments continue cleanup efforts.

The Snoqualmie Casino & Hotel is providing warm, dry shelter for Tribal Members affected by the flooding. For reservations, contact Carrey Galloway at 425-677-0357 or Lonzell Maddock at 425-998-3677.

Tribally-owned structures are not underwater, and assessments of potential damage and structural integrity are underway. More information will be shared once Facilities teams complete their reviews.

For those who have experienced flooding in their homes, resources are available through the Housing Department. The King County Flood Warning Center can be reached at 206-296-8200 or 800-945-9263 for current river flow data and flood preparedness information. Non-emergency assistance for Tribal Members and staff is available at 425-765-6623. In case of a flood emergency, call 911.
