Teen Health Law is a project by the National Center for Youth Law aimed at improving young people's access to healthcare. It offers detailed information on minor consent and confidentiality laws in all 50 states. The initiative seeks to educate healthcare providers about the legal aspects that affect young patients. For more information, individuals can reach out to NCYL at health@youthlaw.org.
