  <p>King County Regional Homelessness Authority is activating <strong>Tier 2 Severe Weather Protocols on Saturday, January 17 through Monday, January 19, winding down the morning of Tuesday, January 20</strong>, with the possibility of extending in alignment with its <a>severe weather policy</a>. <br><br>During this time, Westside Neighbors Shelter will increase cot capacity to 45 people, and add one additional staff member to provide overnight supervision. When the Severe Weather advisory ends, the shelter will return to its normal winter operation of 35 cots and two overnight staff members.<br><br>During severe weather activations, the <strong>Severe Weather Shelter Navigation Hotline (206) 245-1026</strong> is available 8:00 am – 11:30 pm daily. Anyone can use this hotline for information on shelters and navigating to shelter locations. Families with children can call to be connected with a shelter and a ride to that location, as available.  <br><br>Donations of food and warm clothing are needed throughout the area. You can drop off donations for WSNS at the American Legion Hall, 3620 SW Alaska St, or send them via our <a>Amazon wish list</a>. <br><br>Thank you!</p>

King County Regional Homelessness Authority will activate Tier 2 Severe Weather Protocols from Saturday, January 17, to Monday, January 19, with a potential extension. Westside Neighbors Shelter will increase its cot capacity to 45 people and add an overnight staff member for supervision. After the severe weather advisory ends, the shelter will revert to its standard winter operations, accommodating 35 cots and two overnight staff members. During this period, the Severe Weather Shelter Navigation Hotline, available daily from 8 a.m. to 11:30 p.m. at (206) 245-1026, can be used for information on shelter locations and services. Families with children can receive assistance in finding a shelter and transportation to it, if available. Donations of food and warm clothing are needed in the area. Contributions can be dropped off at the American Legion Hall, located at 3620 SW Alaska St, or ordered through an Amazon wish list.
