The job market is rapidly evolving due to advancements in generative artificial intelligence and a shift towards greener practices, with an estimated 170 million new jobs expected by 2030, alongside the displacement of 92 million positions. To remain competitive, individuals need to adapt to emerging skills that are increasingly valued by employers. City University of Seattle emphasizes the importance of transferable skills and offers programs to help adult learners transition into these new fields.

Key skills projected to be in demand by 2026 include adaptability, analytical thinking, business analytics, communication, cybersecurity, data analysis, digital marketing, emotional intelligence, generative AI, project management, and transformational leadership. Technical skills, particularly in IT and digital tools, are essential as organizations adopt automation and AI. Additionally, soft skills such as collaboration and emotional intelligence are critical for success in remote and hybrid work environments.

To build these skills, potential candidates are encouraged to focus on relevant job advertisements, pursue online courses, and take advantage of flexible learning opportunities. Continuous education is crucial for future-proofing careers in a job landscape characterized by change and uncertainty.
