Local artist McKy Karounis will host an abstract painting class designed to help participants create artwork they will cherish. The class will take place on Monday, April 13, from 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM, and attendees will be inspired by music during the session. All necessary supplies for painting are provided. The cost for members is $30, while non-members will pay $35, with sales tax added to the final price.
