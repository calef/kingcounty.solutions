A three-alarm fire in Seattle's Leschi neighborhood displaced residents on December 20. The Seattle Fire Department received reports of an exterior fire on the back deck of a two-story house on 34th Avenue at 7:38 p.m. Engine 6 was the first to arrive and found flames spreading to the roof and interior.

Firefighters launched an aggressive attack on the blaze, working to prevent it from spreading to neighboring homes. A total of 50 firefighters responded to the scene. The fire was brought under control within about an hour, but the structure sustained significant damage.

Two adults and two children were displaced as a result of the fire. They are being assisted by the Red Cross. Fortunately, there were no injuries reported. The cause of the fire is under investigation.
