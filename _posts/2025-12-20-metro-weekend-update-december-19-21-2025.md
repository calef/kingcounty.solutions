Metro will temporarily close stop #8494 at Rainier Avenue S & S Charles Street (northbound) from 7 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Saturday, December 20, due to construction. Riders on Routes 7, 106, and ST 554 should plan for this service impact. Alternative stops will be in place to accommodate passengers during the closure. Metro encourages riders to check their schedules and plan for potential delays. Regular service is expected to resume after the closure period.
