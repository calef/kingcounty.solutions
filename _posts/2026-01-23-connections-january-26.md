  <p><strong>YouthBuild: A Workforce Development Program Where Success is Achievable</strong></p>



  <p>For Victor, stability is about creating a safe future for his family. As a father and husband, he joined YouthCare’s YouthBuild program to earn his high school diploma, gain industry certifications, and build a career in the trades.</p>



  <p>Midway through the program, Victor unexpectedly lost housing, a challenge that could have derailed his progress. With support from his Client Success Navigator, he navigated housing applications, budgeting, and long-term planning while continuing to meet the demands of a full-time program. Today, Victor is on track to graduate with college credits and job-ready skills, a reflection of both his determination and the comprehensive support YouthBuild provides.</p>



  <p>Your donations help young people like Victor gain valuable skills that help them reach their full potential.</p>



  <p>Thank you for helping us ensure all young people have the chance to thrive.</p>



  <p>YouthBuild is a six-month workforce development program that combines education, hands-on training, and individualized support to help young adults build lasting stability. That support comes to life in stories like Victor’s. </p>



  <p>Through a combination of classroom learning, hands-on training, and individualized support, YouthBuild helps participants work toward their GED, gain job readiness and construction skills, and prepare for long-term employment or post-secondary pathways all while getting paid a $50/day stipend. The program is built to support young adults ages 18 – 24 who are motivated to make a change and need structure, stability, and guidance to reach their goals.</p>



  <p>On January 21, Victor will be among 31 young adults graduating from YouthCare’s YouthBuild program. These graduates have committed to a rigorous, full-time schedule, balancing daily GED classes with hands-on construction training. Their resilience and dedication reflect what is possible when young people are supported with structure, opportunity, and care. We look forward to celebrating this milestone.</p>



  <p>These students are not only job-ready; they are life-ready. Each of them has faced and overcome significant barriers to reach this point, and their growth, resilience, and drive have been truly inspiring.</p>

YouthCare is entering the new year with a renewed focus on supporting young people experiencing homelessness. The organization is strengthening its programs and collaborations, relying on the trust and support of its funders to ensure that young people receive essential care and opportunities. Victor, a participant in YouthCare’s YouthBuild program, exemplifies the impact of this support. Despite facing housing instability, he has continued his education and training, working towards his high school diploma and industry certifications in the trades. With guidance from his Client Success Navigator, Victor has managed to stay on track, and he is set to graduate soon with job-ready skills and college credits.

YouthBuild is a six-month workforce development program designed for young adults aged 18-24, providing education, hands-on training, and individualized support while participants earn a stipend. On January 21, Victor will join 30 others in graduating from the program, showcasing their resilience and dedication. YouthCare also expresses gratitude to donors who helped create a joyful holiday season for young people. Opportunities to volunteer and support YouthCare are available, including serving meals or organizing donations.
