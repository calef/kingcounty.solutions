Residents affected by the recent floods in Washington can call (844) 965-1386 for help with home cleanup. Crisis Cleanup is offering assistance by connecting individuals with volunteers from local relief organizations, community groups, and faith communities. The services provided are free, though availability is not guaranteed due to high demand. Those in need of support are encouraged to reach out for assistance in managing the aftermath of the floods.
