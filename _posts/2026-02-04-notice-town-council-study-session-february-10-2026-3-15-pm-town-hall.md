On February 10, 2026, the Town Council of Yarrow Point will hold a Study Session at Town Hall from 3:15 to 4 p.m. The session will feature an open forum discussion, but there will be no opportunity for public comment. Residents can listen to the meeting by joining a virtual Zoom session or by calling in. The meeting will include a presentation by the Regional Coalition for Housing (ARCH) among other agenda items. Participants can join the meeting through a computer, mobile app, or phone using the provided Zoom link and call-in details.
