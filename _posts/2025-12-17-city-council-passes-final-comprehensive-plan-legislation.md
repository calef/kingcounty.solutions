The Seattle City Council approved two key pieces of legislation regarding the city's Comprehensive Plan. The approved bills are CB 120985, which amends the Comprehensive Plan, and CB 120993, which relates to Permanent HB 1110 legislation. Councilmember Joy Hollingsworth, representing District 3, chaired the Select Committee on the Comprehensive Plan. The vote had been delayed previously, but it has now moved forward, setting the stage for Phase 2 of the Comprehensive Plan, which will be taken up by the Select Committee in 2026.
