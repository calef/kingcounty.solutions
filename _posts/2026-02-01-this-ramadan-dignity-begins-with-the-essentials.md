This Ramadan, thousands of families in King County, many of whom are refugees and immigrants, struggle to access basic hygiene essentials like soap, toothpaste, and menstrual products. These items are crucial for health, employment, education, and a sense of belonging, yet they remain unaffordable for many. Families resettling in Washington after fleeing conflict or economic instability face additional challenges due to limited access to hygiene products and food. Essentials First aims to address these urgent needs by providing hygiene distributions, food support, job skills training, youth leadership programs, and policy advocacy. The organization helps families not only survive but also rebuild their lives with dignity. Community support is vital for providing essential hygiene kits, nutritious food, and opportunities for stability for those in need.
