The King County Housing Repair program offers assistance to low and moderate-income homeowners and renters with disabilities across most areas of King County. The program aims to help these individuals make necessary repairs to their homes. To find out more about the application process, available services, and eligibility requirements, interested parties can call the Housing Repair office at 206-263-9095 or visit the program's website.
