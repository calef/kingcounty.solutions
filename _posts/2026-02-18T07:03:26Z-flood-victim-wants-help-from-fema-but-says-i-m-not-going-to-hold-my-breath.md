In Everson, Washington, single mother Ashleigh Barnhart faces significant challenges after floodwaters from the Nooksack River caused approximately $100,000 in damage to her home. With no insurance coverage, she has been relying on limited emergency funds and community support to manage the extensive repairs needed after the flooding damaged half of her home. Barnhart, who has five children and works as an elementary school counselor, expressed frustration over the slow response from federal agencies, particularly as she awaits potential assistance from the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA).

Washington Gov. Bob Ferguson has requested $21 million in individual assistance from FEMA to aid families affected by recent flooding, which has impacted nearly 4,000 homes statewide. However, concerns remain following FEMA's denial of aid for a previous disaster event in the region. Barnhart remains hopeful but cautious, stating, “I’m not going to hold my breath on it, but I would love for the help.” The outcome of the federal request will be crucial for families like hers as they seek to recover from the devastating impacts of the floods.
