The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services declared a Public Health Emergency for Washington state on December 23 due to health issues stemming from severe storms, straight-line winds, flooding, landslides, and mudslides. This declaration follows the president's emergency declaration and allows the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services to provide support and resources to affected areas. The declaration aims to assist individuals and families dealing with the aftermath of these disasters. Additionally, the Disaster Distress Helpline is available for those in need of emotional support and crisis counseling. People can reach the helpline at 1-800-985-5990 or text "TalkWithUs" to 66746.
