  from the Executive Director</h2>\n<p> </p>\n<p>Closing out another extraordinary
  year at Wonderland, a central theme stands out: Partnership. Wonderland and Hope
  Rising Clinic served more kids in 2025 than ever before. We could not have done
  it without the generosity of our supporters, dedication of our staff, and perseverance
  of the families we serve. Each of these pieces, unique and necessary, work together
  in often unseen ways to give children impacted by developmental challenges and prenatal
  substance exposure the chance to thrive.</p>\n<p>We gathered with our community
  throughout the year at Hope Rising Clinic’s 6th anniversary open house, through
  a virtual discussion with Dr. Julia Bledsoe and Hope Rising Clinic providers, and
  a special community event in November celebrating families and our shared impact.
  At each event, we saw people come together to celebrate our mutual accomplishments
  and to renew their commitment to what’s next, energized by the opportunities ahead.</p>\n<p>During
  Season of Wonder, we called on donors, foundations, and corporate partners to support
  the Family Care Fund, which ensures cost is not a barrier to families seeking therapy
  and support. Our community answered, contributing an astounding $372,000 to keep
  our promise that no child is turned away due to family financial circumstances.</p>\n<p>Wonderland’s
  dedicated Early Support and Hope Rising Clinic teams worked tirelessly in 2025 to
  serve more families and serve them better. Our ES providers reached a new recurring
  caseload high of 639 children in December! Hope Rising Clinic served 142 children
  affected by prenatal substance exposure and their families, with special emphasis
  on educating caregivers on prenatal substance exposure as a brain-based disability,
  strengthening their confidence and capacity to support their children.</p>\n\t</div>\n</div>\n\n\n\n\n\t\t\t</div>
  \n\t\t</div>\n\t</div> \n</div>\n</div>\n\t\t<div>\n<div><div><div></div></div></div>\n<div>\n\t<div>\n\t\t<div>\n\t\t\t<div>\n\t\t\t\t\n<div>\n\t<div>\n\t\t<p>Select
  2025 program highlights include:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Early Support for Infants and Toddlers
  (ESIT) delivered 11,044 therapy visits that supported early development and strengthened
  caregivers during the most critical years of growth.</li>\n<li>Hope Rising Clinic
  provided 2,021 therapy visits, delivering our specialized clinical care and caregiver
  education to families from Blaine to Long Beach, and all the way to Spokane.</li>\n</ul>\n\t</div>\n</div>\n\n\n\n\n\t\t\t</div>

  outcomes speak to the efficacy of our unique, tailored services, and to the commitment
  of our parents and caregivers. Wonderland’s providers coach caregivers on strategies
  to help their child progress, but it is the diligent work of these first — and best
  — teachers between visits that help children thrive.</p>\n<p>Finally, we are thrilled
  to share that our in-depth prenatal substance exposure training program was successfully
  launched to 27 providers from around Puget Sound in June 2025 and 63 Wonderland
  providers in October. This multi-day training experience is designed for early educators
  supporting children with PSE, emphasizing both clinical understanding and practical,
  classroom-ready strategies. Our unique prenatal substance exposure curriculum and
  trainings will have exponential impact across the state and country and is the focus
  of our work in 2026 because of its potential for true, systemic change.</p>\n<p>Here’s
  to a year ahead of deepening partnerships, fueled by our shared mission and commitment
  to children and families in our community.</p>\n<p>With gratitude,</p>\n\t</div>\n</div>\n\n\n\n\n\t\t\t</div>

Wonderland's Executive Director Mary Kirchoff reflects on a successful 2025, emphasizing the importance of partnership in serving children impacted by developmental challenges and prenatal substance exposure. The organization, alongside Hope Rising Clinic, reached more children than ever before, thanks to the generosity of supporters and the dedication of staff and families. Community events, including Hope Rising Clinic's 6th anniversary, celebrated collective achievements and renewed commitments to future goals.

In 2025, Wonderland launched the Family Care Fund, raising $372,000 to ensure therapy remains accessible for families in need. The Early Support and Hope Rising Clinic teams served a record number of children, with 639 children receiving early support and 142 children benefiting from specialized care. The Early Support for Infants and Toddlers program delivered over 11,000 therapy visits, while the Hope Rising Clinic provided more than 2,000 visits focused on education and support for caregivers.

The organization introduced a prenatal substance exposure training program, reaching 90 providers to enhance care for affected children. Looking forward to 2026, Wonderland aims to deepen partnerships and focus on systemic changes to better serve children and families in the community.
