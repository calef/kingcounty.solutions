  – Gulf War-era veteran and Pittsburgh native Jim Marszalek has been appointed executive
  director of DAV (Disabled American Veterans) Washington Headquarters, where he will
  direct the organization’s legislative and service programs, which provide free benefits
  assistance to over 200,000 veterans, survivors and family members each year.</p>\n<p>A
  Marine veteran, Marszalek has been an integral member of DAV’s senior leadership
  team and previously served as assistant executive director. In that role, he was
  instrumental in advancing reforms to the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) claims
  and appeals systems, including DAV’s advocacy surrounding the Veterans Appeals Improvement
  and Modernization Act of 2017. Marszalek is a nationally-recognized expert on veterans
  benefits law and has testified numerous times before the Senate and House Veterans’
  Affairs committees.</p>\n<p>“Respected throughout the organization for his strategic
  insight, steady leadership and deep understanding of DAV’s mission, Jim is exceptionally
  well positioned to lead Washington Headquarters forward,” said National Adjutant
  and CEO Barry Jesinoski. “Under his watch, DAV will undoubtedly secure more meaningful
  legislative and policy victories for the veterans we serve.”</p>\n<p>Between Washington
  Headquarters and 88 offices across the country, Marszalek now manages more than
  400 professional and support staff. He also serves as DAV’s principal spokesperson
  before Congress, the VA and the White House.</p>\n<p>“This appointment is a truly
  humbling honor and it’s a charge I don’t take lightly,” said Marszalek. “I’m incredibly
  grateful for the many DAV leaders and advocates who have come before me and paved
  the way for our organization to make tangible differences in the lives of America’s
  veterans and their families. I look forward to the opportunity to lead this incredible
  office of professionals as we continue our great and noble mission.”</p>\n\n</div><p>The

Gulf War-era veteran Jim Marszalek, a Pittsburgh native, has been appointed as the executive director of DAV (Disabled American Veterans) Washington Headquarters. In this role, he will oversee the organization’s legislative and service programs, which assist over 200,000 veterans, survivors, and family members each year. Marszalek, a Marine veteran, previously served as assistant executive director, where he played a key role in reforming the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) claims and appeals systems, including advocating for the Veterans Appeals Improvement and Modernization Act of 2017. Recognized nationally for his expertise in veterans benefits law, he has frequently testified before the Senate and House Veterans’ Affairs committees. National Adjutant and CEO Barry Jesinoski praised Marszalek's strategic insight and leadership skills, expressing confidence that he will lead Washington Headquarters to secure significant legislative victories for veterans. Marszalek now manages over 400 professional and support staff across Washington Headquarters and 88 offices nationwide. He expressed gratitude for the support from previous DAV leaders and advocates and emphasized his commitment to continuing the organization’s mission to support America’s veterans and their families.
