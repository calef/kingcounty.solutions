  <p>With almost a quarter of all Veterans in the United States residing in rural communities, ensuring they receive quality health care close to home is more important than ever. The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) actively works to meet these Veterans in their own communities and promote opportunities to live happy and healthy lives. </p>



  <p>Let’s take another look at the <a>Carl Vinson VA Medical Center (VAMC)</a> located in rural Dublin, Ga., a small town with a big heart, and see if a career serving Veterans is right for you. </p>



  <h2>Carl Vinson VAMC </h2>



  <p>Like at all VA facilities, staff at the Carl Vinson VAMC in Dublin are committed to making a difference, one Veteran at a time. Originally a Navy hospital during World War II, the medical center is named after Georgia native Carl Vinson, an American politician who was known as the “Father of the Two-Ocean Navy.” Today, the Carl Vinson VAMC offers a wide range of health and support services including, but not limited to: </p>



  <ul>
  <li>Primary care. </li>



  <li>Physical therapy and rehabilitation. </li>



  <li>Mental health care. </li>



  <li>Prescriptions. </li>
  </ul>



  <p>As of February 2025, 96% of Veterans report being able to usually or always get a primary care appointment when needed at the Carl Vinson VAMC. </p>



  <h2>The Emerald City </h2>



  <p>Visitors to Dublin will be greeted with southern charm and a community full of life. Named in honor of its first Irish settlers, citizens of Dublin, also known as the Emerald City, paint the town green for a month-long <a>St. Patrick’s Day Festival</a> in February and March each year. History buffs can learn more about the historic downtown by taking a <a>Dublin Walking Tour</a>, a self-guided tour showcasing more than 70 landmarks. Spend time in the great outdoors at <a>Stubbs Park</a> or take a few swings at the local <a>golf course</a>.  </p>



  <p>Located in the heart of Georgia, Dublin is situated at the junction of three major highways, making travel to other Georgia cities a snap. While rich with history, Dublin readily invests in education, infrastructure and culture initiatives that continue to move it forward. </p>



  <h2>Work at VA </h2>



  <p>Ready to take the next step towards a rewarding career at a rural VA like the Carl Vinson VAMC? Visit <a>VA Careers</a> today to apply. </p>

Almost a quarter of all U.S. Veterans live in rural areas, making access to quality health care essential. The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is focused on providing services to these Veterans in their communities. The Carl Vinson VA Medical Center (VAMC) in Dublin, Georgia, formerly a Navy hospital, offers a range of health services including primary care, physical therapy, mental health care, and prescriptions. As of February 2025, 96% of Veterans at the facility report they can usually or always get a needed primary care appointment.

Dublin, known as the Emerald City, is celebrated for its southern charm and community spirit, highlighted by a month-long St. Patrick’s Day Festival each February and March. Visitors can enjoy a self-guided walking tour showcasing over 70 historical landmarks, explore Stubbs Park, or play golf. Located at the crossroads of three major highways, Dublin provides easy access to other Georgia cities and is committed to enhancing education, infrastructure, and culture. Those interested in a rewarding career at a rural VA facility like the Carl Vinson VAMC are encouraged to apply through VA Careers.
