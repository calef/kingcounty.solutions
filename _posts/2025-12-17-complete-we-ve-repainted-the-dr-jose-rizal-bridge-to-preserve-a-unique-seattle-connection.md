The historic Dr. Jose Rizal Bridge, connecting the Chinatown-International District to Beacon Hill, has been newly repainted. This project marks the first significant repainting and maintenance effort for the bridge in over a decade. The work included a thorough cleaning and application of a fresh coat of paint, enhancing the bridge's aesthetics and protecting it from weather-related wear. The project was completed on schedule and aimed to preserve the bridge's historical significance while ensuring its longevity. Community members and local officials expressed enthusiasm for the revitalized appearance of the bridge, which serves as an important cultural landmark. The repainting is part of ongoing efforts to maintain and improve infrastructure within the city, highlighting the commitment to preserving Seattle's historical sites.
