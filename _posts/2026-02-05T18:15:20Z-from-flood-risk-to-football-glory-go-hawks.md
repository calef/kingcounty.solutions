  collect and drain to a common outlet, such as a river, lake, or ocean. Think of
  it as the football field itself. Everything that happens, rainfall, runoff, and
  river flow, unfolds within the basin, just like every play unfolds on the field.

  team has a playbook. For us, that’s our Capital Improvement Program (CIP), a long-term

  game plan, a strong CIP helps us anticipate challenges, invest wisely, and move
  the ball steadily down the field toward safer, more resilient communities. \U0001F6E1️
  Defense Wins Championships (and Reduces Flood Risk) In football, a solid defense
  keeps the other team from breaking through. In flood risk reduction, flood protection
  facilities, like levees, floodwalls, and pump stations, play the same role. They’re
  our defensive line, helping to reduce flood impacts and protect communities when
  rivers rise and conditions get tough. Seattle Seahawks defense. Credit: Seattle
  Seahawks, seahawks.com Black River Pump Station ⚡ Flash Flooding: The Defensive
  Blitz Flash flooding can happen fast, with little warning, often after intense rainfall.
  It’s the flood equivalent of a defensive blitz: sudden, aggressive, and capable
  of catching everyone off guard if you’re not prepared. That’s why forecasting, monitoring,
  and preparation are such critical parts of flood risk management. \U0001F4CF Reading
  the Field: River Gages Quarterbacks read the defense before the snap. Flood managers
  read river gages, which tell us how high water levels are rising in real time. Those
  measurements help inform decisions, just like reading coverage helps a QB decide
  whether to pass, hand off, or scramble. \U0001F6A8 Flood Alerts & Flood Warnings:
  Calling an Audible at the Line Speaking of quarterbacks, great quarterbacks don’t
  just stick to the original play as they read the defense at the line of scrimmage.
  When they spot danger, they call an audible, adjusting the play to protect the team
  and avoid a big loss. That’s exactly how flood alerts and flood warnings work. A
  flood alert is like a quarterback noticing the defense starting to shift. Conditions
  are changing, and it’s time to pay attention, get ready, and be prepared to act.
  A flood warning, on the other hand, is the audible being called, danger is imminent
  or already happening, and it’s time to move quickly and decisively to stay safe.
  Just like a well-timed audible can save a play, early flood alerts and flood warnings
  help people, communities, and emergency responders prepare, adjust, and respond
  before floodwaters cause serious harm. Awareness, communication, and quick action
  can make all the difference between a safe outcome and a costly turnover. \U0001F300
