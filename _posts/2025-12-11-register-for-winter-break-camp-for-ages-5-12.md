Seattle offers a winter break camp for children ages 5 to 12, featuring engaging and supportive activities. The licensed childcare program is designed to provide a safe environment during school breaks. Staff members focus on creating a welcoming atmosphere that embraces diversity, ensuring children can enjoy fun and active experiences. The camp aims to keep kids entertained and engaged while school is out, with various activities planned. Parents can register their children for this enriching program, which emphasizes safety and fun.
