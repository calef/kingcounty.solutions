The St. Luke’s Annual Meeting is set for Sunday, January 18, 2026, after the worship service. It will be held both in person and online, with meeting packets available in print and as a PDF. All members are encouraged to attend as new leadership will be elected, updates on the development project will be shared, and appreciations will be expressed. Attendees are invited to contribute a hot dish, salad, or side for the communal meal starting at 11:30 a.m.

Two candidates for the Bishop’s Committee were introduced. Allison Crowley, an engineer, has been an active member for over a decade and is involved in the Property Development Team. She focuses on stewardship and sustainability to strengthen the church's mission. Taylor Holubar, who has a PhD in psychology, recently moved to Seattle with her family. She values the community at St. Luke’s and aims to support its mission. The meeting promises to be a time for fellowship and forward-looking discussions about the church's future.
