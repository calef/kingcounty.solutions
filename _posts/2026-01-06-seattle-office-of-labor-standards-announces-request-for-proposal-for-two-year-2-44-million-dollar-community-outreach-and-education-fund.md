The Seattle Office of Labor Standards (OLS) has announced a Request for Proposal (RFP) for a two-year Community Outreach and Education Fund totaling $2.44 million. This funding aims to enhance outreach, education, and technical assistance for Seattle workers regarding the city's labor standards. OLS plans to award contracts worth $1.22 million per year for the periods of July 2026 to June 2027 and July 2027 to June 2028. The initiative focuses on supporting local organizations that assist low-wage workers, particularly BIPOC and immigrant communities, who are most affected by workplace violations.

Organizations can choose from various service strategies tailored to specific labor sectors, including retail, hospitality, domestic work, construction, and the arts. Since its inception in 2015, the Community Outreach and Education Fund has helped tens of thousands of workers understand their rights and navigate labor laws. Interested applicants must submit proposals by February 12, 2026, and can attend an optional information session on January 20, 2026, for guidance on the application process.
