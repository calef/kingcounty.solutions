  <p>The Seattle Seahawks World Champions Parade, tomorrow, Wednesday, Feb. 11, is expected to have <strong>significant traffic and operational impacts across downtown Seattle. </strong>With expected attendance ranging from 750,000 to 1 million people, please prepare in advance for longer commutes and detours as needed.</p>
  <p><strong>Parade details: </strong></p>
  <ul>
  <li>The parade will begin at 11 a.m., at Fourth Avenue and Washington Street in Seattle, on Wednesday, Feb. 11.</li>
  <li>The parade will continue northbound along Fourth Avenue, ending at Fourth Avenue and Cedar Street.</li>
  <li>The parade is expected to take two hours, with no further activities planned at Seattle Center following the parade.</li>
  <li>Link light rail will operate as normal, and Sound Transit and King County Metro buses will operate on alternative routes.</li>
  <li>Vehicles exiting I-90 onto Fourth Avenue will be directed to Fourth Avenue in the southbound direction. I-5 and I-90 on and off ramps will be held briefly at the start of the parade but will open shortly after.</li>
  <li>During the event, Pierce Transit is providing <strong>special event bus service </strong>from the 512 Park &amp; Ride and Tacoma Dome Station (TDS) <strong>to Federal Way Downtown Station</strong>, to connect fans to Sound Transit’s 1 Line Link light rail.</li>
  <li>View all parade details <strong><a>here</a></strong>.</li>
  </ul>
  <p><img src="https://assets.socialchorus.com/production/2067/images/d4d26014-047a-4f8a-b512-deeea0ede9d5.png"></p>
  <p><strong>Recommendations: </strong></p>
  <ul>
  <li>Allow additional time for your commute to work on Wednesday.</li>
  <li>Check adjustments for your bus route on King County Metro’s<strong> </strong><strong><u><a>Service Advisories web page</a></u> </strong>closer to the event.</li>
  <li>View real-time traffic information on the WSDOT <strong><u><a>mobile app</a></u> </strong>and the <strong><u><a>real-time travel map</a></u></strong><u><a>.</a></u>
  </li>
  </ul>
  <p>Thank you for planning ahead to ensure you arrive to your appointments on time. We appreciate your efforts and look forward to providing you with exceptional care.</p>

Seattle will hold a championship parade for the Seahawks on Wednesday, Feb. 11, expected to attract between 750,000 and 1 million attendees. The parade starts at 11 a.m. at Fourth Avenue and Washington Street, continuing north along Fourth Avenue and concluding at Fourth Avenue and Cedar Street. The event is anticipated to last about two hours, with no further activities scheduled at Seattle Center afterward.

Traffic will be significantly impacted, with vehicles exiting I-90 directed south on Fourth Avenue. I-5 and I-90 on and off ramps will briefly close at the parade's start but will open shortly after. Public transportation will adjust accordingly, with Link light rail operating normally. Sound Transit and King County Metro buses will follow alternative routes. Pierce Transit will offer special event bus service from the 512 Park & Ride and Tacoma Dome Station to Federal Way Downtown Station, connecting fans to the light rail.

Attendees are encouraged to allow extra time for their commutes and check for updates on bus routes and traffic conditions through local advisories and mobile apps.
