Snoqualmie Valley Health has added Dr. Chitralekha Pasupuleti, an internal medicine physician with over ten years of experience, to its North Bend Clinic team. Dr. Pasupuleti was attracted to the organization for its independence and strong community ties. Her care philosophy focuses on collaborating with patients to manage chronic conditions, promote preventive health, and empower them through education and shared decision-making. She believes in building trust and helping patients understand their health to achieve long-term wellness.

Dr. Pasupuleti has a diverse background in adult patient care, including chronic disease management, preventive care, and patient education. She earned her medical degree from the University of Notre Dame in Perth, Australia, and completed her residency at Southern Illinois University. Her previous roles include serving as medical director for hospitalist services at Virginia Mason Franciscan Health’s St. Clare Hospital and chief of medicine since 2021.

Outside of her medical career, Dr. Pasupuleti enjoys outdoor activities, traveling, and cooking. She is married and has a child in college. Dr. Pasupuleti is currently accepting patients at the North Bend Clinic.
