Harborview Medical Center Street Medicine will offer medical services to clients at the American Legion Hall on December 20 and January 3, from 9:30 a.m. to 11:00 a.m. This multi-disciplinary team targets individuals experiencing unsheltered homelessness in Seattle, providing essential health services that include bridge medical care, mental health triage, and referrals to case management. Although not a substitute for primary care, their services encompass prescription filling, wound care, and laboratory testing. The initiative aims to enhance the health and well-being of vulnerable populations within the community. The Westside Neighbors Shelter expressed gratitude to Harborview Medical Center for their commitment to improving community health.
