  class=\"field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden
  field--item\"><h2>Next phase of construction opens new southbound I-5 exit, closes
  part of existing 1950’s era overpass</h2><p>DUPONT – If the weather cooperates,
  a portion of the new Interstate 5 interchange in DuPont will open by mid-January.
  Workers will also fully open the new roundabout in DuPont.</p><h3>New overpass opens
  to southbound I-5 travelers</h3><p>Once southbound travelers on I-5 begin using
  the <a href=\"https://s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/ehq-production-us-california/b022b1d2f16449c50f8423b25b491f4c1024bc71/original/1763405817/19cd4c9ec1fbfb2300780bb82d381f71_MountsRoad-map-Stage4SouthboundTraffic.jpg?1763405817\">new
  exit 119</a>to Steilacoom-DuPont Road, they will notice a few changes.&nbsp;Those
  going to DuPont will turn right toward the new roundabout, while people heading
  to Joint Base Lewis-McChord will turn left and take the overpass.</p><p>People on&nbsp;<a
  href=\"https://s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/ehq-production-us-california/93d07b23ca0e1e86aa25bda418a4d3fd8dc78445/original/1763408396/f9564fe4b1fca855d2fbdeb9799c991c_MountsRoad-map-Stage4NorthboundTraffic.jpg?1763408396\">northbound
  I-5</a> going to DuPont or JBLM will continue to use the existing exit and the 1957
  Steilacoom-DuPont overpass.</p><p>The Washington State Department of Transportation
  will&nbsp;<a href=\"/construction-planning/search-projects/i-5-mounts-rd-steilacoom-dupont-rd-corridor-improvements\">announce</a>
  the exact date for the opening of the new interchange once it is scheduled.</p><h3>Temporary
  changes to travel between DuPont and JBLM</h3><p>When the new overpass opens, the
  older will close to southbound traffic. DuPont travelers will use the&nbsp;<a href=\"https://s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/ehq-production-us-california/dd4aae6b7c7836054c51967c748691931fafdef4/original/1763408829/72c44a49f7cd2a929c17f01b118edade_MountsRoad-map-Stage4DuPontTraffic.jpg?1763408829\">new
  Steilacoom-DuPont overpass</a> to reach JBLM or northbound I-5. The old overpass
  will provide <a href=\"https://s3-us-west-1.amazonaws.com/ehq-production-us-california/309d952ab664bf38dbb34b0447f126a9dca6e7ad/original/1763408879/3fee9db0bf9165328f918035f05511cb_MountsRoad-map-Stage4JBLMtraffic.jpg?1763408879\">access
  from JBLM to DuPont or southbound I-5</a>. For more information, please visit the&nbsp;<a
  href=\"https://engage.wsdot.wa.gov/i-5-mounts-to-steilacoom-dupont-rd#how-to-get-around\">online
  open house</a>.</p><h3>Coming spring 2026</h3><p>The rest of the new overpass will
  open to two-way traffic as a <a href=\"/travel/traffic-safety-methods/diverging-diamond-interchange\">diverging
  diamond interchange</a> sometime in the spring. The interchange’s design helps traffic
  move more efficiently. The new overpass will also provide improved access for people

Construction of a new Interstate 5 interchange in DuPont is progressing, with plans to open a portion of it by mid-January, pending weather conditions. The new exit 119 for southbound I-5 will direct traffic to a new roundabout for DuPont and an overpass for Joint Base Lewis-McChord (JBLM). Northbound travelers will continue to use the existing exit and the 1957 overpass, which will close for southbound traffic once the new overpass is operational.

The full opening of the new diverging diamond interchange is expected in spring 2026, enhancing traffic flow and improving access for pedestrians and cyclists between DuPont and JBLM. The older overpass, deemed in poor condition and not meeting current clearance standards, will be demolished, requiring overnight lane closures. The overall I-5 Mounts Road to Steilacoom-DuPont Road Corridor Improvements project, costing $240 million, is set to be completed by late 2026. This project also includes previous enhancements such as new HOV lanes and improved access for non-motorized travelers. Updates on the project can be received via email, and real-time information is available through the WSDOT app and travel map.
