Keri Pollock serves as the Director of Marketing and Communications at Aging Wisdom, where she has worked for a decade. Her journey in the aging services field began serendipitously over 30 years ago when she joined a firm specializing in senior housing design, influenced by her grandparents' positive impact on her life. Pollock emphasizes the shift she has observed in clients, noting that adult children who once sought services for their parents are now becoming proactive planners for their own future. She values the collaborative culture at Aging Wisdom, where teamwork is central to problem-solving and support.

Pollock is particularly proud of her role in founding the Creative Aging Program at the Frye Art Museum, which promotes age-friendly engagement in the community. Her motivation stems from the belief that small, committed groups can effect change, inspired by Margaret Mead's famous quote. With over 30 years of experience, Pollock is dedicated to improving the lives of older adults and their families through effective communication and relationship-building. Outside of work, she enjoys exploring the Pacific Northwest and supporting local sports teams.
