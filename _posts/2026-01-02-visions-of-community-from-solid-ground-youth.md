Solid Ground's youth showcased their creativity and vision of community through an art contest for the organization's annual New Year's card. Young residents from the Sand Point Housing and Broadview Shelter & Transitional Housing were invited to express what community means to them. Many artworks depicted festive holiday scenes, but the children's messages centered on themes of caring, togetherness, and love.

Denise, an 8-year-old from the Broadview Shelter, won the contest with her artwork, emphasizing the importance of taking care of the Earth. Other participants shared various interpretations of community, including the value of helping one another, respecting each other, and having fun together.

Solid Ground residents voted for their favorite designs at community holiday parties, and staff selected a finalist from the top choices. Winning artists received special prizes. The children's collective vision highlights the essence of community as a place of joy, kindness, and support, setting a hopeful tone for the year ahead.
