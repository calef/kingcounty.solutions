Seattle Mayor Bruce Harrell released his 2025 annual report, highlighting key achievements of his administration over the past four years. The report showcases progress made under his One Seattle vision, which aims to create a safe, equitable, and thriving city.

Major accomplishments include initiatives to enhance public safety, improve housing access, and promote economic growth. The administration has focused on addressing homelessness and investing in community programs. Additionally, efforts to foster environmental sustainability and build resilient neighborhoods are emphasized.

Harrell expressed pride in the administration's accomplishments and commitment to continuing this work. He aims to ensure that all Seattle residents benefit from the city’s growth and resources. The report serves as a reflection of the administration's dedication to creating a more inclusive and vibrant Seattle for everyone.
