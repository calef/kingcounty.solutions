The PDA Board and Committee meetings will continue to be held electronically for the foreseeable future. Meeting details for 2026 have been announced, with Board of Directors meetings scheduled for the second Monday of each month from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. The first meeting will be on January 12, with subsequent meetings on February 9, March 9, April 13, May 11, June 8, July 13, August 10, September 14, October 19, November 9, and December 14.

Executive Committee meetings will occur on the first Monday of each month from 5:00 to 6:30 p.m., starting January 5. The Finance and Asset Management Committee will meet on the first Tuesday of each month from 4:00 to 5:00 p.m., beginning January 6. The Resident and Property Success Committee will also meet on the first Tuesday from 3:00 to 4:00 p.m., starting January 6, although some meetings are canceled in July and September.

The Property Development Committee is set to meet on the last Thursday of each month from 3:30 to 5:00 p.m., with the first meeting on January 7. Additional meetings, including the Joint Board Retreat and the Annual Meeting, are to be determined.
