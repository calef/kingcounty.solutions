Nicole Amico Kane serves as the Director of Care Management at Aging Wisdom. Initially starting her career in child and family services, Kane experienced burnout after a few years in that field. A pivotal moment came when she was recruited to work for a hospice, which sparked her interest in the aging sector. This transition allowed her to discover her passion for supporting older adults and their families. Kane's journey reflects a common narrative among social workers who seek more fulfilling roles that align with their values and interests. At Aging Wisdom, she focuses on improving care management for seniors, leveraging her experience to enhance the quality of life for her clients.
