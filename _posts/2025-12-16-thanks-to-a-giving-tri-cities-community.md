The Light the World Giving Machine made its debut in the Tri-Cities and achieved remarkable success, thanks to the generosity of community members. This initiative supported local nonprofits, including LCSNW, Boys & Girls Club, Grace Clinic, Grace Collective, Safe Harbor, and My Friend’s Place. Over a span of 19 days, the Giving Machine saw 523 volunteers participate, resulting in more than 2,800 transactions and 13,000 items donated. The total contributions exceeded $300,000. Community members came together to provide hope and assistance to families and individuals in need, showcasing the power of collective kindness and compassion. The event highlighted the positive impact of community involvement and the spirit of giving. Thank you to everyone who contributed to this inspiring initiative.
