Catholic Community Services (CCS) is making a significant impact on individuals' lives in Western Washington, as highlighted in an article by Northwest Catholic. The piece showcases four CCS programs—Bertha’s Place Too, Drexel House, Quince Street Village, and The Community Kitchen—through the personal stories of those they serve. One guest, Raelene, shared that Bertha’s Place Too shelter has "saved my life multiple times," underscoring the critical support these programs provide. CCS focuses on addressing homelessness and food insecurity, with programs designed to offer shelter, resources, and community support to vulnerable populations. The article emphasizes the transformative nature of CCS's initiatives, which not only provide immediate relief but also foster long-term stability for individuals and families in need. The firsthand accounts reflect the profound difference CCS makes in the community, highlighting the organization’s commitment to improving lives during challenging times.
