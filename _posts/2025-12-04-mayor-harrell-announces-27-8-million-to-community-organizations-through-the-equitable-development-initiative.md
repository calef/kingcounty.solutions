Mayor Bruce Harrell announced $27.8 million in funding for 33 projects through the Equitable Development Initiative (EDI). This initiative aims to promote property ownership among Seattle's diverse communities, particularly in neighborhoods facing high risks of displacement and economic challenges. The awarded funds will support various community organizations and projects that focus on equitable development and community resilience. This funding is part of the city’s broader strategy to address disparities and ensure that all communities benefit from Seattle's growth. Mayor Harrell emphasized the importance of investing in community-driven solutions to create lasting change and support the residents most affected by economic pressures. The initiative seeks to empower communities by providing the resources needed to foster stability and enhance opportunities for all Seattleites.
