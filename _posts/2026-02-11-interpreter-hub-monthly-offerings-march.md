  <h2>CDHY Interpreter Hub Monthly Offerings</h2>
  <p><span>Becoming:</span> <em><span>In Community</span></em></p>
  <p>This year’s theme, Becoming, reflects our commitment to growth, transformation, and possibility. Interpreters are always evolving and developing new skills, deepening understanding, and stepping into the next version of themselves. Becoming is about honoring that journey and creating space for continual growth, both personally and professionally.</p>
  <p><strong><span>March 2026 Offerings:</span></strong></p>
  <p>– Growth and Mastery 3-Month Mentorship<br>
  – Community Meet-ups<br>
  – 1:1 with Deaf Mentors<br>
  – 1:1 with Interpreter Mentors<br>
  – Mock Interpreting Practice<br>
  – Classroom Observations<br>
  – Book Club Kits<br>
  – Workshops<br>
  – EIPA &amp; CASLI testing</p>

  <h3></h3>
  <h3><span>Becoming: Immersed</span></h3>
  <p>ASL Zone: Five-Week Receptive Immersion</p>
  <p><em>Presented by: Richard Jacobs and Taralynn Petrites, Ph. D</em></p>
  <p>This five-week cohort provides focused receptive practice through a ninety-minute session each week. RJ and Taralynn will lead as native signers and will guide participants through a structured, high-immersion learning environment. Each session will include natural signer-to-signer conversation, regular comprehension checks, and opportunities for participants to request clarification. RJ and Taralynn will engage in real-time dialogue to support authentic language exposure, and participants will actively monitor their understanding throughout the series. Each session will close with a short review of new signs, hidden meanings, nuanced language use, and relevant cultural context.</p>
  <p><b><i><span>Limit 9 people! </span></i></b></p>

  <h3><span>Becoming: Dynamic</span></h3>
  <p>Prosody: Intonation, Stress, and Rhythm</p>
  <p><em>Presented by: Colleen Roy</em></p>
  <p>Learn about the power of how something is said versus the words used—what sounds natural and how to guide an audience through your interpretation. Participants will explore spoken English prosody and learn to listen for, express, and evaluate prosodic features in their own work. Applying these skills enhances the quality and comprehension of the interpreted message.</p>

The CDHY Interpreter Hub is offering various events in March 2026 under the theme "Becoming," focusing on growth and transformation for interpreters. Key offerings include a three-month mentorship program, community meet-ups, individual sessions with Deaf and interpreter mentors, mock interpreting practice, classroom observations, book club kits, workshops, and EIPA & CASLI testing.

A five-week immersive American Sign Language (ASL) zone led by Richard Jacobs and Taralynn Petrites will enhance receptive skills through weekly 90-minute sessions. Participants will engage in conversation and comprehension checks to improve their understanding of ASL. This cohort is limited to nine participants and runs on March 3, 10, 17, 24, and 31 from 5:00-6:30 PM via Zoom.

Additionally, Colleen Roy will present "Dynamic Prosody" on March 26 from 5:00-7:00 PM, focusing on intonation, stress, and rhythm in interpretation.

Community meet-ups will take place on March 14 from 9:00-11:00 AM at multiple locations across Washington State, encouraging local Deaf and hard of hearing (DHH) individuals and interpreters to connect and build relationships in a casual setting.
