Construction on the northbound Ship Canal Bridge in Seattle will resume on Jan. 9 as part of the Washington State Department of Transportation's (WSDOT) Revive I-5 preservation project. Significant lane closures are expected, so travelers should plan ahead and consider alternatives like public transit or carpooling. Metro will increase bus service on key routes to help mitigate delays caused by the construction. The project aims to extend the life of the aging bridge and enhance safety and ride quality.

During the construction period, two northbound lanes will be closed until June 5, leading to traffic delays. The reversible express lanes will operate northbound only, which may cause southbound delays. Full closures are scheduled on various weekends for work setup and removal, with weather conditions potentially affecting the schedule. The project is expected to be completed by the end of 2026, with all lanes reopening temporarily for the FIFA World Cup matches in Seattle.

Travelers are advised to use transit apps, plan for busier than usual services, and consider flexible travel times to alleviate stress during this period of disruption.
