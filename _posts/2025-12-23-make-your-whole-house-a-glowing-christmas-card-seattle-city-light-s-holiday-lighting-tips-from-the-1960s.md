As the holiday season arrives in Seattle, Seattle City Light shares its holiday lighting recommendations that date back to the early 1960s. The city encourages residents to brighten their homes with festive lights. Resources include a promotional video and a brochure available in the Digital Collections, showcasing tips on how to create a cheerful atmosphere. The message emphasizes making homes a "glowing Christmas card" for the winter holidays.
