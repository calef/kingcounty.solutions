  <p>President Christopher Ross sat down for a conversation with Puget Sound Business Journal (PSBJ) journalist Shawna De La Rosa about the newly completed merger between Compass Housing Alliance and Lutheran Community Services Northwest (LCSNW).</p>



  <p>In the interview, Christopher talked about how Compass and LCSNW will be more than the sum of their parts. We will be able to accomplish more together than either of our organizations could have achieved separately.</p>



  <p>“This relationship gives us the ability to expand our reach beyond King County,” Christopher said. “We are already planning to expand our portfolio. This merger puts a lot of wind in our sails. We will be able to get back into affordable housing and make more homes available.”</p>

President Christopher Ross discussed the recent merger between Compass Housing Alliance and Lutheran Community Services Northwest (LCSNW) in an interview with Shawna De La Rosa from the Puget Sound Business Journal.
