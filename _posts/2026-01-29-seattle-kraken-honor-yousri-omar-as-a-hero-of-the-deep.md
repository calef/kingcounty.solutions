feed_content: "<p>Mary’s Place board member and longtime supporter, Yousri Omar, was
  recognized as a <strong>Hero of the Deep</strong> at a recent Seattle Kraken hockey
  game! </p><p>Yousri was honored for his incredible pro bono legal work with Mary’s
  Place, where he helps families overcome barriers to safe, stable housing. As part
  of a team from Amazon that regularly hosts free legal clinics at Mary’s Place, Yousri’s
  support ranges from resolving past evictions and navigating tenant disputes to addressing
  credit and debt challenges and helping families access unemployment benefits. Even
  seemingly small issues—like old parking tickets or minor infractions—can feel insurmountable
  when you’re facing housing instability. Many people don’t know how to navigate the
  complicated legal system, feel paralyzed by the first step, or worry that what’s
  on their record is worse than it really is. <strong>Yousri’s work removes the guesswork,
  the fear, and the financial barriers, giving families the guidance and confidence
  they need to move forward.</strong></p><p>Through his efforts, families can <strong>secure
  apartments, maintain housing, and regain stability</strong>, giving children and
  parents the foundation to focus on school, work, and family life.</p><p>The Heroes
  of the Deep program, established by The One Roof Foundation—the philanthropic arm
  of the Seattle Kraken and Climate Pledge Arena—celebrates individuals who transform
  lives, build resiliency, and uplift their communities. Each Hero also gets to select
  a nonprofit to receive a <strong>$32,000 Kraken Unity Fund donation</strong>, making
  Yousri’s recognition even more meaningful for the families at Mary’s Place.</p><p><em>“Yousri
  shows up for families every day,”</em> says Dominique Alex, Mary’s Place CEO. <em>“His
  dedication and expertise help families overcome the barriers that too often keep
  them from stable housing and the opportunity to thrive. He truly embodies what it
  means to be a champion for families in our community.”</em></p><p>Thank you, Yousri,
  for your unwavering commitment, your passion, and your tireless work to help families
  secure the housing and stability they deserve. You are making a real difference
  in the lives of children and families every single day!</p>"

Yousri Omar, a board member of Mary’s Place and dedicated supporter, was honored as a Hero of the Deep during a Seattle Kraken hockey game. Recognized for his pro bono legal work, Omar assists families in overcoming obstacles to safe and stable housing. He is part of a team from Amazon that hosts free legal clinics at Mary’s Place, where he helps address issues like past evictions, tenant disputes, credit problems, and unemployment benefits. His efforts alleviate the stress of navigating the legal system, empowering families to secure housing and regain stability. The Kraken's Heroes of the Deep program, run by The One Roof Foundation, celebrates individuals who make a significant impact in their communities. Each honoree selects a nonprofit to receive a $32,000 donation from the Kraken Unity Fund, enhancing the recognition's significance for Mary’s Place families. Dominique Alex, CEO of Mary’s Place, praised Omar for his dedication, stating he embodies the spirit of a champion for families. His commitment plays a vital role in helping families focus on education, work, and family life by securing stable housing.
