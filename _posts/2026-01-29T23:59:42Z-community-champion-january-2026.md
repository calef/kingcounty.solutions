El Centro de la Raza recently honored the legacies of Nancy Skinner Nordhoff and Stephen J. Deal, both influential figures in the community. Nancy, who passed away at 93 on January 7, 2026, was recognized for her philanthropy and commitment to civic engagement, particularly in empowering women and fostering community development. Her support enabled significant initiatives, such as the unveiling of the Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. bust in Plaza Roberto Maestas in 2019, symbolizing justice and solidarity.

Stephen J. Deal, who died unexpectedly on January 2, 2026, at the age of 65, was celebrated for his strategic leadership as Capital Campaign Director at El Centro. He played a vital role in fundraising efforts that contributed to the organization’s growth and sustainability. His dedication helped establish Plaza Roberto Maestas, a cultural and community hub. Both individuals are remembered for their enduring contributions to community building and their unwavering commitment to supporting initiatives that promote equity and opportunity. El Centro de la Raza reflects on their impact, noting that the organization and the community are stronger due to their vision and generosity.
