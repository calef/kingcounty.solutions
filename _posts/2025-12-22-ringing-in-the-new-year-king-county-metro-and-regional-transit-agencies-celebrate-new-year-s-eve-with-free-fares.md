King County Executive Girmay Zahilay announced that Metro will offer free rides on New Year's Eve, Dec. 31. This initiative includes King County Metro, Sound Transit, Community Transit, Everett Transit, Pierce Transit, and the Seattle Streetcar. Riders can enjoy complimentary travel as part of the holiday celebrations. This effort aims to encourage safe transportation options for those celebrating the new year.
