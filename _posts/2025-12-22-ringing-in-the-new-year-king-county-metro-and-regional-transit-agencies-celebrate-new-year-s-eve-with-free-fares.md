King County Executive Girmay Zahilay announced that King County Metro will offer free rides on New Year's Eve, December 31. This initiative is part of a collaboration with other regional transit agencies, allowing riders to travel without paying fares throughout the evening. The goal is to encourage safe celebrations and support individuals who may choose to use public transit during the festivities. The free fare program will be available on all Metro bus routes, as well as services from Sound Transit, Community Transit, Pierce Transit, and other regional agencies. Riders are encouraged to plan ahead and check schedules to ensure a safe and enjoyable New Year's celebration.
