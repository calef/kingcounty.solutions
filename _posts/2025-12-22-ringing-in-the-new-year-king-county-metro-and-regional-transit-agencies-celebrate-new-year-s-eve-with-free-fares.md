King County Executive Girmay Zahilay announced that Metro and other regional transit agencies will offer free rides on New Year’s Eve, December 31. This initiative aims to encourage safe travel as people celebrate the holiday. Free fares will be available on all Metro buses, Sound Transit trains, and Kitsap Transit buses, starting at 8 p.m. on December 31 and continuing until 4 a.m. on January 1. Riders are encouraged to plan their trips in advance and be mindful of transit schedules. The program is part of a broader effort to promote safety and accessibility during the holiday festivities.
