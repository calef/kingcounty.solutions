King County Executive Girmay Zahilay announced that King County Metro will offer free rides on New Year’s Eve, December 31, to help riders celebrate the holiday. This initiative will also include other regional transit agencies, allowing passengers to travel without paying fares. The free service will be available on all Metro buses, light rail, and other transit options. The move aims to promote safety and encourage people to use public transportation to reach their destinations as they celebrate the New Year. Riders are reminded to plan their trips in advance and check transit schedules to ensure a smooth journey. This program is part of ongoing efforts by local transit agencies to provide accessible and safe travel options for the community during festive occasions.
