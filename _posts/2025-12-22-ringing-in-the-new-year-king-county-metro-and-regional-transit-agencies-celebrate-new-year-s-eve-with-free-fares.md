King County Executive Girmay Zahilay announced that King County Metro and other regional transit agencies will offer free rides on New Year’s Eve, December 31. This initiative aims to encourage safe travel for those celebrating the holiday. Riders can enjoy complimentary fares on all Metro buses, Sound Transit, Community Transit, and Pierce Transit services. The free rides will be available from 6 p.m. on December 31 until 6 a.m. on January 1. This program is part of a collaborative effort to promote safety and reduce the risk of impaired driving during the festivities. Passengers are encouraged to take advantage of the free transit options to ensure a safe and enjoyable New Year’s celebration.
