Abdullah Alizada, a student at Renton Technical College, grew up in Afghanistan dreaming of moving to America while watching Hollywood movies. His dream became a reality when he was enrolled in college and working as an activist in Afghanistan, which made it increasingly difficult for him to remain in the country. Alizada eventually reached Western Washington, where he received support from friends that helped him enroll at Renton Technical College. His journey highlights the challenges faced by those seeking safety and education while navigating life-changing circumstances.
