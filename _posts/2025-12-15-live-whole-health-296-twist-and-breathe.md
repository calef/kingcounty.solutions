  <p>Nothing to shout about here! Yoga can be a gentle way to stretch and move the body while practicing gentle breathing. Are you ready to give it a try? Join Katie Herring from the VA New York Harbor Health Care System for gentle introduction to yoga.</p>



  <p>This 28-minute video is done on the floor, mostly from a seated position, with some movements done while lying down. The class begins with breath practice, then a gentle warm up so the joints and muscles are ready to go. Most of the class guides through slow-flowing movements of the body from the seated position on the floor, making this class a great start for anyone new to yoga. This session is also good if you are already practicing yoga, as it gives you a full body stretch in less than 30 minutes.</p>



  <p>As the body moves during this yoga practice, the muscles are stretched and activated to improve posture and relieve discomfort. Maintaining good range of motion by keeping joints limber can reduce injury.</p>

Join Katie Herring from the VA New York Harbor Health Care System for a gentle introduction to yoga in a 28-minute video designed for beginners and experienced practitioners alike. The session primarily takes place on the floor, with most movements done seated or lying down. It starts with breath practice and a warm-up to prepare the joints and muscles. The class focuses on slow-flowing movements that stretch and activate muscles, improving posture and relieving discomfort. Practicing yoga can enhance general wellness, alleviate stress, and improve sleep and balance. This video allows participants to enjoy the benefits of yoga from home and can be repeated as needed. Additional classes are available in the #LiveWholeHealth series for those looking to continue their yoga journey. Yoga promotes a sense of self-awareness, patience, gratitude, and inner peace, fostering a deeper connection to oneself and the surrounding world. For more insight on yoga's health benefits, resources are available from the National Institutes of Health.
