Happy Holidays from LIHI! The organization expresses gratitude for the support received in helping low-income and homeless individuals access affordable housing and essential services like shelters and laundry facilities. LIHI has been committed to addressing the housing and homelessness crisis for 34 years, creating innovative solutions to assist those in need. This year, thanks to generous donations, LIHI has provided support to more than 10,000 people. The message highlights the impact of year-end gifts and encourages continued contributions to improve lives in the community.
