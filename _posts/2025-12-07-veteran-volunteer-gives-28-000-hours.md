  <h2>Veteran and volunteer gives back through art</h2>



  <p>“It all started with art,” said volunteer Maggie DeRee. That’s how she began what is now a 25-year relationship with VA, clocking in nearly 28,000 volunteer hours.   </p>



  <p>DeRee, also a Veteran, went to basic training at a time when women were still referred to as “WACs,” the Women’s Army Corps. She served for 20 years in the Army Reserve as a chef. During that time, she served in Korea, where she learned a lot about kimchi, and also deployed to Saudi Arabia during Desert Storm.  </p>



  <p>Her love of art led her to the Veterans Creative Arts Festival. It was through that experience, and the people she met, that she became a <a>volunteer</a> and began giving back to the Veteran community.  </p>



  <h2>A lifelong mission to serve through creativity and compassion</h2>



  <p>DeRee’s commitment and tireless efforts have touched the lives of countless individuals. Primarily assisting the Recreation/Creative Arts department with events and projects, she has contributed significantly to the <a>Center for Development and Civic Engagement (CDCE) </a>office. Since its inception, she has volunteered at the Monthly Pop-up Veteran Food Pantry, distributing fresh produce to those in need. She also played a vital role in the National Veterans Wheelchair Games, both 20 years ago and again this past summer when the Games were held in Minneapolis. </p>



  <p>DeRee is a pillar of resilience, displaying her unwavering grit and commitment daily. Her radiant energy and enthusiasm infuse the entire office with positivity, lifting spirits and fostering a joyful atmosphere. Even in the face of adversity, she never fails to light up the room with her humor and infectious spirit. </p>



  <p>Unfazed by inclement weather or any obstacles thrown her way, DeRee consistently demonstrates her devotion to serving Veterans. She is a perfect example of selflessness and dedication, enriching the lives of others through her unwavering commitment to VA’s mission.</p>

Maggie DeRee, a veteran and dedicated volunteer, has devoted nearly 28,000 hours to the VA over 25 years. She served 20 years in the Army Reserve as a chef and had deployments in Korea and Saudi Arabia during Desert Storm. Her passion for art led her to the Veterans Creative Arts Festival, which sparked her commitment to giving back to the veteran community.

DeRee primarily supports the Recreation/Creative Arts department by assisting with events and projects. She has played a significant role in the Monthly Pop-up Veteran Food Pantry, distributing fresh produce to veterans in need. Additionally, she contributed to the National Veterans Wheelchair Games, participating both 20 years ago and recently in Minneapolis.

Known for her resilience, DeRee brings positivity and enthusiasm to her volunteer work, uplifting those around her with humor and spirit. Regardless of challenges, including inclement weather, she remains dedicated to serving veterans, embodying selflessness and commitment to the VA's mission.
