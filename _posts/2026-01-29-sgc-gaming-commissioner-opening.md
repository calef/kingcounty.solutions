  <a>Apply For Position</a>
  <p>Department: Snoqualmie Gaming Commission<br>
  Job Status: 4-Year Term<br>
  Salary/Hourly: Stipend<br>
  Reports To: Tribal Council<br>
  Grade/Level: Stipend<br>
  Travel Required: Minimal<br>
  Work Schedule: Varied<br>
  Positions Supervised: None</p>
  <p><strong>POSITION RESTRICTIONS</strong></p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Only Snoqualmie Tribal Member are eligible to apply.</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Must reside in Washington State.</strong></li>
  </ul>
  <p>POSITION DESCRIPTION<br>
  The Snoqualmie Gaming Commission has the authority and responsibility to regulate Gaming Activities within the Tribe’s Indian Lands. This authority includes the power to regulate the Gaming Operations, Gaming Facility, and to license all persons subject to the Commission’s licensing authority under the Tribe’s Gaming Act and Regulations. The Commission ensures that all Gaming Activities conducted within the Tribe’s Indian Lands comply with applicable Tribal, State,  and Federal laws.</p>
  <p>ESSENTIAL DUTIES</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Read, interpret, implement, and disseminate gaming laws and regulations consistent with the Gaming Act.</li>
  <li>Uphold and ensure the Commission has the power, duty, and responsibility to carry out the Tribe’s regulatory requirements under applicable law, enforce such requirements, protect the integrity of gaming activities, and prevent the appearance of impropriety.</li>
  <li>Establish the overall direction of the agency, review the goals and strategic plan, and inform Commission staff of the goals and initiatives to be achieved.</li>
  <li>Supervise, and evaluate the Executive Director of the Commission.</li>
  <li>Monitor Commission departments such as administration, licensing, internal audit and gaming agents.</li>
  <li>Review and prepare the agency’s annual budget for Tribal Council approval.</li>
  <li>Publicize regulations that implement, interpret, and otherwise further the Gaming Act. Provide direction and guidance on amending internal controls.</li>
  <li>Review and approve SGC administrative policies and procedures. Authorize and review agency inspections, investigations, and audits. Evaluate results and determine the need for further action.</li>
  <li>Review daily incident reports and daily log reports. Review all internal audit reports and provide feedback and direction on the audit results presented.</li>
  <li>Review licensing activity, statistics, and investigations affecting a licensee’s suitability for licensure.<br>
  Hold formal hearings and take action as appropriate.</li>
  <li>Issue facility license to the gaming operation upon review of facility inspection reports and audits pertaining to environmental health and public safety.</li>
  <li>Prepare for and attend meetings with the Tribal Council, SGC, and others as directed.</li>
  <li>Develop necessary documentation and protocols. Establish joint cooperative working relationships with federal, state, and local jurisdictions as necessary and appropriate to ensure effective regulation of the Tribe’s gaming  ctivities.</li>
  <li>Act with the Commission as an appellate body for Commission member grievances, Casino employee and vendor license issues, and patron disputes.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>ADDITIONAL DUTIES</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Maintain high ethical standards and ensure agency staff follow all SGC ethics regulations and safety rules and regulations.</li>
  <li>Pursue appropriate professional development activities to maintain a high level of proficiency and working knowledge of the gaming industry.</li>
  <li>The Tribe values its commitment to community service and the well-being of its members. In recognition of this, each Commissioner may be expected to perform duties from time to time which may not be related to their regular  esponsibilities.</li>
  <li>Other duties as assigned</li>
  </ul>
  <p>KNOWLEDGE, SKILLS, AND ABILITIES:<br>
  To perform this job successfully, an individual must be able to perform each essential duty satisfactorily. The requirements listed below are representative of the knowledge, skill and/or ability required.</p>
  <p>POSITION REQUIREMENTS:</p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Only Snoqualmie Tribal Member are eligible to apply.</strong></li>
  <li>Demonstrated professional experience with oversight and monitoring of regulated operations.</li>
  <li>Demonstrated understanding of the gaming licensing process.</li>
  <li>Excellent customer service skills and written and verbal communication. Demonstrated ability to communicate with guests, coworkers, state representatives, federal representatives, and the Casino’s entire workforce in a professional and courteous manner.</li>
  <li>Demonstrated ability to write clear and accurate reports such as formal hearing decisions and orders. Proficiency with Microsoft Office programs.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>EDUCATION &amp; EXPERIENCE</p>
  <p>REQUIRED<br>
  High School diploma or equivalent.</p>
  <p>PREFERRED<br>
  Bachelor’s degree from a regionally accredited institution.</p>
  <p>ADDITIONAL INFORMATION</p>
  <ul>
  <li>This position is appointed by Tribal Council for a term of four years, in-line with the Snoqualmie Tribal Gaming Act.</li>
  <li>Compensation is a monthly stipend for a yearly amount of $63,000 USD.</li>
  <li>This is not considered an employment position, as such, no benefits are provided.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>OTHER REQUIREMENTS</p>
  <ul>
  <li><strong>Must be an enrolled Snoqualmie Indian Tribe Member, unless otherwise advertised.</strong></li>
  <li><strong>Must reside in Washington State.</strong></li>
  <li>Current and valid Washington State driver’s license.</li>
  <li>Must be able to pass a thorough pre-employment background investigation.</li>
  <li>Must be able to obtain and maintain a Gaming License.</li>
  <li>Must possess sensitivity to Native American culture.</li>
  <li>Must be able to work a varied work schedule.</li>
  <li>Must be eligible under the Tribal Gaming Act 7.07 Commissioner Qualifications and Eligibility Determinations.</li>
  </ul>

The Snoqualmie Tribe is seeking applications for the position of SGC Gaming Commissioner, an appointed role with a four-year term and a stipend of $63,000 per year. Only Snoqualmie Tribal Members residing in Washington State are eligible to apply. The commissioner will oversee the regulation of gaming activities on the Tribe's Indian lands, ensuring compliance with Tribal, State, and Federal laws.

Key responsibilities include interpreting and implementing gaming laws, supervising the Executive Director, monitoring various departments, and preparing the agency's annual budget. The commissioner will also handle licensing activities, review reports, and act as an appellate body for grievances and disputes related to gaming operations.

Candidates should have professional experience in regulated operations, excellent communication skills, and proficiency in Microsoft Office. A high school diploma is required, with a preferred bachelor's degree. Applicants must also hold a valid Washington State driver’s license and pass a background investigation. The position is not considered employment and does not offer benefits. The Tribe emphasizes the importance of sensitivity to Native American culture in fulfilling this role.
