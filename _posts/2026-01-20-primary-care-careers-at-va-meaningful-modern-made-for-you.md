feed_content: |-
  <p>Did you know that every Veteran receiving health care through the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is provided a primary care physician? Over 9 million Veterans are enrolled in the VA health care system, so we’re always looking for qualified, caring primary care physicians to join our team and provide the best care to those who served.</p>



  <p>Primary care at VA goes far beyond routine checkups; it’s the foundation of a care model built around each Veteran’s life, goals and well-being. Keep reading to learn more about how primary care physicians fit into <a>VA’s whole health approach</a> while leveraging the latest technology. Explore your next steps toward a rewarding career serving Veterans today.</p>



  <h2>VA primary care is meaningful</h2>



  <p>VA primary care physicians aren’t just diagnosing conditions; they’re also partnering with Veterans on their entire health journey through a whole health approach that considers physical, emotional and social factors. As a key member of a Veteran’s <a>Patient Aligned Care Team (PACT)</a>, primary care physicians are often the first providers to see Veterans as they start their journey to health. They often get to spend more quality time with their patients as they are not bound by the restraints of insurance companies and profitability, resulting in meaningful, trusting relationships.</p>



  <p>“I really encourage people to do primary care because you get to be so close with your patients,” said <a>Dr. Stacey S. Weeks</a>. “You get that doctor-patient relationship that you don’t get with a lot of specialty medicine.”</p>



  <h2>VA primary care is modern</h2>



  <p>With access to some of the most advanced tools in modern medicine, from integrated electronic health records to industry-leading telehealth platforms and innovative prevention initiatives, VA primary care physicians can deliver more coordinated, informed and personalized care than ever before. VA values <a>research and innovation</a> and connects with external partners across industries to ignite transformative changes in health care.</p>



  <h2>VA primary care is made for you</h2>



  <p>VA offers employee benefits that fit your lifestyle, like consistent schedules, generous personal and sick leave, and <a>education incentives</a> to keep you at the top of your game. You’ll also have the flexibility to work at any of our nationwide facilities on one license and with less administrative burden than in the private sector.</p>



  <p>“After being out in private practice, and then being at VA, the benefits at VA are really nice,” said Dr. Weeks.</p>



  <h2>Join VA</h2>



  <p>If you’re all about Veterans like we are, consider a rewarding primary care career at VA. Learn more about working at VA on <a>VA Careers</a>. </p>

Every Veteran receiving health care through the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is assigned a primary care physician. With over 9 million Veterans enrolled in the VA health care system, the VA is actively seeking qualified primary care physicians to provide essential care. Primary care at the VA is more than just routine checkups; it is part of a holistic health model that focuses on each Veteran’s life, goals, and well-being. Primary care physicians work closely with Veterans, forming meaningful relationships that go beyond simple diagnosis. As part of a Veteran’s Patient Aligned Care Team (PACT), they play a crucial role in guiding Veterans through their health journeys. The VA offers access to advanced medical tools, including integrated electronic health records and telehealth platforms, enabling more coordinated and personalized care. Additionally, the VA provides attractive employee benefits such as consistent schedules, generous leave, and education incentives. Physicians enjoy the flexibility to work at various facilities with less administrative burden compared to private practice. Those interested in a fulfilling primary care career dedicated to serving Veterans are encouraged to explore opportunities with the VA.
