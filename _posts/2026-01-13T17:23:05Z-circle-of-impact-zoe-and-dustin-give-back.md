  <p><strong>Meet Zoe Rohaly and Dustin Howe,</strong> the duo behind <a href="https://www.emc-companies.com/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">EMC Companies</a>, a local expert project and vendor management company, rooted in service and care.</p>



  <p>Community is at the heart of the work at EMC Companies. Their love for helping others navigate complex projects naturally evolved into a commitment to giving back. Today, Zoe and Dustin are active supporters of Bellevue LifeSpring.</p>



  <p>Zoe, a local of King County who grew up in Kent, built a multifaceted career in event planning and marine construction, where she met her Fiancé and business partner, Dustin. Originally from Phoenix, Dustin spent most of his life around custom home building before shifting into property management and eventually settling in the Pacific Northwest.</p>



  <p>When building EMC Companies, the couple knew that supporting their community would be central to their vision. As their business grew, they found themselves seeking a meaningful way to give back. Through connections in the community, they discovered Bellevue LifeSpring.</p>



  <p>Their journey with the organization began with small steps. They organized a coat and food drive, volunteered during the holiday season, and attended the Step Up to the Plate Benefit Luncheon, Bellevue LifeSpring’s annual spring gathering that brings together hundreds of neighbors, businesses, and community leaders in support of Bellevue children.</p>



  <p>“After attending the luncheon two years in a row, truly understanding the need in the Bellevue community was a game-changing moment for me,” shared Zoe. “I started bringing Dustin with me.”</p>



  <p>As they learned more, Zoe and Dustin wanted to do more. They volunteered at the Bellevue Family Hub, helping to prepare a welcoming space where Bellevue families can access in-person services, such as nutrition support and emergency rental assistance.</p>



  <p>For Dustin, giving back with Bellevue LifeSpring carries personal meaning. He grew up facing food insecurity—an experience shared by more than 5,000 children right here in Bellevue.</p>



  <p>“It’s not always about the dollar amount you give,” shared Dustin. “Incorporating your skills and time can be incredibly valuable. Zoe and I have so many ideas to share, and I can personally offer perspective from someone who’s been in the shoes of the families Bellevue LifeSpring serves.” &nbsp;</p>



  <p>One of their favorite ways to give back is by participating in the ‘Tis the Season Holiday Giving Program—a collaborative holiday support program hosted by Bellevue LifeSpring, the Boys and Girls Clubs of Bellevue, and Rainier Athletes.</p>



  <p>“When you’re a kid who gets a gift from a giving tree, your parents often don’t know what that gift is; they just hope it’s something you’ll love,” Dustin shared. “What I love about the ‘Tis the Season program is that parents get to choose the gifts themselves. You gave them back that piece of pride, and that makes all the difference.”</p>



  <p>Their experiences volunteering deepened their connection to Bellevue LifeSpring, and for Zoe, it sparked an even greater motivation to get more involved. Last year, she joined the Bellevue LifeSpring One Circle, a group dedicated to supporting children in Bellevue through fundraising, advocacy, and hands-on community engagement.</p>



  <p>“At first, I was definitely intimidated about becoming a circle member,” Zoe admitted. “But, once I joined, I realized everyone shares the same goal: helping our neighbors. One Circle has given me a community of women who care deeply about making a difference.”</p>



  <p><strong><em>Thank you, Zoe and Dustin, for your generosity and support of Bellevue kids!</em></strong></p>

Zoe Rohaly and Dustin Howe, co-founders of EMC Companies, are dedicated to community service through their project and vendor management firm. Both have diverse backgrounds: Zoe's experience includes event planning and marine construction, while Dustin has a history in custom home building and property management. Their commitment to giving back is embodied in their support for Bellevue LifeSpring, an organization focused on aiding local families.

Initially, they engaged with Bellevue LifeSpring by organizing food and coat drives, volunteering during the holidays, and attending the Step Up to the Plate Benefit Luncheon. This experience deepened their understanding of the community's needs, prompting them to volunteer at the Bellevue Family Hub, which provides essential services to families.

Dustin's personal history with food insecurity informs their philanthropic efforts. He emphasizes the importance of contributing time and skills rather than just financial resources. One of their preferred initiatives is the ‘Tis the Season Holiday Giving Program, which allows parents to choose gifts for their children, restoring a sense of pride during the holidays.

Zoe has also joined the Bellevue LifeSpring One Circle, a group focused on supporting local children through fundraising and community engagement, further solidifying their commitment to making a difference in Bellevue.
