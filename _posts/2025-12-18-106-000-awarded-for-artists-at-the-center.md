$106,000 has been awarded to support the Artists at the Center initiative, a collaboration between the Seattle Office of Arts & Culture and Seattle Center. This project aims to promote the work of local artists and enhance community engagement. Funding will be used for various artistic projects and programming that involve the surrounding neighborhoods. The initiative seeks to create a vibrant arts scene while fostering connections between artists and the community. The support from the Uptown Arts & Culture Coalition and local residents is crucial for the project's success, as it emphasizes the importance of community involvement in the arts. The funding will help ensure that artists can showcase their talents and contribute to the cultural landscape of Seattle.
