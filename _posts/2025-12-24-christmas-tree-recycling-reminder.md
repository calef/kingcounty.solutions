Normandy Park WM customers can recycle their Christmas trees for free on Jan. 6 and Jan. 20, 2026. To prepare trees for pickup, they must be clean and free of decorations, including lights, ornaments, and tinsel. Customers are also advised to remove any tree stands. Trees should be placed out for pickup by 7 a.m. on the scheduled dates. This service provides a convenient way for residents to dispose of their holiday trees responsibly.
