  <p><a>WA Dept. of Ecology – Zero Emission School Bus and Fleet Transition Grant Program 2025-2027</a></p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Who can apply: </strong>School bus owners who transport students to K-12 schools overseen by the Washington Washington Office of Superintendent of Public Instruction (OSPI) for the zero-emission school bus projects. Cities, counties, Tribes, public utility districts/co-ops, transit authorities, school districts including skills centers, state governments, nonprofit organizations, and local clean air agencies for the vocational training projects.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Qualifying projects:</strong> Zero-emission school bus projects, and vocational training projects.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Total funding amount: </strong>Approximately $15 million.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Application due date:</strong> April 23, 2026, 4 p.m.</li>
  </ul>



  <p><a>WA Dept. of Transportation – Regional Mobility Grant</a></p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <strong>Who can apply: </strong>Cities, counties, ports, public transit agencies, regional transit authorities, regional transportation planning organizations, and metropolitan planning organizations in Washington state.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Qualifying projects: </strong>Among multiple categories, one applies to electric vehicles. Charging equipment for electric vehicles. Charger projects exclusively for single-occupancy vehicles are not eligible, as they do not result in a reduction of vehicle trips or vehicles miles travelled.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Total funding amount:</strong> In the 2025-2027 biennium, WSDOT had approximately $62.6 million available in grant funding. The funding level for the 2027-2029 biennium will be determined by the Legislature during the 2027 legislative session.</li>



  <li>
  <strong>Application due date: </strong>Pre-applications are due by 3 p.m., March 3, 2026. Grant applications are due by 3 p.m., June 30, 2026.</li>
  </ul>



  <p>For a list of previous and future funding opportunities, please refer to the <a>EV Council Program Catalog</a>.</p>
