In a thrilling men's hockey quarterfinal at the Winter Olympics in Milan, the United States defeated Sweden 2-1 in overtime, securing a spot in the semifinals. The game saw Team USA, led by Quinn Hughes, open the scoring with a goal from Dylan Larkin in the second period. Sweden equalized late in the third period with a goal from Mika Zibanejad, forcing the match into overtime after regulation ended in a 1-1 tie.

The overtime period featured intense action, with both teams creating scoring opportunities, but it was Hughes who ultimately clinched the victory, scoring off a shot that hit the post and slipped past the Swedish goalie. This victory came against a strong Swedish team, which had won three of its four games in the tournament and featured a full roster of NHL players.

As the tournament progresses, the semifinals are set for Friday, with the gold medal game scheduled for Sunday. Canada also advanced after defeating Czechia 4-3 in overtime, raising the possibility of a Canada-U.S. final. Other quarterfinals saw Slovakia and Finland secure their spots in the semifinals as well.
