On January 22, El Centro de la Raza hosted over 40 community members at the Centilia Cultural Center for an event focused on air quality and environmental justice. Led by Gabby, the Weatherization & Environmental Justice Coordinator, participants engaged in discussions about the impact of air quality on health and the importance of community-driven solutions. This event marked the kickoff of the Community Air Quality Workshop Series, which includes five workshops running through 2026, featuring free food and practical resources.

In observance of Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Day on January 19, El Centro staff participated in a morning of learning, reflecting on migration, power, and justice through the documentary "Harvest of Empire." A Whistle Workshop emphasized accountability and civil rights.

On January 26, El Centro de la Raza joined the ICE Out for Good rally in Seattle, advocating for immigrant families and calling for an end to funding for ICE. The organization was represented by leadership and included participants from various backgrounds. El Centro also took part in Seattle’s 43rd Annual MLK Jr. Celebration, emphasizing the theme of continued collective action toward justice and equity.
