  <p><strong>Course Description:</strong></p>



  <p>This presentation explores Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) — the non-medical factors that influence health outcomes. These factors include where people are born, grow, live, work, and age, and they significantly impact a person’s overall health and well-being.</p>



  <p><strong>Course Objectives:</strong></p>



  <ol>
  <li>Defining SDOH: Understanding what social determinants of health are and why they matter.</li>



  <li>How do SDOH impact physical and mental health.</li>



  <li>Emphasize the role of healthcare organizations, policymakers, and community partners in addressing SDOH</li>
  </ol>



  <p><strong>Presenter Bio:</strong></p>



  <p>Alicia Tieder, MSW, LICSW, MBA<br>Vice President, Organizational Learning, Patient &amp; Family Experience, and Health Outcomes<br>Seattle Children’s Hospital.</p>



  <p>Began my career as a Clinical Social Worker (LICSW) and transitioned to Seattle Children’s Hospital in 2015. I currently serve as the Vice President of Organizational Learning, Patient &amp; Family Experience, and Health Outcomes. Through my experience working with patients, I focus on improving systems to reduce barriers to high-quality care and to support a positive and meaningful work experience for staff. In 2019, I became the Director of the Center for Health Outcomes and have since led efforts to drive measurable improvements in patient outcomes, care quality, and workforce engagement.</p>



  <p>I lead multiple teams, including the Center for Health Outcomes; Workforce Community &amp; Connection; Organizational Learning; Patient and Family Education, Patient Family Experience, and the hospital’s Administrative Fellowship Program. The teams I support are responsible for generating insights, developing strategies, and implementing solutions to advance Seattle Children’s vision of delivering exceptional patient and family experiences, improving health outcomes, and fostering an engaged and supported workforce. I strive to emphasize driving system-level change through quality improvement, operational excellence, and collaborative leadership to ensure every patient receives the best possible care.</p>

This recorded webinar offers 1.5 CDE credits and focuses on Social Determinants of Health (SDOH), which are non-medical factors influencing health outcomes. Factors like birthplace, living conditions, work environment, and aging significantly affect overall health and well-being. The course aims to define SDOH, explain their impact on physical and mental health, and highlight the roles of healthcare organizations, policymakers, and community partners in addressing these determinants.

Alicia Tieder, MSW, LICSW, and Vice President for Organizational Learning, Patient & Family Experience, and Health Outcomes at Seattle Children’s Hospital, presents the course. Tieder has a background as a Clinical Social Worker and focuses on improving systems to eliminate barriers to quality care while enhancing staff work experiences. She has led initiatives to improve patient outcomes, care quality, and workforce engagement since joining Seattle Children’s in 2015. Tieder emphasizes the importance of system-level change through quality improvement, operational excellence, and collaborative leadership to ensure optimal patient care.
