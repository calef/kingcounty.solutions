title: Preparing for Potential Wind Storm and Continued Effects of Post-Flooding
topic_titles: []
---

Residents are urged to prepare for a wind advisory and ongoing post-flooding effects. To ensure safety during potential power outages, individuals should stay away from downed power lines and use flashlights instead of candles. It is important to keep refrigerator and freezer doors closed to maintain food safety. Gas ranges and indoor cookers should not be used for heating, and generators must never be operated indoors due to the risk of carbon monoxide poisoning.

To maintain generators, they should be run under a small load for 15-20 minutes monthly, with extra fuel stored safely in approved containers. Fuel supplies should be rotated every few months for freshness.

For vehicle safety, residents should regularly inspect tire tread and pressure, consider winter tires, and ensure all fluids, especially antifreeze and windshield washer fluid, are topped off. Battery performance can decline in cold weather, so testing is recommended. An emergency kit should be kept in vehicles, containing jumper cables, a flashlight, warm blankets, non-perishable snacks, a small shovel, and sand or cat litter for traction.
