  <p><strong>El Centro de la Raza Joins Seattle’s 43rd Annual MLK Jr. Celebration</strong></p>



  <p>El Centro de la Raza proudly participated in Seattle’s 43rd Annual MLK Jr. Celebration, marching alongside community members in solidarity, reflection, and collective purpose. Gathering at Garfield High School, our team joined a multigenerational, multicultural crowd united by a shared commitment to justice, dignity, and belonging.</p>



  <p>Organized by the Seattle MLK Coalition, this year’s celebration centered the theme <em>“Where do we go from here?”</em>—a powerful call to action that invited our community to not only honor Dr. King’s legacy, but to continue organizing and moving forward together. The march served as a reminder that the work toward equity and justice is ongoing, and that collective action remains essential to building a more just future for all.</p>

El Centro de la Raza participated in Seattle's 43rd Annual Martin Luther King Jr. Celebration, marching with community members to honor Dr. King's legacy. The event took place at Garfield High School and featured a diverse crowd united in their commitment to justice, dignity, and belonging. Organized by the Seattle MLK Coalition, this year’s theme was "Where do we go from here?" which encouraged participants to reflect on their collective purpose and take action for equity and justice. The march highlighted the ongoing struggle for a more just future, emphasizing the importance of continued organizing and community solidarity.
