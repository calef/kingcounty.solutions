Recovering from recent flooding can be difficult for many residents. King County is focused on safety and support during this challenging time. The county has launched a dedicated webpage designed to be a one-stop resource for recovery information and assistance. This site will provide essential resources to help individuals and families navigate the recovery process in the coming days and weeks. Residents are encouraged to visit kingcounty.gov/flood-recovery for more details and support.
