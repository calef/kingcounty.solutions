In the latest episode of the podcast "Between Bites," Dr. Jillian Lampert discusses the challenges of recovering from eating disorders, emphasizing the emotional turmoil that often leads individuals to question their commitment to recovery. She acknowledges that while eating disorders may feel protective, they ultimately hinder joy, health, and freedom. Lampert explores personal motivations for recovery, noting that these reasons can evolve over time. The episode provides practical strategies for moments of ambivalence, reinforcing that recovery is a difficult yet rewarding journey for many.

Dr. Lampert, who serves as the Vice President of Strategy and Public Affairs for The Emily Program, has extensive credentials in nutrition and epidemiology, making her insights particularly valuable. "Between Bites" is part of the broader "Peace Meal" podcast series, which addresses various topics related to eating disorders and body image. Listeners are encouraged to engage with the show by suggesting topics or sharing their experiences, fostering a supportive community around the complexities of recovery. The podcast is available on major streaming platforms, including Apple Podcasts and Spotify.
