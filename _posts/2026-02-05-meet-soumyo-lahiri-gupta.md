Soumyo Lahiri-Gupta has joined the Inatai Foundation as a Policy & Advocacy Manager. He has a strong belief in the power of community to create change and has been active in social causes since he was 12, including political campaigns and immigrant rights. Soumyo is excited to support local communities in Washington, emphasizing the importance of their voices in driving systemic change. He identifies with multiple communities, including the Indian American and South Asian diaspora, and is committed to fostering collective action in Clark County and Vancouver.

Soumyo finds hope in his community's resilience, noting that they come together during tough times to support one another. He resonates with Inatai's value of joy and appreciates Vancouver's unique identity, highlighting its strong community ties. He enjoys reading, spending time with friends and family, watching TV shows, and performing stand-up comedy when not working. One of his recent recommended reads is “Everything Is Tuberculosis” by John Green, which explores systemic barriers in health care.
