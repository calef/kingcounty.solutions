Residents of Auburn near Pike Street NE and Pike Place NE are urged to prepare for possible evacuation due to flooding from storm water and the Green River. A Level 2 (Get Set) notice has been reissued, indicating that residents should be ready to leave at a moment’s notice. Authorities advise against walking or driving through moving water or on flooded roadways.
