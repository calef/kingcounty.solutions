  of Washington State I/DD Student Stipends</h2>\n<p>The <a>Arc of Washington State
  Trust Fund</a> offers a <a>Student Stipend Program</a> for the 2026–2027 academic
  year, awarding stipends of up to $5,000 to students preparing for careers in the
  field of intellectual and developmental disabilities.</p>\n<p><img src=\"https://ihdd.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/uw-student-raising-their-hand-in-class.jpg\">The
  program is open to upper-division undergraduate and graduate students enrolled full
  time at Washington State institutions who have demonstrated interest and experience
  in intellectual and developmental disabilities.</p>\n<p>Stipend funds support tuition,
  books, and general living expenses and are distributed in two installments. An initial
  payment is sent to the student’s university in the summer, with the remaining balance
  released in January following confirmation of full-time enrollment.</p>\n<h3>Application
  requirements include:</h3>\n<ul>\n<li>A short essay describing interest in the I/DD
  field</li>\n<li>Academic background and immediate and long-term goals</li>\n<li>Two
  faculty endorsements</li>\n<li>Official transcripts</li>\n</ul>\n<h3>Key dates:</h3>\n<ul>\n<li>Application
  deadline: March 15, 2026</li>\n<li>Award notifications: By April 30, 2026</li>\n</ul>\n<p>The
  application form and full instructions are available <a>ArcTrustFund.org</a></p>\n<p>Questions
  about the stipend program may be directed to <a>info@arctrustfund.org</a>.</p>\n\n\t<div></div>\n</div>\n\n\t</div>\n\n<div></div>\n\n</div>\n\n<div>\n\t\n<div></div>\n\n\n<aside>\n\t<div>\n\t\t<section><div>\n<div>\n\t<div>\n<div><div>\n<div><div><img

The Arc of Washington State Trust Fund is offering a Student Stipend Program for the 2026–2027 academic year, providing stipends of up to $5,000 for students pursuing careers in intellectual and developmental disabilities (I/DD). The program is available to full-time upper-division undergraduate and graduate students at Washington State institutions who show interest and experience in the I/DD field. The funds can be used for tuition, books, and living expenses, distributed in two payments—one in the summer to the university and the second in January after confirming full-time enrollment.

Applicants must submit a short essay about their interest in I/DD, detail their academic background and goals, provide two faculty endorsements, and include official transcripts. The application deadline is March 15, 2026, with award notifications sent by April 30, 2026. Further details and the application form can be found on the Arc Trust Fund website. Questions regarding the stipend program can be directed to their email address.
