  <p class="p1">When heavy rains and winds are forecast for our area, it’s always good to know what procedures you should think of taking to be better prepared and the resources that are available to you in case there is a problem, especially when flooding and high winds are involved.</p>
  <p class="p1">The following are some of the resources you should be familiar with:</p>
  <h2>Sandbags are available</h2>
  <ul>
  <li>Sandbags are available for filling for free at several locations in King County. For more information, visit <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkingcounty.gov%2Fen%2Fdept%2Fdnrp%2Fnature-recreation%2Fenvironment-ecology-conservation%2Fflood-services%2Fbe-ready%2Fsandbag-distribution-use/1/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/PhIs27anOV0esF5U9m06d3lrg9j5Sx1TFDGOMZDSDTI=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">kingcounty.gov/sandbags</a> or call 206-477-4812 (language interpretation available).</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Stay safe and remember to:</h2>
  <ul>
  <li>Never walk or drive over flooded roads or standing water. For more information, visit the <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fwww.weather.gov%2Fsafety%2Fflood-turn-around-dont-drown/1/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/b3djDet9a2M8p-Lu15RtVchnn5QPXuxkzEYghXfTETw=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">National Weather Service’s Turn Around Don’t Drown page</a>.</li>
  <li>Contact the 24/7 Road Helpline at 206-477-8100 if you see something on the road. For the most up-to-date information on road closures and lane restrictions in unincorporated King County, visit the <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkingcounty.gov%2Fmycommute/1/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/oaZE1dgh4Rb5C5cd9H6g26GvTFPjK24hz9qJC5oIrXE=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">MyCommute page</a> (<a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkingcounty.gov%2Fmycommute/2/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/Mtxe3ArdCSuR8Hf7ouTQr_kgqfcbQKTzrgG1G2l7D-0=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">kingcounty.gov/MyCommute</a>).</li>
  <li>Avoid touching flood water, which can have sewage and other chemicals. Wash hands and pets after any contact with flood water. Throw out any food from gardens that have touched flood water.</li>
  <li>Wear gloves, goggles, an N95 mask, and protective gear when cleaning after a flood to protect from cuts, sewage, mold, and other health risks.</li>
  <li>Have a septic system? Don&#8217;t use the system during a flood, visit <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fdoh.wa.gov%2Fcommunity-and-environment%2Fwastewater-management%2Fseptic-system%2Ffloods/1/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/i1duyKMZPA3afq6D4evcldQhTCCRC7JRiAZd6g-x05I=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">https://doh.wa.gov/community-and-environment/wastewater-management/septic-system/floods</a> for tips on how to manage sewage impacts.</li>
  </ul>
  <h2>Landslides</h2>
  <p>Landslides are an increasing threat as hillsides become saturated with water. <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkcemergency.com%2F/2/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/ZdOCAkOPrNKPoVhamLST3fQ9xcSX_J_fmRN6vu7tMVs=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">The King County Office of Emergency Management</a> offers these signs of an impending landslide:</p>
  <ul>
  <li>Bulging ground at the base of a slope</li>
  <li>Outside walls, walkways, or stairs pulling away from structures</li>
  <li>Widening cracks in the ground or on paved areas such as streets or driveways</li>
  <li>New cracks appearing in plaster, tile, brick, or foundations</li>
  <li>Water breaking through the ground in new locations</li>
  <li>Fences, retaining walls, utility poles, or trees tilting or moving</li>
  </ul>
  <p>Visit <a href="https://links-1.govdelivery.com/CL0/https:%2F%2Fkingcounty.gov%2Fdepts%2Femergency-management%2Fhazards%2Flandslide.aspx/1/0100019b0093c7dd-453a1f71-024a-4df1-996f-7fbc531e154a-000000/N4LeBntLJ5nGVGR4-7tg7ECpOHdDA5IsMY_g48WVZOs=434" target="_blank" rel="nofollow noopener noreferrer">kingcounty.gov/landslide</a> for more information.</p>

Residents in King County are advised to prepare for heavy rains and high winds, which could lead to flooding and landslides. Free sandbags are available at various locations to help mitigate flooding risks. Individuals should avoid walking or driving through flooded areas and report road issues to the 24/7 Road Helpline. Floodwater can be contaminated, so it’s essential to wash hands and pets after contact and wear protective gear when cleaning up afterward. 

Landslides pose an additional risk, especially in saturated areas. Signs of potential landslides include bulging ground, widening cracks, and new water flow in unusual places. Residents are urged to stay informed about power outages through their utility providers, including Puget Sound Energy and Seattle City Light. 

If downed trees are encountered, those on power lines should be reported to the respective power company, while fallen trees on private property are the owner’s responsibility. For further assistance, resources such as the "Be Flood Ready" brochure and flood recovery materials are available online in multiple languages. The community is encouraged to stay informed and prepared to ensure safety during severe weather conditions.
