Keith Hughes, founder of Westside Neighbors Shelter (WSN), announced that the organization has successfully met and surpassed its fundraising goals, ensuring it can provide food and shelter for individuals throughout the remainder of winter. WSN's board continues to focus on enhancing services for its clients while ensuring financial sustainability and community safety. The shelter expresses deep gratitude to volunteers, donors, staff, and neighbors for their support and emphasizes its commitment to treating unhoused individuals with dignity and respect. As the cold months continue, there remains a pressing need for cold weather clothing and essentials, including cotton gloves, underwear, and socks. WSN encourages community members to contribute through donations of funds and clothing items, and provides a link to its Amazon wish list for those interested in supporting its efforts.
