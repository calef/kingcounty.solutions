Archbishop Paul D. Etienne emphasizes the need for unity and compassion in his pastoral letter, "A Well-Ordered Society Rooted in Truth, Justice and Peace." He calls for a collective shift away from political agendas and toward fundamental human values such as decency, truth, integrity, and charity. The Archbishop urges individuals to focus on serving the common good, highlighting that all humans are created to live in harmony and peace. He believes this is a universal longing shared by everyone. The message advocates for prioritizing community and mutual respect, suggesting that a well-ordered society can be achieved through these principles.
