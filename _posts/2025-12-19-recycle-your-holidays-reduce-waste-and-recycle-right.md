The holidays often lead to increased waste from gift wrap, packaging, and food. Seattle Public Utilities encourages residents to make small, mindful choices to reduce waste during this festive season. To recycle effectively, it is important to know what materials can be recycled and how to prepare them properly. For example, only clean and dry paper materials should be placed in recycling bins, while items like plastic gift wrap and foam packaging are not recyclable.

Residents are also urged to consider alternatives to traditional gift wrapping, such as using fabric or reusable materials. Additionally, choosing to give experiences or digital gifts can help minimize physical waste. Seattle Public Utilities emphasizes the importance of preventing waste before it starts and encourages everyone to think about sustainable practices during holiday celebrations. By taking these steps, individuals can enjoy a greener holiday season while making a positive impact on the environment.
