---

The City of Normandy Park is celebrating Arbor Day with an all-ages art poster contest themed “Rooted Together.” This theme highlights the connection between trees and people, emphasizing community strength through care and creativity. Contest entries are welcome from various categories: Preschool, Elementary (grades K-3 and 4-5), Middle school, High school, and Adult. Seven winning entries will be selected, with first-place winners receiving prizes. Posters may be used for community outreach and future tree-related events.

All entries must be submitted by April 15, 2026, either by mail or in person at City Hall. The Arbor Day Celebration is set for April 25, 2026, from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. at Marvista Park, featuring a free tree giveaway, crafts, environmental education, and a book exchange. Additional activities include an Ask the Arborist Station, stormwater education, and live music. Overflow parking will be available at Marvista School. The winning posters will be displayed during the celebration, promoting community engagement and environmental awareness.
