  for Participants: Sound Transit Accessibility Study</h2>\n<p>A research team from
  the University of Washington Sociology Department, in partnership with Sound Transit,
  is seeking participants for a <a>study</a> focused on the experiences of commuters
  with disabilities related to hearing, vision, and mobility.</p>\n<p><img src=\"https://ihdd.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/photo-ai-man-wheelchair-waiting-for-train.jpg\">The
  goal of the study is to evaluate and improve a commuter feedback questionnaire and
  to identify accessibility barriers across Sound Transit services.</p>\n<p>You may
  be eligible if you:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Are 18 years of age or older</li>\n<li>Live in
  the Puget Sound region</li>\n<li>Have an accessibility need related to hearing,
  vision, and or mobility</li>\n<li>Use Sound Transit public transportation services
  at least once per week</li>\n</ul>\n<p>Participation details:</p>\n<ul>\n<li>Interviews

title: 'Call for Participants: Sound Transit Accessibility Study'
topic_titles:

---

A research team from the University of Washington's Sociology Department, in collaboration with Sound Transit, is looking for participants for a study on the experiences of commuters with disabilities related to hearing, vision, and mobility. The study aims to enhance a commuter feedback questionnaire and identify accessibility barriers within Sound Transit services. Eligible participants must be at least 18 years old, reside in the Puget Sound region, have an accessibility need related to hearing, vision, or mobility, and use Sound Transit public transportation at least once a week. Interviews will occur in early February, and participants will receive $100 in e-gift cards as a thank-you. Interested individuals can fill out a recruitment form to participate. For more information, contact Anne Tseng at the University of Washington's Department of Sociology.
