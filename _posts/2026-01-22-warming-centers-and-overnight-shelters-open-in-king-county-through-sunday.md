Warming centers and overnight shelters are open across King County from January 22 to January 25 as temperatures are expected to drop below freezing. Residents in cities such as Auburn, Federal Way, and Renton can access these facilities. Additional sheltering information is available through the King County Regional Homelessness Authority. The public is encouraged to check on neighbors and stay informed about emergency alerts and local conditions. For those needing assistance, King County Emergency Management provides resources and updates on current events and weather conditions.
