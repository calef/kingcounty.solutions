CISC Chinese Musical Instrument Group is an ensemble of instrumentalists and vocalists performing traditional and contemporary music. The group features a variety of instruments, including erhu, flute, electric wind instrument, violin, electronic keyboard, and saxophone. Its mission emphasizes unity, respect, understanding, and inclusiveness, promoting holistic well-being among members and encouraging collective enjoyment, especially among seniors. Through their musical performances, the group aims to foster cultural connections and help audiences appreciate the rich heritage of Chinese musical traditions. The group is open for free performances every Wednesday from 9:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.
