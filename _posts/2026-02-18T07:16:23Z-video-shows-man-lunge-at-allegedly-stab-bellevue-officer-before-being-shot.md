Mohamed Morray Bangura, 38, was charged with first- and second-degree assault after allegedly making a false 911 call to lure police to the Bellevue Transit Center on December 12, 2025. Body camera footage shows Bangura attacking officers as they arrived; he pulled a knife and lunged at Officer Guilherme Spagnolo, while Officer Sean Winebrenner was slashed in the face and stabbed multiple times. In response, Spagnolo shot Bangura three times, injuring him in the arm, leg, and hip. Both Bangura and Winebrenner were treated at Harborview Medical Center, with the officer later discharged and Bangura remaining in serious condition in the ICU as of December 15. Bangura's bail is set at $5 million, with a court appearance scheduled for February 23. Prior to this incident, Bangura had interactions with Bellevue police, including a September altercation at a church where he allegedly threatened a security guard. His behavior on the day of the stabbing included claiming the September officer had fabricated charges against him, indicating ongoing issues with law enforcement.
