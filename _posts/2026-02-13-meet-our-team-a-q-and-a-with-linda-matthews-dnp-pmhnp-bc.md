  <p><b> </b></p>
  <h3>Q: Tell us about your role! What do you do at Thira Health and what do you love most about your job?  What specialties or therapeutic approaches do you bring to your work?</h3>
  <p><strong>A:</strong> My Doctor of Nursing Practice education strengthens my clinical leadership, critical thinking, and ability to integrate research into practice, ensuring high-quality and ethically grounded care. My approach is holistic, patient-centered, and trauma-informed. I value collaboration and shared decision-making, working closely with patients, their families, and therapists. I integrate evidence-based medication management with psychoeducation and supportive therapeutic techniques to address the whole person—not just symptoms.</p>

Her Doctor of Nursing Practice education enhances her clinical leadership and ability to provide ethically grounded care. Matthews' holistic, patient-centered approach emphasizes collaboration with patients, families, and therapists, integrating evidence-based medication management with supportive therapeutic techniques.

Driven by a desire to reduce suffering, she finds fulfillment in helping individuals feel heard and understood during vulnerable moments. Matthews highlights Thira Health's commitment to individualized, compassionate care, which distinguishes it from other providers. She appreciates the strong teamwork and open communication among staff, fostering professional growth and high-quality care.

Looking to the future, Matthews hopes Thira Health will continue to innovate and lead in compassionate mental health treatment, improving access for families in need. She wishes for greater recognition of psychiatric nurse practitioners as highly trained clinicians who provide comprehensive care beyond prescribing medications.
