Team USA's cross-country skiers achieved mixed results in the team sprint events at the Milan Cortina Winter Olympics. The men's team, consisting of Ben Ogden and Gus Schumacher, secured a silver medal in the team free sprint final, finishing with a time of 18:30.3—1.4 seconds behind Norway, who took gold. This marks a historic moment for Ogden, who previously ended a 50-year medal drought for the U.S. in cross-country skiing by winning silver in the men's sprint classic earlier in the Games. Norway's Johannes Hoesflot Klaebo, who dominated the final lap, earned his fifth gold medal of the 2026 Olympics.

In contrast, the women's team of Jessie Diggins and Julia Kern finished fifth in their event, clocking in at 20:41.53—11.54 seconds behind the gold medalists from Sweden. Diggins, who was aiming for her second medal of these Games after winning bronze in the 10km freestyle, had a strong start but the team could not maintain pace as Sweden and Switzerland pulled ahead. The women's event showcased a competitive sprint format, with teams of two alternating laps in a race that began with semi-finals.
