Seattle police are investigating a stabbing that occurred in the Central District on the evening of December 18. Officers responded to reports of the incident around 7:00 p.m. in the 1800 block of South Jackson Street. Upon arrival, they discovered a woman inside an apartment with multiple stab wounds. Emergency medical personnel arrived and transported her to a hospital for treatment. Her condition has not been disclosed. Police have not yet identified any suspects or made any arrests. The investigation is ongoing, and officials are urging anyone with information about the incident to come forward.
