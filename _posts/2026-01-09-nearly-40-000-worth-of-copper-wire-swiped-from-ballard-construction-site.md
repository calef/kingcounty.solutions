Thieves have stolen nearly $40,000 worth of copper wire from a construction site in Ballard over the past four months. The latest incident occurred on New Year's Eve, when nearly a dozen power cables were taken. The thefts began at a seven-story apartment building under construction, with multiple break-ins reported since last summer. The ongoing thefts have raised concerns among construction workers and local residents. Police are investigating the incidents but have not yet identified any suspects. The construction site is now taking additional security measures to prevent further thefts.
