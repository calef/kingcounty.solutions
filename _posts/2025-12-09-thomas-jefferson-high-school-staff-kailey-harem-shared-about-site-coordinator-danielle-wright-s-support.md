Thomas Jefferson High School staff member Kailey Harem praised Site Coordinator Danielle Wright for her support in helping families through the Communities in Schools program. Harem expressed gratitude for the generous contributions, both financial and physical, which enabled the program to assist 25 families. Wright, known for her tough exterior, was touched by the overwhelming support. Harem highlighted that, in addition to the food shown in the provided images, five Early Childhood Education and Assistance Program (ECEAP) families received food assistance the previous Thursday. The staff's collective effort made a significant impact on the community, showcasing the importance of collaboration and support in local initiatives.
