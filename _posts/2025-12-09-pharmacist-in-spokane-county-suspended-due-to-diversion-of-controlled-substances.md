The Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission has suspended the license of Spokane County pharmacist Sandra Lee Symington Bang pending further legal action. Bang is accused of falsifying prescriptions to obtain controlled substances for personal use. The suspension is intended to ensure patient safety and protect public health. Bang has 20 days to request a hearing to contest the allegations against her.

Legal documents related to the case are available online and can be accessed through the Provider Credential Search on the Department of Health website, or by calling 360-236-4700. The Department of Health encourages anyone who believes a health care provider has acted unprofessionally to report their concerns using this number.

The Department of Health in Washington is responsible for regulating health care providers to ensure public health and safety. It establishes and enforces standards for licensing, practice, and discipline to promote quality health care delivery across the state.
