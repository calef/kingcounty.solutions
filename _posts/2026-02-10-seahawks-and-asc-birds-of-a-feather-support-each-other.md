Atlantic Street Center (ASC) celebrates the Seattle Seahawks' Super Bowl victory, highlighting the team's long-standing support for the community. Over the years, the Seahawks have actively participated in various initiatives, from providing food gift cards to families to inviting ASC's Summer Academy students to practice sessions. Local sports teams, including the Kraken, Mariners, Seawolves, Sounders, and Storm, have also contributed significantly to ASC's efforts. The Kraken has donated tickets and signed merchandise, while the Mariners have provided tickets for families to create lasting memories. Rick Rizzs and Toys for Kids support ASC by supplying school backpacks and holiday gifts for over 650 children every December. The Seawolves contributed to funding at ASC's recent gala through match tickets, and the Sounders have included ASC in experiences and sponsorships. The Storm continues to ensure that families can attend home games, fostering a love for basketball. ASC expresses gratitude to the Seahawks and all local teams for their teamwork and commitment to community support.
