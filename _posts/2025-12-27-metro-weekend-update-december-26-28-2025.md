Metro will have service impacts for routes 101, 150, ST 545, ST 550, and ST 554 during the weekend of December 26-28, 2025. On Saturday, December 27, the stop at 4th Avenue S & S Jackson Street (Northbound) will be closed. Passengers should plan for alternative stops or routes when traveling. Metro encourages riders to check for updates and plan their trips accordingly. Affected services may have altered schedules, so it is important to verify any changes before traveling. Metro is committed to providing reliable service and appreciates the understanding of its riders during this time.
