  <p>The Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance Traumatic Injury Protection program (TSGLI) is celebrating 20 years of providing short-term financial support to help eligible service members recover from traumatic injuries. </p>



  <p>Established in December 2005, <a>TSGLI </a>is a traumatic injury protection rider under Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance that provides a one-time payment between $25,000 and $100,000—depending on the type of injury—to any member of the uniformed services covered by SGLI who sustains a traumatic injury that results in certain severe losses. The cost for this coverage is just $1 per month.  </p>



  <p>TSGLI was designed to provide severely injured service members who suffer a traumatic injury, such as loss of sight or hearing, amputation or severe burns. The monetary assistance is designed to help the service member and their family through an often long and arduous treatment and rehabilitation period. TSGLI helps to lessen that economic burden by providing immediate financial relief. For 20 years, VA has proudly managed this crucial coverage program. </p>



  <p>Visit <a>the VA Life Insurance website</a> for more information about TSGLI eligibility, retroactive payments and other program requirements. </p>

The Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance Traumatic Injury Protection program (TSGLI) marks its 20th anniversary in December 2025, having provided essential financial support to eligible service members recovering from traumatic injuries since its inception. TSGLI offers a one-time payment ranging from $25,000 to $100,000, depending on the severity of the injury, to members of the uniformed services covered by Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance (SGLI). The program, which costs just $1 per month, covers serious injuries such as loss of sight, hearing, amputation, or severe burns. This financial assistance is intended to support service members and their families during the challenging treatment and rehabilitation process following traumatic injuries. Over the past two decades, the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) has effectively managed this vital program, helping to alleviate the economic burdens faced by those severely injured in service. For more information on TSGLI eligibility and benefits, individuals can visit the VA Life Insurance website.
