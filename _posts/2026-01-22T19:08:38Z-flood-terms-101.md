King County experienced significant flooding in December 2025, prompting the King County Flood Control District (KCFCD) to create a "Flood Terms 101" guide. This initiative aims to enhance public understanding of flood-related terminology to improve preparedness and safety during such events. The guide is designed for residents without technical backgrounds and defines key terms such as flood phase, levee breach, and flood insurance.

KCFCD focuses on implementing flood risk reduction strategies that protect life and property while considering equity, sustainable livelihoods, and environmental stewardship. The glossary includes terms sourced from FEMA, the National Weather Service, and other agencies, detailing concepts like base flood elevation, flood facilities, and channel migration zones. 

Understanding these terms is crucial as they relate to community resilience and emergency management in the face of climate change and flooding risks. The guide emphasizes the importance of clear communication to ensure that residents can respond effectively during flood events. By familiarizing the public with flood terminology, KCFCD aims to foster a more informed community better equipped to handle future flooding challenges.
