Chief of Police Adrian Barnes will continue to serve the Seattle Police Department. He expressed his gratitude for the opportunity to lead and his commitment to the community. Barnes emphasized that public safety is a shared value and highlighted that the department's priorities will remain focused on serving the people of Seattle. He aims to strengthen relationships within the community and work collaboratively to enhance safety measures. Barnes believes in the importance of building trust and maintaining open communication with residents. His dedication to the role reflects his desire to foster a safer environment for everyone in Seattle.
