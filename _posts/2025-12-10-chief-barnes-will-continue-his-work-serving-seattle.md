Chief of Police Carmen Best will continue to serve the Seattle Police Department, expressing gratitude for the opportunity to lead in a city she cares deeply about. In her statement, Best emphasized that public safety is a shared value among the community and the department. She reaffirmed the department's commitment to maintaining its priorities while working collaboratively with community members to enhance safety and build trust. Best's ongoing leadership aims to strengthen relationships between the police and the public, focusing on transparency and accountability. Her dedication reflects a commitment to fostering a safer Seattle for all residents.
