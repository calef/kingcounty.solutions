Upward Bound scholars and their families came together on December 19 for a winter celebration focused on college opportunities. Students from Garfield and Rainier Beach High Schools participated in the event, which included presentations from Upward Bound staff. The gathering aimed to inform families about the college admissions process and the resources available to support students. Attendees engaged in discussions about higher education, helping to foster a supportive community around their academic goals. The celebration also featured holiday-themed activities, creating a festive atmosphere while promoting educational aspirations. The event highlighted Upward Bound's commitment to empowering students through knowledge and resources, ensuring they are prepared for their future academic journeys.
