A multi-vehicle crash in Federal Way, Washington, resulted in one death and two injuries on Sunday morning. The incident occurred around 8:05 a.m. at the intersection of Enchanted Parkway South and South 356th Street, involving four vehicles. According to police, a southbound driver was operating recklessly and collided with another vehicle that was stopped at a traffic light. This impact caused both vehicles to catch fire and pushed them into the intersection, where they struck a third vehicle. One driver was pronounced dead at the scene, while another was taken to Harborview Medical Center with serious injuries. The driver suspected of causing the crash sustained less severe injuries and was arrested on suspicion of vehicular homicide and vehicular assault. Police noted that the suspect showed signs of impairment. The incident is currently under investigation, and further details may emerge as authorities continue their efforts.
