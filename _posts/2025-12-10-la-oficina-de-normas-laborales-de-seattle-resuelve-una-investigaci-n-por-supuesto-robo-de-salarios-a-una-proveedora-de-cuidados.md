Seattle's Office of Labor Standards has concluded an investigation into alleged wage theft involving a care provider. The investigation found that the employer failed to pay workers properly, violating city labor laws. The Office determined that the provider owed back wages to employees and must take corrective actions to ensure compliance with labor regulations. This resolution highlights the city's commitment to protecting workers' rights and enforcing fair labor practices. The OLS continues to encourage employees to report any suspected violations to help ensure a fair workplace for all workers in Seattle.
