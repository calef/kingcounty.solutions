This year’s Light The World Giving Machines have been installed at South Hill Mall in Puyallup and Alderwood Mall in Lynnwood. The machines, available through the end of the year, aim to support neighbors in need of hygiene products. Community members are encouraged to visit and contribute to this initiative. The installation event celebrated the collaboration between Essentials First and the Seattle and Tacoma Giving Machine teams from The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints, highlighting their commitment to combating hygiene poverty.
