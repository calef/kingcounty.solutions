Whatcom County massage therapist Collin Noah Ruginski has had his license suspended by state health officials due to serious legal issues. Ruginski, who pleaded guilty to domestic violence-stalking in April 2025, now faces additional charges including felony stalking, harassment, criminal impersonation, disclosing intimate images, and felony cyber harassment. The suspension is intended to ensure patient safety and public health, prohibiting him from practicing as a massage therapist in Washington. Ruginski has 20 days to request a hearing to contest the charges. Legal documents related to his case are available online through the Provider Credential Search on the Department of Health website, or by calling 360-236-4700. The Department of Health encourages anyone who believes a health care provider has acted unprofessionally to report their concerns. The agency is responsible for regulating health care providers in Washington, ensuring their competency and quality of care through established standards and disciplinary actions.
