The Seattle Kraken's game against the New Jersey Devils on Jan. 25 has been rescheduled to start at 12 p.m. instead of the originally planned time. This change is due to the Seahawks hosting the NFC Championship game at Lumen Field that same day, which begins at 3:30 p.m. Fans attending the Kraken game will have an earlier start time, allowing them to enjoy both events. The game will take place at Climate Pledge Arena.
