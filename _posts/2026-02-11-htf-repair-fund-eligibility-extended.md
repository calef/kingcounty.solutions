  <h2>Claims from July 1, 2025-Jan. 25, 2026, must be submitted by Feb. 27</h2>



  <p>Commerce extended eligibility for the HTF Repair Fund to costs incurred as far back as July 1, 2025. Eligible work at eligible properties completed from July 1, 2025, through January 25, 2026, <strong>must be submitted by February 27, 2026,</strong> to be considered for review and possible award.</p>



  <p><strong>All other award requests must be submitted within 30 days of the date</strong> eligible repair work was completed and paid (payment date) or completed and invoiced (invoice date). Eligible properties can receive up to $200,000 per award for urgent, non-recurring repairs of units or buildings, abatement of potentially hazardous materials, and safety-related structural improvements.</p>



  <p>The Repair Fund will remain open as long as funding is available. Funding was initially distributed on a 10-region format and funding remains available in all regions <em>except King County</em>.</p>



  <p><a>See more details in our updated program guidelines</a>. Eligible properties are encouraged to apply. Funding <em>may</em> be reallocated among regions at a future date depending on the rate of approved claims.</p>



  <h2><strong>How to apply to the HTF Repair Fund</strong></h2>



  <ol>
  <li>Create a SecureAccess Washington (SAW) account <em>if you don’t have one already</em> – <a>learn how on our website</a>
  </li>



  <li>Submit a statewide payee registration form <em>if you haven’t already</em> on the <a>Office of Financial Management website</a>
  </li>



  <li><a>Log in to your SAW account</a></li>



  <li>Input ‘HTF repair fund’ in the search bar, and select ‘add to list of services’</li>



  <li>Enter the HTF Repair Fund portal and follow the directions there</li>
  </ol>



  <p>Applications will be received and reviewed on an ongoing basis as long as funding remains available. Program staff will review applications received to determine regional funding distribution for the first seven days and will begin processing claims for payment at the end of that period. Claims will be processed in the order received and according to regional fund distribution. In regions where claims received exceed available funding, the Repair Fund will prioritize Permanent Supportive Housing (PSH) properties for funding.</p>



  <h2>Questions? </h2>



  <p>E-mail <a>HTFRepair@commerce.wa.gov</a>.</p>

title: HTF Repair Fund eligibility extended
topic_titles:
- Shelter & Housing
---

Eligibility for the HTF Repair Fund has been extended to cover costs incurred from July 1, 2025, to January 25, 2026. Claims must be submitted by February 27, 2026, for consideration. Other award requests should be submitted within 30 days of the completion and payment or invoicing of eligible repair work. Eligible properties can receive up to $200,000 for urgent, non-recurring repairs, hazardous material abatement, and safety-related structural improvements. The Repair Fund will remain open as long as funding is available, with funding distributed across 10 regions, except for King County. Future reallocations of funding among regions may occur based on the approval rate of claims. To apply, individuals must create a SecureAccess Washington (SAW) account, submit a statewide payee registration form, and follow application directions in the HTF Repair Fund portal. Applications will be reviewed on an ongoing basis, with initial assessments of regional funding distribution occurring within the first seven days. Claims will be processed in the order received, prioritizing Permanent Supportive Housing properties in regions with excess claims. For questions, contact HTFRepair@commerce.wa.gov.
