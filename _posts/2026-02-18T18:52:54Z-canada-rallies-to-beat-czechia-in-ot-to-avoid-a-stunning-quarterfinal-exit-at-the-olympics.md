In a thrilling quarterfinal match at the Olympics, Canada rallied to defeat Czechia 4-3 in overtime, narrowly avoiding an upset. With just over three minutes left in regulation, Nick Suzuki tied the game with a deflected shot following Ondrej Palat's goal for Czechia that had put them ahead. Canadian goaltender Jordan Binnington made a crucial save on David Pastrnak's breakaway to send the game into overtime, where Mitch Marner scored the decisive goal just over a minute in. The victory was bittersweet for Canada, as captain Sidney Crosby left the game with a lower body injury early in the second period.

Meanwhile, Slovakia advanced to the semifinals by defeating Germany 6-2, led by Dalibor Dvorsky and Pavol Regenda, who contributed significantly to the team's success. Despite an injury scare for star player Juraj Slafkovsky, who was temporarily sidelined, he returned to assist in the victory. The Slovak team, which previously won bronze in the last Olympics, is now positioned to compete for another medal. They await the outcome of other quarterfinals to determine their next opponent.
