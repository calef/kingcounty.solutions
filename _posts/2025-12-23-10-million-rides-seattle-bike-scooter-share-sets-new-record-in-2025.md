topic_titles:

Seattle's bike and scooter share program has achieved a milestone with 10 million trips in 2025, marking a 60% increase compared to the previous year. The program, which includes both bikes and scooters, has seen significant growth, reflecting rising interest in sustainable transportation options. This record was reached with a few weeks remaining in the year, indicating further potential for usage. The increase in trips is attributed to various factors, including improved infrastructure and expanded service areas. The city’s commitment to enhancing bike and scooter accessibility has contributed to the program's success, promoting healthier and environmentally friendly travel choices for residents and visitors. With these advancements, Seattle continues to lead in urban mobility solutions, encouraging a shift away from traditional vehicle use.
