The Seattle Seahawks are officially up for sale following their recent Super Bowl win, as mandated by the estate of late owner Paul G. Allen. The announcement, made via the team's Instagram account, confirms that the sale aligns with Allen's will, which requires his sister, Jody Allen, to sell all his sports assets and direct the proceeds toward philanthropic efforts. Allen, who co-founded Microsoft, acquired the Seahawks in 1997 and oversaw the construction of Lumen Field. His estate also includes the NBA's Portland Trail Blazers and a 25% stake in the Seattle Sounders of Major League Soccer. The sale process will be managed by Allen & Company, an investment bank, alongside the law firm Latham & Watkins. While the Trail Blazers' sale was announced in 2025, it remains unfinalized. The Seahawks previously made back-to-back Super Bowl appearances in 2014 and 2015, winning their first title in 2014 against the Denver Broncos and securing their second championship in February 2026 against the New England Patriots. Any prospective sale will require approval from NFL owners.
