King County Emergency Management is providing resources and information for residents recovering from recent heavy rain and historic flooding. As the focus shifts from immediate response to recovery, residents are encouraged to access available support services. Key resources include assistance with housing, cleanup, and health concerns. Residents should document damage for insurance claims and seek help if needed. Local organizations and agencies are mobilizing to provide aid, including food, shelter, and mental health services. Community members are urged to stay informed about safety measures and available resources as they navigate the recovery process.
