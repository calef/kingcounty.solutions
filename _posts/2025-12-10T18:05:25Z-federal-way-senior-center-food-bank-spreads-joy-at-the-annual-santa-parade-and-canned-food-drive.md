The Federal Way Senior Center Food Bank participated in the annual Santa Parade and Canned Food Drive, collaborating with South King Fire & Rescue. The event, held on a chilly day, brought together community members to celebrate the holiday spirit and support those in need. Attendees were encouraged to donate non-perishable food items, with the food bank collecting donations to help local families facing food insecurity. The partnership with South King Fire & Rescue aimed to enhance community engagement and spread joy during the festive season. The event not only provided an opportunity for residents to contribute to a worthy cause but also fostered a sense of togetherness among participants. Volunteers from the food bank and fire department worked diligently to make the event a success, ensuring that the collected food would be distributed to families in the area. This initiative highlights the importance of community support and collaboration in addressing hunger and providing assistance to those less fortunate, reinforcing the values of generosity and kindness during the holidays.
