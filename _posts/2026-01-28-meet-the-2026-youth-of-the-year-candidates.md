Eleven outstanding candidates have been selected for the 2026 Youth of the Year title, each representing the mission and values of the Boys & Girls Club of King County. These teens have made significant contributions at their Clubs and within the community. A celebration to honor the candidates will take place on January 31 at 2 p.m. at Smilow Rainier Vista. The event highlights the achievements and dedication of young leaders in the area.
