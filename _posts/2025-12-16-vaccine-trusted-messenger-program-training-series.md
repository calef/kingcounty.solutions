The Washington Department of Health has launched the first module of the Trusted Messenger Program Training Series, called “Maximizing Trust & Reducing Resistance.” This one-hour course provides continuing education credits for healthcare providers. It aims to equip them with strategies to improve communication, manage resistance, and build patient trust. More training modules will be released in the future.
