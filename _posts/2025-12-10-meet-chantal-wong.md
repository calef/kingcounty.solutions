Chantal Wong has joined the Inatai Foundation as an Administrative Associate. She has a background in communications, working with agencies focused on advocacy and social impact projects. Chantal became interested in Inatai's mission and its 50-Year Vision while collaborating with the foundation in a previous role. Excited to learn and contribute, she aims to strengthen processes within the organization.

Chantal identifies as Chinese American and values the importance of community, drawing inspiration from her family's resilience and connection. She believes in uplifting others and finds hope in the power of kindness. One of Inatai's values that resonates with her is joy, which she sees as essential for sustaining efforts against daunting challenges.

Chantal's favorite fact about Washington is that it hosts the largest ferry system in the U.S. Her favorite local spot is Stevens Pass, where she enjoys snowboarding with her husband. In her free time, she enjoys drawing, crafting stickers, and greeting cards, often alongside her cat, Enoki. She also recently recommended the legal thriller "Juror No. 2," noting its lasting impact.
