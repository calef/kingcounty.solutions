The Neighborhood Matching Fund (NMF) is looking for 7-9 committed individuals to join the Community Review Panel for the 2026 grant cycle. Community reviewers will help make important funding recommendations that affect local neighborhoods. Applicants should have a passion for community engagement and an understanding of local needs. The panel will review proposals and provide insights on which projects receive funding. Interested candidates can apply until a specified deadline. This is a chance for community members to influence local development and support initiatives that enhance their neighborhoods. Participating in the panel offers an opportunity to collaborate with others who are dedicated to improving the community.
