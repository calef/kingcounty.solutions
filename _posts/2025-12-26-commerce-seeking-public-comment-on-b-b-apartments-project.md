The Washington State Department of Commerce announced that the B & B Apartments project in Olympia, an affordable housing initiative, will not have a significant environmental impact. This finding will be submitted to the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development (HUD) on Dec. 23, along with a request for the release of funds. The action follows the completion of an environmental assessment required by the National Environmental Policy Act of 1969. The public can submit comments on this decision via email to Fia Phillips by 11:59 p.m. Pacific Time on Jan. 10. After the comment period ends, Commerce will proceed with submitting its Intent to Request Release of Funds to HUD.
