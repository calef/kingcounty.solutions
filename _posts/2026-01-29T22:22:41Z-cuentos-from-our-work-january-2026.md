El Centro de la Raza hosted a gathering on January 22, 2026, at the Centilia Cultural Center, bringing together over 40 community members to discuss air quality and environmental justice. Led by Gabby, the Weatherization & Environmental Justice Coordinator, the event fostered dialogue on how air quality affects health and neighborhoods, emphasizing the importance of community-driven solutions. This gathering marked the launch of the Community Air Quality Workshop Series, a five-part initiative aimed at promoting local environmental action through workshops featuring free food, resources, and community discussions.

Additionally, El Centro de la Raza staff participated in a day of learning on January 19 in honor of Rev. Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Day, engaging with the documentary "Harvest of Empire" and reflecting on issues of migration and justice. The organization also participated in the January 26 "ICE Out for Good" rally in Seattle, advocating for immigrant rights and dignity alongside a diverse group of community members. Furthermore, El Centro took part in Seattle’s 43rd Annual MLK Jr. Celebration, underscoring its commitment to ongoing social justice and community organizing in honor of Dr. King’s legacy.
