Northwest Harvest, a nonprofit established in 1967, is actively addressing food insecurity in Washington. In the 2024 fiscal year, the organization distributed more than 20 million pounds of food to those in need. One of its initiatives, the Fruitvale Community Market, provides free groceries to local residents, helping to alleviate hunger in the community. By offering fresh produce and other essential items, the market plays a vital role in supporting families and individuals facing food shortages.
