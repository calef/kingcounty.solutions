Repairs are underway for a section of US 12 in Naches that was damaged by the Naches River. Apollo, Inc. has been contracted by the Washington State Department of Transportation to perform emergency repairs. The work includes creating a buttress to block the river, rebuilding the embankment, backfilling the highway with gravel, and paving, striping, and installing guardrails. Crews will work seven days a week to expedite the reopening of the road, with completion expected by early January 2026. During construction, US 12 and the nearby Yakima Greenway Trail will remain closed, with detours available via local roads. The Washington State Department of Transportation closed US 12 as a precaution on December 10, after heavy rains led to record flooding and caused more than 300 feet of guardrail and highway to wash out.
