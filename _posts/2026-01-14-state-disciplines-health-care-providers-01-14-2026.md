  \           <div>\n<p><strong>For immediate release</strong>: January 14, 2026  
  (26-008)</p>\n\n<p><strong>Contact</strong>: <a>DOH Communications</a><br>\nPublic
  inquiries: Health Systems Customer Service 360-236-4700</p>\n\n<div>\n<p><strong>OLYMPIA</strong>
  -- The Washington State Department of Health has taken disciplinary actions or withdrawn
  charges against the following health care providers in our state.</p>\n\n<p>The
  department’s Health Systems Quality Assurance Division works with boards, commissions,
  and advisory committees to set licensing standards for more than 80 health care
  professions (e.g., dentists, nursing assistants, counselors).</p>\n\n<p>Information
  about health care providers is on the agency website. Click on “<a>Health Care Provider
  Lookup</a>” under the “Find it Fast” section of the <a>Department of Health website</a>
  (doh.wa.gov).The site includes information about a health care provider’s license
  status, the expiration and renewal date of their credential, disciplinary actions
  and copies of legal documents issued after July 1998. This information is also available
  by calling 360-236-4700. Consumers who think a health care provider acted unprofessionally
  are encouraged to call and report their complaint.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Adams County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  January 2026 the Department of Health released certified nursing assistant <strong>Yvette
  Verduzco-Alvarez </strong>(NC61580284) from the terms and conditions of her 2024
  agreed order and terminated monitoring.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Franklin County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  December 2025 the Department of Health denied<strong> Nicole Valtierra’s</strong>
  (HM61127719) application for a home care aide credential. In April 2025, Valtierra
  was placed on the Department of Social and Health Services’ long-term care abuse
  and neglect registry, which disqualifies her from working with vulnerable adults
  and from being certified or maintaining certification as a home care aide.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>King
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health charged<strong>
  Breana Judkins</strong> with the unlicensed practice of massage therapy and notified
  her of its intent to issue a cease-and-desist order. Judkins does not hold and has
  never held a credential to practice massage therapy in Washington state. In March
  2025, she admitted to providing massage-related services without a license.</p>\n\n<p>In
  December 2025 the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission and <strong><a>Scott Curtis
  Schnaible</a></strong> (VA00071216) entered an agreed order reinstating his pharmacy
  technician credential, subject to conditions including a minimum of two years of
  probation.</p>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health granted <strong>Dylan
  Wayne Vanosdol’s </strong>(CG61612282) application for an agency affiliated counselor
  credential with conditions.</p>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Dental Quality Assurance
  Commission reinstated <strong>Joseph R. Zimmer’s</strong> (DE00006267) dentist credential
  subject to conditions, including payment of a $15,000 fine.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Kittitas
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health granted <strong>Haylee
  Marie Bradley’s</strong> (CG70009231) application for an agency affiliated counselor
  credential, subject to conditions including two years of probation.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Lewis
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health terminated
  the probation on <strong>Courtney C. Pennypacker’s </strong>(NC60766512) certified
  nursing assistant credential.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Pierce County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  December 2025 the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission terminated the probation
  on <strong>David O. Clarke-Levy’s</strong> (VA00047141) pharmacy technician credential.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Snohomish
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Occupational Therapy Practice Board
  released occupational therapist<strong> Colleen Anderson</strong> (OT61036985) from
  the terms and conditions of her 2024 stipulation and terminated monitoring.</p>\n\n<p>In
  January 2026 the Department of Health charged registered nursing assistant <strong>Imogene
  Maxwell</strong> (NA61472027) with unprofessional conduct. In June 2024, Maxwell
  allegedly physically abused a resident of an adult family home where she was employed.
  Maxwell failed to respond to the department’s multiple requests for information.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Spokane
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In November 2025 the Department of Health denied <strong>Phylicia
  Carpio Adams’ </strong>(CG61521385) application for an agency affiliated counselor
  credential. In July 2023, Adams was convicted in Idaho of possession of a controlled
  substance, possession of marijuana and possession of drug paraphernalia—use or possess
  with intent to use, a felony.</p>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health
  charged certified nursing assistant <strong>Megan Vailey Sinn</strong> (NC61145713)
  with unprofessional conduct. Sinn allegedly violated the terms of her 2024 stipulation
  by failing to complete required continuing education hours by June 2, 2025.</p>\n</div>\n\n<h2><strong>Thurston
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In January 2026 the Department of Health charged substance
  use disorder professional trainee <strong>Alyssa Marie Martinez</strong> (CO61534986)
  with unprofessional conduct. Between April and November 2024, Martinez allegedly
  worked without providing required documentation of supervision.</p>\n\n<p><strong>Note
  to Editors</strong>: Health care providers charged with unprofessional conduct have
  20 days to respond to the Department of Health in writing. The case then enters
  the settlement process. If no disciplinary agreement can be reached, the case will
  go to a hearing.</p>\n\n<p>Our <a>website</a> is your source for a healthy dose
  of information. Get updates by following us on <a>social media</a>.</p>\n\n<p>###</p>\n</div>\n

The Washington State Department of Health has announced disciplinary actions against several health care providers as of January 14, 2026. Key updates include the release of certified nursing assistant Yvette Verduzco-Alvarez from monitoring, and the denial of home care aide credential for Nicole Valtierra due to her inclusion on the long-term care abuse and neglect registry. Breana Judkins faces charges for practicing massage therapy without a license. The Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission reinstated Scott Curtis Schnaible's pharmacy technician credential, while granting Dylan Wayne Vanosdol and Haylee Marie Bradley agency affiliated counselor credentials, both under conditions. Joseph R. Zimmer's dentist credential was reinstated with a $15,000 fine. Other charges include unprofessional conduct against registered nursing assistant Imogene Maxwell for alleged abuse, Megan Vailey Sinn for failing to meet continuing education requirements, and substance use disorder professional trainee Alyssa Marie Martinez for working without proper supervision documentation. The Department encourages consumers to report unprofessional behavior by health care providers. For more details on health care provider credentials, the department's website offers a lookup tool.
