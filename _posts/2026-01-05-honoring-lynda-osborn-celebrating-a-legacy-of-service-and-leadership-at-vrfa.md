Her stewardship as Chair of the Finance Committee ensured our financial strength and growth, allowing us to better serve our communities. Thank you, Lynda, for your unwavering dedication and for modeling true public service. Congratulations on your retirement, you will always be part of the VRFA family!</p>

Lynda Osborn, a member of the Board of Governance and City of Algona Councilmember, is being honored for her significant contributions to the Valley Regional Fire Authority (VRFA). Since joining the Board in 2007, Osborn has played a crucial role in shaping the organization, advocating for the communities of Algona, Auburn, and Pacific. As Chair of the Finance Committee, she contributed to the financial health and growth of the VRFA, enhancing its ability to serve the public effectively. Her leadership has made her a respected figure in public service. As she retires, the VRFA expresses gratitude for her commitment and dedication, affirming that she will always be a valued member of the VRFA family.
