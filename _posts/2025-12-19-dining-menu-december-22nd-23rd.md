The dining menu for December 22nd and 23rd at Ballard Senior Center features a Chicken Philly Sandwich with soup, salad, and fruit on Monday. On Tuesday, a Holiday Lunch will be served with Beef Bourguignon accompanied by roasted carrots, mashed potatoes, spinach, and a mandarin salad, followed by cranberry cheesecake for dessert. Ballard Senior Center will be closed from December 24th through January 4th. The center wishes everyone joyful holidays.
