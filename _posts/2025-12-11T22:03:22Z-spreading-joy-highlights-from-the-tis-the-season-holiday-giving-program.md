  <p><strong>The ‘Tis the Season Holiday Giving Program,</strong> in partnership with our friends at the Boys &amp; Girls Clubs of Bellevue and Rainier Athletes, is one of our favorite times of the year because it brings community members together in a festive spirit of generosity, celebration, and care.</p>



  <p>This week, we welcomed <strong>205 families</strong> to our festive holiday gift room, where parents and caregivers chose gifts their children would love, need, and genuinely be excited to open. This focus on <em>choice</em> is at the heart of the program.</p>



  <p>For families experiencing financial hardship, budgets are already stretched thin, and there often isn’t extra for toys, warm clothing, or festive treats. The holiday giving program helps to lift that weight by offering parents the dignity of choosing gifts that fit their children’s ages and interests, as well as the chance to create joyful moments that might otherwise feel out of reach. </p>

Their commitment helped ensure every child walked away with something meaningful and every parent felt supported.</p>

These experiences offer families the opportunity to create cherished holiday memories together.</p>



  <p>And of course, our dedicated volunteers brought the entire program to life. From decorating and organizing the gift room to wrapping gifts, their enthusiasm created an environment filled with warmth.</p>

The 'Tis the Season Holiday Giving Program, organized by Bellevue LifeSpring in partnership with the Boys & Girls Clubs of Bellevue and Rainier Athletes, recently provided holiday gifts to 205 families facing financial challenges. The program emphasizes the importance of choice, allowing parents to select gifts that cater to their children's preferences and needs, thereby enhancing their holiday experience.

Donors contributed hundreds of presents, including toys, winter clothing, and gift cards, while community members assembled handmade treat baskets with items like cocoa kits and baking sets. The Bacon Family Foundation played a pivotal role by hosting a holiday party to raise awareness and gather donations, ensuring every child received meaningful gifts.

Local organizations also supported the initiative by donating tickets for various holiday activities, enabling families to enjoy memorable experiences together. Volunteers contributed significantly by decorating and organizing the gift room, creating a warm atmosphere for families. Ultimately, the program impacted 441 children and teens, fostering a sense of joy and community during the holiday season.
