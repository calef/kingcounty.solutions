Mark Zuckerberg testified in a Los Angeles courtroom as part of a landmark trial examining the impact of Meta's platforms, particularly Instagram, on youth mental health. The case involves a 20-year-old woman, identified as KGM, who claims her early use of social media led to addiction and worsened depression and suicidal thoughts. During the proceedings, Zuckerberg maintained that existing research has not definitively proven that social media harms mental health, despite questions about addiction and the company's policies. Plaintiff's attorney Mark Lanier challenged Zuckerberg on previous statements regarding user engagement metrics and age verification policies. Children’s advocates criticized Zuckerberg's responses as disingenuous, asserting that his testimony failed to address the safety of children on social media. This trial is significant as its outcome could influence numerous similar lawsuits against social media companies. Meta's legal team argues that while KGM experienced mental health issues, Instagram was not a substantial factor. The trial follows previous congressional testimonies from Zuckerberg, who has faced scrutiny over his company's handling of youth safety. The case is being closely watched, as it may set a precedent for future litigation against social media platforms.
