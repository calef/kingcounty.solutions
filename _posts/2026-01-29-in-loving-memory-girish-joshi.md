  Health First Aid facilitator, he helped educate and empower countless community
  members. He also played a significant role in strengthening our partnership with
  the Microsoft community by supporting workshops, panels, and mental health presentations.</p>\n<p>Since

  lasting impact on our mission and the people we serve.</p>\n<p>His wife, <strong>Deepti</strong>,

  strong mental health advocates in their own right. Aarna has contributed greatly
  as part of our Youth Ambassadors program. Our hearts are with their family during
  this incredibly difficult time, and we want them to know that the NAMI Eastside
  community stands ready to support them.</p>\n<p>As we remember Girish and the passion
  he brought to this work, we invite our community to take a moment for reflection,

We are saddened to announce the unexpected passing of Girish Joshi, a dedicated board member of NAMI Eastside. Girish was a strong advocate for youth mental health and worked tirelessly to reduce stigma surrounding mental health issues. As a Mental Health First Aid facilitator, he educated and empowered many in the community. He significantly strengthened the partnership with the Microsoft community by participating in workshops, panels, and mental health presentations. Since joining the board in 2021, Girish made a lasting impact on the organization's mission and the individuals it serves. He is survived by his wife, Deepti, and daughter, Aarna, who have also been active in mental health advocacy, with Aarna contributing to the Youth Ambassadors program. The NAMI Eastside community expresses its heartfelt condolences and support to Girish's family during this challenging time. As a tribute to Girish and his passion for mental health advocacy, community members are encouraged to take moments for reflection and care, such as spending time outdoors. His presence will be deeply missed.
