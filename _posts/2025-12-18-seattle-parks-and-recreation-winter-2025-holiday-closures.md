Many Seattle Parks and Recreation facilities will have closures or modified schedules for the winter holidays. Key closures will occur on Thursday, December 25, and other dates around the holiday season. Specific facilities and programs affected by these closures include community centers, indoor pools, and various recreational activities. Residents are encouraged to check the individual facility schedules for exact details on hours and availability. Some amenities may remain open with limited services. Visitors should plan accordingly and consider alternative options for their holiday activities.
