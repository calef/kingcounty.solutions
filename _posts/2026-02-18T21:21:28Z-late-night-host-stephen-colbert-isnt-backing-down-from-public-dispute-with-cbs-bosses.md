Stephen Colbert is publicly challenging CBS over the network's alleged attempt to prevent him from airing an interview with Democratic Texas Senate candidate James Talarico on “The Late Show.” Colbert claimed that CBS lawyers warned him about potential violations of the equal time rule, which requires broadcasters to provide equal airtime to political candidates. In response, he aired the interview on YouTube, stating that he was surprised by CBS's denial of his claims. During his show, Colbert humorously disposed of the network's statement in a dog poop bag, expressing confusion over CBS's actions. He noted that he had previously received approval from CBS lawyers to discuss the issue and criticized the network for bowing to pressure from FCC Chairman Brendan Carr, who has considered enforcing stricter regulations. Colbert, whose show is set to conclude in May, suggested that CBS's decision may be influenced by political pressures, particularly regarding his frequent criticisms of former President Donald Trump. Talarico's interview has garnered over five million views on YouTube, significantly outpacing Colbert's typical CBS audience, and resulted in $2.5 million in campaign donations for Talarico within 24 hours.
