  \           <div>\n<p><strong>For immediate release</strong>: January 12, 2026  
  (26-006)</p>\n\n<p><strong>Contact</strong>: <a>DOH Communications</a><br>\nPublic
  inquiries: Health Systems Customer Service 360-236-4700</p>\n\n<p><strong>OLYMPIA</strong>
  -- The Washington State Department of Health has revoked or suspended the licenses,
  certifications, or registrations of the following health care providers in our state.
  The department has also immediately suspended the credentials of people who have
  been prohibited from practicing in other states.</p>\n\n<p>The department’s Health
  Systems Quality Assurance Division works with boards, commissions and advisory committees
  to set licensing standards for more than 80 health care professions (e.g., dentists,
  nursing assistants, counselors).</p>\n\n<p>Information about health care providers
  is on the agency’s website. <a>Click on “</a><a>Health Care Provider Lookup</a>”
  under the “Find it Fast” section of the <a>Department of Health website</a> (doh.wa.gov).
  The site includes information about a health care provider’s license status, the
  expiration and renewal date of their credential, disciplinary actions and copies
  of legal documents issued after July 1998. This information is also available by
  calling 360-236-4700. Consumers who think a health care provider acted unprofessionally
  are also encouraged to call and report their complaint.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>King
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Pharmacy Quality Assurance Commission
  and <strong>Lauren Yielding</strong> (VB61208063) entered an agreed order indefinitely
  suspending Yielding’s pharmacy assistant credential. Yielding failed to comply with
  the terms of a 2023 stipulation requiring her to enter a monitoring program.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Pierce
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health summarily suspended
  <strong>Leanna Marie Eaves’</strong> (PC61061150) certified medical assistant-phlebotomist
  credential, pending further disciplinary action. In August 2024, the Department
  of Social and Health Services found Eaves personally exploited and mentally abused
  a vulnerable adult and placed her on its long-term care abuse and neglect registry.
  Placement on the registry prohibits Eaves from working with or having unsupervised
  access to vulnerable adults.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Snohomish County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In
  December 2025 the Department of Health and <strong>Laura Santana </strong>(HM61272025)
  entered an agreed order indefinitely suspending Santana’s home care aide credential.
  Between July 2023 and January 2024, Santana used a client’s credit card for personal
  purchases totaling $277 and later admitted to the conduct.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Spokane
  County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In December 2025 the Department of Health indefinitely
  suspended <strong>Lydia Catherine Cicarelli’s</strong> (CG61227010) agency affiliated
  counselor credential until she meets the terms and conditions of a 2023 stipulation
  requiring completion of a five-year monitoring contract. Cicarelli failed to respond
  to the department’s request for information.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Out of State</strong></h2>\n\n<p><strong>Colorado</strong>:
  In December 2025 the Department of Health indefinitely suspended <strong>Stephen
  Patrick Flansbaum’s</strong> (LH60526071) mental health counselor credential until
  at least December 20, 2026. In September 2024, Flansbaum informed his employer that
  he would be entering inpatient treatment. Flansbaum failed to respond to the department’s
  multiple requests for information.</p>\n\n<p><a><strong>Idaho</strong>:</a> In December
  2025 the Department of Health suspended <strong>Michelle Renea Kokkinen’s </strong>(NC60892961)
  certified nursing assistant credential for at least four years. Kokkinen failed
  to comply with the terms and conditions of a November 2024 order suspending her
  credential by continuing to work until January 2025, constituting the unlicensed
  practice of nursing assistance.</p>\n\n<p>Our <a>website</a> is your source for
  a healthy dose of information. Get updates by following us on <a>social media</a>.</p>\n\n<p>###</p>\n</div>\n

The Washington State Department of Health has revoked or suspended the licenses, certifications, or registrations of several health care providers. This includes immediate suspensions for individuals prohibited from practicing in other states. The Health Systems Quality Assurance Division sets licensing standards for over 80 professions, including dentists and nursing assistants. Consumers can check a provider's status on the department's website or by calling 360-236-4700.

Recent actions include the indefinite suspension of Lauren Yielding, a pharmacy assistant in King County, due to non-compliance with a monitoring program. In Pierce County, Leanna Marie Eaves had her medical assistant-phlebotomist credential suspended after being placed on a long-term care abuse and neglect registry. Laura Santana in Snohomish County had her home care aide credential suspended for misusing a client’s credit card. Lydia Cicarelli from Spokane County also faces an indefinite suspension until she meets monitoring contract requirements.

Additionally, Stephen Patrick Flansbaum from Colorado and Michelle Renea Kokkinen from Idaho had their credentials suspended for failing to comply with treatment and licensing orders, respectively.
