Neighborhood House's Executive Director emphasizes the importance of solidarity with immigrant, refugee, and BIPOC communities. The message highlights collaboration among various organizations, including NWIRP, WAISN, OneAmerica, Children's Thrive Action Network, Protecting Immigrant Families, National Day Laborer Organizing Network, and the ACLU of Washington. These partnerships aim to support and uplift marginalized communities, addressing their unique challenges and advocating for their rights. The Executive Director calls for continued commitment to equity and justice, encouraging all to stand together in solidarity with those facing adversity.
