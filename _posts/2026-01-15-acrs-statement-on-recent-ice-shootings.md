feed_content: |-
  <p>Asian Counseling and Referral Service (ACRS) is deeply troubled by the recent fatal shooting of Renee Nicole Good by an ICE agent in Minneapolis and by a separate incident in Portland in which federal immigration agents wounded two people. These related events reflect a troubling pattern of the use of force, abuse of power, and disregard for human life by federal immigration authorities across the country. Last year, a record number of 32 individuals died under ICE custody.</p>
  <p>The use of lethal force by federal authorities raises urgent and serious concerns about safety, accountability, and lack of guardrails for this administration’s anti-immigrant agenda. Every day immigrant and refugee communities, particularly those who already experience heightened fear, trauma, and barriers to care, are paying the price</p>
  <p>We urge policymakers to take immediate action by closely scrutinizing the use of federal resources and rejecting FY2026 funding decisions that expand or perpetuate immigration enforcement practices that put lives at risk.</p>
  <p>You can advocate alongside us and urge your Members of Congress to refuse additional funding for ICE and CBP <a>here</a>.</p>
  <p>We remain committed to advocating for policies that protect the safety, stability, and well-being of not only the individuals and families we serve but all vulnerable communities.</p>
  <p>The post <a>ACRS Statement on recent ICE Shootings</a> appeared first on <a>ACRS</a>.</p>

title: ACRS Statement on recent ICE Shootings
topic_titles: []
---

ACRS expresses deep concern over recent incidents involving Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) agents, including the fatal shooting of Renee Nicole Good in Minneapolis and the wounding of two individuals in Portland. These events highlight a disturbing trend of excessive force and abuse of power by federal immigration authorities. Last year, 32 individuals died while in ICE custody, raising significant concerns about safety and accountability within the agency's operations. ACRS emphasizes that immigrant and refugee communities, already facing fear and trauma, are disproportionately affected by these actions. The organization calls on policymakers to investigate the use of federal resources and to reject funding decisions for the fiscal year 2026 that could further endanger lives through expanded immigration enforcement. ACRS remains dedicated to advocating for the safety and well-being of vulnerable communities, encouraging the public to join in urging Congress to deny additional funding for ICE and Customs and Border Protection (CBP).
