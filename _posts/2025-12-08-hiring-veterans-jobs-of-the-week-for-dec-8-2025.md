Each week, job announcements from employers and websites seeking to hire Veterans are shared. For the week of Dec. 8, 2025, various job listings include positions such as Business Manager in Yuma Proving Ground, AZ; Sr. Boiler Tech in Denver, CO; and Project Manager at Fort Meade, MD. Employers recognize that Veterans possess valuable skills like discipline, leadership, and problem-solving, which can enhance company culture and drive innovation.

Numerous opportunities also exist within the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA), which values the skills gained through military service. Current openings at VA facilities include positions for Peer Specialists, Radiologists, and Healthcare Engineers, among others.

The Veterans Canteen Service (VCS) is offering roles such as Cook in Prescott, AZ, and Assistant General Manager in Orlando, FL.

Additionally, career fairs are scheduled in Newport News and Norfolk, VA, every Thursday, providing further resources for Veterans seeking employment. Veterans are encouraged to verify job details with the respective organizations and utilize mentorship programs to enhance their job search.
