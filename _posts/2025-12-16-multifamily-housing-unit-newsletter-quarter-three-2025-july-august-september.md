  <p>The days are shorter and the temperatures colder, but you can still count on the Multifamily Housing Unit to deliver updates on our commitment to developing affordable multifamily housing across Washington!</p>



  <h2>HTF applicant lists are live</h2>



  <p><strong>$528 million requested from 112 competitive applications</strong></p>



  <p>Every year, Commerce’s Multifamily Housing Unit disburses millions of dollars to ensure communities have access to multi-family affordable housing. Our largest funding opportunities were released in July, <strong><a>HTF Multifamily Housing NOFA #MHU-2025-01</a></strong> and <strong><a>HOME and NHTF NOFA #MHU-2025-02</a></strong>, for which the application window closed on October 16.</p>



  <p>We received 112 applications which collectively are seeking $538 million, which shows the continued need for affordable housing in Washington communities,</p>



  <p><a>See the application lists on our website</a>.</p>



  <h2>Technical assistance and consultant resources</h2>



  <p><strong>New and less experienced developers: Learn about development resources available to you.</strong></p>



  <p>As a public funder, we’re committed to providing equal opportunity to all contractors. We understand that many affordable housing developers feel like they miss out on many options for essential tools in the development process, especially third-party consultants.</p>



  <p>While we can’t make endorsements or specific recommendations, we <em>can</em> recommend you review the following general resources to get you closer to meeting your development goals:</p>



  <ul>
  <li>If you are a housing provider serving under-represented communities, you may be eligible for <strong>Pre-Development Technical Assistance</strong>. To learn more, <a>visit the Capacity Building, Outreach, and Support (CBOS) webpage</a> or email <a>HTFCapacityBuilding@commerce.wa.gov</a>.</li>



  <li>For developers that have been awarded funds, <strong>your Project Manager can assist you</strong> in browsing resources. Not sure who your PM is? <a>Email Fia Phillips</a>.</li>



  <li>For developers who have already entered the HTF portfolio, you can access <strong>Technical Assistance from our Portfolio Management teams. </strong>For more information, email them at <a>HTFCompliance@commerce.wa.gov</a> and <a>HTFPortfolioMgmt@commerce.wa.gov</a>.</li>
  </ul>



  <p>Developers can also connect with current contractors to learn about their preferred third-party consultants and other development resources, such as construction companies or CPAs. <a>View the lists of awarded projects on our website</a>, under “Funding details and awards”.</p>

The Multifamily Housing Unit in Washington continues its efforts to develop affordable housing, having processed 112 applications seeking $538 million in funding. In the third quarter of 2025, the unit assisted in the creation of 186 new affordable homes and nine shelter beds across seven counties. Notable projects include the Lexington Avenue Home in Kelso, which houses adults with disabilities, and the King Fisher Tiny House Village in Tacoma, providing shelter for previously unhoused adults. Other significant developments include the Searing House in Issaquah, CCAP Modulars in Grays Harbor, and the Crosswalk Teen Shelter in Spokane, each targeting specific populations like youth experiencing homelessness and adults with complex health needs. The unit emphasizes technical assistance for new developers and encourages housing providers serving under-represented communities to seek pre-development support. Property managers and developers are invited to provide feedback on community gardens and suggest content for future newsletters, aiming to enhance community engagement and resources in affordable housing initiatives.
