Rainfall and flooding in King County have led to sewage overflows, prompting health warnings. Residents are advised to avoid contact with all surface water, including lakes, ponds, streams, rivers, and Puget Sound. For those whose homes or businesses have flooded, safety precautions are essential. First, if there is still standing water, turn off the power before re-entering the premises. Avoid touching any electrical equipment while standing in water. Wear protective gear, including gloves and masks, when cleaning up. It is crucial to ensure proper ventilation to minimize health risks from mold and mildew. Disinfect affected areas thoroughly to prevent contamination. If you experience any adverse health effects, seek medical attention promptly. Proper disposal of contaminated materials is important to protect public health. Follow local guidelines for waste disposal and cleanup.
