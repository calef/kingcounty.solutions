A historic flooding event in King County occurred from December 8 to December 30, 2025, marking one of the most significant floods in decades. The King County Flood Control District reported that the flood set several new records, though fortunately, there was no loss of life attributed to the event. This outcome is credited to the Flood Control District's extensive investment in flood risk reduction infrastructure over the past 18 years, which includes the completion of 123 projects and over $623 million in funding.

During the flood, nearly $3 million was allocated for emergency flood mitigation and repairs, with an additional $7.3 million designated for post-flood recovery efforts, focusing on levee and revetment repairs. The report highlights the dedication of first responders and public works teams who worked under challenging conditions to monitor and manage the flood infrastructure. The preliminary flood event report provides an overview of the impacts over the three-week duration, including daily summaries and detailed accounts of the storm's progression, which involved multiple atmospheric rivers and significant rainfall. Further assessments and financial summaries are forthcoming as recovery efforts continue.
