The Seattle Police Department is focusing on reducing sexual exploitation along the Aurora Avenue North corridor. Since October, officers have been photographing license plates of more than 40 vehicles linked to suspected prostitution activities. The initiative is part of a larger awareness campaign aimed at informing vehicle owners about the consequences of their involvement in such activities. SPD plans to send letters to the vehicle owners, explaining the impact of their actions and encouraging them to reconsider their behavior. The department emphasizes that this effort is part of a commitment to community safety and the fight against human trafficking. SPD hopes that by engaging with these vehicle owners, they can deter further exploitation and promote a safer environment for all residents.
