The City of Seattle is investing over $1.45 million in 23 community organizations to enhance food equity in 2026. These partners provide free meals, promote traditional food knowledge, and support both elders and youth. The funding will help combat food insecurity and improve access to healthy food options in underserved communities. In addition to this investment, the city plans to update its grant process to better support these organizations in their efforts. This initiative reflects Seattle's ongoing commitment to addressing food equity and supporting local communities.
