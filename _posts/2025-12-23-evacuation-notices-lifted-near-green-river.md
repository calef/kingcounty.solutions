All evacuation notices near the Green River in unincorporated King County were lifted as of December 23 at 10 a.m. Residents and businesses are allowed to return to their properties where it is safe. Authorities caution individuals to be alert for standing water or debris that could pose hazards. While the evacuation orders have been lifted, conditions may change quickly, and evacuation levels could be reinstated with little notice. Residents are advised to stay updated on the situation.
