In 2025, City Light achieved a significant milestone by creating its 10-Year Strategic Roadmap. This plan outlines a clear vision that aligns daily operations with the needs of customers, employees, and communities. It aims to prepare for upcoming challenges and opportunities in the rapidly changing energy sector. The roadmap emphasizes sustainability, innovation, and community engagement, focusing on enhancing energy efficiency and resilience. City Light also prioritized improving infrastructure and expanding renewable energy sources. Throughout the year, the organization engaged with stakeholders to gather feedback and ensure the plan addresses the diverse needs of the community. This collaborative approach is expected to strengthen City Light's ability to serve its customers better and promote a sustainable future. The commitment to transparency and accountability will guide the implementation of the roadmap, ensuring that progress is shared with the community. As 2025 comes to a close, City Light is poised to build a stronger future together with its customers and partners.
