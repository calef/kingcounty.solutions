Trader Joe's is set to open its first location in Woodinville, Washington, at 14035 Northeast Woodinville Duvall Road, within a shopping center that includes T.J. Maxx and HomeGoods. While an exact opening date has not been confirmed, the store is marked as "coming soon" on Trader Joe's website. The announcement follows a May 2025 statement from Woodinville Mayor Mike Millman, who indicated that the store had been in the works for some time and anticipated a late 2025 or early 2026 opening. Trader Joe's currently operates nearly 30 locations in Washington, with its most recent openings in Lacey and Seattle earlier this year. The company also has plans to expand with new stores in several states, including Arizona, Georgia, Kansas, Louisiana, Texas, and Florida in 2026.
