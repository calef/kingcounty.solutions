Seattle police arrested a 28-year-old felon and two other shoplifters during a Retail Theft Operation at the University Village shopping center on Dec. 23. The operation took place around 7:15 p.m. Officers were focused on combating retail theft in the area. The arrested individuals were involved in stealing items from local stores. The police continue to work on reducing retail crime and keeping shopping areas safe for customers and businesses. Further details about the charges against the suspects were not disclosed.
