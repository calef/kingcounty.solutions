King County will open Disaster Assistance Centers from January 2 to January 5 to help residents affected by recent flooding. The centers will be located at the Auburn Library, Issaquah Library, and Carnation Library, providing support for those applying for the Washington State Individual Assistance program.

The Auburn Library will be open on January 2 from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. and on January 3 from 1 p.m. to 6 p.m. The Issaquah Library will operate on January 4 from 11 a.m. to 6 p.m., while the Carnation Library will be open on January 5 from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m.

To qualify for assistance, applicants must earn 80 percent or less of the area median gross income and show that their primary residence was significantly damaged or destroyed by flooding between December 5 and December 22, 2025. Grant awards may reach up to $1,125, but applying does not guarantee funding.

Residents unable to attend in person can apply online at sahelp.org. Interpretation services will be available at the centers. Additional details will be provided as they become available.
