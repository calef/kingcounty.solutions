The 2026 CISC Friendship Dinner is an annual event that celebrates community and opportunity, emphasizing support for immigrant families. Hosted by the Chinese Information and Service Center (CISC), the dinner highlights the organization's commitment to assisting seniors, caregivers, newcomers, and children. Attendees will experience an evening filled with energy, inspiration, and cultural celebration, reinforcing the idea that every family deserves a chance to thrive. The event aims to empower resilience and foster a brighter future for all participants. Tickets for the dinner can be purchased online, and the organization encourages community involvement to further its mission of support and inclusivity.
