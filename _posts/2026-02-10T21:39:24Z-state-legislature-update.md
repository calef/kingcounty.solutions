The 2026 state legislative session is progressing quickly, with several significant bills under discussion that could affect tenants and vulnerable families. Key legislation includes SB 5169, aimed at reducing trauma for children who witness violent crimes by allowing them to avoid courtroom testimony; this bill has passed the Senate and is pending in a House committee. Additionally, SB 5362 and HB 1487 focus on stabilizing funding for crime victim services, while HB 2266 and SB 6069 promote various forms of housing, including permanent supportive housing and indoor emergency shelters, and prevent local governments from hindering housing development. Another important bill, HB 1099, seeks to establish a tenant assistance program, and HB 2489 would prevent local governments from criminalizing homelessness when adequate shelter is unavailable. SB 5855 proposes banning ICE officers from wearing face masks; it has passed the Senate and awaits a House committee review. The legislature encourages public engagement through writing to lawmakers, submitting testimony, and participating in hearings.
