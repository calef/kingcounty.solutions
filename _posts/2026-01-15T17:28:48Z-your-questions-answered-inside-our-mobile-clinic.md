Care Net's mobile medical clinic offers STI and pregnancy care directly to communities in Washington state, emphasizing convenience, confidentiality, and no-cost services. Operating as a fully equipped medical facility on wheels, the clinic adheres to state healthcare standards and is staffed by licensed professionals. Inside, patients will find essential medical equipment and private consultation spaces, ensuring a safe and comfortable environment.

Services include pregnancy testing and consultations, viability ultrasounds, limited STD/STI testing, pregnancy options consultations, and community resource referrals. All services are free, with no insurance required, addressing financial concerns for those facing unexpected pregnancies.

Appointments are encouraged, but walk-ins are welcome. Each visit is confidential, with only the patient, a registered nurse or RDMS, and a client advocate present. The mobile clinic operates in various locations, including Bellevue, Kent, Burien, and Auburn, with specific hours for each site. Care Net aims to provide compassionate care and support, ensuring that individuals can access the information and resources they need in a judgment-free setting.
