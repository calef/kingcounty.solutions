The City of Auburn, Washington, has announced resources for residents affected by recent flooding. Following significant rainfall, several areas in Auburn experienced flooding, prompting the city to take action to assist those impacted. Residents are encouraged to report flooding issues and damages through the city’s website or by contacting city officials directly. The city has also set up a dedicated webpage with information on assistance programs, including financial aid and recovery resources.

Auburn officials are working in collaboration with local agencies to provide support and ensure residents have access to necessary services during this challenging time. The city emphasizes the importance of safety and advises residents to avoid flooded areas. Additionally, information on emergency preparedness and flood response will be available to help residents better prepare for future events.

Residents can stay updated on the situation through the city’s official communications and are urged to take precautions to protect their homes and properties. The city remains committed to supporting its community in the aftermath of the flooding and ensuring effective recovery efforts are implemented.
