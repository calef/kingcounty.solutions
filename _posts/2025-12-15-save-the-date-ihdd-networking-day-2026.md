Join the IHDD Networking Day on May 11, 2026, at the University of Washington South Campus Center. This event focuses on connection, learning, and collaboration in the field of human development and disability. It brings together researchers, clinicians, community partners, self-advocates, and trainees involved in intellectual and developmental disability research and training. Attendees can expect a range of activities, including lightning talks and poster presentations showcasing projects from various IHDD programs, such as IDDRC, UCEDD, and LEND. Presentations from disability network partners will also be featured, providing opportunities to connect across disciplines and communities. IHDD Networking Day aims to create a welcoming environment for exchanging ideas and fostering new collaborations. Additional details will be available early next year, and registration for the event will open soon.
