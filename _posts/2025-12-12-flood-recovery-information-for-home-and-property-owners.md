Property damage from flooding can be overwhelming for homeowners. King County is focused on safety and recovery support for those affected. Residents are encouraged to report any damage to their homes or properties. Resources may become available to assist with recovery efforts. For more details on how to report damage and access support services, residents should visit the King County Flood recovery resources website.
