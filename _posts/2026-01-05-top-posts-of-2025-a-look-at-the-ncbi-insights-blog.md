As 2025 begins, the NCBI Insights Blog highlights its most viewed posts from the previous year. Notable updates include the upcoming default database for protein BLAST searches, which will shift to the NCBI ClusteredNR database, enhancing search efficiency. A new PubMed Central (PMC) Beta search will replace the current search in September 2025, as part of an effort to improve full-text search functionality and user experience. The PMC website modernization continues with a beta version of the updated search available for preview. Additionally, changes to My Bibliography will take effect on October 1, 2025, aligning with the 2024 NIH Public Access Policy by using the earliest publication date listed in PubMed records. The release of Database of Single Nucleotide Polymorphisms (dbSNP) Build 157 includes around 1.2 billion Reference SNP records. The Multiple Comparative Genome Viewer (MCGV), currently in beta, is introduced to allow visualization of multiple eukaryotic genome alignments, enhancing analysis capabilities. The NCBI Insights Blog aims to help users understand NCBI resources and encourages feedback and engagement on its social media platforms.
