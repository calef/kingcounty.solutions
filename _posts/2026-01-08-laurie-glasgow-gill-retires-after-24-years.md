  <p>Laurie Glasgow‑Gill, one of Mercy Housing’s longest serving team members, retired at the end of 2025, marking nearly 24 years of remarkable contribution. As Investor Relations Manager, Laurie was responsible for overseeing MCC’s investor portfolio, preparing investor note documents, meeting compliance reporting requirements, and tracking interest payments, among other duties.</p>
  <img src="https://live-mercy-housing.pantheonsite.io/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/LaurieGlasgowGillHeadshot_square.jpg">
  <p>Laurie had previously worked in the community development and legal departments in the banking industries. Her experience, honesty and humor have helped drive the company forward and she will be missed.</p>
  <p>Thank you, Laurie!</p>

Laurie Glasgow-Gill retired at the end of 2025 after nearly 24 years with Mercy Housing, where she served as the Investor Relations Manager. In her role, she managed the investor portfolio, prepared documents, ensured compliance with reporting requirements, and tracked interest payments. Glasgow-Gill brought valuable experience from her previous work in community development and legal departments in the banking sector. Her contributions, characterized by her honesty and humor, significantly advanced the organization. She will be greatly missed by her colleagues and the community.
