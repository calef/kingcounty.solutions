Lakeside-Milam has been named one of America's Best Addiction Treatment Centers by Newsweek for the fifth consecutive year, ranking as the top facility in Washington State. This recognition highlights Lakeside-Milam’s commitment to evidence-based clinical practices and exceptional patient outcomes. The center is accredited by the Commission on Accreditation of Rehabilitation Facilities (CARF) and certified by the American Society of Addiction Medicine (ASAM), reinforcing its reputation for quality care.

With over 40 years of experience, Lakeside-Milam has helped more than 100,000 individuals start their recovery journeys. The center's treatment methods include cognitive behavioral therapy, medical assessments, and family involvement, ensuring comprehensive support for patients. Lakeside-Milam offers a continuum of care, including detox, residential treatment, and outpatient programs.

The annual Newsweek evaluation considers peer recommendations, quality indicators, and patient satisfaction to determine rankings. Lakeside-Milam’s consistent top ranking provides reassurance to families and individuals seeking reliable addiction treatment options. The leadership at Lakeside-Milam emphasizes that this honor reflects the dedication of their staff and the effectiveness of their programs in helping clients reclaim their lives.
