The dining menu for the Ballard Senior Center from January 26 to January 30 features a variety of meals. On Monday, guests can enjoy grilled chicken and spinach salad, rolls, soup, and fruit. Tuesday offers pork posole with a cheese quesadilla, salad, fruit, and dessert. For Wednesday, the menu includes vegetarian moussaka, garden salad, garlic bread, and fruit. Thursday's meal is cheese raviolis with meat sauce, Caesar salad, fruit, and dessert. Friday features an egg salad sandwich, soup, salad, and fruit. Additionally, there is a Wednesday night dinner, which requires an RSVP, serving pasta Bolognese, salad, garlic bread, and dessert.
