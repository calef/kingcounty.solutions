  <p>For 17 years, Amelia’s sons, Jamisen and Cian, relied on AFW flights to access the specialized care they needed at Children’s Hospital Los Angeles. Amelia shares their story below, grateful that other families will continue to find hope and support through the gift of flight. </p>



  <p>Amelia shares: </p>



  <p>I remember the moment we heard about Angel Flight West (AFW) from Nurse Kathy at Children’s Hospital Los Angeles. It was such a turning point for our family. Without AFW all of the amazing volunteers, we could not have gone to Jamisen and Cian’s eye specialist for so many years. </p>



  <p>We have been blessed to fly in so many planes and meet so many amazing people. Some pilots have joked that Jamisen and I have more flight hours than most pilots. It has cultivated a lifelong love of flying. </p>



  <p>Our last flight with AFW last spring was more emotional than we all anticipated. We were not prepared to be aged out of Children’s Hospital LA. It is sad to know we won’t be flying any longer, but we’re happy to know others will be able to. </p>



  <p>We thank you all from the bottom of our hearts; you help turn a scary or sad situation into a bit of fun and peace of mind. </p>



  <p>With all of our love and gratitude, <br><br>Kevin, Amelia, Jamisen, Cian, and Teagan</p>

Amelia reflects on 17 years of support from Angel Flight West (AFW) that enabled her sons, Jamisen and Cian, to receive specialized care at Children’s Hospital Los Angeles. She recalls the pivotal moment when Nurse Kathy introduced them to AFW, which became essential for their trips to the eye specialist. Amelia expresses gratitude for the many volunteers and pilots who made these flights possible, sharing that her family formed lasting friendships and a deep love for flying. Their final flight with AFW last spring was emotional, as they were not prepared to age out of the hospital's care. While sad to conclude their journey with AFW, Amelia finds comfort in knowing that other families will continue to benefit from these flights. She thanks everyone involved for turning challenging experiences into moments of fun and peace of mind for her family.
