The City of Burien is set to host its 2026 Lunar New Year Community Celebration, which aims to honor and celebrate the diverse cultures within the community. This event is expected to feature traditional performances, food vendors, and various activities that reflect the rich heritage associated with the Lunar New Year. Organizers are encouraging community participation and are actively seeking volunteers to help with the event's planning and execution. The celebration will provide an opportunity for residents to come together, learn about different customs, and enjoy a festive atmosphere. The city plans to share more details about the event as the date approaches, ensuring that the community is well-informed and engaged in the festivities. This celebration not only marks the Lunar New Year but also emphasizes Burien’s commitment to inclusivity and cultural appreciation.
