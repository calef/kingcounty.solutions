From January 26 to January 30, the dining menu at Ballard Senior Center includes a variety of meals.

On Monday, the menu features a grilled pastrami and Swiss sandwich served with citrus slaw, potato soup, and fruit. Tuesday offers beef and veggie stew over mashed potatoes, accompanied by a garden roll, salad, fruit, and dessert.

Wednesday’s lunch includes falafel, Greek salad, pita, and fruit. For Thursday, chicken stroganoff over noodles is served with seasonal veggies, garden salad, fruit, and dessert.

On Friday, diners can enjoy a tuna sandwich, veggie soup, fruit, and yogurt.

A special Wednesday night dinner requires an RSVP and features smothered chicken with rice, seasonal veggies, soup, and dessert.
