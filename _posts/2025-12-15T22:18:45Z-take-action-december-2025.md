El Centro de la Raza encourages community involvement to promote social change through volunteering and civic engagement. Individuals can volunteer in Federal Way to provide free tax assistance, helping families retain more of their income for essential needs. The organization also features the #LightTheWorld Giving Machine at Alderwood Mall, allowing people to donate essentials like clothing and food directly to local families in need, with all proceeds going to participating organizations, including El Centro de la Raza.

Additionally, El Centro seeks ongoing support through one-time or recurring donations to sustain its programs amidst funding cuts. The organization emphasizes the importance of civic participation, urging community members to register to vote and keep their information updated to ensure representation. This call to action reflects the organization's commitment to empowering individuals and families in moments of crisis and celebration. El Centro de la Raza remains a vital resource, advocating for community strength and involvement year-round. For more information and to get involved, visit their website.
