Residents of Auburn near Pike Street NE and Pike Place NE off the Green River are under a Level 3 Evacuation order due to flooding from a recent storm system. This order specifically affects five homes in the area, which have been notified. The rest of the neighborhood remains at Level 2, indicating a less urgent situation. Authorities are monitoring the conditions closely and urge residents to remain aware of updates regarding the flooding and evacuation status.
