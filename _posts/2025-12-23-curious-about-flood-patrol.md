King County's Flood Patrol is actively monitoring flooding conditions around the clock during a historic flood event. The team is working closely with partner agencies to strengthen levees to prevent breaches and is prepared to make quick repairs if any levees fail. Residents can find more details and watch a new video from the county's Department of Natural Resources and Parks.
