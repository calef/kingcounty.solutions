Severe flooding in Washington has led to evacuations, road closures, and disruptions in many communities. Northwest Harvest is actively monitoring the situation and providing support by delivering emergency supplies, including ready-to-eat food and bottled water, to partners assisting affected areas. As the full impact of the flooding unfolds, the organization plans to expand its efforts to help food-insecure communities during both the immediate response and recovery phases.

Three urgent needs have been identified: ready-to-eat and shelf-stable food items, bottled water, and flexible support for partners facing increased demand or access challenges. Organizations impacted by the flooding can request emergency support by contacting Northwest Harvest at partner-emergency@northwestharvest.org. Additionally, Northwest Harvest offers to amplify these requests for help through their website and social media. Community members are encouraged to assist by reaching out with details about their needs. Northwest Harvest remains committed to supporting its partners and ensuring continued access to food for affected communities during this crisis.
