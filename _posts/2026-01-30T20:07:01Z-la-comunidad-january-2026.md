El Centro de la Raza participated in Seattle's 43rd Annual Martin Luther King Jr. Celebration, joining a diverse group of community members at Garfield High School. The event, organized by the Seattle MLK Coalition, featured the theme "Where do we go from here?" emphasizing the importance of honoring Dr. King's legacy while encouraging continued activism for justice and equity. Participants reflected on the ongoing struggle for dignity and belonging, underscoring the necessity of collective action in pursuing a more just future. The march served as a unifying event, highlighting the commitment of attendees to work together in addressing social issues and fostering community solidarity.
