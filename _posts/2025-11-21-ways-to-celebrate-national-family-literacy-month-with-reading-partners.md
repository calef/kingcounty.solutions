November is National Family Literacy Month, a time to highlight the importance of reading and engaging with children to promote literacy. Families are encouraged to take part in activities that strengthen their reading skills together. Simple ways to celebrate include reading aloud as a family, visiting local libraries, and participating in community events focused on literacy. Parents can set a good example by reading themselves and sharing their favorite books with their children. Creating a reading routine, such as bedtime stories, can also foster a love for reading. Engaging in discussions about books can enhance comprehension and critical thinking. Reading Partners emphasizes the role of families in supporting children's literacy development. By making reading a fun and interactive experience, families can help children build essential skills for their future.
