The Husky Health Center and ASUW are offering a free testing event for all University of Washington Seattle campus students. The event aims to raise awareness about chlamydia and gonorrhea, which often go undetected, with over 70% of cases showing no symptoms. Students are encouraged to get tested before symptoms arise. The testing will take place from November 4 to November 18, 2025, at the Husky Health Center. This initiative is part of a broader effort to promote sexual health and well-being on campus. All currently enrolled students are welcome to participate.
