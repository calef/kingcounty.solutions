Celebrate the arrival of spring by learning origami, featuring the creation of butterflies, a symbol of the season. Participants can transform colorful squares of paper into delicate butterflies through a few simple folds. Additional projects, such as making a cute gift box, will also be available. The event will be led by JonAnn Cruver on the first day of spring, March 20, from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. The cost is $4 for members and $8 for non-members, with tax included. Join in for a fun and creative experience!
