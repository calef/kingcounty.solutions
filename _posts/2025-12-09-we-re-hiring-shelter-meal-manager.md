Hunger Intervention Program (HIP) in Seattle is hiring a full-time Shelter Meal Program Manager with a pay range of $29.81 to $32.81 per hour, depending on experience. The role involves overseeing the Shelter Meal Program, which serves three shelters in Northeast King County. The manager will work with a team to create menus, manage food orders, and coordinate meal delivery, while also supervising the Shelter Meal Coordinator and volunteers.

Candidates should have a culinary degree or two years of kitchen experience, and must demonstrate management skills and the ability to engage with diverse communities. Responsibilities include meal preparation, maintaining inventory, ensuring food safety, and tracking meal data. The position requires physical stamina, as it involves lifting, standing, and active engagement throughout an eight-hour shift.

HIP offers a comprehensive benefits package, including medical insurance, retirement options, and paid time off. To apply, submit a resume and a cover letter to the provided email address by December 12 for priority consideration. HIP encourages applications from individuals of diverse backgrounds, emphasizing a commitment to equity and social justice.
