The One Heart/One Community Party With a Purpose, organized by One Circle, successfully engaged hundreds of guests to support the mission of preventing hunger among children in Bellevue. Held on February 12, 2026, the event raised over $563,497, thanks to the generosity of sponsors and attendees. One Circle member Schanon Odell emphasized the collective effort of 72 women dedicated to fundraising while fostering community spirit. Guests participated in meaningful conversations, reinforcing their commitment to helping local families regain stability and prosperity.

Gardenia Banks, another One Circle member, expressed hope that the event would inspire families served to eventually give back to others, illustrating a cycle of support and empowerment. A poignant reflection from a former recipient of assistance highlighted the transformative impact of generosity, as she now contributes to the community after overcoming her own challenges as a single mother.

The event was supported by key partners, including Anu and Naveen Jain of Viome, and benefited from the leadership of co-chairs Liz Hale, Annemarie Marcus, Trish Carpenter, and Cathi Hatch. The gathering not only raised funds but also strengthened community ties in Bellevue.
