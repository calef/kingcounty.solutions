This season highlights a profound truth: we are not alone. At Christmas, the celebration centers on God becoming flesh and choosing to dwell among humanity, stepping into a broken world to meet us in our suffering. The Creator did not stay distant but drew near, living among us, sharing in our struggles, dying for us, and rising again. This act offers a promise of new life and unity with Him, ensuring we are never separated again. This message serves as a source of hope and deep comfort, especially during challenging times.
