The Arcora Foundation offers a recorded webinar providing 1.5 continuing dental education credits focused on modern caries management strategies in dentistry. The course highlights recent evidence-based changes in preventing and treating dental caries, aligning with the American Dental Association's Clinical Practice Guidelines. It covers caries risk assessment and minimally invasive dentistry (MID) techniques, including chemical treatments and the ADA Caries Classification System. Participants will learn to assess caries risk, classify lesions, and implement selective caries removal, silver diamine fluoride, and glass ionomer cement when necessary.

Dr. Doug Young, a recognized authority in cariology and minimally invasive dentistry, leads the webinar. As Professor Emeritus at the University of the Pacific, he has co-founded influential organizations in the field and has contributed significantly to the ADA's efforts in caries management. The webinar aims to enhance the skills of dentists, dental therapists, hygienists, and other dental professionals. The course is recognized by the ADA CERP, which assists dental professionals in identifying quality continuing education providers. The original release date for the course is February 2026, with an expiration date of October 2028.
