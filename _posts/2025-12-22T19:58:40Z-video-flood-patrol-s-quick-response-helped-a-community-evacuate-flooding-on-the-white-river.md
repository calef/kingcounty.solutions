In the early hours of Tuesday, King County Flood Patrol members Josh Kubo and Craig Garric were inspecting a flood barrier along the White River when they encountered a sudden breach in the structure. Recognizing the urgency of the situation, they swiftly transitioned from monitoring to rescue operations. Their prompt response facilitated the evacuation of local residents at risk of flooding. The flood patrol's rapid action exemplified the importance of preparedness and quick decision-making in emergency situations. The incident highlights the ongoing challenges faced by communities in flood-prone areas and the critical role that emergency responders play in safeguarding lives during natural disasters.
