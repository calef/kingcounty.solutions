Nancy Guthrie, an 84-year-old woman and mother of NBC's "Today" show host Savannah Guthrie, was last seen on January 31 and reported missing the following day. Pima County Sheriff Chris Nanos is leading the investigation, which has yet to identify any suspects or persons of interest. Authorities believe Guthrie may have been taken against her will that night. Despite the lack of major breakthroughs, Nanos remains hopeful, stating, “We’re going to go with faith and hope that we find Nancy... safe and sound.”

Investigators are analyzing DNA evidence collected from Guthrie's home and a pair of black gloves found nearby, which may be linked to a person seen tampering with her doorbell camera. The gloves’ DNA has not produced any matches in the CODIS database, but Nanos suggests further genealogical analysis could be pursued. Detectives are also following leads related to gun shops and specific clothing items seen in the footage. While two individuals were detained and subsequently released, they are still part of the investigation. Nanos has cleared Guthrie's children and their spouses as suspects. The sheriff continues to urge anyone with information to come forward.
