In the men's snowboarding slopestyle final at the 2026 Winter Olympics, Chinese snowboarder Su Yiming captured the gold medal with a top score of 82.41 on his first run. Japan's Taiga Hasegawa secured silver with an 82.13, while Team USA's Jake Canter earned bronze with a score of 79.36 after a strong final run that included a 1980-degree rotation. The event featured a competitive field including 2018 gold medalist Red Gerard and Canadian Mark McMorris, who has previously won three consecutive bronze medals.

Despite a solid first run scoring 76.60, Gerard's hopes for a podium finish were dashed after a missed landing in his final attempt, leaving him in 11th place. Oliver Martin, another American competitor, finished ninth after improving his score throughout the event. McMorris faced challenges during qualifying but managed to perform well enough to compete. The slopestyle event, known for its susceptibility to weather conditions, requires snowboarders to navigate ramps, rails, and obstacles. The competition was initially at risk due to a storm but proceeded as scheduled.
