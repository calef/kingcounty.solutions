Essentials First will hold its first annual Day of Action on January 26, 2026, in Olympia. The event aims to highlight the need for access to basic hygiene and the dignity associated with it. Community members and advocates will gather to prioritize hygiene access as a legislative issue in Washington State. The goal is to promote policies that address the challenges faced by those experiencing hygiene insecurity. This Advocacy Day seeks to advance the idea that hygiene access is a fundamental right. Participants are encouraged to attend at the Washington State Capitol in Olympia to support hygiene equity. Registration details are available for those interested in joining the cause.
