Yakima nonprofits are increasing food aid due to uncertainty around Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP) benefits. Many families rely on these benefits, with some, like Annaleise Flowers, experiencing delays and waiting for their assistance. Flowers previously received $59 a month in Oklahoma but has faced challenges in accessing her benefits. Local organizations are stepping in to provide crucial support for those affected by the potential changes in SNAP, ensuring that families continue to receive necessary food assistance during this uncertain time. The increased aid aims to address the rising food insecurity in the community as people worry about the future of their benefits.
