Mikaela Shiffrin ended her eight-year Olympic medal drought by winning gold in the women's slalom at the 2026 Winter Olympics in Milan Cortina. The 30-year-old American skier secured her victory with a time of 1:39.10, finishing with a 1.5-second lead after starting the second run with a 1.05-second advantage from her first run. This win marked her first medal at the Milan Cortina Games and added to her previous gold from the 2014 Sochi Olympics, where she became the youngest slalom gold medalist.

Shiffrin's performance in the slalom concluded the women's alpine skiing events, providing her a last opportunity to return to the podium after a disappointing showing at the 2022 Beijing Games, where she failed to medal in six events. Competing alongside her were teammates Paula Moltzan, who briefly held the lead during the second run before finishing off the podium, AJ Hurt, and Nina O'Brien, who did not finish her first run.

Shiffrin, who holds the records for 71 World Cup wins and nine slalom globes, aimed for redemption in Cortina after her struggles in Beijing and expressed a desire to approach her races with a flexible mentality.
