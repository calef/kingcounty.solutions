Empower Youth Network will distribute Weekend Power Packs to Riverview students on Friday, December 12, from 10 a.m. to 12 p.m. The distribution will occur at two locations: Empower Youth Network at 31951 E. Bird Street, Carnation, and the Riverview School District Office at 15510 1st Ave NE, Duvall. Families are encouraged to stop by during this window to pick up the Weekend Power Pack for their students.
