House Bill 2238, aimed at enhancing statewide food security, is scheduled for a hearing in the House Agriculture and Natural Resources Committee on Tuesday, January 13, at 8 a.m. Supporters are encouraged to sign in favor of the bill to demonstrate widespread backing to lawmakers. This effort aims to raise awareness about the importance of food security in the community.
