Federal benefits for food assistance are in uncertainty, causing stress for many Washington residents who rely on them to feed their families. Local food banks and pantries are feeling the impact as they serve thousands each month. As concerns grow about the future of the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program (SNAP), food banks are struggling to meet increasing demands. The situation has led to a frantic week for both beneficiaries and food providers, highlighting the challenges faced by those in need and the organizations that support them. With the potential for changes to SNAP, the pressure on food banks intensifies as they work to ensure that essential resources remain available for vulnerable communities.
