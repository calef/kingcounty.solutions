Tribal Staff will work remotely on December 15 and 16, 2025, to prioritize safety. They will return to the office on December 17, 2025. During the remote work period, the Operations and Facilities teams will assess buildings and infrastructure for any weather-related damage. Staff members should confirm their remote work assignments with their direct supervisors to ensure operations continue smoothly. Employees are advised to avoid non-essential travel in affected areas and to follow local emergency recommendations. The tribe appreciates staff's flexibility and commitment during this state of emergency.
