Sound Generations provides more than just meals through its Meals on Wheels program for home-bound seniors in King County. The initiative delivers a week’s worth of nutritious, ready-to-eat meals to older adults, promoting their health, independence, and a sense of community. Each delivery includes seven meals, which are frozen and packaged for easy heating. Menu options feature vegetarian, vegan, low-sodium, and kosher meals, catering to diverse dietary needs.

Eligibility is open to residents aged 60 and older who struggle with shopping or cooking. A suggested donation of $6 per meal helps sustain the program. Interested individuals, caregivers, or friends can connect with Sound Generations by calling (206) 448-5767 or 1-888-308-6325, emailing mealsonwheels@soundgenerations.org, or applying online or via mail. The program aims to alleviate loneliness and hunger while ensuring older adults receive essential support and companionship.
