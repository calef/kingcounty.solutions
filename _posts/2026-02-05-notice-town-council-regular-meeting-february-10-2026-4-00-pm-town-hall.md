The Town of Yarrow Point will hold a regular Town Council meeting on February 10, 2026, at 4:00 PM. The meeting will take place in hybrid format, allowing both in-person attendance at Town Hall and virtual participation via Zoom. Residents wishing to speak must email the mayor by 2:00 PM on the day of the meeting and can submit comments via email or mail.

The agenda includes a call to order by Mayor Katy Kinney Harris, a pledge of allegiance, and roll call of council members. The council will approve the agenda and review staff reports. Public comments will be allowed, with each speaker given three minutes.

The consent calendar will address routine items, including financial approvals and the adoption of the Private Property Tree Code Amendments. Regular business will cover discussions on town finances and short-term rental regulations. There will also be updates from the mayor and various committees, followed by a second opportunity for public comment.

An executive session will discuss legal matters related to town enforcement and potential litigation. The meeting will adjourn, with the next Town Council meeting scheduled for March 10, 2026.
