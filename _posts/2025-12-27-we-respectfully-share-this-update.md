Joint Services for Tribal Elder Mary Anne Hinzman and Tribal Member Kara Ann Hinzman will take place at Snoqualmie Casino & Hotel Ballroom on January 3, 2026, at 10 AM. Following the service, a burial will occur at the Fall City Cemetery. Attendees are invited back to the Ballroom for a lunch and celebration afterward. A block of rooms has been reserved for guests needing accommodations. To make reservations, call (425) 888-1234.
