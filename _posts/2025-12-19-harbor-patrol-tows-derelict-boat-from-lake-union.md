Seattle's Harbor Patrol Unit towed a derelict boat from Lake Union earlier this month. The boat, which had been leaking fuel, was removed after a response effort that started in October. The operation involved multiple agencies, including the Seattle Police Department and the Department of Natural Resources. The removal aimed to protect the environment and ensure the safety of the area around Lake Union. This incident highlights ongoing efforts to address abandoned vessels in local waterways.
