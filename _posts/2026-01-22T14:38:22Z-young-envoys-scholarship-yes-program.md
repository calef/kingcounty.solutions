City University of Seattle will honor approximately 1,740 graduates during its 2025 commencement ceremony. Among the graduates, around 550 students are expected to participate in person at the event. The university will celebrate the achievements of students from various programs and locations, emphasizing its commitment to providing quality education and support. The commencement will reflect the diverse backgrounds and experiences of the graduates, showcasing their hard work and dedication throughout their studies. The event serves as a significant milestone for the graduates as they transition into their professional careers or further education. CityU continues to adapt and support its students through innovative programs that cater to the needs of a global student body.
