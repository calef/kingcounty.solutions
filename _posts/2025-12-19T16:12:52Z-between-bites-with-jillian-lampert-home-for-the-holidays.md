In the December 19, 2025 episode of "Between Bites," Dr. Jillian Lampert discusses the challenges of returning home for the holidays while dealing with an eating disorder. Drawing from her personal experiences, Lampert provides guidance for young adults who may be facing similar situations, whether their eating disorder is known or hidden. She shares practical strategies to help navigate holiday gatherings and emphasizes the importance of preparation and communication with loved ones. The episode aims to create a supportive space for individuals struggling with eating disorders and offers advice for family members on how to provide care and understanding. Dr. Lampert, who serves as the Vice President of Strategy and Public Affairs for The Emily Program, holds advanced degrees in Nutrition and Public Health and has extensive experience in the field of eating disorders. The podcast, part of The Emily Program's "Peace Meal" series, seeks to address topics related to eating disorders and body image, inviting listeners to contribute their ideas for future episodes. "Between Bites" is available on platforms like Apple Podcasts, Spotify, and YouTube.
