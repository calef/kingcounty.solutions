title: We share with prayers and sadness the passing of beloved Tribal Elder Karen
  Boney
topic_titles: []
---

The Snoqualmie Tribe mourns the loss of Tribal Elder Karen Boney, who passed away on December 30, 2025, at the age of 82. Boney, born on October 1, 1943, was a cherished member of the community. In honor of her memory, tribal flags are flying at half-staff, in line with the tribe's policy. The tribe plans to share more information about her life and contributions in the future.
