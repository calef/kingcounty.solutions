Over $5,000 was raised during the 2025 Power of Home event, contributing to a total of over $500,000 for affordable housing initiatives. The gathering highlighted the importance of stable housing as a foundation for economic growth and opportunity. Attendees enjoyed a live performance by Ezrael Orlino, Izel Rosas, and Percy, presented by Youth on Record. The event featured a lively auction led by Halie Behr and the Behr Benefits team.

Alison George, State Director of Housing for the Colorado Division of Housing, received the 2025 Community Impact Award, while Alliance Construction Solutions was recognized with the 2025 Real Estate Development Partner Award. Organizers expressed gratitude for the support and partnership that enables them to continue their mission of providing program-enriched affordable housing. The event was described as the most successful yet, emphasizing the community's role in making a difference. Attendees were thanked for their contributions, which help bring people home and create stable, vibrant communities.
