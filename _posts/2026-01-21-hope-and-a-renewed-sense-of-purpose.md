The past year highlighted the strength of community support, as MSC faced significant funding challenges but also received an outpouring of aid from supporters, foundations, and partners. This collective effort showcases the importance of uplifting others and reinforces MSC's determination to continue its mission. The organization expresses gratitude to those who have contributed, whether by joining initiatives like Hope Builder or #hungersquad, making donations, promoting events, or simply spreading the word.
