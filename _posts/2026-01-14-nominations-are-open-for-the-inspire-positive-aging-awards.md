  <p>In the past twenty years, Sound Generations has celebrated the stories of over 550 older adults in Western Washington at the annual Inspire Positive Aging Awards.</p>



  <p>Last year, the number of nominations grew dramatically, increasing to a record 63 nominations (previous years recorded an average of 25-30). All nominees were celebrated at a community luncheon featuring a full house of nominees, their guests, and a host of community partners and organizations.</p>



  <p>This year, the event is expected to be even larger. And we want to make sure your inspiring loved ones are a part of it.</p>



  <div>
  <div><a>Learn more</a></div>
  </div>



  <h2>Inspiration Blooms in King County</h2>



  <p>The story of a lotus flower is one that resonates with our community. Lotuses sit underground for years putting down roots before sprouting up.</p>



  <p>Just like the lotus, the wisdom and lived experience of older adults have formed deep roots over time before rising into full bloom, revealing stories shaped by resilience, memory, and meaning.</p>



  <p>Through simple actions or lifelong commitment, older adults make our world a better place. In the past, we’ve seen nominees from all walks of life, including nonprofit founders, senior center kitchen volunteers, social service workers, mothers and fathers, and more.</p>



  <h2>How to Nominate</h2>



  <p>To be eligible, the nominee must be age 60 or older and live in Western Washington. We are accepting nominations in the following six categories:</p>



  <ul>
  <li>Advocacy &amp; Activism</li>



  <li>Community Service</li>



  <li>Defining Inspiration</li>



  <li>Health &amp; Wellness</li>



  <li>Intergenerational Impact</li>



  <li>Lifelong Learning</li>
  </ul>



  <p>Nominations can be made from January 14 – March 12 at <a>https://go.sgkingco.org/inspire</a>. </p>

title: Nominations are Open for the Inspire Positive Aging Awards
topic_titles:
- Senior Services
---

Sound Generations is accepting nominations for the annual Inspire Positive Aging Awards, which honor the achievements of older adults in Western Washington. Over the past 20 years, the awards have recognized more than 550 individuals. Last year saw a record 63 nominations, up from the usual 25-30, and all nominees were celebrated at a community luncheon attended by guests and local organizations. This year’s event is anticipated to be even larger, encouraging the community to recognize inspiring elders.

Nominees must be 60 years or older and reside in Western Washington. There are six categories for nominations: Advocacy & Activism, Community Service, Defining Inspiration, Health & Wellness, Intergenerational Impact, and Lifelong Learning. Nominations will be accepted from January 14 to March 12. The event highlights the significant contributions of older adults, whose life stories reflect resilience and wisdom, much like the lotus flower that blooms after years of growth underground.
