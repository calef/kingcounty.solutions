Seattle police arrested a 29-year-old man early Tuesday morning for setting a recycling dumpster on fire under an occupied apartment building and assaulting officers. At 1:26 a.m., officers responded to the arson call in the Capitol Hill neighborhood. Upon arrival, they found the dumpster engulfed in flames, which posed a serious risk to the building and its residents.

The suspect, identified as Kevin F. Allen, was seen near the scene and tried to flee but was quickly apprehended by officers. While being taken into custody, he physically resisted and assaulted two officers. They suffered minor injuries but continued to perform their duties. Allen was booked into jail on charges of arson and assaulting a police officer.

Firefighters extinguished the flames before they spread to the apartment building. Authorities are investigating the incident further, and no injuries to civilians were reported. Residents of the building were evacuated safely during the emergency response.
