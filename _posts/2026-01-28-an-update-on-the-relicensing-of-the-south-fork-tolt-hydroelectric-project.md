---

On January 26, 2026, the City of Seattle submitted an Initial Study Report (ISR) to the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC) as part of the relicensing process for the South Fork Tolt Hydroelectric Project. The ISR details the progress of 24 studies required for the relicensing, including those mandated by FERC and agreed upon by the City, Tribes, and other Licensing Participants. It provides updates on the implementation of these studies and preliminary results.

Of the 24 studies, 19 will continue into a second year of research, while five studies are in draft form and will be reviewed by Tribes and other Licensing Participants. The City plans to hold public meetings on February 4 and 5, 2026, to discuss the ISR. Following these meetings, participants may request changes to the studies, which FERC will evaluate.

The second year of study activities will occur in 2026, with an Updated Study Report due by February 17, 2027. These studies aim to enhance understanding and guide the development of the new project license. Interested individuals can contact Sarah Davidson for meeting attendance and receive updates via Seattle City Light’s mailing list.
