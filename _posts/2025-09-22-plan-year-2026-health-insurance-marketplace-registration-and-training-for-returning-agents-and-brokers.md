Plan Year 2026 Health Insurance Marketplace registration and training is now available for returning agents and brokers. The resources include dynamic list information and webinar slides, with the data dated Sept. 22, 2025. This training focuses on the necessary steps and requirements for agents and brokers to participate in the Health Insurance Marketplace for the upcoming plan year. Returning agents and brokers can access these resources to ensure they are prepared for the enrollment period. The registration and training materials are designed to provide comprehensive guidance for successfully navigating the marketplace.
