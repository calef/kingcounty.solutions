The Snoqualmie Tribe announced the distribution of tickets for the Seahawks Playoff Game 2 at Lumen Field on January 25, 2026. Tickets will be sent electronically, and recipients will receive email links to access their tickets. Tribal members who cannot attend the game are asked to notify the tribe as soon as possible at TCR@snoqualmietribe.us.

Ticket recipients include Jordan Ventura, Megan Cotton, Kathleen Beeby, Dan Willoughby, Damian Moses, Jake Repin, David Mobley, Jamie Shaffer, Brandi Munn-Sycks, Mishelle Sycks, Angela Wymer, Tonya Knisley, Keenan Chuckulnaskit, Carolyn Lubenau, Zachary Cassady, Mark Eddy, and Marvin Kempf. The game will feature the Seahawks facing off against the Rams in the NFC Championship.
