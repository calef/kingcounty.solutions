The Seattle Indian Health Board (SIHB) is hiring for several positions aimed at enhancing Native healthcare. Current openings include Senior Program Manager, Mental Health Specialist/Case Manager, Community Relations Advocate, and Medical Assistant. The Community Relations Advocate greets patients and manages appointment scheduling while addressing concerns. The Medical Assistant plays a crucial role in clinical teams by preparing patients for visits, offering education, and performing lab tests. No prior experience is necessary for Medical Assistant applicants, as training will be provided. The Senior Program Manager will oversee public health grants and programs, contributing to the Urban Indian Health Institute's data and research initiatives. The Mental Health Specialist/Case Manager offers outpatient psychological services and helps patients create care and safety plans. SIHB provides a strong benefits package that includes medical, dental, and vision insurance, alongside a retirement plan with a 6% company match after 90 days of employment. Interested candidates can explore the job board for more details and apply to join the team focused on improving Native health care.
