Seattle Parks and Recreation will host a celebration to honor Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. on January 17, 2026. The event will feature a youth-led march, focusing on reflection, unity, and community action. Participants will gather to celebrate Dr. King's legacy and promote social justice. The march will start at 11 a.m. at the Northwest African American Museum and will proceed to the Dr. Martin Luther King Jr. Memorial. Community members are encouraged to join in the festivities, which aim to inspire and empower the next generation to continue King’s work. The celebration will also include speakers, performances, and opportunities for community engagement. This annual event not only honors Dr. King’s contributions but also serves as a reminder of the ongoing efforts needed to achieve equality and justice for all.
