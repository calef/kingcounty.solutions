The federal Transportation Department has ordered the closure of over 550 commercial driving schools across the United States due to significant safety violations. These institutions, which are responsible for training future truck and bus drivers, were found to have failed to meet essential safety standards. The decision aims to enhance road safety by ensuring that only properly accredited schools can operate. The department's directive highlights ongoing concerns about the quality of training provided to commercial drivers, which is crucial for public safety on highways. The closures are part of a broader initiative to enforce regulations and maintain high standards within the driving education sector. The Department emphasizes the importance of rigorous training for commercial drivers to prevent accidents and ensure safer transportation for all road users. As a result of these actions, many students currently enrolled in these programs may need to seek alternative training options to obtain their commercial driver's licenses.
