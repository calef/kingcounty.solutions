Residents and businesses along South 277th Street in Auburn, including those on Frontage Road, D Street Northwest, B Street Northwest, D Street Northeast, I Street Northeast, and in the Trail Run community, are being warned about potential flooding. Rising water levels north of the area pose a risk of flood conditions. Officials are urging everyone to stay alert and take necessary precautions to ensure their safety. Residents are encouraged to monitor local news and updates for further information on the situation.
