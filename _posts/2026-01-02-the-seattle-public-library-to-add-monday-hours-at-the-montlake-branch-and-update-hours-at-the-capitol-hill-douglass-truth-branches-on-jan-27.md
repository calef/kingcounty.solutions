  <p>Starting Tuesday, Jan. 27, The Seattle Public Library will update hours at three locations and expand the number of libraries that are open seven days a week – from 13 to 14 locations – with the addition of new Monday hours at the Montlake Branch.</p>



  <p>The <a>Capitol Hill Branch</a> (425 Harvard Ave. E.) will continue to be open seven days a week, but will reduce its schedule by two hours on Tuesdays and Wednesdays; and will shift its schedule two hours earlier on Thursdays.</p>



  <p>The <a>Douglass-Truth Branch</a> (2300 E. Yesler Way) will also continue to be open seven days a week, but will reduce its schedule by two hours on Wednesdays and Thursdays.</p>



  <p>By making these schedule adjustments, the Library can start offering Monday hours from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m. at the <a>Montlake Branch</a> (2401 24th Ave. E.). The branch will also shift its schedule two hours earlier on Wednesdays and two hours later on Thursdays.</p>



  <p>All locations, with the exception of the University Branch, which is closed for renovations, continue to be open at least six days a week. See the table below for details. Changes to hours are noted in red.</p>

title: The Seattle Public Library to add Monday hours at the Montlake Branch and update
  hours at the Capitol Hill, Douglass-Truth branches on Jan. 27
topic_titles:
- Education & Learning
---

Starting Jan. 27, The Seattle Public Library will expand hours at the Montlake Branch and adjust schedules at the Capitol Hill and Douglass-Truth branches. The Montlake Branch will now be open on Mondays from 10 a.m. to 6 p.m., increasing the number of libraries open seven days a week from 13 to 14.

The Capitol Hill Branch will remain open every day but will reduce its hours by two on Tuesdays and Wednesdays and will close two hours earlier on Thursdays. The Douglass-Truth Branch will also stay open daily but will cut two hours from its Wednesday and Thursday schedules. All library locations, except for the University Branch, which is undergoing renovations, will continue to operate at least six days a week.

These changes aim to better serve the community while managing resources effectively. Detailed hour changes for each branch will be available to the public.
