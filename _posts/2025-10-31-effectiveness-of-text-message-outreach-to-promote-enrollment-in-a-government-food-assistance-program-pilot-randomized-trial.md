A pilot randomized trial involving 916 households in Arizona found that outreach text messages did not effectively increase enrollment in the United States Special Supplemental Nutrition Program for Women, Infants, and Children (WIC). The study aimed to assess the impact of text message outreach on eligible households but concluded that this method was ineffective in boosting participation in the program.
