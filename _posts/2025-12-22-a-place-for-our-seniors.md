Compass Housing Alliance offers emergency services, shelter, and affordable housing for families, individuals, Veterans, and Seniors. One such Senior, Eugenia, faced a life-changing illness that left her unable to work, leading to financial strain and housing loss. Having never experienced this situation before, she reached out for help and learned about Angle Lake Court, an affordable senior housing program in SeaTac. Angle Lake Court features 80 units of independent living and fosters a caring community among its residents. Eugenia expressed her gratitude, stating, “It’s a wonderful place. I’ve made so many new friends in my years here. It’s home, and I love it.” The organization encourages donations to support its mission of providing dignity, love, and respect to Seniors like Eugenia.
