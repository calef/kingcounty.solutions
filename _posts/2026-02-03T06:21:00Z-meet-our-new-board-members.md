ELAP has welcomed four new members to its Board of Directors: Heather Bresnahan from Auctane, Brad Goergen from HomeStreet Bank, Rekha Thomas from Path Forward Marketing, and Haley Tynes from Microsoft. They will work alongside the existing board members, which includes Board President Pamela Anderson of Perkins Coie, Board Vice President Ramina Dehkoda-Steele of Wong Fleming, Board Treasurer Tasha Murphy from the University of Washington, and Board Secretary Matthew Widmyer of Davis Wright Tremaine, LLP. Other notable members include representatives from T-Mobile USA, Microsoft, and various community members. The board, composed of volunteers from the legal field and other industries, plays a crucial role in shaping the organization’s strategic vision. ELAP encourages others to learn about opportunities for involvement within the organization.
