Start the year strong by focusing on positive habits and goal setting. Setting goals allows for manageable changes over time, which is essential for establishing lasting habits. A daily habit tracker is available for printing to help monitor progress and maintain motivation for New Year’s resolutions. In addition to this guidance, potential residents are encouraged to schedule personal tours or request exclusive resources to aid in their senior living journey. Transforming Age offers promotions, including up to three months of free rent and no second-person fees for new residents.
