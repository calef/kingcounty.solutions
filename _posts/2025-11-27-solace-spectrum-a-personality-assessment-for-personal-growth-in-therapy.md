A study evaluated the SOLACE Spectrum, a new tool for personality assessment aimed at enhancing understanding of individual differences and fostering personal growth. Drawing on data from 1,021 adults, the research identified six key components of personality: Stability, Optimism, Leadership, Achievement, Compassion, and Extroversion. The SOLACE Spectrum showed strong internal consistency and high test-retest reliability, suggesting it is a stable measure over time. The findings indicate that the SOLACE Spectrum can effectively inform understanding of personality traits and relationship dynamics. This tool is expected to support professionals in therapeutic, counseling, educational, and organizational settings by providing detailed feedback on personality dimensions, identifying strengths, and pinpointing areas for personal reflection and insight.
