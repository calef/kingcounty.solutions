  class=\"field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden
  field--item\"><h2>Six miles of northbound HOV lane between Sumner, Pacific to become
  express toll lane</h2><p>SUMNER – Drivers who use the northbound HOV lane on&nbsp;<a
  href=\"/travel/roads-bridges/toll-roads-bridges-tunnels/sr-167-high-occupancy-toll-hot-lanes\">State
  Route 167</a> between Sumner and Pacific should prepare for new toll rules beginning
  Monday, Jan. 12.</p><p>The&nbsp;<a href=\"/construction-planning/search-projects/sr-167-sr-410-sr-18-nb-congestion-management\">6-mile
  HOV lane</a> will convert to an express toll lane to match the rest of the SR 167
  express toll lane corridor. The lane conversion — originally planned for October
  2025, when the initial toll-system changes took effect — was delayed during work
  to repair the SR 167 bridge over Third Avenue Southwest. Crews have now completed
  the necessary repairs, allowing tolling on the HOV lane to be fully implemented.&nbsp;</p><p>Carpoolers
  who want to continue to use the express toll lanes for free must have a&nbsp;<a
  href=\"http://www.mygoodtogo.com/\"><em>Good To Go!</em> account</a>, a Flex Pass
  set to HOV mode installed in their vehicle, and at least one passenger in the vehicle.
  Motorcyclists also will need an account and a&nbsp;<em>Good To Go!</em>&nbsp;motorcycle
  pass to continue using the lanes for free. The updated rules provide a more streamlined
  and predictable experience for drivers across the region, consistent with the Interstate
  405 express toll lanes.</p><p>Drivers who use the express toll lanes without an
  account will be mailed a bill following their trip. They&nbsp;will be charged the
  higher&nbsp;<a href=\"https://mygoodtogo.com/EN/learn/how-to-pay/tolls\">pay by
  mail</a>&nbsp;toll rate ($2 more per trip) regardless of the number of passengers
  in the vehicle. Non-tolled, general-purpose lanes on SR 167 will remain available
  for drivers who choose not to use the express toll lanes.&nbsp;</p><h3>Get&nbsp;<em>Good
  To Go!</em>&nbsp;and a free pass</h3><p>With these changes, the Washington State
  Department of Transportation encourages drivers to open a&nbsp;<em>Good To Go!</em>&nbsp;account.
  It’s free to sign up, and drivers can get a&nbsp;<a href=\"https://tolling.wsdot-sites.com/sr-167-express-toll-lanes/\">promotional
  code</a> for a free Flex Pass or motorcycle pass while supplies last.&nbsp;</p><p>Passes
  also can be purchased by calling 866-936-8246. Callers should expect longer-than-usual
  wait times, as account setup and pass purchases typically increase when new rules
  take effect.</p><p>Visit&nbsp;<a href=\"http://www.mygoodtogo.com/\">MyGoodToGo.com</a>&nbsp;for
  more information about&nbsp;<a href=\"https://mygoodtogo.com/EN/learn/how-to-pay/accounts\">account
  types</a>, including options that require either a credit card on file or a prepaid
  balance that automatically replenishes.</p></div>\n      \n  </div>\n\n</article>\n\n\n

title: Tolling on Northbound SR 167 HOV lane in Sumner begins Jan. 12
topic_titles: []
---

Drivers using the northbound HOV lane on State Route 167 between Sumner and Pacific will face new toll rules starting Monday, Jan. 12. The 6-mile HOV lane will become an express toll lane, aligning it with the rest of the SR 167 express toll corridor. This change was delayed due to repairs on the SR 167 bridge over Third Avenue Southwest, which have now been completed. Carpoolers wishing to use the express toll lanes for free must have a Good To Go! account, a Flex Pass set to HOV mode in their vehicle, and at least one passenger. Motorcyclists also need an account and a Good To Go! motorcycle pass for free access. Drivers using the express toll lanes without an account will receive a bill after their trip, incurring a higher toll rate of $2 more regardless of passenger count. General-purpose lanes on SR 167 will still be available for those opting out of the express toll lanes. The Washington State Department of Transportation encourages drivers to open a Good To Go! account, which is free and includes the option for a complimentary Flex Pass or motorcycle pass while supplies last.
