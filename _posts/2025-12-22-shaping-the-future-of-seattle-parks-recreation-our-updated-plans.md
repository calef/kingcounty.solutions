Seattle Parks and Recreation has released an Updated 2020-2032 Strategic Plan and a new 2025-2028 Action Plan aimed at enhancing the city's park and recreation system. The updated strategic plan outlines a vision for a healthy, thriving, and vibrant community. It emphasizes the importance of accessibility, sustainability, and community engagement in shaping the future of parks and recreation in Seattle.

The new action plan details specific initiatives to be undertaken over the next few years, focusing on improving facilities, expanding programs, and fostering inclusivity. Community input has been a crucial element in developing these plans, reflecting the needs and desires of Seattle residents. The updated plans aim to create a more connected and equitable park system that benefits all community members.

Seattle Parks and Recreation encourages residents to review the plans and engage with the ongoing development of the city’s recreational spaces. These initiatives demonstrate a commitment to enhancing the quality of life for all Seattleites through improved access to parks and recreational activities.
