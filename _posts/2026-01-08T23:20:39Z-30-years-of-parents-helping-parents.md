Changes Parent Support Network, founded in 1996 in Seattle, celebrates 30 years of providing crucial support to parents facing challenges related to their children’s harmful or self-destructive behaviors. The organization operates on a peer-led model, emphasizing the importance of parents helping one another by sharing lived experiences and wisdom rather than offering professional advice. This grassroots approach has fostered a sense of community and solidarity among families, allowing them to confront their struggles in a non-judgmental space. 

Despite significant societal changes over the decades, the core needs of parents remain constant, highlighting the enduring relevance of Changes' model. The network has grown from a small local group to a nationwide presence, offering resources and connections to thousands of families. The support structure encourages parents who have found stability to "pay it forward" by assisting others in need. As the organization looks to the future, it continues to thrive on the principles of compassion, accountability, and resilience, reinforcing the idea that real change occurs when parents support one another. For those seeking help, Changes offers accessible resources and personal assistance to evaluate their family situations.
