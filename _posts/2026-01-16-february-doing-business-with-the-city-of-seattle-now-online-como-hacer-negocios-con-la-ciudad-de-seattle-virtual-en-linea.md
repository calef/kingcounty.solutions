The City of Seattle is hosting an online event titled "Doing Business with the City of Seattle" on February 6, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 11 a.m. PST. This event aims to inform firms interested in contracting opportunities with the city about its purchasing processes. Participants can engage with Purchasing and Contracting staff from various sectors, including consultant contracting and public works.

The sessions will cover topics such as the types of products and services the city purchases, the bid processes, and the Consultant Roster program. There will also be a Spanish-language workshop from 2 p.m. to 3 p.m. PST on the same day. Attendance is free, and no prior registration is required.

The workshops will be conducted via Microsoft Teams, with separate links and meeting IDs provided for each session, including sessions focused on goods and services, consultant contracting, and construction/public works. Participants will learn about contract opportunities and resources for women- and minority-owned businesses.
