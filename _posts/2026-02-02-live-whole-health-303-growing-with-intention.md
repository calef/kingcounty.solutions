feed_content: |-
  <p>As a Health Coach, I know what is most important to clients by simply looking at their calendars. Appointments, kids’ activities, church functions—these all tell me what really matters in their lives. As we work together, they begin to add health and well-being goals to the schedule.</p>



  <p>Adding their goals to their calendars helps them to achieve them. If they want to be more active, we block time for them to take dance lessons or hit the gym. If they want to learn how to cook, we look for classes they can attend. If they want to build more relationships, we explore volunteer opportunities or meet-ups in their community. When they set a goal, we put action steps on the calendar to make time for that goal.</p>



  <h2>Grow what matters to you by putting it on the schedule</h2>



  <p>Scheduling your goals into your day-to-day is important because “that to which we give attention grows.” Focus your attention to what you want in your life and make a commitment to it. In addition to your health and fitness goals, consider ways you can bring more peace and happiness into your life.</p>



  <h2>Make a commitment to your peace with a simple meditation</h2>



  <p>This 22-minute loving kindness meditation led by Dr. David Kearney from VA Puget Sound is a perfect way to bring more peace into your life by focusing on wishing health, happiness and peace for ourselves and others. As we focus on thoughts for peace, we find it growing in ourselves.</p>

As a health coach, the author emphasizes the importance of scheduling health and well-being goals alongside daily activities. By examining clients' calendars, the author identifies their priorities and helps them incorporate health objectives, such as exercise, cooking classes, or community engagement. Setting specific action steps on the calendar aids in achieving these goals. The author encourages individuals to focus their attention on what they want to grow in their lives, underscoring the idea that “that to which we give attention grows.” In addition to health goals, the author suggests incorporating peace and happiness into daily routines, recommending a 22-minute loving kindness meditation led by Dr. David Kearney from VA Puget Sound. This meditation aims to cultivate health, happiness, and peace for oneself and others. The concept of Whole Health focuses on personal aspirations rather than problems, inviting individuals to reflect on what they wish to achieve in the year ahead and to take actionable steps toward their success.
