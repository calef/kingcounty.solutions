Lil Poppa, the Jacksonville rapper born Janarious Mykel Wheeler, has died at the age of 25. His death was confirmed by Georgia's Fulton County Medical Examiner’s Office, but the cause remains undisclosed. Wheeler was pronounced dead on Wednesday at 11:23 a.m. ET. He gained recognition after signing with Yo Gotti’s Collective Music Group (CMG) in 2022, following the success of his "Under Investigation" mixtape series. The most recent installment, “Under Investigation 3,” was released that same year, and he also launched his album "Almost Normal Again" in August 2022, featuring popular singles like “Bout My Respect” and “Myself Again.” He toured nationally, including a 20-date "Almost Normal Again Tour," and had previously performed with Rod Wave on the “Last Lap Tour.” Lil Poppa had an upcoming performance scheduled for March 21 at The Fillmore in New Orleans and released his latest single, “Out of Town Bae,” on February 13. His untimely death has shocked fans and the music community, as he was actively creating and promoting new music.
