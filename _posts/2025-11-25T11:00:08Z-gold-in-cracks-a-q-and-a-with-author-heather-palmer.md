Heather Palmer, a 500-hour certified yoga teacher and author, discusses her memoir, "Gold-In Cracks," which explores themes of resilience, recovery, and personal transformation. The book recounts her journey through significant life challenges, including motherhood, the loss of her first husband to cancer, and a long battle with an eating disorder. Palmer emphasizes that her struggles have become sources of strength, inspired by the Japanese art of kintsugi, which mends broken pottery with gold, symbolizing beauty born from hardship.

In her recovery, she learned to embrace imperfection and presence, moving away from a perfectionist mindset that hindered her well-being. Through practices like yoga and visualization, she developed self-trust and transformed her relationship with her body. Palmer stresses the importance of spirituality in her healing process, emphasizing the need to connect inwardly rather than seeking external validation.

Palmer aims to inspire others who face similar struggles, encouraging them to recognize their own strengths and the potential for healing. Her message is clear: everyone has "gold" within their cracks, and recovery is possible at any stage in life. For those needing support, resources are available through The Emily Program.
