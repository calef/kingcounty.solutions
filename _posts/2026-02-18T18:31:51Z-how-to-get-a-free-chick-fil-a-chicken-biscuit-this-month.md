Chick-fil-A is celebrating its 80th anniversary with a “Newstalgia” promotion, offering a free Chicken Biscuit to Chick-fil-A One rewards members from February 17 to February 28. The promotion allows members to redeem the breakfast item through the Chick-fil-A app during breakfast hours, from restaurant opening until 10:30 a.m., while supplies last. Each member can claim one biscuit without needing to make a purchase. The Chicken Biscuit features a seasoned, hand-breaded boneless chicken breast served on a freshly baked buttermilk biscuit. This giveaway is part of a yearlong celebration that includes new menu items, retro packaging, and a nationwide sweepstakes for free food for a year. The Chicken Biscuit was first introduced in 1986, coinciding with the opening of Chick-fil-A's first standalone restaurant. Khalilah Cooper, the company's vice president of brand strategy, expressed the aim of the celebration as a way to honor the memories and experiences shared by customers over the years. New menu additions also include Frosted Sodas and Floats, enhancing the brand's commitment to quality and hospitality.
