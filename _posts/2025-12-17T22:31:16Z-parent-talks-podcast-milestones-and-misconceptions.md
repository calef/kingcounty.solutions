The latest episode of the Parent Talks podcast features Encompass Developmental Specialist Emily Arkus, who addresses common developmental milestones in children and the myths surrounding them. Arkus is part of the Early Support for Infants and Toddlers team and offers insights on when caregivers should seek professional help if they have concerns about their child's development. The episode aims to provide clarity on important parenting questions related to child growth and developmental expectations. Listeners are encouraged to tune in for expert advice and valuable information regarding childhood development. The podcast is produced by Encompass, an organization focused on family enrichment. For more episodes, listeners can visit the Encompass website.
