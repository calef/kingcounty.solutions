The Seattle Department of Neighborhoods' P-Patch Community Gardening Program plans to increase plot fees for the 2025-2026 biennium. This adjustment is allowed under Seattle Municipal Code Section 3.35.060, which gives the Director of the Department of Neighborhoods the authority to modify base P-Patch fees. The proposed changes aim to support the program's sustainability and maintenance. Community members are encouraged to provide feedback on the proposed fee increase. A public comment period will be open, allowing residents to share their thoughts and concerns regarding the changes. The department will consider this feedback before finalizing any adjustments to the fees. The new fee structure will be implemented in 2025 and is intended to ensure that P-Patch community gardens remain accessible while covering necessary operational costs.
