  class=\"field field--name-body field--type-text-with-summary field--label-hidden
  field--item\"><p>ENUMCLAW – Emergency work to repair the roadway on State Route
  410 in Enumclaw will begin as early as Monday, Dec. 22.&nbsp;</p><p>Construction
  crews working under an emergency contract will repair the damaged portion of SR
  410 near milepost 26 at Boise Creek. Emergency repairs include rebuilding the foundation
  of the roadway and repaving the road surface. Once construction begins, contractors
  will work around the clock to complete the repairs and restore travel in the eastbound
  lane.</p><p>Maintenance crews working for the Washington State Department of Transportation
  closed the highway Wednesday, Dec. 10, between Enumclaw and Greenwater (mileposts
  25-43) due to water over the roadway. The eastbound lane of SR 410 was severely
  eroded by floodwater near milepost 26. On Tuesday, Dec. 16, crews reopened the highway
  with a single-lane, temporary gravel bypass constructed on the westbound shoulder
  of SR 410.</p><p>The route remains limited to a single lane with limited access,
  a lower speed limit and no estimated time for restoring two-way traffic. People
  traveling in the area, including those with limited reservations at Crystal Mountain,
  should expect delays and traffic backups due to the temporary signal that will alternate
  single-lane traffic. Once construction begins, travelers may encounter additional
  delays in either direction for construction activities.</p></div>\n      \n  </div>\n\n</article>\n\n\n

title: Emergency repairs set to begin on SR 410 in Enumclaw
topics: []
---

Emergency repairs on State Route 410 in Enumclaw will start as early as Monday, Dec. 22. Crews will work under an emergency contract to repair the roadway near milepost 26 at Boise Creek. The repairs will involve rebuilding the road's foundation and repaving the surface. Construction crews will operate around the clock to restore travel in the eastbound lane.

The Washington State Department of Transportation closed the highway on Dec. 10 between Enumclaw and Greenwater due to flooding. The eastbound lane was severely eroded by floodwaters. A temporary gravel bypass on the westbound shoulder was opened on Dec. 16, allowing single-lane traffic.

Currently, the route is limited to one lane with reduced access, a lower speed limit, and no estimated timeline for restoring full two-way traffic. Travelers in the area, including those with reservations at Crystal Mountain, should prepare for delays and potential traffic backups caused by a signal that alternates single-lane traffic. Construction will likely cause further delays in both directions once it begins.
