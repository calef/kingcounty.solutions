Contractor crews for King County Metro will begin construction on the Atlantic Base Yard Facility, focusing on upgrading the overhead wires that power trolley buses. This project aims to enhance the facility's infrastructure, ensuring better service and reliability for public transportation. Key improvements will include modernizing the power supply system and making other necessary upgrades to support the trolley bus operations. The construction is part of Metro's commitment to maintaining and improving its facilities to meet the growing demand for public transit. The upgrades are expected to contribute to a more efficient and sustainable transit system in the region.
