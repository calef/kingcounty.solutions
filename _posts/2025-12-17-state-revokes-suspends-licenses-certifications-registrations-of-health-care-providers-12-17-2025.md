  March 2024. Placement on the registry prohibits Adams from working with or having
  unsupervised access to vulnerable adults. She did not respond to the department’s
  requests for information.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Chelan County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In

  to the department’s request for information.</p>\n\n<h2><strong>Clallam County</strong></h2>\n\n<p>In

The Washington State Department of Health has revoked or suspended the licenses, certifications, or registrations of multiple health care providers due to various violations, including financial exploitation and abuse of vulnerable adults. Key actions include the indefinite suspension of Lisa Ann Adams, who was found to have financially exploited a vulnerable adult, and Jose Antonio Vasquez, who faces a similar charge. Amanda Renee Dorame's credential was rescinded after she applied despite a felony narcotics conviction. Antonio Terrell Dailey's medical assistant credential was suspended following charges of sexual misconduct. Other suspensions include Akira Trudie Jackson for failing to complete a required mental examination and Amber Lei Martorana for mentally abusing a vulnerable adult. Michelle L. Gonzales and Kiara Joanne Respo also received indefinite suspensions for failing to meet continuing education requirements and financial exploitation, respectively. Additionally, Nancy Ann Cope's medical assistant credential was suspended pending further disciplinary action. The Department encourages consumers to report any unprofessional behavior by health care providers. This ongoing effort aims to ensure public safety and uphold the standards of health care practices in Washington.
