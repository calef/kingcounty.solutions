The Seattle Police Department collaborated with the Seattle CARE Department, Accenture, and Operation Warm to support students at Rising Star Elementary School. This partnership resulted in the distribution of hundreds of new winter coats to children in need. The initiative aims to ensure that every child stays warm during the cold months and promotes a sense of hope and community. The effort highlights the commitment of local organizations to improve the lives of young students and their families during the holiday season.
