feed_content: |-
  <p>With overnight temperatures below 30°F in the forecast, King County Regional Homelessness Authority is shifting to <strong>Tier 3 Protocols as of 5:00 pm Thursday, January 22, lasting through Saturday, January 24 and winding down in the morning on Sunday, January 25</strong>, with the possibility of extending in alignment with its severe weather policy.</p>



  <p>Tier 3, the most severe level, means KCRHA will</p>



  <ul>
  <li>coordinate with transit and transportation partners to shelter sites (for example, for medically fragile or disabled individuals)</li>



  <li>open emergency daytime and overnight warming centers and day centers, including libraries</li>



  <li>where applicable: coordinate with cities to open local city halls and other city-owned properties for warming centers and/or 24/7 emergency shelters.</li>
  </ul>



  <p>Westside Neighbors Shelter will continue with increased cot capacity of 45 people and one additional overnight staff member. When the Severe Weather advisory ends, the shelter will return to its normal winter operation of 35 cots and two overnight staff members.</p>



  <p>Donations of food and warm clothing are needed throughout the area. You can drop off donations for WSNS at the American Legion Hall, 3620 SW Alaska St, or send them via our <a>Amazon wish list</a>.</p>



  <p>Thank you!</p>

King County Regional Homelessness Authority is activating Tier 3 Protocols starting at 5 p.m. on January 22, due to forecasted overnight temperatures below 30°F. This severe weather response will last through January 24, with a potential extension based on weather conditions. Under Tier 3, KCRHA will work with transit partners to ensure access to shelter sites for vulnerable populations, including medically fragile individuals. Emergency warming centers will be available, including libraries, and local city halls may also serve as warming centers or 24/7 emergency shelters. The Westside Neighbors Shelter will increase its cot capacity to 45 people and add an overnight staff member during this period. Once the severe weather advisory concludes, the shelter will revert to its standard winter operations of 35 cots and two staff members. Donations of food and warm clothing are urgently needed in the community. Items can be dropped off at the American Legion Hall located at 3620 SW Alaska St, or through an Amazon wish list.
