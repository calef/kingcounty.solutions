Burien will host its annual Empty Bowls event to raise funds for local food banks. The event is set for March 7, 2024, from 5 p.m. to 7 p.m. at the Burien Community Center. Attendees can enjoy a simple meal of soup and bread while selecting a handmade bowl created by local artists. Each bowl serves as a reminder of those in need. Tickets for the event are $15 and can be purchased in advance or at the door. All proceeds will support local food banks to help combat hunger in the community. The event aims to bring together residents to help those facing food insecurity while enjoying a sense of community.
