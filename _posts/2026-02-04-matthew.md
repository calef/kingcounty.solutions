feed_content: "<h4><em>“I don't have to worry about food.”</em></h4> \n<p>When Matthew
  was 4 years old, he left South Korea to be adopted by an American family. Unfortunately,
  he was a victim of abuse and was placed in the foster care system. He was adopted
  by three different families during this time, leading to instability from an early
  age.</p> \n<p>With a childhood marked by abuse, death, and sorrow, he went to the
  streets to escape.</p> \n<p>“I was on the streets out here for 15 years. I had been
  to the Mission before. I had low income housing, but 5 days before Christmas the
  apartment management put us out on the street. Ended up losing my apartment, so
  I ended up back at  the Mission.”</p> \n<p>Tired of living in instability on the
  streets, Matthew came to the Bread of Life Mission seeking stability and hope.</p>
  \n<p>“My biggest challenge before coming to the Bread of Life Mission was trying
  to socialize more,” Matthew says. \"I I don't know. I just- I don't really go out.
  I was just working and going home.\"</p> \n<p><strong>“The most important thing
  I have learned is how to get along with people, because I'm one of those people
  that just stays to myself.”</strong></p> \n<p>After enduring so many painful circumstances,
  Matthew is relieved that the Mission is there for him. “I don't have to worry about
  food. I don't have to worry about a place to sleep.” he says.</p> \n<p>In response
  to people criticizing homeless people, he says \"You're one paycheck away from being
  on out here where I'm at. They don't realize how hard people that are on the streets
  have it - the struggles they go through from day-to-day, even just to get a meal
  or a place to sleep.\"</p> \n<p><strong>Your support has filled Matthew’s life with
  stability, a roof over his head, and newfound hope for the future. </strong></p>

Matthew, who was adopted from South Korea at age 4, faced a tumultuous childhood marked by abuse and instability. After being placed in foster care and adopted by three different families, he found himself on the streets for 15 years following the loss of his low-income housing just days before Christmas. Seeking stability and hope, Matthew returned to the Bread of Life Mission, where he found support and relief from his daily struggles.

He reflects on his challenges with socialization, stating he previously preferred isolation, but has learned to connect with others through his experiences at the Mission. Matthew expresses gratitude for the Mission’s assistance, saying, “I don’t have to worry about food. I don’t have to worry about a place to sleep.” He highlights the misconceptions about homelessness, reminding people that anyone could find themselves in a similar situation. With the help of the Mission, Matthew has gained a sense of stability and hope for the future, transforming his life amidst the difficulties he has faced.
