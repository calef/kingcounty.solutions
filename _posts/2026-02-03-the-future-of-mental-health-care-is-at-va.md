feed_content: |-
  <p>The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is leading the nation in transforming mental health care for America’s Veterans and we’re looking for passionate mental health care providers to join us on that journey. </p>



  <p>As patient needs continue to evolve, VA offers a unique environment where evidence-based care, cutting-edge research and innovative technologies come together to support healing, recovery and resilience. For mental health care providers seeking a career with purpose, support and impact, there’s never been a more exciting time to explore opportunities at VA.</p>



  <h2>Impactful innovation and research</h2>



  <p>VA’s Office of Research and Development is one of the largest health research organizations in the country, and <a>mental health</a> is a core priority. VA researchers are advancing our understanding of mental health conditions like posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD), depression and suicide risk, while trialing novel treatments and delivery models that improve outcomes for Veterans. Highlights include:</p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <a>Immersive technologies</a> like virtual reality are being used to treat substance abuse, depression and anxiety.</li>



  <li>VA’s <a>National Center for PTSD</a> (NCPTSD), the world’s leading research and educational center of excellence on PTSD, makes strides in care via evidence-based therapies, medications, behavioral interventions and therapeutic devices.</li>



  <li>
  <a>Telehealth offerings</a> to increase mental health care access for Veterans living in rural and underserved areas.</li>
  </ul>



  <h2>Mental health care roles at VA</h2>



  <p>VA has openings for qualified and caring mental health professionals at all stages of their career, including:</p>



  <ul>
  <li>
  <a>Psychiatrists</a>.</li>



  <li>
  <a>Psychologists</a>.</li>



  <li>
  <a>Social workers</a>.</li>



  <li>
  <a>And more</a>.</li>
  </ul>



  <p>Whether you’re early in your career or an experienced clinician, VA supports your professional growth through continuing education, interdisciplinary collaboration and avenues into research.</p>



  <h2>VA job benefits to support your lifestyle</h2>



  <p>VA takes pride in caring for our employees as they care for our Veterans. VA offers comprehensive employee benefits, predictable schedules, generous leave and a strong support system. It’s <a>rewarding work</a> with rewarding benefits.</p>



  <h2>Join our team</h2>



  <p>The future of mental health care is being shaped each and every day at VA, and we’re always looking for qualified professionals to join our mission. If you’re All About Veterans like we are, consider a mental health care career with VA.</p>



  <p>Learn more about mental health care careers at <a>VA Careers</a>.</p>

The Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) is transforming mental health care for Veterans and is seeking dedicated mental health providers to join its efforts. The VA combines evidence-based care, innovative technologies, and research to address the evolving needs of patients. Its Office of Research and Development focuses on mental health, studying conditions such as posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD), depression, and suicide risk while exploring new treatments. Technologies like virtual reality are being utilized to treat substance abuse, depression, and anxiety. The National Center for PTSD is a leading research institution that develops evidence-based therapies and interventions.

The VA is hiring mental health professionals, including psychiatrists, psychologists, and social workers, offering support for career growth through education and collaboration. Employee benefits include comprehensive packages, predictable schedules, generous leave, and a strong support system. VA emphasizes the importance of caring for its employees as they care for Veterans. The VA is committed to shaping the future of mental health care and encourages qualified individuals to consider a career with its team.
