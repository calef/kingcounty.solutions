title: Warming centers and overnight shelters open in King County through Friday
topic_titles:

As temperatures in King County, Washington, are expected to drop below freezing through Friday, local authorities are opening warming centers and overnight shelters to support those in need. The initiative aims to provide safe refuge for individuals experiencing homelessness during the severe cold. Locations in cities such as Auburn, Burien, and Renton will offer shelter options. For more detailed regional sheltering information, residents are encouraged to consult the King County Regional Homelessness Authority. The announcement emphasizes the importance of community awareness, urging residents to check on neighbors and stay informed about local services and alerts. Residents can utilize an experimental mapping tool to see if they are in an alert zone. King County Emergency Management recommends that individuals prepare for cold weather and remain updated through local media and social media channels.
