  <p>SPOKANE, Wash. — <a>MultiCare Valley Hospital</a> has been recognized as a Top Hospital and given an “A” grade. Both awards were given by <a>The Leapfrog Group<span></span></a> in fall 2025, recognizing the highest commitment to patient safety and quality of care.</p>
  <p>Valley Hospital is the only hospital in the Inland Northwest region to receive this honor.</p>
  <p>The Leapfrog Group, a Washington, D.C.-based organization aiming to improve health care quality and safety for consumers and purchasers, released its new Hospital Safety Grades December 15. Known as the “gold standard” of patient safety ratings, Leapfrog rates and assigns A, B, C, D or F grades to approximately 2,500 general hospitals across the country.</p>
  <p>“At MultiCare, we are committed to patient safety and high-quality care, and to be recognized by Leapfrog is a distinct honor,” said Wade Hunt, MultiCare’s Inland Northwest region president and market leader. “I’m so proud of the commitment MultiCare Valley Hospital demonstrates to our community on a daily basis.”</p>
  <p>To qualify for the distinction as a Top Hospital, hospitals must rank top among peers on the Leapfrog Hospital Survey, which assesses hospital performance on the highest standards for quality and patient safety, and achieve top performance in their category. MultiCare Valley Hospital in Spokane, Washington, has been awarded the title of Top Hospital and an “A” grade by The Leapfrog Group for fall 2025, highlighting its commitment to patient safety and quality of care. It is the only hospital in the Inland Northwest to receive this distinction. The Leapfrog Group, based in Washington, D.C., aims to enhance healthcare quality and safety for consumers. Its Hospital Safety Grades, released on December 15, evaluate around 2,500 general hospitals nationwide, assigning grades from A to F. Wade Hunt, president of MultiCare's Inland Northwest region, expressed pride in the hospital's dedication to the community and the recognition from Leapfrog. To achieve the Top Hospital designation, institutions must excel in the Leapfrog Hospital Survey, which measures performance against the highest standards for quality and safety.
