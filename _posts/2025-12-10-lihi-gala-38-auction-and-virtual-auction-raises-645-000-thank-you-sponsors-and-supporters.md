Rev. Lawrence Willis of Love-To presented gifts to Sharon Lee, the executive director of the Low Income Housing Institute (LIHI), during the Gala & Auction event. The event took place on Friday evening at the Four Seasons and included a Virtual Auction that ran from November 19 to December 3. Together, these efforts raised a record total of $645,000. This was the first in-person Gala & Auction since the pandemic, and attendees enjoyed a lively evening filled with bidding on auction items, games, and desserts. LIHI expressed gratitude to all participants and supporters who contributed to the success of the event.
