Firefighters responded to a residential fire in the Capitol Hill neighborhood of Seattle on Wednesday afternoon. The Seattle Fire Department dispatched crews to the 2300 block of 12th Avenue East after receiving reports of flames visible from a single-story home that had been converted into a triplex. Upon arrival, firefighters quickly accessed the fire through a window and extinguished the flames by approximately 3:30 p.m. Fortunately, no injuries were reported. The cause of the fire remains unclear, and officials have not yet disclosed information regarding potential damage or whether any residents were displaced. Fire crews continued to monitor the scene as the situation developed.
