  <p>The 2026 Legislative Session is underway! Beginning on January 12, this year’s session will be a “short session” – a 60-day session that is the second year of the biennium (two-year budget period). During this session, the legislature can make adjustments to the budget passed last year, continue with legislation introduced last year that did not make it all the way through the process, and/or introduce legislation for new or revised policies or budget considerations.</p>



  <p></p>



  <hr>



  <p></p>



  <h3><strong><strong>2026 Legislative Priorities</strong></strong></h3>



  <div>
  <div>
  <p>During this year’s session, BrightSpark will be collaborating on supporting Child Care Aware of Washington’s Campaign priorities:</p>



  <ul>
  <li>Protect Washington’s Early Learning Safety Net</li>



  <li>Codify the Cost of Quality Care Rate Model</li>



  <li>New, Progressive Revenue to Stabilize our State’s Economy</li>
  </ul>



  <p>Over the next few weeks of the 2026 Legislative Session, we will help breakdown these priorities and help you understand what these mean for everyone in our community. But for now, stay up to date with the <a><strong>Child Care for Washington Campaign</strong></a>.</p>



  <p></p>
  </div>
  </div>

  <h3>Track Bills Throughout the Session</h3>



  <p>As legislation makes its way through session, we will be following bill progress using our partner <a><u><strong>Start Early’s bill tracker</strong></u></a>; which is updated each week with the early learning bills that are under consideration by the legislature.</p>

The 2026 Legislative Session began on January 12, kicking off a 60-day short session during the second year of the biennium. During this session, the legislature will adjust last year's budget, continue unfinished legislation, and introduce new policies. BrightSpark is collaborating with Child Care Aware of Washington to support key priorities, including protecting Washington's early learning safety net, codifying the cost of quality care rate model, and advocating for new revenue to stabilize the state's economy.

BrightSpark plans to provide updates on these priorities and their implications for the community throughout the session. An Advocacy Day is scheduled for February 3, 2026, from 9 AM to 4 PM at United Churches of Olympia, inviting community members to participate.

As legislation progresses, BrightSpark will track bill developments using a partner's bill tracker, offering weekly updates on early learning bills. Advocacy Alerts will notify the public about time-sensitive actions regarding specific legislation. BrightSpark encourages advocacy year-round through its Advocacy Pods, providing monthly updates and meetings for supporters. Financial contributions from the community are essential to sustain BrightSpark's advocacy efforts.
