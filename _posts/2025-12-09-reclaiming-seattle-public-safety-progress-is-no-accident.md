In 2023, Seattle voters emphasized the importance of public safety, urging City Hall to prioritize it. The city has focused on its fundamental duty to protect and enhance the health, safety, environment, and welfare of its residents. Nearly two years after this voter mandate, significant efforts have been made to improve public safety. The commitment to these goals reflects the city's ongoing work to address the needs of the community and reinforce safety as a core responsibility.
