The Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS) provides a customizable letter in Spanish for preventive services. This resource is part of the Coverage to Care initiative, which aims to improve health outcomes by encouraging the use of preventive services. The letter can be tailored to meet individual needs, making it easier for Spanish-speaking communities to understand and access available preventive services. This effort reflects CMS's commitment to enhancing health care access and promoting wellness among diverse populations. The customizable letter is designed to facilitate communication about preventive care options, ensuring that language barriers do not hinder access to vital health information and services.
