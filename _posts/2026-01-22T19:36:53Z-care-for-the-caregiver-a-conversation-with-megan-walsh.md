In a recent episode of the Parent Talks podcast, Megan Walsh, the Pediatric Therapy Director at Encompass, emphasizes the crucial need for self-care among parents and caregivers. The podcast discusses the common tendency for caregivers to prioritize the needs of others over their own, which can lead to stress and burnout. Walsh uses the metaphor of putting on one's own oxygen mask first, highlighting that caregivers must take care of themselves to effectively support their families. She encourages listeners to recognize the signals their bodies send when they need help and offers strategies for finding relief and support. The conversation aims to empower caregivers to prioritize their well-being, ultimately enabling them to be more present and effective in their roles. Additional resources, including a blog post on nervous system care and a parenting workshop titled "Balanced and Brave," are also provided to further assist caregivers in their self-care journey.
