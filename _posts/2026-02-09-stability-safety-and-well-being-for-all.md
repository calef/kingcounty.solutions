At MSC, the mission is to provide essential services and safety-net programs for individuals and families in the community, focusing on reducing suffering and promoting safety, dignity, and belonging. The organization believes everyone deserves stability, housing, food, and care, regardless of their background or challenges. In response to national harm, MSC emphasizes the importance of community and partnerships to create a positive impact.

The organization remains dedicated to delivering services and increasing equitable access, relying on community support to continue its work. Many families served are immigrants who depend on MSC for consistent access to food and basic needs. MSC aims to ensure that all individuals can access care without fear or discrimination, standing in solidarity with immigrant families.

MSC prioritizes compassion, respect, and confidentiality in its services and appreciates partner organizations that offer legal aid and advocacy for those facing immigration challenges. The local restaurant community is recognized for its support, particularly during events like Restaurant Week, which helps raise funds for MSC's initiatives. The organization thanks the community for its ongoing commitment to care, dignity, and collective action.
