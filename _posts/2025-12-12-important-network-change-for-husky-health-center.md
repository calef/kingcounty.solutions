Individuals and their dependents with health insurance through specific plans may be affected by a network change at Husky Health Center and other UW Medicine facilities. Starting January 1, 2026, these facilities will no longer accept certain insurance coverages, potentially limiting access to in-network care for those enrolled in the affected plans. Those who currently have coverage through the specified plans should review their options to understand how this change may impact their healthcare access in the future.
