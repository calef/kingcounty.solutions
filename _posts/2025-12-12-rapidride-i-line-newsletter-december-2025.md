King County Metro is expanding its RapidRide system to better serve the growing population. As part of this initiative, Metro is inviting artists to submit proposals for the "Moving Stories" project. This project aims to enhance the RapidRide experience by incorporating artwork that reflects the community's stories and culture. Selected artists will have the chance to create visual installations along the RapidRide routes, helping to make public transit more engaging and meaningful for riders. The call for artists is open to individuals and groups, encouraging a diverse range of artistic expressions. Interested applicants should prepare to submit their ideas and qualifications, with a focus on projects that resonate with the values and experiences of the community. The deadline for submissions is approaching, and Metro emphasizes the importance of creativity in transforming public spaces. This initiative not only aims to beautify the transit system but also to foster a sense of connection among riders, making the RapidRide experience more enjoyable.
