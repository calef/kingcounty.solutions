Veterans can access a wide range of year-round discounts without fees or outdated information, thanks to a new verified list published by the VA. This list, which was last updated on December 12, 2025, covers various categories including automotive, clothing, electronics, health, travel, and more.

Some notable discounts include 10% off at Advance Auto Parts, 15% off at Adidas, and $500 off select vehicle purchases from brands like Ford, Honda, and Toyota. In the realm of entertainment, eligible veterans can enjoy discounts at Disneyland, Universal Studios, and Cinemark. Health and wellness discounts are also available with 10% off at BodyBuilding.com and various offers on fitness memberships.

The discounts extend to home goods with 10% off at Home Depot and special pricing on mattresses. For those interested in subscriptions, services like Disney+ and Hulu offer reduced rates for veterans. The VA emphasizes that while the list is comprehensive, individuals should verify details with the respective organizations, as restrictions may apply. This initiative aims to help veterans maximize their savings and enhance their financial well-being.
