The Atlantic Street Center (ASC) celebrates the Seattle Seahawks' recent Super Bowl victory, highlighting the team's longstanding support for the organization. ASC has benefited from the Seahawks' engagement, with players and staff actively participating in community events and providing assistance to families in need. Former quarterback Geno Smith, for instance, distributed food gift cards before Thanksgiving. The Seahawks have also sponsored events, given presents to families during the holiday season, and invited ASC's Summer Academy students to practice sessions.

ASC’s success is further bolstered by other Seattle sports teams, including the Kraken, Mariners, Seawolves, Sounders, and Storm. The Kraken have donated tickets and signed merchandise, while the Mariners have provided game tickets to create memorable experiences for families. The Storm ensures youth can attend basketball games, enhancing community involvement. Additionally, Rick Rizzs and Toys for Kids support ASC with school supplies and holiday gifts for over 650 children annually. These collaborations underscore the role of sports in fostering community unity and supporting local families. The ASC expresses gratitude to the Seahawks and all Seattle teams for their contributions.
