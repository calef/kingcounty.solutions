STEVENS PASS – Repairs are underway on US 2 near Stevens Pass, specifically between Scenic (milepost 58) and Mill Creek Road (milepost 71). Work started on Dec. 18 to address several damaged locations. Crews have made enough progress in clearing debris and stabilizing the roadway to allow for the reopening of this section to daytime pilot car operations beginning Monday, Dec. 29.
