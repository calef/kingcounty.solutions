Ballard Senior Center will be closed on Monday in observance of Presidents’ Day. The dining menu for the week of February 17th to 20th includes a variety of meals.

On Tuesday, the menu features Beef Picadillo over Rice, Quesadillas, Lime & Citrus Slaw, along with Fruit and Dessert. Wednesday's offering includes Cheese Raviolis with veggie marinara, Garden Salad, and Fruit.

Thursday's meal consists of Chicken Tiki Masala with Rice, Cucumber & Tomato Salad, Nanna, and Fruit and Dessert. On Friday, a Sub Sandwich will be served alongside Veggie Soup, Yogurt, and Fruit.

Additionally, there will be a Wednesday Night Dinner, which requires an RSVP, featuring Jambalaya with Rice, Garden Salad, Cornbread, and Dessert.
