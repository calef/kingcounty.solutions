In a pastoral letter dated January 27, 2026, Archbishop Paul D. Etienne emphasizes the importance of unity and common good among humanity. He advocates for a society grounded in truth, justice, and peace, urging individuals to prioritize basic human decency and integrity over political affiliations and agendas. The Archbishop calls for a collective effort to foster harmony and serve the common good, highlighting that this is a fundamental aspiration shared by all people. He encourages readers to abandon divisive loyalties and instead focus on building a well-ordered society that benefits everyone. The letter reflects the Archbishop's commitment to promoting values essential for peaceful coexistence and mutual respect within communities.
