  <p><span>“Jason brings steady, proven leadership to this critical role, positioning the UW for sustained excellence in financial management,” Jones said. “His unwavering commitment to strategic financial stewardship will be instrumental as he leads the charge in advancing our institution’s fiscal integrity and operational effectiveness.” </span></p>

His leadership during COVID was instrumental in structuring a successful FEMA emergency funding grant application that resulted in $30 million in initial COVID grant awards. </span></p>

  <p><span>“It is a privilege to continue serving the UW in this capacity and I am grateful for the trust placed in me. I look forward to continuing to collaborate with the community in our shared work to advance the UW’s mission,” Campbell said. </span></p>

Jason Campbell has been appointed as the chief financial officer and senior vice president of finance, planning and budgeting at the University of Washington, effective Jan. 1. Campbell has served as interim CFO since Aug. 1, following the departure of Sarah Norris Hall. University President Robert J. Jones praised Campbell's leadership, emphasizing his commitment to strategic financial stewardship and operational effectiveness. Since joining the UW in 2017, Campbell has demonstrated consistent leadership and has improved financial operations and budget processes. His work during the COVID-19 pandemic was notable, as he successfully led a FEMA emergency funding grant application that secured $30 million in initial COVID grants. Provost Tricia R. Serio highlighted Campbell's strategic vision for central finance and his focus on operational excellence and financial transparency. Prior to his role at UW, Campbell held senior positions in higher education, including associate vice president for business and finance at the Keck Graduate Institute and senior director of operations for the Western University of Health Sciences. Campbell earned his bachelor's degree from the University of California, Santa Barbara, and a master's degree in accounting from California State University, Sacramento.
