Boyer Children's Clinic played a crucial role in the life of Jack and his family during a time of need. Jack's mother, Gretchen, expressed gratitude for the dedicated care he received, highlighting how the team viewed him as an individual with unique personality and challenges. The therapists not only celebrated Jack's achievements but also supported him through difficulties, emphasizing that progress varies for each child.

The clinic's playgroup provided a safe and enjoyable environment for Jack to learn with his peers, while also allowing his family to connect with others facing similar experiences. Boyer became a second home for them, fostering Jack’s growth and offering essential support.

Saying goodbye to Boyer was emotionally challenging for Gretchen and her family, as they had formed strong bonds with the staff, who had become like family. The clinic not only aided Jack’s development but also equipped his family with the necessary tools to move forward. The family remains thankful for the love and care received at Boyer, which significantly impacted their journey.
