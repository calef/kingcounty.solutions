  <div>
  <p><strong>Everyone loves a party, but there’s something special about attending one that leaves you with a true sense of purpose. </strong>That’s exactly what hundreds of guests experience at <a>One Circle’s</a> annual One Heart/One Community <em>Party With a Purpose</em>.</p>



  <p>The beloved One Heart/One Community event brings together neighbors, philanthropists, and local business owners in support of a shared mission: preventing hunger for children in Bellevue. </p>
  </div>
  </div>



  <h3><strong>A Heartwarming Success</strong></h3>



  <p>Circle members poured their hearts and energy into making the event a meaningful success. From generous sponsorships to guests raising their paddles in the room, more than <strong>$563,497</strong> was raised.</p>



  <div>
  <div>
  <figure><img src="https://www.bellevuelifespring.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/One-Heart-2025-212-edited-1200x1200.jpg"><figcaption>Guest raising her bid card to give.</figcaption></figure>
  </div>



  <div>
  <figure><img src="https://www.bellevuelifespring.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/One-Heart-2025-294-edited-1200x1200.jpg"><figcaption>Guest raising his bid card to give.</figcaption></figure>
  </div>



  <div>
  <figure><img src="https://www.bellevuelifespring.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/One-Heart-2025-338-edited-1200x1200.jpg"><figcaption>Guest raising her bid card to give.</figcaption></figure>
  </div>
  </div>



  <p>“The One Heart/One Community event represents 72 hardworking women who are willing to go above and beyond,” said One Circle Member Schanon Odell. “At the end of the day, our goal is to raise money as a community. If we can raise funds and have a wonderful time doing it, that’s a win-win.”</p>



  <p>The energy in the room was contagious, with guests engaging in meaningful conversation and showing their commitment to giving back.</p>



  <div>
  <div>
  <figure><img src="https://www.bellevuelifespring.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/One-Heart-2025-186-edited-1-1200x900.jpg"><figcaption>Guests mingling together.</figcaption></figure>
  </div>



  <div>
  <figure><img src="https://www.bellevuelifespring.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/One-Heart-2025-378-edited-1200x900.jpg"><figcaption>Bellevue School District Superintendent Dr. Kelly Aramaki and One Circle Member Elise Lee.</figcaption></figure>
  </div>
  </div>



  <p>“Our role as a circle is to help families get back on a path toward stability and long-term prosperity,” shared One Circle Member Gardenia Banks. “I hope our party plants a seed. One day, the families we serve may be in a position not only to thrive, but to give back and serve others. That cycle—receiving help, regaining stability, and eventually becoming a giver—is the true, lasting impact.”</p>



  <p>Gardenia shared a reflection from one of her business partners, whom she invited to the event and who once received assistance herself. “I was one of those families. A single mom with two young boys, and someone’s generosity helped me get back on my feet. Now, I’m grateful to contribute because I’ve seen firsthand that it truly makes a difference. I believe in what Bellevue LifeSpring is doing.”</p>



  <p><strong>Special Thank You</strong></p>



  <p>The event’s Presenting Partners were Anu and Naveen Jain, founders of Viome, whose continued support and generosity help fuel Bellevue LifeSpring’s mission.</p>



  <p>The event would not have been possible without the leadership of One Circle Co-Chairs Liz Hale and Annemarie Marcus, and One Circle event Co-Chairs Trish Carpenter and Cathi Hatch.</p>

Hundreds gathered at the One Circle’s annual One Heart/One Community Party With a Purpose, a fundraising event aimed at preventing hunger for children in Bellevue. The event raised over $563,497, showcasing the community's commitment to a shared mission. One Circle Member Schanon Odell emphasized the collaborative effort of 72 women dedicated to making a difference. The atmosphere was vibrant, with guests engaging in meaningful conversations and demonstrating their desire to give back. Gardenia Banks, another One Circle Member, highlighted the importance of helping families regain stability, aiming for a cycle where those once helped can eventually give back. Anu and Naveen Jain, founders of Viome, served as Presenting Partners, significantly contributing to the event's success. The leadership of Co-Chairs Liz Hale and Annemarie Marcus, along with event Co-Chairs Trish Carpenter and Cathi Hatch, was pivotal in organizing the gathering. The event not only raised funds but also inspired attendees to support one another, fostering a sense of community and purpose.
