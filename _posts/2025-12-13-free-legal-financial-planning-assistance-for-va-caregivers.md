Caregiving often involves complex legal and financial issues. To assist with these challenges, the Program of Comprehensive Assistance for Family Caregivers (PCAFC) offers legal and financial planning services tailored to injured Veterans and their primary family caregivers. This program provides support through knowledgeable concierges who can answer questions and direct caregivers to necessary resources.

Services include one-on-one support from certified concierges, personalized guidance on legal and financial matters, referrals to trusted attorneys, and educational tools. Caregivers can access 24/7 website and phone support.

The program is designed for those who are uncertain about legal or financial planning, need help managing finances, or seek guidance on finding legal resources. To use the service, caregivers connect with a concierge, receive personalized support, and utilize resources as needed—all at no cost.

Caregivers do not have to navigate these issues alone; the PCAFC aims to provide confidence and support, allowing them to focus on their primary role of caring for Veterans. For assistance, caregivers can visit the Legal and Financial Planning page or call the 24/7 Support Line at 1-833-422-7382.
